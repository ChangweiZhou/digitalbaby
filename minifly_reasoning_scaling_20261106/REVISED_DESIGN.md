# Revised reasoning benchmark and engineering scope

**November 6, 2026 · Proposed scientific experiment**

This revision keeps the [original scaling design](DESIGN.md) for capacity, exposure, diversity, and continuation. The engineering cycles revise how the test separates graph structure from the learned meaning of the operation bytes.

## Structural and opcode meaning tests

The primary structural pair begins with two active edges on one path of a diamond. Swapping one with the edge at the same depth on the other path leaves one active edge per path. Neither route can then carry the move. This preserves both the total active count and the active count on the queried diamond. An important symmetry remains. Reversing both opcode meanings preserves the expected answers to this whole panel. The original structural pairs alone cannot establish which opcode meaning the learner acquired.

The separate polarity panel has one edge of one opcode and three of the other on the diamond. Each prefix stays literally identical across the old and reversed laws. Its correct answer flips under the reversal. Evaluate it in A and again after A′. Compare the edited and no-edit continuations on identical prefixes, scoring each against its own law. Record both-correct across the two branches for each prefix, with observed-old and revised accuracy separately. This shows whether answers follow the teaching history when the query bytes stay fixed. The simpler opcode-sensitive result alone does not establish structural composition. Interpret the two endpoints jointly.

The structural interaction remains the proposed primary scaling contrast. A claim of learned rule reuse additionally requires the polarity results to distinguish edited and no-edit histories above matched cold and fixed-prompt controls. Set its quantitative margin and uncertainty rule before opening scientific worlds. A fixed, history-independent mapping of a single prompt can satisfy at most one of the two opposite outcomes.

## Differences from the original implementation proposal

- Each synthetic environment has a fixed actor layout, initial location ordering, and edge serialization order. A shared deterministic random relabeling of locations changes each 16-record block. This preserves nested dose prefixes and provides fresh records within repeated environments. The four-versus-32 contrast changes the distribution of layouts, while the learning rule, motif schedule, marginal actor exposures, and answerability remain fixed. Report actual distinct experiences and marginal imbalances before admission.
- The held-exposure check uses a conservative rule. A query with two candidate routes of length two is excluded from every feedback stream, regardless of actor names, edge order, or whether either path is active. The primary structural benchmark scores complete own answers on never-taught topologies. Holdout topology and feedback scope must be checked again on the full generated streams.
- Both widths use the archived V216 per-record sensor reset and continuing NORMAL updates. The wider path shares the stock first layer, expands the second layer and its fixed feedback, and rescales the outgoing head by the square root of the fan-in ratio. This remains a test of a defined width change within the V216 learner family.
- The engineering entry point runs a small rehearsal with public fixture seeds. Freeze fresh science families, the complete shortcut ladder, the polarity and retention guards, and the resource envelope separately before launch.

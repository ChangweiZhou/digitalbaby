# MiniFly reasoning scaling experiment design

**November 6, 2026 · Proposed development experiment**

**Question.** Does additional experience improve genuine held-out composition more when the learner has greater capacity, and does experience diversity change that result? Cross width, dose, and diversity on a single V216-derived learner and a single raw-byte task. Keep the original V216 million-event question separate because its held panel was fully solvable by canonical lookup. This design uses a new task and a new prospective lock. [Scaling program memo](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/78f6f167ceee59218a2a4cc94e3bc1627faa6b46/PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_SCALING_PROGRAM_RESET_20261106.md) · [V216 source and saved comparisons](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/tree/74d1f1544ae1d0c540cfa44f89bd0f865f6f2a1e/minifly_v216_experience_pretraining_design_20261104)

## Task and held cases

**Task.** Extend V216's location consequence records to directed links. A record initializes eight actors' locations, presents six distinct candidate edges in shuffled order, moves one actor, and asks for another actor's location. Each edge is active or inactive. Two observable opcode bytes encode these states, with their meaning randomly assigned per family and learnable from observed consequences. A move propagates along active edges. The requested actor either moves to the destination or stays at its own initial location. Use V216-style raw byte frames, one location byte as the answer, and the learner's own EOS. Put the mover and queried actor in different roles and choose a destination distinct from the queried actor's initial location for the primary panel. Keep the prefix at 47 bytes and training record at 49 symbols including the answer and EOS in all arms. The evaluator parses the graph. The candidate receives ordinary bytes and revealed next bytes under the original V216 objective. Preserve V216's per-record sensor reset and carry the learned weights and optimizer state between records.

**Teach the parts and withhold their composition.** Train on a two-edge chain, where both links matter, and on a direct path plus an indirect path, where either route is sufficient. Fill the remaining slots with disconnected edges. Hold out a diamond with two alternative two-edge paths and no direct edge. The held orbit is identified by the directed candidate topology rooted at the mover and queried actor. Strip actor names, row order, and location spellings in the orbit audit. Exclude the diamond and its equivalent forms from all feedback-bearing streams, including warmup, B, and correction. Use fresh actor names on the primary panel and report the same-vocabulary panel separately. Keeping all arms on the same task prevents a wider arm from receiving a better representation by construction.

**Make both answers in a pair matter.** Start with a diamond whose two active edges form one complete route. Swap an opcode on that route with the inactive edge at the same depth on the other route. Each path now has one active edge, so neither carries the move. The swap preserves the active-edge count even within the diamond, as well as the multiset of opcodes, locations, actors, and bytes. Counterbalance which member comes first, which route is live, whether the swap changes the first or second hop, and where the edited rows appear. Keep a separate invariant panel whose swap affects only disconnected edges. Probe each member independently from the same checkpoint. Score the full output and log premature termination and truncation.

**Qualify the evidence before training.** An independent parser and reachability solver must recover opcode meanings from taught examples and solve every held pair. Check that the taught witnesses distinguish the registered alternatives, including direct-only propagation, requiring every route to be active, and allowing inactive links to carry a move. This establishes determinacy within a declared rule family. Fit exact lookup, V216-style normalized lookup, frequency, recency, bag-of-bytes, active-edge counts, and one-link heuristics on the allowed history. A parser that explicitly follows both routes belongs with the rule solver. Reject the held roster before learner training if a surface shortcut exceeds 25% both-correct or a raw or canonical held orbit appears in feedback. Require a separate worked example and an automated check of the causal and invariant swaps. ## The scaling matrix

| Factor | Lower setting | Higher setting | What stays fixed |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Capacity | Second hidden layer 64 | Second hidden layer 128 | V216 sensor and first layer, 257 output classes, fixed-feedback NORMAL update, event format, optimizer, and warmup |
| Experience dose | 10,000 A events | 100,000 A events | Earlier checkpoint is a prefix of the same history, with the same evaluation panel |
| Diversity | Four environment instances per family | 32 instances per family | Event count, record length, rule, taught motifs, opcode and outcome balance, and total optimizer steps at each dose |

Use **eight paired development families** and four continuing lives per family. Each family uses the same rule assignment and held probes in all four lives. Within a diversity arm, the two widths consume identical byte streams. Across diversity arms, environment instances vary actor bindings, initial state, and distractor arrangements under the same rule. Balance the same byte vocabulary and marginal role exposures in each arm. Report distinct records and repeats since equal total events can carry different amounts of information. Lock 32 changed pairs and a separate invariant panel per family. Treat families as the independent units and report every family's results.

Keep the stock width-64 path unchanged and qualify any implementation refactor against V216 outputs and updates. At width 128 only the second hidden layer, the corresponding fixed feedback bank, and the connecting weight shapes expand. Use the same initialization family with the outgoing head scaled for its changed fan-in. Trainable scalars are **37,313** at width 64 and **57,921** at width 128. Width 128 is an experimental reference beyond the original V181 parameter envelope. The physical footprint and throughput require measurement. ## Measurements and decisions

**Primary endpoint.** Both answers in a changed pair must be exactly correct, including EOS. The primary interaction is the width-128 advantage over width 64 at 100,000 events minus that advantage at 10,000, under the 32-instance stream. A paired mean interaction of at least **+5 percentage points** with a 95% interval above zero supports the hypothesis at these settings. Report the other width, dose, and diversity contrasts with their paired uncertainty. Use exact individual answers, invariant pairs, first byte, termination, and taught-graph recall to explain the result. Include a same-birth no-prior-exposure probe. Preserve native outputs before joining them with gold answers.

**Carry the longer histories through later learning.** From the 100,000-event checkpoint, each life learns 10,000 B events using a different pair of observable opcode bytes. The A opcodes retain their meanings during B. Then use 10,000 A′ events in which the meanings of the original two A opcodes reverse. A matched no-edit continuation from the same post-B state sees the same prompts and old-law outcomes. Probe changed A cases, A cases whose outcome is invariant to the reversal, and B cases at the end. Measure correct revision, obsolete responses, and survival of previously correct cases separately. This measures retention of the learned consequence behavior after other learning. The lower-dose checkpoints support the scaling comparison in A.

**Decision rules.** Advance a setting to a fresh-family confirmation only if it beats the strongest shortcut on held pairs by at least **5 points** with a paired lower bound above zero, and passes the declared retention guard. Use a **10-point** noninferiority margin for still-valid A and B behavior across their relevant checkpoints. Report the denominators and use a one-sided family-level interval for the guard. If too few previously correct items support the guard, mark retention unresolved. Claim revision only if corrected A′ responses beat matched no-edit with a positive paired lower bound. If the primary interaction is inconclusive, report it separately from any positive standalone setting. If every learner remains near the paired floor, use taught acquisition and invariant results to localize the failure. A low taught score directs work toward acquisition. Strong taught scores with weak held composition direct work toward transfer. A floor result provides no configuration to promote, and an imprecise comparison leaves the interaction unresolved.

## Cost and launch boundary

The full proposed roster has **32 main lives**, each with 100,000 A, 10,000 B, and 10,000 A′ events, plus 32 matched 10,000-event no-edit continuations. That totals **4.16 million training events** and **203.84 million training target symbols** before engineering, probes, and comparators. A smaller engineering fixture should verify the generator, opcode witness, exposure audit, width-64 parity, width-128 update path, own EOS, matched restart, and evaluator isolation. Profile both widths using representative record streams, including checkpoints and scoring. Measure worker CPU, peak process-tree RSS, model and sensory bytes, checkpoint size, and retained outputs. Freeze a resource envelope and roster before main training. If the full roster exceeds the admitted envelope, record the resource outcome and bring any reduced design back as a separate proposal.

This is a development design. The existing V216 benchmark remains a separate prediction and presentation-transfer reference. Any million-event continuation, fresh-family confirmation, or model adoption requires its own recorded decision.

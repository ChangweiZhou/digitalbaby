# V156 design review

This is a source and design review, not a three-cycle exercise or scientific audit. No model, solver or diagnostic continuation was run.

Two independent reviews checked the finite-feasibility claims and the native fork/read protocol. A separate source review checked the full historical mechanism registers and Claude's latest GitHub work. Resolved corrections:

- Saved OBS features are normalized; their free readout family is distinct from reachable native stores.
- Positive certificates must verify the declared unit margins after scaling; numerical rank failure and approximate Farkas residuals cannot prove infeasibility.
- Greedy-generation induction requires exact or certified feature parity, not merely matching sample outputs.
- Matched idle uses seconds through the base rest API; the final 128-step probe uses the native step API.
- The initially-correct retention subset contains OLD records only. Empty acquired subsets make retention unmeasurable.
- Forks are whole-record, correlated counterfactuals. Finite margin effects are not a stationary linear learning kernel.
- The counts reconcile at 12 family/case checks, 16,320 native query pairs, 144 maximum new observed record applications and 120,832 output distributions, including technical reserves.
- Claude's completed text headroom instrument and proposed V155 key-learning design are separate. This proposal is V156 and retains addition as its goal. Best-found text BPS is not a certified capacity ceiling.

Remaining execution risks are explicit: numerical cases may stay unresolved, sparse constraints and numeric logs must fit the resource cap, snapshot/read/cache parity still needs later technical qualification, and four short late-learning starts cannot diagnose every early warmup failure. A resolved finite witness still earns no native realizability, efficient learning or unseen-addition claim.

`STATIC_CHECKS.json` covers metadata/count/hash consistency only. Running the proposed work requires a subsequent instruction; this delivery does not launch or publish it.

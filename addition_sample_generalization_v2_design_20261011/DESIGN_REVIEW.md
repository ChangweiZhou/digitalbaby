# Prospective review: SAMPLE-GEN V2

2026-10-11. This reviews the new design, not the efficacy of an implemented core.

## Decision

The redesign is suitable for implementation and the subsequent three qualification
cycles. It is **not runnable or scientifically qualified yet**. Zero native
learner runs and zero science jobs have been executed for this version.

The previous accepted STABLE result remains valid for its original finite task.
Repeated development against that fixed task motivates a stronger next benchmark;
it does not retroactively invalidate the accepted result.

## What changed substantively

1. Random opaque numeral meanings replace magnitude-coded operands in the primary
   suite. Common input contains identity and role, not numeral values.
2. Five teaching pair-orbits and ten permanently withheld pair-orbits replace the
   repeatedly reused split. Both orientations are withheld together.
3. Confirmation withholds an entire curriculum geometry from learner development,
   as well as fresh worlds. Ordinary decimal inputs remain a separate guard.
4. The primary score summarizes exact emitted answers on unseen pairs across
   external sample budgets, after delay and later learning. Taught recall cannot
   select a winner.
5. Correlation-aware residual learning, true rewiring and bounded replay have
   matched controls on the same input. All seven non-reference configurations are
   eligible, including the simpler controls. One winner can advance.

## Checked now

The standalone standard-library fixture validates:

- all 300 PATH_LOOP, 5 STAR_LOOP and 12 ODD_CYCLE teaching masks;
- five taught and ten withheld semantic pairs, both-role coverage, ten records
  per epoch, and exact independent-role design rank 9;
- clean teaching-only additive fits under two distinct latent permutations per
  mask, totaling 15,850 exact predictions;
- simple countermodels over all 120 permutations per mask, totaling 380,400
  held-out cases. These are clean environment-side calculations, not noisy
  native-model results;
- synthetic noisy/missing streams, shuffled-label prefix histogram preservation,
  no withheld teaching exposure, and a separate learner payload schema;
- sample/fork/job arithmetic and normalized learning-curve weights;
- seven rejected geometry, semantic leakage, sample-accounting and payload
  negative fixtures.

See [BENCHMARK_FIXTURES.json](BENCHMARK_FIXTURES.json). The check imports no
scientific core or runner.

The initial fixture attempt encountered a metadata-key iteration error before
producing a result. The script was corrected to enumerate the two declared noise
regimes explicitly, and the complete fixture then passed. This was a task-check
script defect with zero native trajectories.

## Limits explicitly retained

- Additive separation and ordinal rendering are engineering priors. Success is
  learning unknown contributions under those priors, not discovery of an
  arbitrary operator.
- All tested symbols have teaching evidence. Cold-symbol prediction is not a
  legitimate demand of this test.
- Five latent values and one-byte answers remain a bounded domain. Fresh entropy
  and a withheld geometry reduce direct leakage; they cannot prove immunity to
  all benchmark overfitting.
- Only two clean permutations per mask are fitted by the exact witness. All masks
  have verified rank; the 120-permutation enumeration belongs to countermodels.
  No claim of 38,040 separately fitted learners is made.
- The new-cohort alphabet is disjoint. Its effects do not establish retention
  under every overlapping-context condition.
- Three causal histories and six checkpoint continuations are paired,
  correlated observations within a world, not extra statistical replicates.
- Replay may improve sample efficiency while costing more computation.
- The eight-hour and disk limits are qualification gates, not measured runtime
  estimates. This design does not authorize shrinking the roster or sample size
  when a gate fails.

## Still required before execution

Implement all nine arms and independently audit equations, state ownership,
forbidden OLD auxiliary changes, probe isolation, compact output parity, graph
mutations and replay exposure. Qualify actual memory/operation costs, full-job
timing, checkpoint/fork parity and detached operations. Complete the three
design–audit–test cycles, source-lock actual files, then obtain execution
authorization. Any failure is resolved prospectively; DEV endpoints are not
parameter-tuning data.

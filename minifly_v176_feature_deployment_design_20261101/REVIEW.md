## Evidence retained

1. WCFI supports usable acquisition and a write-associated stability/plasticity cost, not solved persistent memory.
2. V9.9A/B show narrow native learned-category reuse; do not replace the history with a claim that the fly only memorizes exact keys.
3. V167/V169 show useful local predictive features on two source/target text pairs. External trained probes reveal that signal; their answers are not the native fly's.
4. V169's own local predictor lags counting and did not show forgetting on its A→B continuation. More memory protection is not a universal cure for predictive weakness.
5. V171/SP-COND's fixed bounded output gate did not reliably deploy the native information; optimization movement/lower loss was not complete-output success.
6. V175's coactivity inhibition did not give the specified separation or stable capability gain. Its recent hostile audit is adopted as a limitation, not a request to tune that model on opened worlds.
7. The complete historical254-entry version/recipe catalog was read as operation-family coverage. CATALOGUE_COVERAGE.json preserves its old counts and hash. These are not254 independently tested mechanisms or a new survivor count. Memory protection/replay/capacity families remain conditional tools; predictive-feature formation/use is the selected missing computation.

## Why this is the next design

The main opportunity is to make a known weak positive representation signal usable through the actual memory path. This is narrower and more falsifiable than building another full online recurrent kernel, and more relevant to the three-layer problem than another capacity or inhibition sweep.

The source-trained H does not yet participate in a continuously learning native loop. Freezing it in B deliberately removes address drift so a native-deployment failure is not automatically mixed with changing representation. TRUE/FROZEN×WRITE/NOWRITE separates prior feature effects on native expression from usefulness of enabling native value learning. All comparisons remain bounded to the fixed bridge and dataset/dose.

## Remaining risks, openly retained

- The auxiliary generic predictor is actual pretraining machinery. This is permissible only under the disclosed training-only interpretation; it is not a native-only training architecture. A stricter ban on auxiliary heads would require a different proposal, not hidden relabeling.
- The fixed adapter is a hash-fold with only88 distinct column patterns. It has no guaranteed metric preservation; no new bridge seed or inverse is selected on outcomes.
- Native hard selection and the writer's conditioning may make informative features unusable. Probe success cannot certify reachability.
- The source recipe has changed from V167 in meaningful ways; it may not recreate the earlier signal.
- The existing text pairs are already opened development fixtures. Two pairs do not establish population-level architecture superiority.
- One-byte plus own EOS does not solve variable-length generation, reasoning or action. Addition and later interaction require independent prospective designs.
- Dense source parameters/Adam and extra projection are costly. Local dependency of an update is not sparse physical work or biological implementation.
- The proposed counts are materially larger than a minimal smoke test. A charged full-plan forecast can stop it before learning; no running experiment or automatic budget increase follows from this delivery.

## Deferred, not silently forgotten

A saved-artifact analysis of V175 address/code shifts is distinct from replaying OLD16 states through a new2×2 training intervention. The latter would be a new experiment and is not part of V176. A future online feature/native integration, memory protection, compliant memory-feedback computation and held-relation/action task are separate gates after this bridge, not bundled success criteria.

# MiniFly extended research bibliography — 44 source-linked papers

**Cutoff:** 2026-10-25. Curated for mechanism comparison, not a claim of complete coverage. P0/P1/P2 encode MiniFly relevance. All years/titles are grounded in publisher or preprint records; check each source for exact claims. Methods relying on BP, trained teacher signals, or outside-code contexts are labelled rather than silently treated as fully biological.

## A. Local credit assignment and feedback dynamics (14)

|---|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | [Random synaptic feedback weights support error backpropagation for deep learning](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms13276) — Lillicrap et al. ## Coverage boundary

This is a targeted research collection rather than an exhaustive bibliometric search. A positive benchmark does not imply that the relevant method is implementable by native Full151 plasticity. A reviewer should inspect the precise credit signal and the actual optimizer before treating the method as local in space *and* time.

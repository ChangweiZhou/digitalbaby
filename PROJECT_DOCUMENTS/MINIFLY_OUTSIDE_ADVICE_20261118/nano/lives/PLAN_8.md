## 1. The evidence this follows

`PLAN_3.md` (development): bookmarks of the trusts, mixed by shares, take away 38 to 66% of what a life costs on a stream read earlier. `PLAN_1.md`, diagnostic D3a: when the living set itself starts again from the trusts of the stream's own stage and learns on, more goes (tiny Shakespeare 1.394 against the mixture's 1.443; the book 1.456 against 1.477). The difference is that a bookmark answers from old trusts that never learn again. `PLAN_4.md` tried to close it by sending the living set back to the bookmark and failed: between twins of the living set the shares say nothing, and each return threw away learning.

## 2. The mechanism, exactly (`awake.py`)

`PLAN_3.md` with one addition, in which nothing is ever overwritten. **At each byte, if the set with the largest share (before the byte) is a bookmark, that bookmark learns from the byte as well as the living set:** its trusts and its parts move by its own mismatch (its own answers, first and second, from the same messages), by the same rule of the step, with sums of its own (copied from the living set when the bookmark was taken). The living set learns at every byte exactly as before. A bookmark that wakes when its kind of text returns thus goes on from where it stood; a twin that wakes does no harm, since the living set loses nothing.

Two systems: (i) the small system of `PLAN_1.md` with bookmarks at the pace 65,536 (the system of `PLAN_3.md`), with and without the addition; (ii) the system chosen in `PLAN_7.md` (bounded memories and the cell's mixture, all speaking) with bookmarks and the addition: the three remedies of the night together.

**Check before any run** (`python3 awake.py check`): the compiled kernel against a reference written out in full (bookmarks with their own sums, the waking and its learning, with bounded kinds and the cell's mixture); with the addition off the losses are those of the kernels it is built on; no answer moves when its own byte and all later ones are replaced.

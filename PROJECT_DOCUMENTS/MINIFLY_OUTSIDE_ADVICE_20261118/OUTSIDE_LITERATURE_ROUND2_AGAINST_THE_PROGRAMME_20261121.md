## 1. What bears on the question now at the centre

**The question** (`PLAN_11.md`, which records Mr. Xu's words and translates them). "A trust, as I built it, is not one connection's strength." It is one number shared by every cell of a kind at a level of counts. In the small system three quarters of the loss on returning streams sits in those numbers; in the large system, on two orders, a third (`PLAN_1.md`, outcome: 0.74 and 0.35). Mr. Xu: "我们反复强调是local learning" (the plan's translation: "we have stressed again and again that it is local learning"). His question, in the plan's translation: what is used for language and for mathematics may overlap, "but in theory not much. Why does forgetting keep appearing?"; and if the learner had "the rule of addition", forgetting "should then no longer be a problem. Where is the problem now?" The report answers this only from the side. Each part below says first what it does not have.

**(i) Where forgetting sits when what is stored for two tasks hardly overlaps.**

- *Nothing on the reading.* The report states no finding about a shared readout, an output layer, a shared normaliser or a last layer. The words "readout", "output layer" and "last layer" do not occur in it. Such findings exist in the literature on classes learned in sequence, as I recall it (not from the report, unverified, names to check: Wu and colleagues 2019 on a bias of the last layer; Ramasesh and colleagues 2021 and Davari and colleagues 2022 on what earlier layers still hold). No prompt asked for them.
- *The definition that states plan 11's premise.* "Riemer et al. 2019 (MER; title confirmed) define transfer and interference by the sign of gradient dot products." (Prompt 6, answer 1). Mine: by that definition two tasks interfere only through numbers that both move. If sums and English are held in different cells, the only such numbers are those the reading shares. The title alone was confirmed.
- *A pooled number is wrong for each kind.* "Lifetime Dirichlet counts are exact Bayes under stationarity. Under regime switching they converge to the pooled average of regimes, which is wrong for each. So not forgetting does not mean being right on return." (Prompt 3, answer 6). Mine: said of counts. A trust shared by every cell of a kind is a pooled estimate of how far such cells are to be believed, and the sentence fits it word for word. It gives the direction of plan 11's H1 and no size.
- *What two texts share whatever is done.* "Interference is measured per stream, not per context class. Most of it may sit in a few high-frequency order-1–2 contexts (spaces, newlines), which a targeted fix could handle." (Prompt 6, missing). Mine: a guess of the report's authors. No life of ours has been scored by class of context.
- *Forgetting that is only apparent.* "Hetherington & Seidenberg 1989 (not re-verified) argued savings persist after apparent forgetting." (Prompt 8, answer 3). Mine: the one sentence of the report that describes a loss in the reading with the stored knowledge whole. It is the pattern of the relay network in plan 11 so far (section 3).

**(ii) A weight for one connection or one context against a pooled one; what protects local weights.**

- *Nothing measured.* The report compares no learner with a weight for each context to one with pooled weights. On how fine to cut, it found nothing: "granularity theory and direct comparisons of input selection with pools over time: not found" (Prompt 7, answer 2). Units gated by their input and taught each by a local loss are named (Sezener et al. 2021) with no result: "All three were not re-verified this round, so results are not stated." (answer 1).
- *Kept by definition, and the price.* Of learners that answer from stored items: "Established only definitionally for stored items." What fails instead: "revision: stale neighbours vote forever unless weighted by recency"; "generalisation: bounded by the frozen representation"; "cost: grows with the store" (Prompt 3, answer 3; images, a frozen pretrained extractor). Mine: learner 2 of plan 11, a trust for every cell, is such a learner in its reading. Its keeping is close to true by construction; what a run can show is the price.
- *What is local does not spread.* "A count model revises each context separately, so the ripple of a change will not propagate. This argues for some shared, abstracted key (word-level or class-level contexts) before revision can generalise." (Prompt 2, missing). Mine: the same holds for cases never shown. A number that answers a case never met is a number that many cases pass through. A rule is a shared number. So "local" and "has the rule" pull against each other at the reading. What matters is which texts can reach a shared number: one that only its own kind of input reaches is safe from other text.
- *The limit of protection by input.* "by construction, selection by input cannot separate regimes with identical input statistics" (Prompt 7, answer 3). Ours agrees: cells that join for lines never met kept 85.8% of addition through subtraction when the second task brought a byte never met, and 0.4% when it brought none (roadmap, row K1, as the lines report). Mine: English and sums share almost no byte, so they are the case this route can keep; subtraction in addition's bytes is the case it cannot. Plan 12 names the same limit: subtraction after addition, "which no silence can keep apart".
- *The programme's own principle, written while I read, and the report beside it.* `PLAN_12.md`, section 1, as its author's reading of plan 11: "what survived was held in numbers that change only while their own sending cell (or line, or context) is active". The report has this only as a rule for the members of a pool, and with a floor that goes against it: `member i: step size eta_i = eta * max(r_i, eps)`, "Add a floor ε on the responsibility, so no member ever stops learning entirely." (Prompt 1, rule and answer 3). It has no measurement of it.
- *Inside the cell is not enough, by our own figure.* The report proposes to weigh a context's memories by "a per-context selector" (Prompt 6, experiment 3). Plan 7 built it. Beside the mixer it is lower on every test in all five orders, and at the median it removes 6% of what a stream loses over a life: "The weighing that is forgotten stays in the trusts." (`PLAN_7.md`, outcome).
- *The report does not see the difference.* Its rule gives each context a strength of its own, "beta_c learned online by gradient of log loss inside the unit (allowed)" (Prompt 6, rule). Its experiment pools it: "A per-order learned β_c against a fixed one." (experiment 1). One strength for an order is a trust in our sense. For the pool it does see it: "Per-context-type weighting of copies ... may matter more than who learns." (Prompt 1, missing).

## 6. Questions only the owner can settle, which this report sharpens

1. **What "local" covers** (it changes question 2 of the first reading's section 7). That question asked whether a number shared by all cells of a kind counts as one unit's own. Mr. Xu's sentence on local learning is now in `PLAN_11.md`. Plan 12 states a principle of its author's own beside it: a strength changes only while its own cell speaks. What remains Mr. Xu's to say: does local learning rule out every number that several cells share (the parts of the second answering cell, the room for the unmet, the shares over bookmarks, a strength learned for each order as the report proposes), or only those that can change while their own cell is silent? And may a shared number serve as the value a cell is born with (experiment 1)?
2. **The raw stream** (it sharpens question 3 there). The report asks for the decision in writing "before running any more retention experiments". The programs already keep the whole history for recall and every count for life. Is that practice the rule, or is a bound to be stated, and on what: bytes kept, cells, stored copies?
3. **The definition and the bar.** No published definition requires (a) to (e). Which references must a claim beat (a fresh system, separate systems, the stored stream, recent bytes only), by what margin fixed in advance (the report offers 0.01 nats a byte), and over how many returns of a kind?
4. **What "having the rule" means.** Plan 11 scores never-shown sums named whole. In the counting learners these are answered from cells keyed on some of the places. Is that the rule of addition in the sense of Mr. Xu's question, or must the test be one that no stored case can answer (experiment 2)?
5. **Whether forgetting what does not return is a fault.** The report's nearest definition argues that a bounded agent "must track, not store". Property (a) asks for keeping. Under a bound one of the two gives way. Which, and who is to say that a thing will not return?

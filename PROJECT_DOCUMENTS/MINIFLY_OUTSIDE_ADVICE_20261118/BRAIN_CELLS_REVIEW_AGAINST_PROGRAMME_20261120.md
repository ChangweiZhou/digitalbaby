## 2. The five I would run, ranked (all mine)

**1. A store of fixed size, pruned by disuse** (the store grows with the text). The number of cells is fixed, and each cell keeps the time it last counted, as companions do. A new context with no free place takes the place of the cell that has counted least and, among those, been silent longest, chosen among a few places as in `nano/search`'s store. (`PLAN_19` left cells unborn by the surprise at their birth; this removes by use.) *General:* it reads a cell's own count and time. *Experiment:* the bare system of `PLAN_20` with the second answering cell, in stores of 100, 50, 25 and 12% of its unbounded size; the test text, then three books, then `nano/suite`'s streams; beside places taken by chance. *Refuted if* at half the store any stream's last tenth is over 0.010 nats worse, or the first text after the books rises by over 0.02. *Cost:* one number a cell; a few comparisons at a birth. *Chance:* 0.35 at half the store on three kinds of stream; 0.15 at a quarter. The old text should break first: its once-met cells are the longest silent.

**2. A trust in two memories** (keeping; the reading). Each trust, and each part of the second answering cell, gets a second part born at nothing, whose value and stored sum (`PLAN_22`) both halve over H bytes: it moves for life and forgets. The sender is heard at the sum of the two, so one lately wrong is heard less for a while. *General:* it is what companions are for counts, moved to where the programme has three times located its loss of an old text or task (section 7, item 3). *Experiment:* `gate.py life`, the lives of `PLAN_15`, then streams of other kinds in turn; H of 10,000 and 100,000 bytes; a control whose second part reads a constant. *Refuted if* the first stream, asked after the others with nothing learning and the fading parts left to fade, is not 0.005 nats better on three kinds, or any stream's own tenth is 0.003 worse. *Cost:* twice the trusts and parts. *Chance:* 0.30. Scored with the time standing still it can show nothing.

**3. An answering cell that divides by how much it hears** (numbers set for each cell and size). At a byte, every step is divided by the number of trusts and parts that move at that byte; nothing is named. *Why:* "The second cell wants a smaller number than the first" (`PLAN_22`, addition 1); `nano/layers`' wide cell loses on 164 kinds (1.4884 against 1.4740), and that line listed "a step that knows how many parts move at once" as owed. The document adds only the reading: inhibition in step with excitation. *Refuted if* the wide cell still loses there, or the two cells' best numbers still differ by a factor of three. *Cost:* one count a byte. *Chance:* 0.45 that one number then serves; 0.20 for 0.005 nats on three kinds.

**4. Probation: a lasting cell is born at the second meeting** (the store). A context met for the first time gets only a fading cell (half-life H, as `PLAN_6`). Met again while that cell lives, it gets a lasting cell with those counts; otherwise the place is given back. It reads counts and times only. *Experiment:* the lives of `PLAN_19`, judged by that plan's own lines (two thirds of the cells, 0.010 nats; refuted above four fifths or 0.020). *Cost:* fewer cells; one test at a birth. *Chance:* 0.25. Contexts that return further apart than H never get a lasting cell.

**5. One number sent to every trust that reopens its step** (a new kind of stream late in life). The answering cell sends the ratio of a short to a long running mean of its mismatch; above one, each trust's stored sum is reduced in proportion. *Against it already:* the same signal did nothing on the relay's steps (C6), and the size of the mismatch did not mark a change of task (`nano/keep`). *Refuted if* the first 50,000 bytes after a change of stream are not 0.01 nats better on two changes of three. *Cost:* three numbers; one multiplication for each trust used. *Chance:* 0.15; only if proposal 2 fails.

Nothing in the document bears on word ends or on depth with a chance I would put above one in five.

## 3. Sounds relevant; would be ad hoc or would not transfer (mine)

- **Inhibition by named types (Rec. 2).** A wiring that evolution found for cortex. Laid down by hand it is the designer's knowledge; its three functions exist already, and `nano/layers` shows that gates can be drawn and kept by what they earn.
- **Compartments and bursts as the way to depth (Rec. 1).** This is the relay system described again. The document shows what one neuron can compute, not that learning reaches further through such units. Depth failed here because every further cell adds to one sum (`nano/layers`).
- **An accumulator, or a rhythm, as a clock for word ends.** A cut made when summed surprise crosses a line depends on what came before the passage, so one word is cut differently on different occasions: the fault `nano/ends` located. Blind clocks are refuted.
- **Learnable delays as timing (Rec. 4).** The document's own limit: "learnable delays matter for spiking networks that use timing codes" (Q4 item 12).
- **Atlases and connectomes to set kinds or connection statistics (Rec. 6).** Constraints on a model of tissue.

## 4. Warnings for the programme's claims and words

1. The Recommendations are for modelling tissue: "This is the best-supported and highest-leverage change" (Rec. 1) is a judgement about fidelity, and the document reports no task on which any addition makes a learner better. The programme's own rule applies (`OLD_LISTS_AGAINST_THE_LOOP_20261120.md`): "A resemblance to a circuit is not evidence that a mechanism will help."
2. **Cell.** The document's baseline is "a point neuron, y = f(Σ wᵢxᵢ)" (Q4). A cell that counts is one stored context with exact counts, made at first meeting and never removed, about two new ones a byte. Nothing in the document works so; the nearest thing is a synapse.
3. **Trust.** One number shared by every cell of a kind and level. No synapse is shared: local here means that a number reads its own record, not that it sits at one synapse.
4. **Kinds.** Biological types are laid down in development, and the lowest levels may be "states rather than types" (Q1 §1.1). Kinds found by search have no counterpart in the document.
5. **Signals.** Modulators act over "hundreds of milliseconds to minutes" (Q4 item 2). A mismatch exact at every byte, sent to all, shares the form only.
6. **Births, replay, dreams.** Of adult neurogenesis: "scale and functional significance in adult humans are not established" (Q3); Rec. 5: "Do not hard-code them." Replay is not in the document.
7. "Most causal cell-type evidence is from rodents and zebrafish." (Caveats). Nothing should be claimed of the human brain on this document's strength.
8. **Sources.** Many are news pages or lookups; reference 41, given for the myelin experiment, links a dyslexia trial protocol; bursts, tagging and the nets carry no source. Two items dated late 2026 I could not check.

## 5. How much it helps (mine)

Little. Its three leading additions are already in the programme, tried or under other names; its circuit items (cerebellum, basal ganglia, feedback, gating, thalamus) repeat the lists set against the loop the same day. What is new is glia, chemical signals and slow plasticity. It says nothing about predicting streams, scale or cost, and nothing usable on word ends or depth. It gives a graded checklist for the programme's vocabulary, and three second-rank mechanisms that touch two open problems: the store (pruning by disuse, capture by repetition) and the reading (two time scales). I would try the fixed store with pruning by disuse first: the programme's own count says 77 to 79% of cells have been met once; the one step that made the store smaller took a fifth of it, at a price (`PLAN_19`); the rule reads only a cell's own count and time; `nano/search` has the machinery; and either outcome gives the price of a bound, which a system meant to live long has to know. For both problems the nearer literature is compression by mixing of contexts, which `PLAN_23` already cites; the biology supplies names more than forms.

## 6. Note by the coordinating session (mine)

I have read the reading above against the document's headings and the roadmap, not line by line against the document. I agree with its verdict: the document's three leading additions (compartments in a cell, a slow signal sent to all, delays that can be learned) are in the programme already, tried or under other names, and it has nothing to say about predicting streams. What I take from it for the work:

1. Of its five proposals the first and the fourth meet an open problem that Mr. Xu's steers leave standing, the growth of the store, with rules that read only a cell's own count and time: a store of fixed size pruned by disuse, and a lasting cell born only at a second meeting. They are taken up as the next plan of `nano/table` (the price of a bound), and offered to `nano/suite` for its streams.
2. Its second proposal (a trust in two memories) meets the finding that the second answering cell follows the recent text and does not keep; it is second in line.
3. Its warnings about words (section 4) are right and I adopt them: a cell here is a stored context, a trust is shared by a whole kind, and nothing in this programme should be written "as in the brain". Section 18 of the roadmap already says where a number tuned by hand turned out to be an estimate of something the cells can measure; that kind of statement, about what is measured, is the one to keep making.

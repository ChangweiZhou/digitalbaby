## What holds

1. **One system with one setting, going on learning as it reads, is below xz on six kinds of real byte stream** (two of prose, Python source, C headers, a table of numbers, markup): 1.34, 1.39, 0.95, 0.71, 0.34, 0.28 nats a byte against xz's 1.75, 1.79, 1.13, 0.72, 0.38, 0.43. It uses cells that count runs of up to six bytes and, beyond them, only parts that cost no cells. One line's figures, not yet repeated; xz is not the state of the art of compressors (section 20).
2. **What that system is made of, each piece kept only because it gained on at least three kinds of stream:** a second answering cell whose trusts know the byte at each delay (sections 11, 14: repeated to the digit by a second line; holds on fresh seeds, on three-digit sums and on books not seen before); senders that say what just happened (a cell that recalls the last place where the present bytes stood; each cell's last counted byte); one rule for the step of every trust in place of two hand-set schedules (sections 17, 18).
3. **The system first proposed (the relay) learns a rule well and can be kept:** 95.5% of never-shown two-digit sums after 400,000 lines; with replay from the counting cells' store 97% and, after a second task, 94% (sections 9, 12; the second figure repeated by a second line's program).
4. **A number I had tuned by hand turned out to be something the cells can measure** (how often a cell of a given kind and age is followed by something new), and the hand-set value was an estimate of it (section 18).

## What was corrected or refuted today, and stands corrected

- Never-shown sums had been scored in a situation no system meets while reading; scored rightly, the relay system is 11 to 16 points better than recorded (section 9; my fault).
- The figure 1.4421 on tiny Shakespeare is a sound measurement but was set against the wrong bytes of the reference network, leaned on hand-given word ends and on a convention about time, and I had understated the system's size. With nothing given by hand the system stands at 1.467, a little above that network run, not below it (sections 14, 16).
- "Counting systems lose with long reading" is true of a stream that holds nothing new after its first tenth; the loss is the system believing that stream (section 13). Two of my explanations on the way were refuted and are recorded as such.
- With learning stopped, almost every idea of the day harms at least one kind of stream. The frozen last tenth of one English text was the wrong yardstick for a system meant to go on learning (section 20).

## What is open

- **Keeping:** the second answering cell follows the recent text; over a long life of two tasks it can lose the first.
- **What was given by hand about English** (word ends) is worth 0.015 nats and has no general replacement in this line's system; `nano/merge`'s kinds made while reading gain as much on books in its own.

- **Depth:** cells on cells added nothing in the one form tried.
- **Independent repeats** are owed for the results of `nano/suite` and `nano/merge`.

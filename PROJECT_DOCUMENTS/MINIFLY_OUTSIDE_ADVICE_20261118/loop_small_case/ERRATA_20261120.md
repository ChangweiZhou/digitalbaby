# Errata to the report of the small case, found on 20 November 2026

These are faults of mine in `loop_small_case`, found after the report's sections were written. Each is stated with what it touches and what it does not. The report's text is left as it was written; this file and the plans named here carry the corrections.

## 1. The score of subtraction is mostly not a score of lines never shown

Found by an independent helper on 20 November while it checked the keeping line's files; confirmed by me from `cells2.py`.

`cells2.py` holds out 500 ordered pairs (a, b) for each operator and shows 8,000 others. For subtraction, `text()` writes the larger number first whatever the order of the pair. A held-out pair (a, b) and the shown pair (b, a) therefore write the same line, and about four held-out subtraction lines in five are lines that training also writes: 399, 404 and 420 of 500 on the seeds 73129, 93683 and 116089 (recomputed from the program's own split). Addition is not touched: an addition line is written in the order of its pair, and no held-out addition line is written in training.

*What this touches.* Every figure for subtraction that this report and the lines built on `cells2.py` call "never-shown": sections 11 and 16 of `REPORT.md` and `PLAN_5.md`, `PLAN_13.md` here; the subtraction columns of the lines `nano/keep`, `nano/replay`, `nano/sparse` and `nano/joint` where they use this scoring. Such a figure is a score on a mixture of about four fifths shown lines and one fifth never-shown ones. For a system that answers shown and never-shown lines alike it changes little; for a system with a part that holds shown lines exactly (cells that count) it can flatter.

*What this does not touch.* Every figure for addition, and with it every statement about how much of addition is kept through subtraction, which is what the keeping track's criterion is stated in.

*How large it is, where it has been measured* (added later on 20 November, as the lines report). The line `nano/replay` redid all 70 of its runs with a clean score beside the old one (the never-shown pairs whose line no shown pair writes): for the relay system, with and without replay, the old score is above the clean one by 0.4 to 0.5 points at the median of fifteen seeds (single runs from 4.0 below to 6.0 above), so for that system it overstated nothing that can be measured, and every reading of that line stands on the clean score. The line `nano/keep`, first file back: its final candidate has 85.1 on the 101 clean pairs against 86.8 on all 500. For cells that count (`nano/sparse`) the size is not yet reported.

*What is owed.* A score of subtraction on the held-out pairs whose written line no shown pair writes (about 80 to 100 of the 500), beside the present one, in any further run; asked of the lines on 20 November.

# MiniFly — hostile source audit of neuron-level persistent-learning dossier

**Date:** 2026-10-26  
**Decision:** **RETAIN AS A CORRECTIVE, SOURCE-QUALIFIED MEMO; DISCARD THE UNCORRECTED DOSSIER AS AN ENGINEERING PLAN.**  
**Scope:** Human learning mechanisms, with fly neuroscience as comparative background only. Documentation only: **no mechanism implemented, no science run, no experimental claim promoted, and no model adopted.**  
**Input:** user-supplied “Neuron-Level Mechanisms of Human Learning (with Drosophila comparison, clustering, and evidence strength),” 261-line paste, 53 numbered bibliography slots, [17] absent. This memo is an audit, not a reproduction of the dossier.

## 0. Why it earns a memo, but not an implementation

The dossier is useful **only after surgery**: several current (2026) primary papers provide worthwhile updates about *which specific biological phenomena are observed and in which organisms*, while several references are misidentified and some purported direct-human evidence is actually MRI proxy, rodent experimentation, a review, or a news story. The high-level mechanisms are mostly **already in** Claude's [150-entry human-learning catalog](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/04c2873efb5ca8cc70eacc45052ec9e71231a93f/human_learning_mechanisms_20261023/CATALOG.md), the [2026-10-24 source-resolved audit](../HUMAN_LEARNING_AUDIT_20261024/REVISED_AUDIT.md), and the [2026-10-25 local-learning literature review](../LOCAL_LEARNING_LITERATURE_20261025/README.md). In particular, **BTSP is already CELL-08**; STDP CELL-02; BCM/metaplasticity CELL-03; three-factor eligibility CELL-09; dendritic nonlinearities CELL-10; clustered spines CELL-11; inhibitory plasticity CELL-12; slow consolidation CELL-15; cascade states CELL-16; myelin CELL-17; adult neurogenesis CELL-19; replay/CLS SYS-01/15 and SLP-02; dopamine RL-01.

The mathematical/engineering bottleneck is **not** the absence of a list of biological mechanisms. MiniFly's target is generalizable complete *self-generated* raw-byte + EOS responses on **unseen addition relations**, at bounded exposure/feedback/CPU cost, without hard-coded arithmetic structures, input parser, hidden answer cue, forced stopping, attention or Transformer. The present [V157 A1 result](../../minifly_v157_dev_20261026/REPORT.md) reports no held-addition gain versus controls, and distinct retention losses in longer histories. Literature cannot upgrade an E0/E1 observation to E3.

**Source-validation method:** audited each independently named mechanism/claim family below against its linked evidence and original source's stated organism, modality, and inference level. For high-impact 2026 papers, followed the DOI/PubMed or journal page to check abstract, title, date, venue and experiment. **Not** a complete, full-text read of all 53 references or meta-analysis; where not checked, bibliography is explicitly listed as *background/unrechecked*. No invented claim of 53/53 article-level independent validation.

## 1. Audit notation

* **Accept (A):** mechanistic phenomenon well established; **does not** mean the stated exact human evidence or MiniFly implementation exists.
* **Qualify (Q):** core phenomenon supported, but organism, level, strength, causal/functional inference, timescale or framing is overstated.
* **Reject claim (R):** the specific assertion does **not** follow from the cited paper; the general research area may still be legitimate.
* **Background (B):** not an independent mechanism-specific empirical validation of human persistent learning, or not pertinent to immediate MiniFly bottleneck.

A key grading correction: evidence for **(i) molecular plasticity, (ii) human counterpart, (iii) causal relevance to behavior, and (iv) utility under MiniFly's local rules** are four different axes. The dossier collapses them into a single “High/Medium/Low.” Strong rodent data + human MRI is *not* strong direct human synapse-level evidence. Human TMS/PAS **does not identify a specific synapse or a uniquely STDP plasticity rule**.

**Corrected human evidence tiers:** direct human experimental intervention/imaging is present for some *phenomena* (PAS effects, pharmacological effort learning, sleep consolidation, high-field MRI indices); direct, single-synapse human **causal learning** evidence is much narrower. Animal mechanistic data should be labeled **animal**, not upgraded by cross-species analogy. Computational usefulness is a **fourth untested question**.

## 5. Dispositions and **conditional** falsifiable MiniFly implications

Do **not** add a “complete neuronal learning stack.” Nor should LTP, STDP, eCB, astrocytes, pruning, AHN, myelination and sleep be counted as 8 independent improvements: most interact, many are tissue maintenance, and none alone is an identified solution to learned computation.

| Condition established by *existing or new controlled measurements* | Narrow candidate inspired by biology | Required contrasting experiment and stop criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Native observed input/state cannot carry earlier byte distinctions; alternate exact/sufficient comparator can | **State persistence + context-sensitive gating** (ACh/gating; catalog CTRL-01 etc) | Counterfactual prefix/distractor probes; equal capacity fixed-gate/no-gate; no arithmetic slots, supplied counters or hidden pointer. Fail if improves taught-only but not complete own held. First fix known signal/noise/readout issue rather than adding new biology. |
| Relevant local updates have delayed consequences not credited under legal scalar reward | **Seconds-scale eligibility + optional nonlinear modulatory gate** (CELL-08/09, STDP) | Verify unbiased/biased update estimators on tiny enumerated stochastic graphs, compare matched parameter/exposure baselines, legal feedback phases, randomized timing, cut eligibility, shuffled labels. No assumption a biologically plausible rule is a useful gradient. |
| A correct taught answer is *lost after further teaching*, and matched no-write forks identify causal damage | **Local competition, selective LTD, plasticity thresholds** (H04/H09/H10/H15) | Match OLD-exposure and elapsed time; distinguish forgetting from never-learned items, time/noise, reward arbitration and incorrect EOS. Compare no-write/idle and selective normalization lesions under same memory/CPU budget. |
| Robust E1 exists; rules are inferred but repeated specific cases cause interference | **Selective replay/tagging and fast→slow consolidation** | Ablate replay; distinguish preserved OLD from new rule generalization; charge all replay/exposure/CPU; do not re-announce CLS retention as E3. |
| Native computation passes E1 but fails changed presentation E2 and unseen relation E3 | **Shared learned latent transition / factorized relational dynamics** (not a specific neuron molecule) | Shared-time-parameter state learner; no preinstalled digit/carry operation; held unordered orbits (both directions), legitimate own generation and EOS, position/length swaps, stronger fixed-kernel and evaluator exact-gradient controls. |

**Current precondition matters:** [V157 (2026-10-26)](../../minifly_v157_dev_20261026/REPORT.md) found **0 native W–SH_OLD held advantage** in each tested world/schedule; V157 world 5702's **5/64 acquired OLD items fell to 0/64** in both correlated branches. That sharpens the retention question *without identifying which specific write/time mechanism caused each loss*. Some phenomena above can motivate **future discriminating controls**, not an immediate new mechanism release.

### Work items worth retaining

1. **Document-only** repair of source IDs, organism labels and study methodology **(this memo; complete)**.
2. **Do not enlarge architecture yet.** Reconcile the latest V157 retention and acquisition evidence with the existing [research roadmap](../MINIFLY_RESEARCH_ROADMAP_20261026/MEMO.md), which already gates workstreams A/B/C on causal diagnosis. The newest negative outcome should be retained, not replaced by optimism about a biological paper.
3. If a later test confirms **true causal between-record interference**, propose a single-factor **locally competitive plasticity versus homeostatic scaling** comparator with equal resource, and use [Li 2026](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aec3961) as animal-level motivation, **not a human mechanism implementation blueprint**.
4. If the problem instead is **temporal credit**, compare a *specified* seconds-scale trace/plateau gate to V6 existing local score traces, including delayed scalar reward without hidden teacher bytes. BTSP is existing catalog material, **not new discovery**.
5. Reject stand-alone implementations of adult neurogenesis, oligodendrocytes, microglia, epigenetic marks, “clock plasticity,” or a literal sleep-cycle orchestrator as currently **unjustified by causal evidence**.

## 6. Final decision and limitations

**Retain this corrective memo; do not ship the pasted dossier, copy its confidence labels, create more catalog rows, or change the active learner.** Its only incremental value is correcting inaccurate bibliographic claims, making 2026 updates accessible and providing a bounded differential diagnosis. The **human** learning objective remains controlling. A preprint is not a validated mechanism; a peer-reviewed review is not new causal data; a rodent study is not direct human evidence; biological existence is not model learnability; structural proxy is not single-synapse observation.

**Verification level:** claim-family audit (all major rows and takeaways); primary abstracts/metadata checked for high-impact contested examples; other bibliography classified as background without full-text verification. This note is **not** a systematic review, and its design ideas are **unexecuted hypotheses**. No numerical MiniFly results were recomputed; project figures are sourced to existing versioned reports.

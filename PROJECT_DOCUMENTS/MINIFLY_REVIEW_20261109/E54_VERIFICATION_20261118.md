**Terms.** The terms of the brief are used as the brief defines them; those on which this document relies are repeated here. *G* is the dense network (256 cells, each connected to every cell), *F10* the limited network (10 fixed connections per cell in a neighbourhood of a 16 by 16 grid), *P8* the periodic network (a limited network whose cells update every 1, 2, 4 or 8 positions and whose connections are replaced at every 256th update, an *event*, by E42's rule with theta = 0.5). *Rule B* is back-propagation; *rule S*, the sweep, passes the error backward by the signs of the weights. *f* is the taught fraction of the pairs of operands. *Score A* is the share right on untaught pairs of addition and subtraction at 12 letters; *score R* the share right on 1,000 untaught pairs of each of addition and subtraction whose answer needs the reduction by the modulus. A *scored row* (here simply *row*) is the set of scores taken every 256 updates. *Updates to 80* is the first of three consecutive rows at or above 80%. A *level* is the mean of a score over the three rows ending at an update. The *last rise* of R is the mean of R over the nine rows ending at the last update less the mean over the nine rows ending 8,192 updates earlier; the *rise before it* is that second mean less the mean over the nine rows ending 16,384 updates before the last update; a curve *has flattened* if both rises are smaller than 3 points in size. The *margin* is the difference of end levels of R that the brief requires before a difference counts (10 or 20 points). A *receipt* is the JSON record that a run writes; the *batch digest* is the SHA-256 over all batches presented so far, recorded at every row. A *saved state* is the file from which a run of parts 1 to 3 continues after a stop; a run is *resumed* when it continues from a saved state, and the *replay* is the regeneration of the stream up to the saved update before training continues. A *reading* is one of the brief's fixed words for a comparison (*generalises better*, *generalises worse*, *not distinguished*, *not decided within the run*).

## 2. Defects, in order of weight

### Defect 1. The scored items have fixed numbers of letters, which the brief's account of what K can reach does not consider

**Place.** "The question" (the distances and the shares 4 of 13 and 12 of 13); the runner's point 3; the first fixed sentence ("with this K the error of an answer reaches the first operand in p of the items"); the Terms (definition of A, R, T).

**What is wrong.** The brief's account of what K can reach is made for training items, whose number of letters is uniform on 0 to 12. The threshold that decides every reading is on R, and the items of R have no letters; the items of A and of T have twelve. With no letters the first operand stands 4 and 5 steps before the first answer position, so K = 5 reaches both of its digits in every item of R (and K = 4 the second digit). With twelve letters it stands 16 and 17 steps before, so only K of 17 or more reaches both digits in the items of A and T, and K = 16, the largest K run, reaches only the second. A run with K between 5 and 16 can therefore be taught items of R's form directly (one training item in 13 has no letters), while A and T are reached only through what the shared weights carry from shorter items. A reader of the brief would expect the opposite order of difficulty between R and A from the brief's paragraph, and the shares p of the fixed sentence say nothing about R.

**Evidence.** `exp42_cells.panels`: `A = {... E.panel(t, th[t][1], seed, ks=(12,), n=300)[12] ...}`, `TA = {... ks=(12,) ...}`, `Rs[...] = [E.render(t, a, b, 0, Random(0)) for a, b in s]` (part 2). Part 3.7 gives the distances; part 3.6 the shares (K = 16: both digits in 12 of 13 training items; second digit in 13 of 13).

**Proposed text.** Add at the end of "The question": "The scored items do not have the distribution of the training items. The items of R are written with no letters (`exp42_cells.panels`: `E.render(t, a, b, 0, Random(0))`), so that the first operand stands 4 and 5 steps before the first answer position and K = 5 reaches both of its digits in every item of R. The items of A and of T are written with 12 letters (`ks=(12,)`), so that the first operand stands 16 and 17 steps before the first answer position and only K of 17 or more reaches both digits; K = 16 reaches the second digit only. With K between 5 and 16, items of R's form are among the items in which the error reaches the first operand (one training item in 13 has no letters), whereas the items of A and T are reached only through what the weights, which are the same at every position, carry from shorter items. The report states beside every reading on R that its items have no letters, and beside every reading on A or T that their items have 12." In the Terms, after "A: 600 items of addition and subtraction with 12 letters", insert "; R: ... (with no letters)" and "T is the share right on 600 taught items with 12 letters". In point 3 add: "and, for the panels, the distances of the two digits of the first operand from the first answer position (R: 5 and 4; A and T: 17 and 16)". In the first fixed sentence replace "with this K the error of an answer reaches the first operand in p of the items" by "with this K the error of the first answer position reaches both digits of the first operand in p of the training items (point 3) and, when K is at least 5, in every item of R, which has no letters".

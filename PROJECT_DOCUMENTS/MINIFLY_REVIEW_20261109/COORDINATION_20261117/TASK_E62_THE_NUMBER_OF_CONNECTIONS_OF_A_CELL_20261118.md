## Terms

- *The network* is that of E50's stage 3 (the setting `C46-one` of `exp50_code.py`) with the number and the choice of a cell's connections changed: 256 recurrent cells of the gated kind of E26; the input is a fixed code of 512 values in which each of the 46 used bytes turns on 11 values of its own, and each cell reads 46 of the values, one of every used byte; the readout is dense. The byte table and `l1` take no part.
- *N* is the number of other cells that a cell reads; it also reads itself. The connections are fixed for the run. *From all cells* means that a cell's N connections are drawn from the 255 other cells (`e42_cells.Layer(m, seed, N=N, wide=True)`); *from the neighbourhood* means from the 48 cells around it on a grid of 16 by 16 (the square of 7 by 7 cells centred on the cell, with the grid's edges joined; `wide=False`). With N = 255 every cell reads every other, and the choice has no effect.
- *Reached within k steps:* the cells whose state at position t + k can depend on a given cell's state at position t, the cell itself included (every cell reads itself). With connections from all cells and seed 73129, N = 2 leaves 40 cells that no other cell reads and 49 cells that cannot reach the remaining 207, and N = 4 leaves 4 such cells; from N = 8 on, every cell is reached from every cell, within 5 steps at N = 8, 4 at N = 10 and 16, 3 at N = 32, 2 at N = 64 and 128 and 1 at N = 255 (5 in the record's network). The figures for every setting are in section 2 of the check named above.
- *The sweep* (rule S, `local=True`) sends the error back through each linear map by the signs of its weights times one scale for the map; *the exact gradient* (rule B, `local=False`) sends it back through the weights. Both are arms, each named for what it is (decision 31). By Mr. Xu's statements of 18 November the exact gradient is outside the principle on any network; its arm is a comparison and is never called a candidate. In every run the error is passed back over the whole item, as in the record.
- *The record* is the two runs of E50's stage 3 with N = 10 from the neighbourhood, `$D:$M/exp45/e50/e50_C46-one_<S|B>_73129.json`. They are not repeated.
- A run is scored every 256 updates (a *scored row*). A threshold is *reached* at the first of three consecutive scored rows at or above it; the first single row at or above it is given in brackets. A *level* at an update is the mean of the three scored rows that end there. *A* and *R* are the shares right on never-taught pairs (A: 600 items of addition and subtraction with 12 letters; R: the 2,000 items whose answer needs the reduction by the modulus); *T* is the share right on taught pairs. These are E50's definitions and E50's scorer.

## The question

How does learning depend on the number of cells that a cell reads? Ten was fixed by us. Mr. Xu asks that a number we must fix be varied on a grid of powers of two and the effect read, before a later step asks whether a cell can set the number itself. The connections are drawn from all cells, so that the size of a neighbourhood is not a second number fixed by us; one run with N = 10 from all cells stands beside the record's N = 10 from the neighbourhood, so that the effect of that choice is measured once.

## What it cannot show

- Anything beyond one seed, one family, half the pairs and this network; other seeds follow only on the coordinator's word.
- A number of connections that suits a larger network. With 256 cells, every cell is reached from every cell within four steps at N = 16, three at N = 32 and two at N = 64, so that Mr. Xu's example of several passes (1, 50, 50², 50³) cannot be reproduced at this width; the grid shows the dependence on N at this width and nothing about how N should grow with the number of cells.
- How many connections a cell needs when its input or its error must come through other cells: here every cell reads one value of every used byte and the readout reads every cell, so that a cell receives input and error directly; N governs only how cells combine what they hold over positions.
- Differences smaller than those between draws: each N is one draw of connections, the draws for different N are not nested, and N = 10 from all cells shares 112 of 2,560 connections with the record's network.
- Computation: the masked map is computed as a dense product, so that CPU time does not fall with N; the report compares the networks by updates and by the multiply-adds of the receipt's rows, and states no saving of computation from CPU time.
- The effect of N apart from the scale of the start, which `L42.Layer` changes with N so that a cell's recurrent input keeps its variance.
- Connections that change during the run, or a cell that sets its own number (step 6 of the chain of steps): the connections are fixed here.
- Relay over a limited number of steps (E61): the error passes over the whole item in every run.
- Requirement A, and anything about a life.

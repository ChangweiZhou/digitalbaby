## How to read the table

Terms. A *backward pass* is any computation in which an error formed at the output is sent back to earlier units, whether exactly (the gradient) or through the signs of the forward weights or through separate feedback weights. A *local rule* changes a connection only from the activity of the two cells it joins and a signal present at that connection. A *teaching signal* is a quantity derived from the right answer and delivered to a unit. An *engineered device* is a part of the answer path computed by the program rather than learned by cells: a hash of recent bytes used as an address, a code chosen by the program, or a readout fitted by regression or by gradients. In the September and October platform, a *PN* is one of the 88 input channels driven by the bytes and a *KC* is one of the 5,177 units of the fixed sparse layer that the PNs feed; *Full151* is the project's native fly model whose value stores are written by teaching (`D/MINIFLY_MECHANISM_VARIANT_CATALOG_20260928.md`, lines 26 to 29). A *GRU* is a gated recurrent unit; *AdamW* is the optimizer of the November networks, which keeps running averages for each weight and a step count shared by all weights. *E0 to E4* are the levels of evidence of `minifly_b/package/DOCS/MINIFLY_OBJECTIVE_CONTRACT.md`, lines 11 to 17. *Steps 0 to 8* and the conditions are those of `PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_REVIEW_20261109/COORDINATION_20261117/ROADMAP_TO_END_TO_END_PERSISTENT_LEARNING_20261118.md` (sections 4 and 5, here "roadmap"); Mr. Xu's rules are those of `.../COORDINATION_20261117/WHAT_A_NEURON_HAS_20261118.md`, sections 7 to 10 (here "page"). *Rule B* is exact back-propagation and *rule S* (the sweep) is the rule that passes the error backward through each linear map by the signs of its weights times one scale for the map, over all positions of the item (`.../COORDINATION_20261117/INVENTORY_STRUCTURE_AND_WORLD_20261117.md`, line 22).

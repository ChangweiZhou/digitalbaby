### 1.2 The definitions of the script against the audit's text and the source

- **Form (a) is the brief's update.** `relay_brief` sums the readout's error with K rounds of the one-step backward applied to the errors at every position at once, shifted one position back (`shift` is `d[:, :-1] = g[:, 1:]`), and every path is cut, the cell's own included (the three parts `own_z`, `own_w` and `cross` are all moved one position). This is point 2 of the brief (`relayed_grads`, steps (d) and (e)), and the script's `cell_grads` is step (e). Its line 2.7 confirms that K = 0 gives the readout's error alone, and its line 2.3 reproduces the record's stored cosines with K = 64.
- **Form (b) is what the audit's sections 3.1 and 3.7 define.** `relay_crossings` carries a cell's own path to its end by a fixed-point iteration over the positions (the term `z * h(t-1)` and the cell's reading of itself, `own_z + own_w`), then lets the result cross once (`cross`), and repeats X + 1 times. `loss_levels` is the "X + 1 copies" definition of section 3.7 written in numpy: copy v takes a cell's own earlier state from itself (`hs[v] * Wd`, and `z * hs[v]`) and the other cells' earlier states from copy v + 1 (`other @ Wc.T`, the recurrent map with its diagonal set to zero); the last copy takes them from the unchanged network's states held constant; the readout reads copy 0. **"A cell's own path" is taken as both the term `z * h(t-1)` and the cell's reading of itself in the recurrent map**, in the text (section 3.1), in the torch line of section 3.7 (`rec(other) + own(hs[v]) - own(other)`) and in the script (`own_z` and `own_w`; `Wd` in `loss_levels`).
- **The sign rule on the diagonal.** In the audit's script a cell's reading of itself is sent back under the sweep by `Sh_diag`, the diagonal of `sign(Wh) * scale`, with the scale of `e42_cells.Layer.sign_scale` (the mean magnitude inside the mask, diagonal included): the same value as a crossing, which is what the record's `L42.MaskedSign` does. The torch line of section 3.7 says the same ("`own(h)` the diagonal of the recurrent map applied to `h` with the same backward value as the crossing"), so that the audit's definition is unambiguous there. Its section 3.1, read alone, does not say what the sweep sends back over the diagonal, and a reader could take the cell's own weight, which a cell has. The choice matters: on the test network of part 1, sending the diagonal back by the weight instead of `sign(W)` times the scale changes the gradient at X = 2 by 0.13 (largest relative difference), and with that choice the whole item no longer equals the sweep's update of the record (difference 0.13). Revision 3 fixes the record's choice in its Terms. In this agent's implementation (part 1) the diagonal is likewise `diag(sign(W) * scale)` under the sign rule and `diag(W)` under the exact rule.
- **The central differences of the script** test form (b) against `loss_levels` under the exact gradient only, for X = 0 to 3 (its line 2.6); the sign rule cannot be differentiated by central differences directly, and the script does not test form (b) under it.

### 1.3 An independent implementation of form (b)

Part 1 of `e61_revision_3_check.py`. The method differs from the script's: **one backward pass over the positions** that carries X + 1 errors at the states of a position, indexed by the number of crossings made; at each position the one-step Jacobian is split into its diagonal part (the term `z * h(t-1)` and the cell's reading of itself) and its off-diagonal part (the crossings); the diagonal part keeps the index, the off-diagonal part moves the error to the next index, and an error that would pass index X is dropped (the function `relay`, which also gives the whole item with one index and form (a) when the diagonal part moves the index as well). It is tested against the audit's "X + 1 copies" definition differentiated by central differences, on a small random gated network of the form of `e50_sparse_code.forward` in double precision (9 cells, each reading 3 others and itself; a masked input map; a readout through `l2`, `lm` and `l3`; 14 positions; 4 items whose three answer positions differ). **For the sign rule** the copies definition is written so that the ordinary derivative is the rule's: every map whose error the rule sends back by another value than its weight is written as `W h_ref + S (h - h_ref)`, with `h_ref` the states of the unchanged network and `S` the rule's backward value at the unchanged weights, both held constant, so that the values are unchanged at the unchanged weights and the derivative with respect to `h` is `S` (for the recurrent map, `lm` and `l3`; the gates and `z * h(t-1)` are differentiated exactly).

### Defect 1 (high). The rule on messages conflicts with the rule on stopped machines, and its premise is not true of the message tool.

(a) Brief, section 10: "A message reaches you only when your turn ends. Keep waits to ten minutes at most, so that a message is read within that time, and push work in progress every 30 minutes." Section 6: "The host stops a machine a few minutes after a session's turn ends."

(b) Read together, the two sentences tell the worker to end its turn at least every ten minutes, and ending the turn stops the machine on which the three lives run. The tool that the brief names in section 11 (`send_message`) offers, in its own description, a priority "next" that "joins the running turn without stopping it", so the first sentence is not true of that tool. The lives take about 3.7 CPU-hours each (script, part 8), so they need a turn that stays open for about four hours.

(c) A worker that ends its turn to read messages would have its lives killed every time. Each such stop loses up to 64 visits, about 13.6 CPU-minutes, because a state is saved only every 64 visits. The lives then wait, with no process running, until a message wakes the session.

(d) Replace the bullet of section 10 by: "Do not end your turn while a check or a life is running: the machine is stopped a few minutes after a turn ends. Wait in steps of at most ten minutes (one waiting command at a time), and commit and push work in progress at least every 30 minutes. The reviewer sends its messages with the priority that joins a running turn. If the session is nevertheless stopped, continue as section 6 says when it is started again."

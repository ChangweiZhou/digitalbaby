## Terms

- *The network* is E47's at 32 cells a side in its arm `one` (`$D:OPUS_TASK_E47_LOCALITY_AT_THE_INPUT_AND_THE_OUTPUT_20261116.md`): 1,024 cells on a square grid whose opposite edges are joined (a torus), each reading its own previous state and ten cells within three rows and three columns of it, every cell updated at every position of an item. The 256 *interface cells* of one 16 by 16 patch receive the code of the current byte and are read by the readout. The 768 *far cells* receive no input and are not read. A far cell's *ring* is its grid distance to the patch (the larger of its distances in rows and in columns, with the edges joined), divided by 3 and rounded up; it is the least number of connections by which a value can travel between the cell and the interface. Ring 1 has 228 cells, ring 2 has 300, ring 3 has 240. On the connections drawn, 103 far cells need one or two connections more than their ring.
- Beside the cells the network has a table of 8 numbers for each byte (the *byte table*), a map `l1` from these to 512 values (the *code* of the byte), the *input map* from the code to the three gates of each interface cell, and the *readout*: a map `lm` from the interface cells' states and a map `l2` from the code to 512 units, and a map `l3` from these to the 257 possible output bytes.
- *The recurrent weights* are the layer's tensor `Wc`: for each of the three gates and each cell, eleven weights, on the cell's own state and on its ten sources (33,792 numbers). At E47's start they are drawn uniformly between −1/√11 and 1/√11. The *size* of a weight is its absolute value.
- *Back-propagation* (rule B) is the exact gradient; *the sweep* (rule S) passes the error backward across each connection by the sign of its weight times one scale, the mean size of all recurrent weights.
- *The first family* is the task of E26: four operations on two numbers below 89 (addition and subtraction modulo 89, the larger and the smaller of the two), each written as a line of bytes with 0 to 12 capital *letters* that carry no information between the operands. Half of the pairs of operands of each operation are taught; the other half are never shown. Every 256 updates the network is scored; the scores of one such point are a *row*. Score A is the percentage of right answers on 600 untaught pairs of addition and subtraction written with 12 letters. Score R is the percentage on 2,000 untaught pairs of addition and subtraction whose answer needs the reduction modulo 89, written without letters. *The length test* is the percentage on 300 untaught pairs of addition written with 12 and with 40 letters. A score *reaches 80* at the first of three consecutive rows at or above 80, and *updates to 80* is the number of updates at that row. A *level* is the mean over the last three rows.
- *The two diagnostics* are E47's: a trained network is scored with the far cells held, after every position, at zero, or at their mean over the scored items at that position.
- *The share that reaches a ring* is the reviewer's measure (`$V:$R/SIGNAL_PASSAGE_AT_THE_START_20261117.md`): two items that differ in one byte are run through the network, and the root-mean-square difference of the states of a ring's cells, over the later positions, is divided by that of the interface cells. *The way back* is measured by adding a small difference to the states of the cells of ring 3 at one position; it is the root-mean-square difference that then appears in the interface cells over the later positions, divided by that of ring 3.
- *The trial* is E47's runs of seed 73129. A *receipt* is the file that a run or a check writes with its settings, its rows and the hashes of its sources. A *dry run* is a run of fewer than 24,576 updates, made to test the runner.

## The question

E47's trial showed that a network whose input and output are confined to a boundary learns the first family, and that its cells beyond the first ring take no part: holding rings 2 and 3 at their mean changes no score by more than 0.7 of a point (`$V:$R/E47_TRIAL_VERIFICATION_20261117.md`).

Two settings could prevent those cells from taking part, and neither was varied. The first is the start: at E47's starting weights a difference between two items hardly reaches them. The second is the weight decay of 0.25. The record supports the first and not the second. In the trial's arm `one` the decay did not shrink the weights of these cells (see "The size of the weights"); it did so in the arm `place`, which this job does not run. The decay is varied all the same, because it has had one value in every run of a local network (rule 1 of section 4 of the plan) and because it is not known whether a start three times larger survives it. **Do the cells beyond the first ring come into use, and is the family learned in fewer updates, when the network is trained from a start at which a difference between items reaches the third ring at a seventh of its size at the interface (a twentieth in items without letters), with the trial's weight decay and without it? Does removing the decay alone change either?**

A result that these cells are *used* would let item 12 of the plan (a second task learned in cells away from the interface) begin from that start. A result that they are *not used* does not close item 12: the first family is learned by 256 cells, so that nothing in this job requires the outer cells, and item 12 is then itself the test, on a task that the interface cells cannot hold alone.

## How the result is stated (fixed beforehand)

# Verification of E52 on the other two seeds (18 November 2026)

**What this is.** An independent check, from the committed files alone, of the twelve runs that the coordinator's decision 19 called for in E52 (the two learning rules under other learning rates), as delivered on the worker's branch (the remote branch whose name ends in `e52-learning-rates-sdga8c`) at commit `5cb22315` (`5cb2231504590a365c0ec96dcd76a67070a5a302`): the report `MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/E52_SEEDS_REPORT.md`, the tables `MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp45/e52_seeds/E52_SEEDS_TABLES.md`, and the receipts, weights and logs under `MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp45/e52_seeds/`. The worker's message to the reviewer is treated as a set of claims and checked in the same way. The reference texts are the brief `COORDINATION_20261117/TASK_E52_THE_TWO_RULES_UNDER_OTHER_LEARNING_RATES_20261117.md` (revision 2), message 4 of `COORDINATION_20261117/MESSAGES_TO_WORKERS_20261118.md` (decision 19 and its three points of practice), and the verification of the first seed, `E52_VERIFICATION_20261118.md`, with its script `e52_verify.py`.

Every figure below is printed by `e52_seeds_verify.py` (beside this file); the number in brackets after a figure, written "(S1)" to "(S6)", is the section of the script's output that prints it. The script reads the branches only through `git show`, `git ls-tree -r --name-only` and `git rev-parse <commit>:<path>`, uses the Python standard library and numpy, executes no file of the worker's branch, and writes no file. Its functions for reading weight files, for the panels and for the forward of the network are copied from `e52_verify.py`; its readings and margins were written here from the brief's words and from the definition of the margin that the report states. Run from the repository root:

    OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -I PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_REVIEW_20261109/e52_seeds_verify.py

The run from a clean start after the last edit of the script took 25.5 seconds. The script prints its sections in the order 1, 3, 2, 4, 5, 6, because the comparisons of section 2 use the readings of section 3. Written at about 17:07 UTC on 18 November 2026.

**Terms.** The terms of the brief are used as the brief defines them. *Rule B* is back-propagation; *rule S*, the sweep. The *half width* is H = 256, U = 128; the *present width* H = 512, U = 256. *f* is the taught fraction of operand pairs. *Score A* is the share right on never-taught add and sub pairs at 12 letters; *score R* the share right on never-taught add and sub pairs whose answer needs the modulus. *Updates to 80* (a *count*) is the first scored row from which three scored rows in a row are at or above 80% (rows every 256 updates); a run that does not reach 80 has no count, and its *last recordable count* is its dose less 512. A *setting* in this document is a width with a fraction (half width with f = 0.50, half width with f = 0.25, present width with f = 0.25); a *case*, as in the brief, is a rule at a setting. The *lag* is the sweep's count divided by back-propagation's at one setting; at the *own rates* each rule's count is taken at its best rate among those tried. The words *faster*, *slower*, *the same*, *gains*, *smaller*, *larger*, *gone* and *the same within the scatter of one seed* are the brief's. A *row* is a scored row of 256 updates. The *margin* of a reading is the fewest rows by which one count to 80 (of one run, on one score) must move for the reading to change, under the definition in Section 2 of the report: a count c moves to c ± 256k, never below 256; moved past the last recordable row it becomes "not reached"; a count not reached becomes reached at the last recordable row less 256(k − 1); every reading of the setting is recomputed from the moved counts. "None within 200" means that no such move of up to 200 rows changes the reading.

Ordered by weight. Each is marked as an error of fact, an overstatement, an omission or a matter of form. No error of fact was found.

**1. Omission: the statement that the explanation is not refuted on seed 73129 rests on one setting, and its margin is not given.** The brief's test refutes the explanation for a seed "if the sweep gains at no width and fraction, or if, wherever the sweep gains, back-propagation gains too and the lag is not smaller". On seed 73129 the sweep gains at the half width with f = 0.25 and at the present width with f = 0.25 (S3). At the first, which is the case the explanation names, back-propagation gains too and the lag is the same on R and on A (2.12 at 0.001, 2.90 at the own rates on R, three rows from "larger"). The explanation therefore escapes refutation on this seed only through the present width, where back-propagation does not gain (none within 200) and the lag is gone. The report states the facts at the named case (its Section 6) but gives no margin for the verdict; the message says only "The explanation is not refuted by the brief's test on any seed", without saying that on 73129 it holds through the present width alone and that at the named case the lag on that seed is not smaller.

# Verification of the final report of E52 (18 November 2026)

**What this is.** An independent check, from the committed files alone, of `MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/E52_REPORT.md` (the two learning rules under other learning rates) at the worker's commit `cb5cacfc` (`cb5cacfcb01384073c9c1a6e6071e73614a2b8a2`), against the brief `TASK_E52_THE_TWO_RULES_UNDER_OTHER_LEARNING_RATES_20261117.md`, revision 2. Every figure below is printed by `e52_verify.py` (beside this file), which reads the branches only through `git show` and `git ls-tree -r --name-only`, uses the Python standard library and numpy, executes no file of the worker's branch, and writes no file. Run from the repository root:

    OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -I PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_REVIEW_20261109/e52_verify.py

Written at about 10:52 UTC on 18 November 2026. The run from a clean start after the last edit of the script took 17.2 seconds (the first run, which also fetched the 43 weight files into the repository's object store, took about 42 seconds). The reviewer ran the script again (the run ended at 10:53 UTC and took 20.7 seconds): the output holds the same 1,334 comparisons with the same two differences. The reviewer set the numbers of sections 3 and 8 beside that output and found them as printed; its one correction concerns finding 8 and is marked there.

**Terms.** The terms of the brief are used as the brief defines them, and are repeated here where this document relies on them. *Rule B* is back-propagation; *rule S*, the sweep, passes the error backward through each linear map by the signs of its weights times one scale per map. The *half width* is H = 256, U = 128; the *present width* H = 512, U = 256. *f* is the taught fraction of operand pairs. *Score A* is the share right on never-taught add and sub pairs at 12 letters; *score R* the share right on never-taught add and sub pairs whose answer needs the modulus, without letters. *Updates to 80* (a *count*) is the first scored row from which three scored rows in a row are at or above 80% (rows every 256 updates); a run that does not reach 80 has no count, and its *last recordable count* is its dose less 512. A *case* is a rule at a width and a fraction. The *lag* is the sweep's count divided by back-propagation's at one width and fraction. A count at another rate is *faster* below two thirds of the count at 0.001, *slower* above 1.5 times it, and *the same* between (the *band*). A *row* in this document always means a scored row, that is, 256 updates; "one row from a different reading" means that moving a single count to 80 by 256 updates would change the reading. A *receipt* is the JSON record a run writes; a *partial receipt* is one written during a run that did not finish. A *checkpoint* is the saved state from which `exp52_resume.py` continues a run. *Resumed* means continued from a checkpoint; *restarted* means started again from update 0.

## 3. Findings

Ordered by weight. Each is marked as an error of fact, an overstatement, an omission or a matter of form.

**1. Omission: the readings that carry the report's conclusions are one to three rows from a different reading, and the report names only two of them.** The report writes: "Two of these readings sit close to the band's edge", and names the lag on R at present width with a quarter of the pairs and the lag at present width with half of the pairs. The script moves each count to 80 by whole rows and finds the fewest rows that change a reading:
- the lag on R at present width, f = 0.25, *smaller*: one row (back-propagation at 0.001 at 4,608 instead of 4,352 gives "not decided");
- the lag at present width, f = 0.50, *the same*: one row (back-propagation at 0.001 at 4,352 gives "larger", on R and on A);
- the lag on R at half width, f = 0.25, *smaller*: two rows (back-propagation at 0.003 at 5,120 instead of 5,632 gives "the same"). This is the reading on which P52.4 and the report's statement on the explanation under test rest, for the case the explanation names; the report does not say that it is two rows from the band's edge;
- back-propagation *does not gain* at present width, f = 0.50, and at present width, f = 0.25: one row each (at 0.001 at 4,352 instead of 4,096, or at 0.003 at 2,816 instead of 3,072, it would gain); at half width, f = 0.25: three rows (at 0.003 at 4,864 instead of 5,632).

## 7. The runner as text

- **Only the rate differs, and in the control the decay.** `exp52_rate.make_opt` calls `exp26_candidate.make_opt` (which asserts the standing groups), then sets `lr` to the run's rate in every group and, only with `--product`, sets group 0's `weight_decay` to `0.25 * 0.001 / rate`. Betas, epsilon, the byte table without decay, the update (`exp26_candidate.step`), the stream, the panels and the start network are E41's. The groups are asserted at the start and at the end of each run. `exp26_candidate.step` uses no random numbers.
- **Scored on untaught items.** A and R come from `exp41_scale.panels` on the held-out pairs; the length test from the run's own held-out split, asserted free of taught pairs; every training batch is asserted to hold only taught pairs. The taught panel is scored and tabled but enters no reading.
- **Divergence.** A row whose losses are not all finite stops the run and records it as diverged; no run diverged.
- **Saving and resuming** (`exp52_resume.py`): every 4,096 updates, after the row and the partial receipt are written, the model's state, the optimizer's state, the receipt so far, the CPU counters and the wall clock are saved atomically. On `--resume` the stream is rebuilt from (seed, fraction) and advanced by drawing and digesting every batch, the digest is asserted equal to the checkpoint's last row, and only then are the states loaded. The position in the stream and the record of rows are therefore restored; no random-number state is saved, and none is needed by the update. No hash of the checkpoint is kept. The rest of the run is `exp52_rate.run` line for line.
- No departure from the brief was found in the runner beyond the resuming the report declares (Departure 1), which replaced the brief's rule that an interrupted run is started again from the beginning, for three runs, on a request of the reviewer that was in no committed file when this verification was made (now the appendix).

## 8. What the report may be read as showing

On seed 93683, by the brief's rules, recomputed here without exception: at 0.001 the eight runs are E41's, and the lag is E41's (1.53 and 1.38 at half width with half of the pairs, 3.31 and 2.29 with a quarter, 1.19 and 1.19 at present width with half, and above 2.70 (a bound from the last recordable count) and 2.79 with a quarter, on R and on A). Each rule's best rate among those tried is 0.003 in seven of the eight cases (the eighth is a tie of 0.001 and 0.003). At each rule's own best rate the lag is 1.53 and 1.42 at half width with half of the pairs (the same), 2.05 and 1.93 at half width with a quarter (smaller on R, the same on A), 1.73 and 1.73 at present width with half (the same), and 1.75 and 1.33 at present width with a quarter (smaller on both). It is nowhere gone and nowhere larger; the smallest lag at the rules' own rates is 1.33.

The report may therefore be read as showing, for one seed: that the sweep's lag behind back-propagation does depend on the learning rate where a quarter of the pairs is taught (on R at both widths, and on A at the present width), because at 0.003 the sweep gains much more than back-propagation there; and that the lag does not vanish when each rule runs at its own best rate among those tried, at any width or fraction. Both parts of the brief's question receive an answer of "in part": some of the lag at a quarter of the pairs belongs to the one learning rate, and a lag of at least a third remains at every width and fraction. The explanation under test is not refuted on this seed. The brief's rules for the next steps are met as the report states: the other two seeds are called for at half width with both fractions and at present width with a quarter of the pairs, and the condition for asking again for the sweep's long lives (the sweep gains at the half width) is met, at 0.003.

The report may not be read as showing any of the following. That the lag is smaller at a quarter of the pairs as a property of the rules rather than of this seed: the two "smaller" readings on R are one and two rows from "not decided" and "the same", and E41's three seeds already differ at 0.001 by as much as the change measured here (at half width with a quarter of the pairs, the lag on R is 2.12, 3.31 and 3.31 on seeds 73129, 93683 and 116089; on A 1.70, 2.29 and 3.23). That back-propagation does not gain from its own rate at present width: that reading is one row from the opposite at both fractions. That 0.003 is the best rate of either rule in any sense finer than "fewest updates among 0.0003, 0.001, 0.003 and 0.01": the grid is three-fold, and a faster rate does not end higher (back-propagation at half width with half of the pairs ends at 91.25 on R at 0.003 against 93.97 at 0.001). That the sweep's R is steadier at a smaller rate: at equal updates it is not (finding 2). Anything about lives: a birth made at a rate other than 0.001 is not admitted to a life by `exp26_candidate.admit_e26`, as the report says, so a long life at 0.003 needs a design decision before it can be run. The product control gives one observation outside the brief's fixed readings, which should not be read as a result: with decay 0.0833 at 0.003 the sweep reaches 80 on R before back-propagation (5,632 against 6,912, a ratio of 0.81; on A 1.00).

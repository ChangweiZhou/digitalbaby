## Terms, material and method

**Terms**, as the brief and the verification of seed 73129 define them. *Rule B* is back-propagation (the exact gradient); *rule S* is the sweep, which passes the error backward through each linear map by the signs of its weights times one scale. *The first family* is add, sub, max and min under modulus 89; its *reduction pairs* are the pairs of add and sub whose answer needs the modulus (3,916 of each operation). *The reduction score* is the mean of the two percentages of whole answers right on the reduction pairs without letters (E43's score 1). *The new task* (also called the unrelated task) is E43's `unrelated` task; its *held-out score* is the percentage of whole answers right on E43's panel of 300 items that are never taught. *The birth* is E26's network of the seed, trained on the first family alone. *An update* is one optimizer step on a batch of 64 items. *A row* is a number of updates at which E43's full scores are recorded; *the probe* is a light score of 400 reduction pairs (drawn by `Random("e51|<seed>|probe")`) and of the new task's held-out panel, recorded more often. *The share k* in B1 means that each item is a first-family item with chance 1/k. In B2 a *cycle* is a *gap* of 256 updates on the new task alone followed by a *review* on the first family alone, which stops when the probe reaches 90% of its value at update 0 (*the goal*) or after 1,024 updates. *A receipt* is the file of results that a run or a check writes. *The data digest* is a running SHA-256 over the batches as tensors; equal digests mean that the same items were given in the same order.

The brief's fixed readings: **kept** at a share, if the reduction score at update 4,096 is within 5 points of row 0; **the least share that keeps**, the smallest share that is kept with every larger share also kept; **the review shortens**, if the review of cycle 8 needs at most half the updates of the review of cycle 1; **more is left**, if the first-family probe at the end of the gap of cycle 8 is at least 20 points above that of cycle 1. The predictions: PB.1, a share of 1/4 keeps the first family under both rules; PB.2, a share of 1/32 does not keep it under either rule; PB.3, the review shortens under back-propagation; PB.4, more is left after a gap at cycle 8 under back-propagation.

**Material.** At commit 2abb0c3f: the report `E51_REPORT.md` (section 9), `partB/E51_TABLES_B_SEEDS.md`, `partB/E51_READ_B_SEEDS.json`, for each new seed the eight receipts of B1, the two of B2, the two runs of check 8 (`partB/check8/`), the two runs of B3 at rate 0.001 that serve check 9, the four check receipts (`partB/checks/`), 28 weight files and 34 logs, and the worker's new reader `e51_partb_seeds.py`, which was read and not run. The references (E43's `unrelated` receipts, the second addendum's `unrelated-rehearse` receipts, E26's births) of the new seeds were read on the worker's commit and are byte-identical to those on the default branch at 7cabb13a.

**Method.** Nothing was trained, torch was not used, and no code of another branch was executed: every file was read with `git show` into memory. The script is `e51_partb_seeds_verify.py` beside this file, called *the reader* below. It imports the verification of seed 73129 (`e51_partb_verify.py`) and, through it, the verification of Part A (`e51_trial_verify.py`), and points their functions at commit 2abb0c3f and at each seed in turn: the items, the streams, the digest of the batches, the readings of B1 and of B2 (B2 from the trace of probes rather than from the receipt's own cycle records), the checks of a receipt's settings, the reader of weight files, the forward pass in double precision and the generation of answers. ## 6. What Part B shows over the three seeds, and what it does not

Part B starts, for each seed and rule, from one network trained on four operations under modulus 89 and teaches it an unrelated task. Without rehearsal the first family's reduction pairs are lost completely on every seed and rule while the new task is learned. When one item in k is a first-family item, what remains after 4,096 updates falls as the share falls, without exception, on every seed and rule; the new task reaches at least 98.7% at every share. Half of the stream keeps the first family within 5 points on every seed and rule; a quarter does so on one seed of three, by 1.3 points under the sweep and by 0.02 points under back-propagation; a thirty-second never does. With reviews between gaps, nothing of the first family is left after the first gap on any seed and rule, and from the fourth gap on 39 to 78% of the probe is left after a gap, so more is left at cycle 8 than at cycle 1 on all six pairs; the review needed in cycle 8 is at most half that of cycle 1 under the sweep on all three seeds, but under back-propagation on one seed only. These are results of three seeds of one network at one size, one birth per seed and rule, one learning rate and one ordering of the streams per run. Part B does not show how the threshold share would vary with other seeds (the least share that keeps is a reading at the edge of a fixed grid, and on one seed it turns on two answers), does not test shares between 1/2 and 1/4, does not separate the effect of earlier reviews in B2 from the fact that the new task is already learned when the later gaps begin, and says nothing about B3 on the new seeds, about more than two tasks, about a share that changes with time, or about runs longer than those of the brief.

## What could not be checked

The updates were not repeated, so the training is verified through the data digests, the hashes and the scores of the saved weights, not by rerunning it. Only the final weights of each run are saved, so the earlier rows and every probe, including every probe of B2's cycles, are verified from the receipts only. Of the 28 final weights, two were scored with the forward pass and the others were checked by their file hashes. The CPU times are the receipts' own. Whether the forecast of check 11 was made before the second starts of seed 93683 cannot be established, since its receipt carries no time; the absence of a machine restart is the worker's statement. The worker's reader `e51_partb_seeds.py` was read and not run; its output files were checked against the reader's own computation instead.

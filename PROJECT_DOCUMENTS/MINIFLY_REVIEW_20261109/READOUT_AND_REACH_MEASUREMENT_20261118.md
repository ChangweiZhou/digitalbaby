# What the readout needs, where the answer becomes readable, and how far the operands have reached at `=`: measurements on the six saved networks of E50

Written 18 November 2026, 21:56 UTC (clock read with `date -u`). Read-only: nothing was trained, no file of an experiment folder
was executed, nothing was committed. Script: `PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_REVIEW_20261109/readout_and_reach.py` (numpy and the
standard library; reads every file with `git show`; writes no file; 289 seconds on one core). Every figure below is printed by
that script under the section number given here.

## Terms

- **The networks.** The six saved final networks of E50, seed 73129, read from
  `origin/codex/minifly-consolidated-20261023` (commit 5a27108ef79c), folder `MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp45/e50/`:
  `e50_<setting>_<rule>_73129.pt` for the settings C46-one (each cell reads 46 of the 512 values of the fixed code, one value of
  each of the 46 used bytes), C16-in (each cell reads 16 values) and C-dense (each cell reads all 512 values), and the rules S
  (the rule by signs) and B (the exact gradient). In all six, each cell reads itself and 10 other cells, and every readout
  unit reads all 256 cells.
- **The forward pass.** At each position the code z1 of the current byte is the row of the fixed code table stored in the
  weight file. Each cell computes its new state h(t) once, by a GRU step, from z1 through the input map (multiplied by the input
  mask) and from h(t-1) through the recurrent map (multiplied by the recurrent mask, which contains the diagonal). The readout
  computes z2 = tanh(W2 z1 + b2 + Wm h + bm) (512 units) and the output W3 z2 + b3 (257 units). This follows
  `e50_sparse_code.forward` with period 1. The masks, the code table and the weights are taken from each file.
- **Positions.** Position 0 holds the start symbol; the prompt follows; the position of `=` is the last prompt position. The
  output at `=` is the first answer digit; the output at the next position (whose input is the first answer digit) is the
  second answer digit; the output at the position after that is the end symbol.
- **The panels.** Rebuilt as in the experiment's scorer (code copied from the outside audit's script `audit_e61_relay.py`):
  A, never-taught pairs at 12 letters, 300 items for each of add, sub, max, min (1,200 items); R, pairs that need the reduction,
  no letters, 1,000 items for each of add and sub (2,000 items); T, taught pairs at 12 letters, 300 items for each of the four
  operations (1,200 items). Within one operation of one panel all items have the same length.
- **Exact answer.** Under greedy generation, the three generated bytes (two digits and the end symbol) equal the right ones.
  An item is exact under greedy generation if and only if the three answer outputs are right when the right bytes are fed;
  this holds also under every intervention below, because each intervention is a fixed function of the position and of the
  input. Section 0 checks the equivalence on the intact networks.
- **The direct path** is the term W2 z1 of the readout; **the path through the cells** is the term Wm h. **Removed**: the term
  set to zero, its bias (b2 or bm) kept; for Wm h this is the source's own `silence` option. **Replaced by the mean**: the term
  replaced, at each position, by its mean over the items of the same operation of the same panel in the intact network. Both
  interventions act on the readout only; the cell states are unchanged, so only the readout's outputs at the three answer
  positions can change the score.
- **Eta squared** of a cell for a digit (b2, b1, a2 or a1): the variance of the group means of the cell's state at `=`
  (groups: the values of that digit, weighted by group size) divided by the total variance of the state over the panel's items.
  Groups with no item are left out (b1 and a1 take 9 values on R and on A, b2 and a2 take 10). For a variable unrelated to the
  digit the expected value is (g - 1)/(N - 1): 0.0045 on R (N = 2,000) and 0.0075 on A (N = 1,200), both well below 0.05.
- **A cell that reads a digit byte directly** (C16-in only): a cell whose input mask contains at least one value owned by at
  least one of the ten digit bytes. In C16-in, 8 cells read no value of any digit byte, 87 read values of 1 or 2 digit bytes,
  131 of 3 or 4, 30 of 5 to 7. In C46-one and C-dense every cell reads values of all ten digit bytes. A cell that reads no
  digit byte can receive a digit's value only from other cells through the recurrent map; this follows from the forward pass.

## Method

For each network, the file and its receipt were read into memory with `git show` (one at a time; nothing written to disk),
the forward pass was run in double precision with the right bytes fed (teacher forcing) on every item of A, R and T, and
greedy generation was run separately for the check against the receipt.

Section 3 uses a least-squares classifier: features standardized with the training half's mean and standard deviation, ridge
regression on one-hot targets with penalty lambda times the number of training items, lambda chosen from 1e-4 to 1e2 (seven
values) by 5-fold cross-validation inside the training half, and the class taken as the largest fitted output. For the answer
digits a multinomial logistic (softmax) classifier was also fitted (L2 penalty chosen from 1e-4, 1e-3, 1e-2 by the same
cross-validation; 300 steps of Nesterov's accelerated gradient). The items of a panel were pooled over its operations and split
at random into halves by a fixed seed: R 1,000 training and 1,000 test items; A 600 and 600. The features are the 256 cell
states h or the 512 readout units z2, at `=` (target: the first answer digit) and at the next position (target: the second
answer digit; the input there is the right first digit). Controls from h at `=`: b2, b1, a2, a1 (ten classes) and the
operation (two classes on R, four on A). Beside each share stands the share of the most frequent training class in the test
half.

## What the figures show

1. In all six networks the readout gives almost no exact answer from the current byte alone: with the term Wm h removed, 0 to
   1.33 per cent of the items are exact and the first digit is right about as often as one in ten. Every exact answer
   therefore depends on the readout's input from the cells.
2. The readout also uses the direct path. Removing W2 z1 lowers the exact share on every panel and in every network, by 18 to
   37 points under B and by 37 to 66 points under S (for example R: C46-one S from 85.75 to 37.10, C46-one B from 95.15 to
   73.50). The loss already appears at the first digit, where the byte is `=` for every item, so part of what the direct path
   supplies there is a contribution that does not depend on the item; the replacement by the mean, which keeps that
   contribution, lowers the score less, and its losses lie at the second digit and the end symbol, where the input is an answer digit.
3. At `=`, h holds the operands and the operation in a form that a linear map reads almost without error (100 per cent for b2,
   b1 and the operation on R, and 81.5 to 100 per cent for the others), while the answer digit is read from h linearly on only
   40.6 to 59.0 per cent of the test items (softmax: 42.3 to 64.6), and from z2 on 67.3 to 97.4 per cent. Because the byte at
   `=` is the same for every item, z2 at `=` is a fixed function of h alone; so h determines the first digit as far as the
   network does, but in a form that is not linear, and the readout's hidden layer produces a linearly readable form of it.
4. At `=`, the value of b2 (read one position earlier) accounts for more than 5 per cent of the variance of 116 to 223 of the
   256 cells and for more than 20 per cent of 44 to 101 cells. In C16-in, of the 8 cells that read no value of any digit byte,
   4 to 6 exceed 0.05 for b2 and 2 to 4 exceed 0.20; in these cells the value of b2 can only have arrived from other cells.

## What the figures do not show

- They do not show which cells or connections carry the operands, nor whether the dependence of the 8 C16-in cells on b2 serves
  the answer; the measurement of eta squared is descriptive and isolates no cause.
- Eta squared for one digit also contains whatever part of the variance is shared with the other digits through their
  correlation in the panel (on R the pairs are restricted to a + b at least 89 or a below b; A pools four operations); the
  counts are therefore not counts of cells that hold one digit only.
- A low share for the linear map from h does not show that the cells have not computed the digit in another form; it shows
  only that the digit is not linearly separable in h with 600 or 1,000 training items. The two classifiers agree within a few
  points on most entries; on R the softmax classifier reads the first digit from h 9 to 14 points better in C46-one and
  C-dense (and the second digit 17 points better in C-dense B), so the figures for h depend somewhat on the classifier.
- The removal of W2 z1 changes the readout's operating point as well as its access to the byte; the measurement does not
  separate the two. The interventions act on the readout only and leave the cells' own use of z1 untouched.
- C-dense was scored at its last row (update 24,576), the other two settings at update 49,152; differences between settings
  are differences between these saved networks and are not attributed here to the setting.

## What was not measured

The networks at earlier updates; other seeds; the length test (40 letters); the operations max and min on R (R contains add
and sub only); an intervention on what one cell receives from other cells (the outside audit's part 5 measures that for
C46-one); the position of b2 itself and the positions between b2 and `=` other than `=`; nonlinear classifiers from h with a
hidden layer; the dependence of the end symbol on the cells beyond the counts printed in section 2.

# Verification of the report of the trial of E44, job L2, release 1 (world 1, two lives of 1,024 visits), 18 November 2026

**Verdict.** Every figure of the report (`E44_L2_TRIAL_REPORT.md`) and of its tables (`E44_L2_TRIAL_TABLES.md`, `E44_L2_TRIAL.json` and the two CSV files) for the quantities D2 to D10, D14 and D15, the blocks of 64 visits, the medians of the first-visit level and the learning probe was recomputed here from the event files and list files, and every one is confirmed: no figure differs, at full precision against the JSON file and character by character against the 76 table rows that this verification rebuilt. The two receipts are complete, record 1,024 visits and the settings their names state, and record the one resume from the state after 768 visits; the two event files are continuous across that resume, with no visit or update missing or doubled, and every recorded column that can be recomputed from the world (task, wrap, mirror, correct, blind and the per-task counter `seen`) equals the recomputation over all 4,194,304 rows of each file. The report reads the four stop conditions of the roster note rightly: none holds, under either reading of the two conditions whose wording admits two readings. Three sentences say more than their figures support: the sentence that `naive`'s two quantities in the D15 table do not rise with the number of earlier visits (the figure over the first 16 updates is higher in the last cell than in the first in all three classes of absence), the D15 sentence on `replay`, which is accurate as a description of one life but is not separated from the age of the life at which the returns fall, and the sentence that the probe branches after 1,024 visits "are done", which the committed files do not show. One record is incomplete without affecting any figure: the receipts' list of saved states omits the state after 768 visits, from which both lives were resumed.

The worker's commit is 118b6047 (the worker's branch for the first long lives); every file named below lies under `MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp45/e44/L2/` at that commit unless another place is given. The script is `PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_REVIEW_20261109/e44_l2_trial_verify.py` (called "the script" below), run from the repository root as `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -I PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_REVIEW_20261109/e44_l2_trial_verify.py <part>`; each figure below names the part that gives it.

## Terms

- A **life** is a sequence of visits; in a **visit** of 256 updates the network is taught one **family** of four tasks (addition, subtraction, maximum and minimum under one modulus). Each update holds 48 taught items and 16 **asked** items; an asked item is answered by the network before the update and is never taught.
- The **event file** (`lives/<life>.npz`) holds one row for each asked item: its update, visit, task, whether the answer was right (`correct`), whether a predictor that repeats the last taught answer of the task was right (`blind`), whether the pair needs the reduction by the modulus (`wrap`), the three generated bytes, and two counters (`seen`: items of the task taught so far; `replayed`: stored items of the task replayed so far). The **list file** (`lives/<life>_list.npz`) holds the network's answers to a fixed list of 200 reduction items for each family met, at the end of every eighth visit and at the end of each visit to the family. The **receipt** (`lives/<life>.json`) records the settings, digests of every visit's stream and taught batches, losses, CPU time and resumes.
- `naive` is the arm without rehearsal; `replay` keeps a store of taught items (capacity 55,209 items, the bytes of one copy of the weights) and fills 24 of the 48 taught places of every update from it (ρ = 0.5).
- A **continuation** is a visit to the family of the visit before (an absence of 1); a **return** is a visit to a family met before after an absence of 2 or more visits. D1 to D15 are the definitions of the roster note (the file at the repository root whose name ends in `_TASK_E44_L2_ROSTER_FIRST_LONG_LIVES_20261117.md`, revision 2 with the reviewer's D15, at 118b6047), D1 to D14 being those of section 5 of `AUDIT_E44_L2_ROSTER_NOTE_20261117.md`.
- The reviewer's tools used: `e44_world_reader.py` (a second implementation of the world), and part J (`l2_quantities`, `kept_on_list`, `answer_class`) and part K of `e44_l2_checks.py`, imported by the script. Nothing of the worker's branch was executed; its files were read with `git show`.

## 3. The stop conditions of the trial

The roster note (section "The trial", identical in this respect on the default branch and at 118b6047) says that the worker continues without waiting unless one of four conditions holds. Each is restated with the figures that decide it (part H).

1. **A loss that is not finite.** Every visit's mean, first and last loss is finite in both receipts; the largest mean loss of a visit is 0.4741 (`naive`, visit 184) and 0.3849 (`replay`, visit 54). Does not hold.
2. **The buffer below capacity after visit 64.** The capacity is 55,209 items (1,269,828 bytes). The store is a reservoir offered 48 new items at every update, which adds items until it is full and afterwards only replaces them; it is therefore full from update 1,150, in visit 4, onwards. The receipt records 55,209 items at the end, a largest count of 55,209, and 12,582,912 items offered; the norms file records 55,209 items and 938,638 bytes in the state after 64 visits (a figure the worker read from the state, which is not read here). Does not hold.
3. **The score by age on reduction pairs below 0.05 over visits 512 to 1,023 in both arms.** The mean of D2 over those visits is 0.6727 (`naive`) and 0.7147 (`replay`); medians 0.689 and 0.717; lowest visits 0.137 and 0.432. Does not hold under either reading.
4. **A forecast for release 1 above 75 CPU-hours.** The two lives took 3.417 and 3.443 CPU-hours (12.01 and 12.10 CPU-seconds a visit, the list and the stream's regeneration included). At these costs the 32 lives of release 1 (8 lives of 1,024 visits, 8 of 256 and 16 decay lives of 256) take 48.0 CPU-hours, as the worker states; 48.3 with the probe. Does not hold.

The report's "None holds" and the JSON's `any_stop_condition_holds: false` are right. The note's further rule for the trial, that `replay`'s median first-visit level below 0.20 would remove the trend of learning of `replay` from reading and add ρ = 0.25 to release 2, does not apply: the median is 0.595 (0.393 over the families met in the first 256 visits) (part D).

## 4. Sentences that say more than their figures

The report states in its first paragraph and in section 5 that the trial is one world on one birth and that no statement in it is a result of the roster. Most of its sentences keep to that. Three do not, and one is worded more strongly than its source.

1. *"`naive`: neither quantity rises with the number of earlier visits at a fixed class of absence; kept stays between 0.000 and 0.035 in every cell, and the first 16 updates between 0.050 and 0.157."* The figures for kept at return support "no rise": the cells are non-monotone and all at or below 0.035. The figures over the first 16 updates do not support "neither rises" as stated: in every class of absence the cell of 16 or more earlier visits is higher than the cell of one earlier visit (0.157 against 0.132 after 2 to 4 visits, 0.101 against 0.068 after 5 to 32, 0.112 against 0.055 after 33 or more), although the sequences are not monotone and the cells hold 12 to 123 returns of one life, with no interval. What the figures support: kept at return shows no rise; the figure over the first 16 updates is not monotone in the number of earlier visits, and its last cell exceeds its first in all three classes.
2. *"`replay`: both rise with the number of earlier visits in every class of absence."* As a description of this life it is exact: both sequences rise monotonically in all three classes (part E), and they also rise under the second reading of "earlier visits". The table does not separate the number of earlier visits from the age of the life: the returns with 16 or more earlier visits fall later in the life than those with one (median visit 539 against 264 after 2 to 4 visits, 642 against 434 after 5 to 32, 696 against 572 after 33 or more; part E). The report's parenthesis names one quantity the table does not separate (what the store holds of the family) but not this one. What the figures support: in one life of one world, kept at return under `replay` is higher at returns to families visited more often, which are also returns later in the life.
3. *"The branches after 1,024 visits are done and enter the final report."* At 118b6047 the probe folder holds, for the branches from the states after 1,024 visits, only the start records (01:42:30 and 01:42:54 UTC; the commit is dated 01:45:39 UTC), and no event file, receipt or completion marker. The committed files show that the branches were started, not that they are done.
4. *"Check 5 showed that a resumed life is equal to an uninterrupted one in weights, optimizer, events, learned batches and list."* Check 5 (`checks/E44_L2_CHECKS.json`, passed) compared one `replay-wd0` life of 8 visits stopped after 4. For the two trial lives the files show less and more than that: the weights and optimizer state after the resume cannot be compared with an uninterrupted run, since none exists, but the event files are continuous on every recomputable column, and the 29 and 23 visits run both before and after the resume print the same share right in both runs (section 1). The sentence is accurate about check 5; it should not be read as a check of these two lives' weights.

Two smaller points of wording, neither changing a figure. The report's D8 table heads its count column "Returns" while its first row (absence of 1 visit) counts continuations. The figure "11.65 and 11.73 CPU-seconds a visit" is the mean of the visits' own CPU time, without the list and other work; the total CPU time divided by 1,024 is 12.01 and 12.10 seconds, and the forecast rightly uses the latter.

## 5. What the trial shows and does not show

The trial shows that both lives of world 1 ran to 1,024 visits with finite losses, a full store under `replay` from the fifth visit on, and scores far above the stop condition's floor, so that the roster could continue; and that the files are internally consistent, including across the resume after 768 visits. On this one world and one birth, without rehearsal the network keeps almost nothing of a family across an absence of two or more visits (1.5 to 2.6% right on the first update of a return, against 81% on a continuation), and the most common wrong answer at such a return is the right answer under the modulus of the family taught in the visit before (42 to 48% of answers); with the replayed store it keeps about half (47 to 53%), answers under the modulus of the family taught last almost vanish (1 to 2%), and the wrong answers are mostly the right answer under the modulus of another earlier family (16 to 29%) or some other two-digit number (17 to 25%). With the store, the late level is higher (0.730 against 0.669) and the level reached on a family's first visit is lower (median 0.595 against 0.757; 0.393 against 0.653 over the first 17 families). The trend of learning over first visits is positive in both lives at 1,024 visits and slightly negative at 256. The trial does not show any of the comparisons that the note states over worlds: with one world there is no interval, so no difference between the arms can be called higher, lower or not distinguished in the note's sense (D11), no comparison can be closed (D12), and neither arm can be called stuck or still learning (D13). The D15 table, likewise, shows how kept at return varies with the number of earlier visits in one life, without separating that number from the age of the life or from the contents of the store.

## What could not be checked

- The saved states (`*_state_v*.pt`) need torch and were not read: the store's content after 64 visits, the weights and optimizer state at the resume, and the norms are taken from the worker's records. The `replayed` column could be checked only for monotony, since the store's sampling depends on the generator state inside the state file.
- The times at which the machine and the container stopped (21:15 and about 00:57 UTC) are not recorded in any committed file.
- Host facts (Python, torch and numpy versions, peak memory, CPU times) are the receipts' records and were not measured.
- The worker's checks 1 to 7 and the checks of the runner change were not rerun; only check 5's recorded result was read.
- The learning probe's branches after 1,024 visits have no committed results.

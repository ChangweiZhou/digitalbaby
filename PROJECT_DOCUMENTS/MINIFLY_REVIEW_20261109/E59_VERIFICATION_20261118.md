## 1 What was verified, and from which commit

The subject is the report `MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp45/e59/E59_REPORT.md` of E59, the experiment in which E43's unrelated task is learned from four starts (a fresh, a born, a lived and a shuffled born network) with 2,048, 512 and 256 taught pairs under the two learning rules. The report was verified at the worker's commit 4594ffc9 (4594ffc927ff5aab92aa172349e5d5d5ea3f4562). The standard against which it was read is the brief, revision 2 (`PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_REVIEW_20261109/COORDINATION_20261117/TASK_E59_THE_UNRELATED_TASK_FROM_A_FRESH_A_BORN_AND_A_LIVED_NETWORK_20261117.md`), whose text at commit 39057293, where the worker read it, is byte for byte the text on the reviewer's branch. After the main work the worker's branch was fetched once; its head was then 55d480c7, two commits after 4594ffc9 (section 5.4 and finding 9).

Terms used here are those of the brief. In addition: a *receipt* is the JSON file a run or a check writes; a *row* is one scoring of a run (273 rows per run, at update 0, every 16 updates to 512 and every 32 updates to 8,192); *updates to 80* is the first row at which the held panel at 12 fillers is at 240 of 300 or above and stays so at every row up to 64 updates later; a *seed's word* and a *comparison's word* are the brief's fixed readings (*fewer*, *more*, *about the same*, *not decided*, *neither reaches*); a *presentation* is one update, a batch of 64 items, so that updates to 80 count presentations; the *data digest* is the running SHA-256 over the tensors of every batch fed to the network (`exp26_candidate.Digest`); `tensors_sha256` is the SHA-256 over the names, types, shapes and values of all tensors of a network (`exp20_reference.tensors_sha`). The rules are written S (the sweep) and B (back-propagation).

The verification was read-only. Nothing was trained, the training library was not used, and no file of the worker's branch was executed; its code was read as text. Every figure comes from the script `PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_REVIEW_20261109/e59_verify.py` (Python standard library and numpy), which reads the branches only through `git show` and `git ls-tree -r --name-only`, writes no file, and prints every figure used below, section by section. ## 3 Findings

The findings are ordered by weight. None of them changes a figure or a reading of the report's tables.

**Finding 1 (omission): the section on limits leaves out the number of seeds, the scatter between seeds, and the runs that do not reach the threshold or whose curves have not flattened.**
The report's text: "The brief's list stands: [...] In addition:" followed by two items, "The source of the saving at 256 pairs under back-propagation" and "Fewer than 256 pairs". Both added items are correct. Missing are the following, each of which bears on how the readings may be used.
(a) Three seeds, and the scatter between them. With 256 pairs, the updates to 80 of one start vary between seeds by a factor of 1.93 (fresh, S: 3,456 to 6,656), 2.72 (born, S: 2,624 to 7,136), 2.31 (fresh, B: 2,176 to 5,024), 2.53 (born, B: 608 to 1,536) and 3.39 (lived, B: 992 to 3,360). Under the sweep with 256 pairs, the ratio born over fresh is 1.39, 0.68 and 0.76 on the three seeds, so the word *about the same* there rests on differences that change sign between seeds. Two comparison words rest on two seeds with the third seed showing no difference: "lived against fresh" under B with 256 pairs is *fewer* with ratios 0.53, 0.99 and 0.46, and "born against fresh" under S with 2,048 pairs is *more* with ratios 2.08, 1.43 and 1.54. The brief's rule allows both words; the report does not say how little the third seed adds.
(b) Runs that do not reach 80 within the run. `lived` under the sweep with 256 pairs does not reach 80 on seeds 73129 and 116089 (levels at the end 73.7 and 72.9). The words *more* for "lived against fresh" (S, 256) and for the two rules (lived, 256) use the run's length L = 8,128 in place of the missing value, as the brief prescribes; for seed 73129 the ratio so obtained is 8,128/5,152 = 1.58, just above 1.5. The report gives the "–" in its tables but does not list as a limit that these readings rest on runs that have not reached the threshold.
(c) Curves that have not flattened. With 256 pairs the fresh curve has not flattened in five of six runs (S: 93683 and 116089; B: all three), nor has the born curve in four of six or the lived curve in four of six. The levels at the end with 256 pairs are therefore not final levels, and the condition (iii) of "saves pairs" would fail on those seeds even if (ii) held.
(d) The spacing of the rows against the differences read. Under back-propagation with 2,048 and 512 pairs all values lie between 192 and 320 updates, that is, within 8 rows of 16 updates; the word *about the same* there means a difference below the brief's 48 updates (3 rows) or a ratio within 1.5, not an equality.
The level of evidence (E2, a first measurement that confirms nothing) is stated at the head of the report and need not be repeated.

## 7 What the report may be read as showing, and what not

On the recomputed figures, the report may be read as showing the following, for this one task, this one size of network, three seeds and three numbers of taught pairs, at the level of a first measurement.

*Under the sweep*, a network that has learned before does not need fewer presentations than a fresh one. The born network needs more with 2,048 pairs (320 to 400 updates to 80 against 192 to 224; *more* on two seeds, *about the same* on the third) and about the same with 512 and 256 pairs; the lived network needs more at every number of pairs (448 to 608 against 192 to 224 with 2,048 pairs; 736 to 1,280 against 256 to 400 with 512), and with 256 pairs it does not reach 80 within 8,192 updates on two of three seeds. *Under back-propagation*, a network that has learned before needs about the same number of presentations as a fresh one with 2,048 and 512 pairs (all between 192 and 320 updates), and fewer with 256 pairs: the born network on all three seeds (608 to 1,536 updates against 2,176 to 5,024), the lived network on two (the third showing no difference).

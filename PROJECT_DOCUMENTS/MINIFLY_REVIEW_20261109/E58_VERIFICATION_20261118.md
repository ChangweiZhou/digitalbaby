## 3. Findings

Ordered by weight. Each is marked as an error of fact, an overstatement, an omission or a matter of form.

**Finding 1 (overstatement).** Report, §5.4: "The gated term is therefore slower in presented items at every fraction, as in E48." The evidence the report gives is the first row at which the reduction score reaches 80. At that level the term is later than the free arm in 33 of the 36 comparisons (rule, fraction, seed, `free` or `free-wd0`), but not in three: under the sweep at f = 0.05 on seed 93683 (`term` and `free` both at row 512), at f = 0.05 on seed 116089 (`free` never reaches 80; `term` at 512), and at f = 0.20 on seed 116089 (both at 512). At the level of 90, which the brief requires in the same table, the term reaches it earlier than the free arm, or the free arm never reaches it, in 16 of the 36 comparisons, for example under the sweep at f = 0.10 (`term` at rows 1,024, 1,024 and 4,096; `free` never; `free-wd0` only on seed 93683, at 2,048). The supported sentence is narrower: the gated term reaches 80 later than free learning in most runs, at every fraction under back-propagation, while at 90 it is often earlier. The sentence as written is stronger than the figures.

**Finding 2 (overstatement).** Report, §5.3, comparisons across the rules: "No sentence is stated: under the sweep, the gated term does not reach from fewer pairs the level that conventional free learning by back-propagation reaches from more." When condition (b) fails the brief prescribes only: "the report gives the deficit by seed". The failure of (b) withholds the licence; it is not a demonstration of the contrary, in the same way that "not distinguished" licenses no sentence. The second half of the quoted sentence states the contrary as a result. On (0.05, 0.10) the deficit is −1.8 on seed 73129, that is, on one seed the term under the sweep at 0.05 lies above the higher free arm under back-propagation at 0.10. The sentence should be removed; the deficits (−1.8, +7.9, +19.6; +2.1, +2.5, +9.6; +8.5, +13.3, +22.6) are correct and suffice.

**Finding 3 (omission).** The limits (§7) do not set the scatter of one arm beside the sizes that decide the one licensed sentence of Part B, and say nothing on where the scatter was not measured.

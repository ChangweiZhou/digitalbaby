## Terms used here

The terms of the brief hold. In addition: a *receipt* is the JSON file that a run writes; the *scored panels* are the 31,684 items of the first family without letters and the 1,200 held-out pairs written with 12 letters, which under teacher forcing have at most 22 positions; the *40-letter panel* is the runner's set of 300 held-out pairs of addition modulo 89 written with 40 letters, which have 50 positions under teacher forcing; the *reserve's map* is 0.5 I + 0.25 Wn, with Wn the weights of the candidate rows of the cells in reserve from the cells in reserve (rows 640 to 767, columns 128 to 255 of the recurrent matrix), whose largest eigenvalue in magnitude (the *eigenvalue*) gives the factor by which a small state of the reserve grows from one position of a first-family item to the next; the *lead* of an answer position is the difference between the largest and the second largest output there; *double precision* and *single precision* are numpy's 64-bit and 32-bit floating-point arithmetic, and *the runner's arithmetic* is the training library's 32-bit arithmetic as recorded in the receipts.

## 6. The runner

*The forward pass.* `exp51_reserve.fwd` has one line that reads the item, `used = KEY["in_use"][x[:, 1]]` (its line 81), and it stands under `if KEY["in_use"] is not None:` (6.1). `exp55_reserve.load_start55` calls `R51.install(None)` before anything else is done with the network (line 73), `run` asserts `R51.KEY["in_use"] is None` before the first scored row (line 258), and the only other assignments of the key in the run are `KEY["shut_all"] = True` and `False` around the second scoring of the new task (lines 208 and 210; 6.2). The update calls the same `fwd` (6.5). In the runs of the arm `blind` the forward pass, in the updates and in the scoring, therefore contains no line that reads the item. The run loop asserts that every item of `unrelated` has bytes of 128 and above and every item of `conflict` bytes below 128 (lines 269 and 271; 6.6); these assertions read the item but change nothing that is computed.

*What the program does at the change of task.* (a) It builds E51's embedded network, in which the cells in reserve and the added units are put in (`R51.load_start`, which builds the network again and asserts it against E51's committed start). (b) It computes the bytes in use and the directions in use from 2,080 taught items of the first family; `R51.build_start` computes them with the keyed forward pass installed (`install(in_use)`, then `directions`), and `load_start` replaces them by the directions saved with E51's start. (c) It projects the rows of the reserve and rows 256 to 511 of `lm` and `l2` away from the directions in use and sets the named entries to zero (`make_blind`). (d) It makes a new optimizer with no weight decay.

*What it does after every update.* Before the optimizer's step, `Rule55.before` projects the gradient of the rows kept blind, and E51's `Rule.before` projects the gradient of the old rows and of rows 0 to 255 of `lm` and `l2`. After the step, E51's `Rule.after` projects the step at those rows and restores `l1`, the old columns and the bias of `l3` and the rows of the byte table of the bytes in use; `Rule55.after` then sets the old rows to their start plus the projection of their whole change from the start, projects the whole of every row kept blind and sets the named entries to zero again (6.4).

*The four acts of decision 14.* Each is confirmed in the code: the directions computed at the change of task (b), the rows named (the rule's row sets and the restored tensors), the reserve added at that moment (a), and the change of task known (the start made and the optimizer made new at that moment, d). No act of the program falls outside these four. Two parts of them are not named in the short form of decision 14 and should be: the set of bytes in use, a second record kept by the program and used after every update to restore the rows of the byte table, which the brief counts under "the rows named"; and the use of the keyed forward pass, the line that reads the item's first byte, once at the change of task to compute the directions on the first family's items, which the brief counts under "the directions computed".

*The never-taught items.* For `unrelated` the score is taken on 300 items whose pairs of symbols are all outside the taught pairs; for `conflict` on 800 items of addition and subtraction modulo 97 at 12 letters whose pairs are all outside the taught quarter (4.0). That the script's panels are those of the runner is shown by the reproduction of every count of score 3 (4.5).

*"With the reserve shut".* `KEY["shut_all"] = True` sets the update gate z of cells 128 to 255 to 1 at every position of every item, so that their states stay at zero; it does not set the added readout units to zero (the second added line acts only through `KEY["in_use"]`). The score with the reserve shut is therefore that of the network without its cells in reserve but with its added readout units and the new columns of `l3`.

The text of `run` was read against `R51.run`; the differences seen are of the kinds the brief lists. A line-by-line difference of the two texts was not produced.

## 7. What the four runs may be read as showing, and what not

In the terms of decision 14 the runs agree with the statement it fixes, and the statement is the brief's and no wider. On seed 73129, with the plain forward pass, which contains no line that reads the item, the first family's 32,884 scored answers on items of up to 22 positions were identical to the start's at every one of the twelve scored rows of all four runs, the reduction score did not move, and the task on bytes that the first family does not use (`unrelated`) was learned under both rules, from row 8,192 under the sweep and from row 2,048 under back-propagation, with a never-taught score of 0.0 when the cells in reserve are held shut. The task on the first family's own bytes (`conflict`) was not learned within 16,384 updates (69.4 and 63.2 at the last row), although the cells in reserve, which receive only the operation byte and their own states, raised its score by about 34 points. The weights reproduce the scored answers, the new task's scores and the constraints of the arm exactly.

The runs do not show the following.

- *Keeping on longer items.* On items of 50 positions the first family was lost in `unrelated` under back-propagation (21 of 300 right answers against 274), and the state of the reserve on those items grows in all four runs (findings 1 and 2). The eigenvalue is above 1 at row 16,384 in every run, so that a small state of the reserve is multiplied at every position of a first-family item; the brief's construction leaves the weights among the cells in reserve free and bounds nothing here. "Kept exactly" holds for items of up to 22 positions, on one panel of each length, and is not a property of the first family at any length.
- *Exactness as a property of the network.* What reaches the reserve on a first-family item is a rounding remainder, so that the counts on long items depend on the arithmetic (finding 3), and the outputs move by more than the smallest lead without changing an answer on these panels (finding 5).
- *Generality.* One seed, two tasks and one panel of 40 letters; no third task; no first-family item that contains a byte not in use.
- *Learning on the first family's bytes.* By construction a cell in reserve can read only what the first family does not present; `conflict` was not learned, and the cells in reserve could carry only the operation byte.
- *Requirement A.* Decision 14 states that it is not met by such a result. The four acts of the program remain (section 6), with the set of bytes in use and the one use of the keyed forward pass to compute the directions as parts of them. The task was learned more slowly than in E51's `keyed`: three doublings of the number of updates later under the sweep and two under back-propagation.

## 8. What was not verified, and why

- The runs were not repeated: the training library is not installed and must not be. The runner's arithmetic was therefore not reproduced; numpy's single precision gives the same order of magnitude but not the same digits where rounding is amplified (finding 3).
- Figures of the scored rows that have no saved weights (all but 0, 64, 4,096 and 16,384) were checked against the receipts only, among them the 21 changed answers at 40 letters at row 2,048 of `unrelated` under back-propagation and the first row (2,048) at which the 12-letter state passes 1e-4 there.

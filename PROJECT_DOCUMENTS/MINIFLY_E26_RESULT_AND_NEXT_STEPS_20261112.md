## Where the difference first appears

The sign sweep began life with 98–100% on the hard single-operation panel, close to its matched reference. At the first composition checkpoint, F2.1, it already trailed backprop on pooled untaught plus-minus by **15.7, 32.6 and 46.0 percentage points**. Seed 93683 later reached both marks at F2.2 (86.7% and 58.2%), then dropped to 83.8% and 44.5% at F3.2. This gives separate observations about early acquisition and later preservation. The causes remain to be localized. In all six runs, silencing the recurrent readout at the final checkpoint yielded zero exact answers on the held combinations. This intervention ties performance to the recurrent readout under the tested conditions.

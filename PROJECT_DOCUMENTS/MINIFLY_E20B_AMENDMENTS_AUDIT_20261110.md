## Scientific interpretation

- **G0 versus G3 measures the added connection as a package.** Pair the seeds, initial shared parameters, training batches, optimizer settings and evaluation panels on the shared host. G3 adds a 64-by-512 matrix with 32,768 trainable weights and its associated work. A positive P20.10 supports the effect of adding that connection. A claim about the placement of the feedback specifically would require a control with an equal number of extra weights. The registered four-arm roster is sufficient for the package comparison. [G3 specification](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/1f6f798d107151fcc4dcaf040f429f97b5036fd3/OPUS_TASK_E20_COMPOSITION_FROM_MEMORY_20261110.md#L66-L73) · [Bank dimensions](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/1f6f798d107151fcc4dcaf040f429f97b5036fd3/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp11_cell.py#L45-L51)
- **The windowless reference test measures held-out behavior under the registered decoder.** The decoder retains the prompt and generated prefix and reruns the model for every output byte. A causal recurrent model can pass this assay without a model-side input window. Measuring single-pass inference or its memory cost requires an incremental decoder and agreement with the registered scorer. [Decoder](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/1f6f798d107151fcc4dcaf040f429f97b5036fd3/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp11_cell.py#L188-L214)
- **The panels support a development feasibility result.** The standard composition panels exclude taught operand triples and keep held operator pairs separate. The inherited strict audit covers single operations, and the shortcut helper measures a few final-answer heuristics. Report whole answers with EOS as the primary endpoint and final suffixes as diagnostics. A causal reuse or E3 promotion claim would also require the relevant prior-teaching control and a recitation audit at the semantic level.

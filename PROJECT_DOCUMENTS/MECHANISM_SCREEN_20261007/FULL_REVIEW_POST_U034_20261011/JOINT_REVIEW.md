# Joint mechanism review: what remains live after U034

11 October 2026. This is an interpretive companion to the full M001–M108, C001–C136 and version register being assembled in this folder. It is **not** an additional screen, a new experiment, or a count of scientifically surviving independent mechanisms. The project objective remains a bounded persistent learner that uses earlier byte experience on relevant never-reinforced inputs after delay and interference. The present limited-addition milestone adds a specific low-sample, noisy-feedback E3 assay; its score is not a universal ranking of all MiniFly mechanisms.

The status of a mechanism must therefore record **task/feedback provenance, E0–E4 level, exact version, matched comparator, statistical unit, compute cost, and validity of the causal branch**. Historical association screens, current arithmetic and native byte observation cannot be sorted by a single percentage.

## What is worth doing now, without shrinking the whole project to arithmetic

1. **Current bounded-addition efficiency:** U047 information-complementary replay is one candidate for *recipe design*, not the default winner or a science launch. Its novelty must be the selector and matched substrate, not extra examples or a second covariance contraction. Equal external observations, cache, replay opportunities, selection cost and correction work are required; compare COV-SUPPORT, COV-R, residual-priority and uniform replay on the same implementation. U034's current recipe is closed. U059 is a separate fallback only if fixed-budget allocation is the stated target; do not combine it with U047 in a first attribution test.
2. **Original persistent-core breadth:** retain a parallel, explicitly scoped architecture question: can the native observation-driven path use old byte experience on never-observed combinations after delay and interference? The native E1 baseline and engineered E3 core answer different parts. U044/U062 are possible content/coordinate operations, but neither should be promoted until an actual same-path E3 failure is localized and the proposed operation is distinct from simply attaching the engineered arithmetic head. This is a **research dependency**, not authorization for another large screen.
3. **Retention/revision and circuit structure:** preserve ERROR, STABLE and R3 as references. Activate U021 or structural/teaching ideas only when a new matched assay shows a deficit those operations can specifically repair. The earlier failed/invalid T/S and R/Z versions cannot be silently recycled as untested mechanisms; nor should their exact outcomes ban every topology or eligibility mechanism.

Two fuller-ledger operations deserve separate *conditional design* status alongside U047. First, M034's sufficient-statistic history motivates asking whether later conflicting evidence should **retroactively reweight actually observed constraints**; M106 adds a separate warning that the reference used to score those constraints may drift. Current COV-R already applies an arrival-time Huber-like residual factor and SUPPORT downweights a conflicting same-key arrival; neither revises the weight of an earlier record after later evidence. A new bounded implementation must change the fitted weights and covariance coherently, or rebuild them from a bounded cache, with state and compute charged. It cannot merely reopen covariance as in U034. Second, M077/M105 leave open **data-earned coupling between left- and right-role parameters**. The current full-COV model correlates separate role weights but does not explicitly learn their equality; the earlier positive-pair metric ran on designed transformations, and one protected metric form failed elsewhere. Any coupling must be learned from permitted observations, not fixed arithmetic tying disguised as discovery, synthetic swapped answers, or an assumption that equal outcomes identify equal cues. These are unconfirmed operations, not retained winners; the first needs arrival-only Huber/SUPPORT controls, and the second needs untied, fixed-tied and invalid-coupling controls at matched resource cost.

The practical filter is **several concrete, testable addition-efficiency operations under design, one broad native-reuse integration question, and conditional reserves triggered by measured failure**. This is not an assertion that only these mechanisms survive scientifically. No new run or source modification follows from this review.

# MiniFly V9.7 — bank addressing and persistent byte learning

Fresh paired worlds 801–1056 (n=256), five frozen arms, one V9.2 parent checkpoint. No model or router was selected on these worlds.

## Decision

V9.7 identifies a useful **architecture-level gain**, but not a repair of continued-write interference. `HASH8` finishes at **74.61% old choice** and **82.45% mean new choice**, versus REF's **64.19%** and **70.61%**. The planned Holm-adjusted comparisons confirm both absolute gains. Its own old-memory write penalty is nevertheless **−22.14 percentage points** versus REF's **−10.94**, giving a **−11.20-point DID** (adjusted *p* = 1.07×10⁻⁶). These are different findings, not a contradiction: eight separated stores start from a much stronger old-memory state (**96.74%** before new cohorts versus REF's **75.65%**) and retain a higher absolute score despite losing more of that advantage during continued writing. Keep REF as the causal-repair benchmark; retain HASH8 as an absolute-performance component worth further diagnosis, not as the accepted persistent-learning core.

The concurrent `GEOM4+B` replication is positive for absolute old and mean-new D4 choice but unresolved for DID. Removing B from that four-bank architecture produces no resolved old-D4 or DID difference; its +1.01-point mean-new difference does not survive the planned ten-test correction. No result here supports transferring the PN-calibrated B vector as the main byte-memory solution.

Collision is descriptive opportunity, not a causal loss estimate. `ADDRESS_DIAGNOSTICS.csv` and `RESULTS.json` include phase occupancy and final-write L1 mass, prechallenge and no-write-query collisions, cue migration, and write-to-read route agreement.

The direct capacity-for-router comparison is unusually clean in bytes: `HASH8` uses **3,630,192** deployable bytes, only **32 bytes more** than `GEOM4`'s **3,630,160**. In a predeclared *descriptive*, unadjusted comparison, HASH8 is +5.47 points in old D4 choice and +6.15 points in mean new D4 choice, while its old-choice DID is −8.33 points. Its new-write/old-query collision fraction is 12.8% versus GEOM4's 45.3%. Lower collision opportunity therefore did not by itself yield lower aggregate write damage in this assay; unequal prechallenge competence and analog expression scale prevent a direct per-memory causal interpretation.

The common old-pair identities help expose that limit. Among the **581** pairs REF got right before new learning, HASH8 already got **569** right; **123** of those transitioned to wrong by D4 under writing, compared with **121** transitions for REF. HASH8 also got **174** additional pairs right that REF missed before the new cohorts, of which **51** transitioned to wrong. These are exploratory counts, not another confirmatory family. They show why a larger own-state DID cannot simply be described as greater fragility of the memories both models had learned. The planned DID remains the correct aggregate write-flag contrast, but it does not match the arms on prechallenge competence.

The Python process loads the historical B vector for every arm through the shared module, but the deployable resource ledger charges it only to GEOM4+B. The ledger separately shows process-loaded B and duplicate native alpha scratch. Evaluator write traces are excluded from deployable state and listed separately.

## Checks and limits

Engineering world 401 reproduced archived V9.5 REF and live V9.6 GEOM4+B, plus both one-bank identities. Bank decay, clone, disk save/load, read purity, nested hash routing, and corruption rejection passed before science. Each receipt retained the exact schedule hash and independently recomputed probe scores. See `PREFLIGHT.json`, `SOURCE_LOCK.json`, and `POSTRUN_AUDIT.json`.
Paired intervals describe world-to-world variation for one fixed model seed and this synthetic byte lifetime. A null adjusted test does not prove equivalence. A higher absolute score without positive DID is architecture utility, not demonstrated repair of incremental write harm. This experiment is separate from PN-cue A11.

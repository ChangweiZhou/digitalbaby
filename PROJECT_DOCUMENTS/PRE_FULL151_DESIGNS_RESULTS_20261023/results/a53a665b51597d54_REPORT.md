# V71 / Iteration 16 — Safe receiver witnesses exist, but write-history priority is not a complete repair

**V71 is complete. All 384 planned snapshots and their ordinary controls ran without the missing exact-dose cells seen in V70. Privileged placement found full-mass, safety-constrained solutions in 94 of 128 primary snapshots. The proposed unsigned-history rule improved the worst-memory damage in NEW tasks, including against shuffled-history controls, but did not establish a revision benefit and introduced isolated choice losses at load 48. It failed the combined progression screen.**

The accepted core remains the unchanged **5,177-KC iteration10 reference**, representing iteration5/bi, with the frozen iteration8 reader. No continuously deployed new learner, fresh validation bank, physiology fit or accepted model was produced. This round provides narrower mechanistic evidence, not a general lifelong-learning result.

## What changed after checking the proposals

The supplied V70 grid arithmetic largely reproduced, but its interpretation needed several corrections.

- The high-dose REVERSE loss-rate comparison used **15 completed histories**. SPACE was worse in 7, tied in 8 and better in 0. “None improved” was not “every history worsened.” The selection of that cell after inspecting a grid makes its interval exploratory.
- Losses among PARENT-correct old memories are different from total old accuracy. In that same cell, blind-minus-SPACE **total old joint accuracy** was only +0.000088, with an interval spanning zero, despite the negative additional-loss contrast. Losses and rescues can offset one another. V71 records both.
- The recorded 109 feasibility shortfalls were tiny, but their concentration in small groups does not establish that prior load causes smaller cue support. A 90% budget might avoid those recorded boundaries; it would be a new policy and would not guarantee feasibility after changing all future trajectories.
- DROP versus SPACE did not isolate donor quality. The statement “donors are mostly right” remains a hypothesis.
- A failed restricted oracle cannot prove that no safe allocation exists. We therefore report validated **capacity witnesses**, not a global M_safe estimate.
- H records unsigned past writes. In the existing simulator, identical full parent states have identical future behavior regardless of how cancelled writes produced them. H can supply additional policy information; it does not reveal hidden memories already affecting the parent.

The new primary outcomes protect old choices and the worst-damaged old memory, while retaining acquisition/revision guards. Mean damage cannot compensate for extra choice errors. [Retrospective feedback audit](results/V70_feedback_audit.csv) records the prior-cell values, denominators and ties.

## Executed experiment

We reconstructed each of the 16 V67 histories from zero, checking the stored V69 parent checkpoints at loads 48/96. We used the same reserved NEW cues and assigned REVERSE associations, then followed the original full-SPACE episode. Before both presentations at bouts 2/6/12 we cloned a common pre-write state. This produces **384 assigned snapshots**, with no selection based on subsequent harm.

At a snapshot, all arms share raw writes, fast-state changes, adaptation, conflict donors, donor subtraction proportions, receiver eligibility, route/sign and per-coordinate caps. Only the receiver allocation changes. Fractions **1.0 and0.9** of that snapshot's SPACE budget were both fixed before outcomes;90% is a distinct headroom condition, not a V70 repair. Common prestates remove the need to demand a budget from an already-diverged sham trajectory.

The arms were PARENT; local NO_WRITE for the selected event; proportional receivers (PROP); BALANCED using |slow+u|; opposing-history priority (OPP); four random receiver draws; four shuffled-liability mapping controls; and a privileged minimax receiver solver. Technical draws were averaged within a snapshot and the two presentations within a history before bootstrap intervals over 16 histories.

Every intervention was evaluated at 300s/day in two phases: **ISOLATED**, with no subsequent learning, and **CONTINUED**, after returning to PARENT for the remaining episode through bout 12. The oracle optimized only isolated outcomes. This distinction prevents its endpoint solution from being presented as an optimizer of later nonlinear learning.

There were **42,848 snapshot/arm/endpoint metric rows** and **1,152 oracle problems**. Primary conditions were NEW/bout 6 and REVERSE/bout 12, at both loads, isolated day endpoint. The data remain synthetic tasks on an anatomy-constrained effective model and previously examined histories, not independent flies or fresh confirmation.

## Geometry: many full-mass groups offer no receiver choice

Across 2,073 repeated group-write opportunities, **1,172** used the entire recipient capacity and **538** had only one recipient. The union was **1,217 opportunities (58.7%)** with no receiver-allocation freedom at full dose. Ninety of the 378 snapshots with nonzero transport had no receiver freedom anywhere; another six snapshots had no transport at all.

This is a structural constraint inside the current intervention, not a failure of a scoring formula. If every recipient must receive its maximum, all admissible receiver rules coincide. The90% condition can introduce room, though a single-recipient group still has only one allocation. These counts refer to repeated model task states, not independent anatomical motifs or a population estimate from flies. See [geometry accounting](results/geometry_summary.json).

## Capacity witnesses: useful positive evidence, limited negative evidence

The capacity LP maximizes one common fraction of all original group budgets. It preserves old PARENT-correct choices, limits each old-margin deterioration, protects target margins/choices and keeps worst old damage no worse than PARENT at both isolated delays. It retains PARENT's clipping regions and uses evaluator-only old-cue information. Every reported solution was checked in the original nonlinear model.

| Primary condition | Assigned snapshots | Full-mass safe witness | Positive partial witness | Only zero fraction | No transport needed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| NEW, load 48 | 32 | **26** | 3 | 3 | 0 |
| NEW, load 96 | 32 | **21** | 7 | 3 | 1 |
| REVERSE, load 48 | 32 | **23** | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| REVERSE, load 96 | 32 | **24** | 7 | 1 | 0 |

That is **94 full-mass witnesses,22 positive partial witnesses,10 zero-fraction results and2 no-transport states**, out of 128 assigned primary snapshots. Two snapshots per history are correlated; these are feasibility counts, not128 independent statistical samples. Fractions classified as full differ from1 by at most floating-point rounding (the minimum across all such cases was 0.9999999999999998).

The full-mass solutions show that a universal “there is nowhere safe to write” account is inadequate for these isolated states. Conversely, zero or partial results are **not proof of a biological capacity shortage**: the LP searches one clipping region, one donor definition, one eligibility set and one common scaling of group budgets under specific safety guards. A validated positive fraction is a lower bound on achievable safe transport under the assay. Zero is not evidence of positive capacity, and other clipping regions, group-budget tradeoffs or temporal policies remain outside the search.

Across all snapshots, the capacity LP returned 378 nontrivial-input validated results and 6 no-transport states, including 37 zero-fraction optima. The fixed-budget minimax LP produced 716 validated allocations, 12 no-transport cases and 40 restricted infeasibilities across both fractions. We did not average only successful oracle solutions into an all-assigned learner score.

## The added-state rule: a small acquisition signal, not a sufficient repair

The reconstructed trace follows the parent's update order:

\[
H_{t+\Delta t}=(H_t+|u_t|)e^{-\Delta t/\tau_s},\qquad
L_i(\sigma)=(H_i-\sigma s_i)/2.
\]

H tracks the actually applied slow increment and uses the existing slow decay constant. The trace and signed state decompose accumulated positive and negative **write history**, not distinct memories. For same-sign addition, opposing history L itself stays constant before decay. OPP's capped water fill on L+a is therefore a priority heuristic, not the physical update of opposing occupancy. Four within-route/sign permutations of L test whether its coordinate mapping contributes anything.

At the prespecified 90% budget, the following are reductions in mean **worst old positive damage** relative to PROP after one selected write and24h. Positive values favor OPP; all intervals resample the 16 histories after averaging presentations.

| Primary condition | PROP − OPP worst damage, Hz | Shuffled-L − OPP worst damage, Hz | Old-choice outcome relative to PROP |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| NEW, load 48 | **0.00402 [0.00198,0.00621]** | **0.00521 [0.00309,0.00737]** | One additional old-pair error at one presentation |
| NEW, load 96 | **0.00424 [0.00053,0.00844]** | **0.00578 [0.00168,0.01053]** | No change |
| REVERSE, load 48 | 0.00296 [−0.00046,0.00648] | 0.00308 [−0.00027,0.00654] | One fewer correct old pair at one presentation |
| REVERSE, load 96 | 0.00315 [−0.00155,0.00778] | 0.00461 [−0.00064,0.00987] | One additional correct old pair at one presentation |

The NEW results support a modest use of the history-to-coordinate mapping in this model. They do not establish hidden cancelled memories. Mean target actual margins improved by roughly 0.003–0.006Hz across these conditions, with unchanged mean target joint-choice rates. The small old-choice differences are not claims of significant broad deterioration, but the exact development screen forbade an increase in mean old-choice losses or a decrease in total old accuracy.

The NEW worst-damage benefit persisted after subsequent PARENT learning: **0.00428Hz [0.00247,0.00635]** at load 48 and **0.00585Hz [0.00180,0.01017]** at load 96. Revision benefits remained uncertain. This shows transfer of some single-write effects through subsequent learning; it does not test repeatedly deploying OPP at every write.

Importantly, OPP was **not demonstrably superior to zero-extra-state BALANCED on worst damage** in any of the four primary comparisons: every paired OPP-versus-BALANCED interval included zero. Thus the extra state has not earned its cost merely because it beats a shuffled mapping. The trace requires **41,416 additional mutable bytes**, raising the reference's 165,688 bytes to 207,104, a **25% increase**. No extra neurons are implied.

## Acceptance, integrity and disposition

OPP passed 77 of 84 locked checks but failed the combined screen. The failures were the load 48 NEW old-choice/loss guards, the load 48 REVERSE total-choice guard, and the positive worst-effect/history-mapping interval requirements for both REVERSE loads. The load 96 NEW cell passed all its checks. These are constituent development criteria, not independent statistical replications or a 77/84 success probability.

**No future dynamic test was licensed by this iteration, and no learning model was promoted.** BALANCED was a comparator, not an alternative candidate chosen after OPP failed. Full-budget results and other snapshots/delays remain descriptive and were not used to rescue the primary screen. The governing decision is in [progression gates](results/progression_gates.csv) and [decision.json](results/decision.json).

All **319 startup checks and217 final audit checks passed**. PARENT/SPACE replay, history reconstruction, branch isolation, exact transport, fixed-reader offsets, all-assigned denominators and source/parent hashes were checked. The maximum validated oracle prediction discrepancy was **1.07e−14Hz**; its maximum constraint residual was 1.56e−10 and budget error 1.78e−15. Ordinary receiver controls had no missing cells.

A pre-freeze synthetic derivative test initially changed slow state without updating its corresponding H, and correctly failed the H≥|s| invariant. The test fixture was corrected before freezing or generating campaign outcomes. No scientific source, input, gate or configuration changed after the final freeze. Unlike V70, this campaign required no post-launch numerical amendment or rerun. An explicit source allowlist separates frozen science from later audit/reporting scripts.

Summed worker time was **693.3s**, using two workers; analysis took 25.0s. Peak reported per-worker RSS was **572MB**. These are process measurements, not combined Mac memory or total elapsed assistant time. No large synapse mother table was loaded and no data file was downloaded.

The cited bouton study supports heterogeneous calcium plasticity and compartment-specific release depression, not H or a safe-capacity allocator. It concerns local bouton physiology; a single H scalar per KC in this effective model is a different level of description. [Davidson, Kaushik & Hige, eNeuro (2023)](https://www.eneuro.org/content/10/10/ENEURO.0275-23.2023).

The next justified question is the gap between evaluator-known safe placements and inexpensive state-local information, with explicit protection of individual choices and revision. These results do not require a new memory substrate, sleep/reset mechanism or additional KCs. They also do not establish that H is the missing ingredient. Any later repeatedly deployed rule still needs fresh prospective learning, reversal and physiology checks. Those experiments were not started here.

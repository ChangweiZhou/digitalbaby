# Second hostile audit of W3 byte-arithmetic warmup

**Verdict: NO-GO for the eight-hour evolution/confirmation run as currently worded; GO for a small Gate-0 engineering implementation.** W3 fixes the original V2 API, feedback-band, recitation, and probe-state problems. This re-audit found two remaining flaws that can change what a *passing* result means. It did not modify W3 or run a learner. [Reproducible calculations](AUDIT_METRICS.json) are design checks, not outcome data.

## Blocking before full confirmation

**P0 — The final gate can pass without one fly handling all four operations.** [W3 Gate 3](../BYTE_ARITH_WARMUP_REVISED_20260926/DESIGN.md) requires a median 64/128 total, a *separate* median 8/32 for each operator, and six of eight lives with 64/128 total. Those are marginal conditions. The [explicit eight-life score matrix](AUDIT_METRICS.json) gives every life 32/32 on two operators and 0/32 on the other two. All eight lives reach 64/128; every operator's across-life median is 16/32; **zero lives** meet 8/32 on all four. The error-tail and causal-gain terms do not logically repair this: nonexact responses can be near misses, and controls can remain well below 51/128. Gate 1 and Gate 2 have the same marginal-versus-joint defect. A survival test for an *individual* arithmetic learner needs a joint per-life operator floor, then a prespecified count of lives meeting that joint floor. Otherwise the conclusion must be limited to a population mixture of partial specialists.

**P0 for the stated teacher-path attribution — the answer stream may be decorative.** [W3's teaching contract](../BYTE_ARITH_WARMUP_REVISED_20260926/DESIGN.md) says `#answer;` enters the same incremental sensory path, but the cross-entropy update is allowed to take the raw answer bytes directly as target token IDs. A compliant implementation can discard the post-choice sensory activations, use those target IDs in ordinary supervised backpropagation, and still pass the first-choice, wrong-answer, no-write, and random-key controls. Those controls show that *labels* mattered, not that the fly sensory path read the teaching event. Before calling this same-path byte teaching, freeze the actual tensor dependency and run a teacher-sensory lesion: hold target token IDs, question input, optimizer step, and timing fixed while replacing or disconnecting the `#answer;` sensory activations. If nothing changes, report an explicitly supervised byte-target learner, not a result about sensory interpretation of observed answers. This does not invalidate answer-revealing warmup as an engineering scaffold.

## Material design risks

**P1 — The anti-mode rule makes division confirmation a partly different distribution.** Among 148 hard exact-division pair groups, 104 have quotient 2–9 and only 44 have quotient at least 10. A 32-item final with at most two copies of each answer can include at most 16 of the eight low quotients; it must therefore contain **at least 50% high quotients**, versus **29.7%** in hard support. Quotients 22–31 each have only one hard pair, so selecting one for final leaves no different hard pair with that quotient for warmup. The illustrative checker seed selected 18/32 high-quotient items and seven singleton-quotient items. This may be a worthwhile extrapolation stress test, but a failure would confound learning-rule failure with output-range extrapolation. Freeze separate in-support and rare/new-quotient strata, report both, and decide explicitly which is primary. Do not tune the final roster after seeing a candidate.

**P1 — The expensive search lacks an arithmetic efficacy preflight.** The earlier [warmup preflight](../BYTE_ARITH_WARMUP_PREFLIGHT_20260926/REPORT.md) trained a raw-byte GRU with 512 answers and obtained only 10.9% and 5.5% exact on two held-out one-operator splits, while W3 asks for 50% on a hard final after at most 384 answers. These are different fixtures and models, so they are not a bound. They are enough to make a five-hour evolutionary sweep a poor first arithmetic check. W3's 8–32-individual microbenchmark is defined for timing and memory. After locking its gene config, add a tiny development-only causal arithmetic pilot and report train exact, held-out exact by operator, and true-answer minus permuted/no-write gain. If it shows no learning signal, repair the representation or teaching rule before spending the search budget.

**P1 — The acceptance arithmetic and uncertainty rule are not fully locked.** “Strongest shortcut” does not specify whether the comparator is selected by exact count per life, by pooled exact count, or by a combined exact/q90 score. The final gate uses medians across eight lives on the *same* 128 items but requires no minimum count of positive paired causal gains or lower uncertainty bound. Freeze comparator selection and the life/item uncertainty calculation before qualification. A positive conditional score on this one roster should not be described as a population estimate over independent arithmetic facts.

The grade-only tier remains separate, which is a genuine repair. Its +7/128 median gain can arise from one operator while the others remain flat; if grade-only learning across all four is intended, add a joint per-life coverage guard there too. Specify the exact grade-shuffle permutation and seeds before using that arm as a causal control.

## What remains valid and what to do next

W3 correctly scopes a success to **bounded E3 transfer on previously unreinforced operator–pair groups**, with no delayed/intervening-learning evidence and no persistent-core promotion. Exact-key recitation is blocked within each operator; the widened fixture is combinatorially feasible; V2's grade boundaries are explicit. A raw-byte-only winner would be an engineered supervised learner, and a KC+raw winner needs its route ablation before any Full151 contribution is claimed.

Repair the two P0 findings in a new design version, decide the division primary/stress strata, then implement only the byte interface and Gate 0. Keep the existing W3 file and all V2 results frozen. No broad population run should start from this audit alone.

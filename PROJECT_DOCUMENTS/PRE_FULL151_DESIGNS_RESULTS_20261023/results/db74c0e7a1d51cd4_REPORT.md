# V70 / Iteration 15 — A spatial signal survives exact transport matching at load 96

**V70 is complete. At load 96, SPACE reduced mean collateral damage relative to eight exactly yoked location controls by 0.01690 Hz [95% paired interval 0.00913, 0.02571]. At load 48, one of sixteen histories could not complete all eight controls under the declared numerical feasibility criterion. The two-load gate therefore remains unresolved, and Block B was not run.** This is evidence for a particular spatial allocation policy at one load, not a negative result for spatial learning and not a finished persistent-learning core.

The accepted reference is unchanged: iteration5/bi through the **5,177-KC iteration10 model**, with the frozen iteration8 reader and **165,688 learned mutable bytes**. No selector, physiology, reader or new learner was fitted. The conditional receiver allocator was implemented and its allocation mathematics tested, but it was not subjected to the conditional campaign or promoted.

## What was refined before testing

The supplied proposal correctly identified two separate V69 issues. Its mean benefit had an unresolved transport-amount control, and its average improvement could coexist with worse individual memories. Checking the actual V69 table reproduced all the cited tail observations: SPACE improved mean damage in 16/16 load 48 and 15/16 load 96 histories; worst damage worsened in 6/16 and 9/16. At load 96 the mean worst-damage change was +0.03086 Hz, and three histories lost an additional old joint choice.

V70 retained the proposal's bounded sequence: exact-dose dynamic comparison first, one receiver allocator only if both loads qualify. The refinements were:

- Count **net** mass removed and added on disjoint coordinates. A coordinate cannot donate and receive in the same event to inflate the transfer count.
- Give each blind arm SPACE's external budget at every event, fixed anatomical route group and write sign. Its location allocator sees its original write and that budget, never slow state, old cues or outcomes.
- Keep fractional proportional removal/addition, as in SPACE. Randomize the feasible donor partition; do not quietly change to a more concentrated receiver rule. This is a specified null, not uniform sampling of every feasible spatial mask.
- Terminate a sham at a failed exact allocation. Do not score a reduced-dose continuation or average the remaining shams as if the cell were complete.
- If receiver balancing qualifies, count the current base write in occupancy, using **|slow+u|**, before allocating extra mass. The proposal's |slow| omits that impending write. Full dynamic balancing would also be yoked to SPACE, with a separate common-prestate assay to isolate receivers.
- Treat the new 0.01-Hz threshold as a declared mechanism-screen criterion. It does not overturn or reclassify V69's failed architectural threshold.

The real-data constraints used here are the existing model's KC projection, side and exact normalized alpha teaching-route rows. This iteration did not extract another connectome motif or add a biological cell type. The interventions operate on an anatomy-constrained effective model; their accessibility to an actual fly circuit remains unproven.

## Executed assay and primary results

All sixteen V67 development histories were reconstructed from the saved V69 checkpoints at loads 48/96. NEW used the same reserved V69 cues; REVERSE used the same prespecified focal association. Each block ran 12 bouts, with independent neutral probes after bouts1/6/12 at +300s and +24h. Arms were PARENT, exposure-matched NO_WRITE, unchanged SPACE and eight YBLIND draws. There were **64 assigned cases**, **293,572 scored pair-outcome rows** and **138,179 event/group audit rows** in the final corrected campaign. Terminated branches account for the difference from a fully populated matrix.

Each old damage is max(NO_WRITE actual margin − arm actual margin, 0). All old associations are included regardless of previous success; only the deliberately revised focal is excluded from reversal collateral damage. Eight sham results are averaged within a history before paired bootstrap over sixteen histories, using 5,000 fixed-seed draws. Histories are development units, not independent flies. New target joint choices require both actual and reader criteria.

The primary endpoint is NEW, six bouts, +24h:

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 48 | **15/16** | 0.11626 Hz | 0.14243 Hz | **Not estimated for all assigned histories** | **Unresolved** |
| 96 | **16/16** | 0.14487 Hz | 0.16149 Hz | 0.16177 Hz | **0.01690 Hz [0.00913, 0.02571]** |

At load 96 SPACE beat the within-history blind mean in **15/16 histories**. Its location effect exceeded the locked 0.01-Hz floor and had a positive interval. Mean target actual/reader change from PARENT was +0.06928 Hz [−0.00111, 0.15408]; target joint choices were unchanged at 11/16. At load 48 the unchanged SPACE target difference was −0.09121 Hz [−0.15832, −0.03160], within the declared −0.25-Hz guard, with unchanged choices at 14/16. These point-estimate guards are not a powered noninferiority demonstration.

The largest realized event/group net-dose error in the primary load 96 controls was **1.78e−15**. Maximum cumulative mass mismatch was **2.84e−14**, or **2.30e−16 relative**. Thus the V69 approximately 10% transfer-volume mismatch is removed for this complete primary comparison. Endogenous base slow-write dose still follows each arm's feedback trajectory, rather than being clamped; its mean relative difference here was about 7.09e−12, maximum 3.35e−11. The result concerns the dynamic consequence of the chosen locations under fixed transport budgets, not identical pre-write states throughout learning.

## What prevented the load 48 comparison

The unresolved primary history was **6701008**. At the first presentation of bout 6, six of eight sham branches could not meet the required route 1, negative-write transfer. The group had five active coordinates. SPACE requested **1.57774581093504** units; those branches' maximum disjoint transferable masses ranged approximately **1.57774581081578–1.57774581082967**. Their deficits were **1.05e−10 to 1.19e−10**, just above the allowed 1.016e−10 tolerance, only about **6.7e−11 to 7.6e−11 of the requested amount**.

An independent replay converted the original floating-point weights to exact binary rational integers, enumerated subset capacities, and certified all **109** failed groups in the final campaign against the declared aggregate donor/receiver criterion. All had at most 17 coordinates. It replayed the existing failure states and generated no alternative learner outcomes. The certificates and excluded raw-weight hashes are retained.

**This is a numerically brittle feasibility boundary, not evidence of a biologically meaningful capacity shortage.** SPACE requests the maximum available conflict/nonconflict transfer on its own state. Even extremely small feedback-induced changes in a sham can move a capacity boundary past that request. Fractional allocation inside a partition does not guarantee that a disjoint donor/receiver partition with the required capacities exists. The exact-subset certificates settle that finite allocation question under the frozen criterion; they do not establish that a slightly different budget or model would fail.

We did not loosen the tolerance, reduce the budget, fill in missing outcomes or qualify the load using fifteen survivors. Therefore the full load 48 primary effect is not reported. It would be wrong to turn this unresolved gate into a claim that SPACE's earlier benefit was mainly dose confounding.

## The victim tail remains a separate problem

At load 96, SPACE's mean worst-old-memory damage was **1.26586 Hz**, versus 1.23501 for PARENT. The paired difference was +0.03086 Hz [−0.06397, 0.12780]. Relative to exact blind controls, SPACE also had no demonstrated worst-tail benefit: blind minus SPACE was −0.02755 Hz [−0.12628, 0.06936]. These intervals include zero; do not claim a significant increase in the population mean worst tail. The three additional old-choice-loss histories remain concrete developmental failures.

The positive average location effect therefore does not show that the current receiver rule protects vulnerable memories. Block B's slow-occupancy water fill and common-prestate receiver assay remain untested experimentally because the required two-load A gate did not pass. No state-local liability claim follows from the implemented formula alone.

## Implementation amendments and verification

This was an **amended development experiment**, not an untouched preregistration. Two numerical implementation defects were found and corrected; every affected campaign was retained and the entire bank rerun:

1. The initial generic MILP could return an approximately feasible subset that failed the much stricter original-unit checks. A ten-coordinate example had a feasible subset by direct enumeration. We added finite meet-in-the-middle search for groups up to 36 coordinates and exhaustive comparison tests. The first lock, source versions and outcomes remain under `amendments/001` and `data/implementation_audit_1`.
2. The necessary total-capacity precheck allowed one tolerance although donor and receiver inequalities each allowed one. We corrected that factor of two and tested both sides of the feasibility boundary against all subsets. All actual dose, cap and sign tolerances remained unchanged. The second record is under `amendments/002` and `data/implementation_audit_2`. The complete load 96 estimate from that run had already been seen; the correction was justified by independent arithmetic, not by selecting a favorable effect.

No arm, input, requested budget, scientific endpoint or progression threshold changed during either correction. A separate launch was rejected by the source guard before any outcomes when a verification-only file was briefly edited; its exact frozen content was restored and the supplementary verifier placed in a separate file. That rejected launch is logged.

The final run passed **382 startup checks and 306 final audit checks**. Final verification covers hashes, original-run preservation, all assigned denominators, every realized event/group budget, unchanged PARENT/NO_WRITE/SPACE outcomes, adaptation equality, neutral probes and independent failure certificates. Audit success does not mean the scientific progression gate passed.

Final campaign worker time summed to **65.45s**, analysis 4.48s, and independent feasibility replay 2.59s. Peak final-run per-worker RSS was **447MB** with two workers. The two retained implementation runs used another approximately 130s of summed worker time; their highest per-worker RSS was 557MB. These are process measurements, not total elapsed assistant time or aggregate Mac memory. No large synapse mother table was loaded and no data was downloaded.

## Consequence for the next decision

The strongest new observation is that **location matters at load 96 even when transport volume is matched to floating-point precision**. V69's general spatial lead should not be retired on this evidence. Its architectural acceptance result remains unchanged, and the current action is not safe enough to promote.

A subsequent design should first remove the maximal-budget boundary problem: a common-prestate location assay can hold raw writes and budgets identical, while a dynamic assay would need a prespecified budget feasible for all paired branches, with explicit headroom. That latter test changes the dose schedule and requires a new frozen experiment; it is not a retrospective repair of the present missing cell. A shared evaluator budget would remain a mechanistic control, not a deployable runtime rule. Only after that should the single receiver-liability hypothesis be evaluated. No such extra assay or V71 run was started here.

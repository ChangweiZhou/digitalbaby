# Full151 byte bridge, Step 14: target-channel-only teaching

## Result

**The policy rescue was not demonstrated.** On 32 fresh paired confirmation worlds, the target-only policy answered **359/512 (70.1%)** directly taught canonical cues, versus **458/512 (89.5%)** under the prior opponent-writing policy and **128/512 (25.0%)** with plastic writing disabled. Target-only missed both predeclared uniform-recall conditions: at least 85% overall and at least 24/32 correct for **every** cue. Seven of the 16 cues fell below the per-cue threshold.

The prespecified focal cue `1+1=` did improve: **16/32** correct under target-only versus **7/32** under opponent writing. Its paired gain was **+28.1 percentage points**, 95% Student-*t* interval **[+5.3, +51.0]**. This local effect does not overturn the failed overall gate. Across all 16 cues, target-only was **19.3 points worse** than opponent writing, paired 95% interval **[−25.4, −13.3]**. It remained better than no writing by **45.1 points**, interval **[+39.7, +50.5]**.

| Canonical cue | Opponent writing | Target-only |
|---|---:|---:|
| `0+1=` | 26/32 | 15/32 |
| `1+1=` (prespecified focal cue) | 7/32 | 16/32 |
| `1+2=` | 28/32 | 5/32 |
| `1+3=` | 27/32 | 12/32 |
| `2+1=` | 31/32 | 16/32 |
| `3+1=` | 20/32 | 7/32 |

The full per-cue counts, per-label counts and world-paired intervals are in [the machine-readable summary](results/SUMMARY.json). Opponent writing also failed its uniform-recall gate, mainly on `1+1=` and `3+1=`. Its 89.5% overall score on fresh worlds is close to Step 13's 90.0%, so the difficult-cue pattern was not removed by a new sample.

## What was tested

The primary endpoint is **E1 taught-association recall**, not arithmetic, generalization, or a persistent learning core. Each world taught 16 byte strings of the form `a+b=` directly, 12 exposures per string. There were four possible answer bytes (`0`–`3`) and 192 teaching records per world. Nine labels were fixed and seven were shuffled into balanced classes per world; the full mapping cannot be explained by addition. The model chose its output before the next answer byte arrived and selected its own output byte from four native Full151 stores.

Input code, checkpoint, learner stores, cue bytes, answer bytes, record order, clock, teacher calls and output rule were held fixed between the paired policies. Every store received the answer byte and a native teaching call. Only the plastic `write` flags changed: opponent writing updated all four stores on every record (**768 plastic writes per life**), target-only updated just the arriving byte's store (**192**), and no-write updated none. This compares **whole teaching policies**. It cannot isolate the effect of removing nontarget writes from the fourfold reduction in total plastic dose.

All canonical scored cues were reinforced. An exact-key table could solve the E1 task, so the result establishes no compositional rule. The never-taught spacing variant was a secondary **E2 presentation check**: target-only scored **346/512 (67.6%)**, opponent writing **439/512 (85.7%)**, no-write **128/512 (25.0%)**. A fixed whitespace canonicalizer plus exact-key memory could solve that variant, so it does not establish E3 transfer. There was no E3 probe in Step 14.

## Execution and checks

The [frozen design](DESIGN.md) and source lock preceded all science. Technical world 14000 passed the independent technical audit, including six deliberate corruption tests. The disjoint eight-world pilot passed its coarse feasibility gate (target-only 70.3%; opponent 89.1%), which authorized the predeclared 32 fresh confirmation worlds 14101–14132. Pilot observations were not pooled into the confirmation interval. Confirmation finished in **521 seconds** of learner wall time and committed about **1.38 MB** of compact world receipts. No prior trajectory was rerun, and no replacement world was used.

The [independent confirmation audit](results/AUDIT.json) passed all 32 receipts. It regenerated the expected byte streams and checked labels, output-before-teacher order, argmax outputs, sensory equality, write flags and counts, clock equality, source lock and reductions. The audit verdict is `TARGET_POLICY_RESCUE_NOT_DEMONSTRATED`.

## Interpretation

Removing nontarget plastic writes helped one crowded address, `1+1=`, but made several other cues much worse. Thus Step 13's error cannot be repaired by switching wholesale to target-only writing, and it would be premature to ascribe that error solely to opponent-channel suppression. Under this 16-cue load, the prior opponent policy remains better in aggregate, yet neither policy achieves reliable recall on every taught cue. This experiment closes one bounded teaching-policy comparison; it does not justify another unbounded mechanism search or a claim that the byte encoder–memory bridge works at scale.

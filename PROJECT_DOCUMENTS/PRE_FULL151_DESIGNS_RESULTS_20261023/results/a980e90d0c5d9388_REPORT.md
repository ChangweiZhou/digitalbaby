# V68 / Iteration 13 — Decodable information, unsuccessful control

**V68 found a substantial information result and rejected its single learning-rule pilot.** Population statistics of the existing active KC state distinguish reversal from unrelated learning much better than individual local observations. This survives whole-stream holdout, a memory-removal intervention, stable-association controls and comparison with averaged local scores. However, the proposed two-score gate made fresh-stream retention and revision worse. The preferred learner remains **iteration5/bi**, instantiated by the unchanged iteration10 reference and iteration8 reader. V68 does not deliver a better persistent learning core.

The merged experiment is complete. All **120 implementation/provenance audit checks** passed. The learning candidate passed only **2 of 11 acceptance gates** and is rejected. These two counts measure different things.

The supplied ownership-information proposal contributed the information scope ladder and same-cue memory-removal/current-consequence controls. Its absolute provenance and hash are in the manifest. Our [prior V68 review](../v68_review/REPORT.md) contributed the separate question of action feasibility. We added averaging of frozen V67 coordinate-local probabilities as a fair control: a population score must beat aggregation, not just one noisy coordinate. The main [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), exact [feature schema](feature_schema.json), configuration and scientific source hashes were frozen before V68 replay outcomes. The [conditional pilot protocol](PILOT_PROTOCOL.md) was frozen only after S1 passed the information gates, but before threshold calibration and fresh validation generation, as specified in the main protocol.

This iteration used the **existing real-connectome-constrained interface and simulated trajectories**, not new recordings of a fly's learning. The parent contains 5,177 KCs, 165,688 mutable numeric bytes and four pooled MBON/output channels. The previous anatomy audit found 1,226 KCs with both selected alpha teaching and readout support; pair representations were distinct. Those facts motivated the bounded questions. We did not change the encoder, KC count, feedback parameters, physiology fit or anatomical routing. No specimen identities were joined. No additional download was needed.

## Information: the useful signal appears above one coordinate

We replayed 16 existing V67 streams, 64 focal associations and four conditions: true reversal, one anomalous A/B bout, stable association, and a predetermined unrelated association. All 64 focals met the pre-existing eligibility criterion. Added features comprise 6,144 presentation rows plus 6,144 matched omit-focal-history rows. V67's 196,608 coordinate observations and their original classifier predictions were reproduced, not refit.

Every new classifier held out an entire stream, standardized on training streams only, and used one fixed L2 logistic specification. Training used bouts 2–12. No cue IDs, stream IDs, bout number, evaluator outputs, future consequences or old-cue vectors entered features. All **192 new diagnostic fits converged**. Reported AUROC is the mean within-stream AUROC, with descriptive 95% intervals from a 16-stream bootstrap; intervals are not multiplicity-adjusted. These are development streams already examined in V67, not fresh confirmation.

The S0 aggregation result changes how V67 should be interpreted: weak coordinate-level decoding was not evidence that the model lacked all usable information. S1's additional improvement over averaged S0 is about **0.120 AUROC**, with a positive paired interval. S3 did not beat averaged S0 on the primary unrelated comparison. S1 and S4 passed every scope gate; the prespecified preference selects S1. This preference does not establish the anatomically smallest possible circuit.

![Held-out information scopes](figures/information_scopes.png)

The controls constrain the interpretation:

- **Identical prefix:** reversal and anomaly have exactly chance AUROC in the identical first bout, in all scopes. We did not manufacture advance knowledge of which anomaly would persist.
- **Same cue, altered stored history:** omitting that focal's earlier writes lowered S1's first-bout reversal probability by **0.148 [0.091, 0.205]**, standardized paired effect **1.26**. Exposure/adaptation was preserved. Other associations were replayed naturally, so this is an intervention on stored history and its downstream feedback effects, not selective molecular erasure.
- **Stable familiarity:** the reversal-versus-unrelated S1 classifier labelled only **0.21%** of stable post-prefix observations as reversal at threshold 0.5, while reversal sensitivity was **87.36%**.
- **Consequence timing:** pre-outcome features were invariant to a same-state consequence swap; teacher changes matched the existing consequence coefficient. Raw write signs were not required to flip.

S1 pools intracellular trace magnitudes, alignment and current writes across active KCs. This access exists in the simulator; an anatomical mechanism that computes those statistics has not been established. Accurate decoding of episode category is also not identification of the correct action at each plastic synapse. The saved logistic models are offline diagnostics, never the runtime learner.

## Action: a narrow oracle witness, with limited benefit

On each development stream at loads 12 and 48, we compared a new normal learning episode with an exposure-matched no-write twin. The optimizer could only retain or attenuate the resulting existing slow-write direction, with **exactly 80% of its endpoint L1 mass** retained. Both fast traces were fixed to the intact endpoint. It could use old-cue identities and margins, making it explicitly privileged. It could neither invent a new write direction nor amplify coordinates.

The LP preserved the uniform-control alpha clipping regions, required the new memory to stay within 0.25 Hz of intact learning (and retain intact joint correctness), and limited each old-memory loss relative to the uniform control. The objective minimized mean old positive margin damage at +24h. This is an endpoint L1 comparison, not V67's L2 norm sham or a dynamically realized raw teaching-dose match.

| Load before new association | Feasible cases | Old damage change vs uniform, feasible paired cases | New margin change vs uniform |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 12 | 10/16 | −0.0215 Hz [−0.0311, −0.0101] | +0.4486 Hz [0.3227, 0.5607] |
| 48 | 7/16 | −0.0053 Hz [−0.0190, 0.0086] | +0.3268 Hz [0.1428, 0.5043] |

There was no old-choice improvement: the feasible paired difference was zero at load 12 and −0.30 percentage points at load 48. Feasible-case effects must not be compared directly with all-16-stream baseline means. All denominators and infeasible statuses are retained. Exact nonlinear replay of every feasible mask agreed with the LP prediction to **8.9e-15 Hz**; maximum constraint residual was **3.3e-13**.

This establishes limited room for selective endpoint allocation in some cases. Fifteen infeasible cases restrict only the declared budget, direction and clipping region. They do not prove that a different dynamic learning process is impossible. Conversely, feasible masks are not implementable ownership detectors.

## Prospective pilot: the intuitive two-score gate fails

S1's information gates licensed one bounded test. The rule used only two scalar population summaries, computed from the current pre-write state: activity-weighted slow-state magnitude F, and fast activity aligned with the current write relative to slow conflicting activity E. A conflicting slow write was retained only when both exceeded fixed thresholds. Both fast increments and nonconflicting slow writes were unchanged from the same pre-state. It adds **zero mutable state** and 16 fixed numeric bytes.

The calibration procedure, twelve threshold pairs and fallback selection were frozen before calibration. On the first eight development streams, **none** retained the specified 80% of late reversal conflict mass; all had the wrong selectivity direction. The predetermined fallback selected F >= **1.9271521**, E >= **0.25**. It retained **3.27%** of reversal-conflicting mass versus **26.83%** of unrelated-conflicting mass in the shadow calculation. The fresh run was therefore a bounded falsification test of the simple family, not a promising candidate promoted despite a warning. No replacement family was introduced.

On the other eight development streams, ordinary from-zero training calibrated a scalar slow-dose control to **0.8966145**. Thresholds, scalar, sources and acceptance criteria were then locked before generating **16 fresh streams**, **1,536 associations**, and **3,072 unique cues**, globally disjoint from all 24,312 prior cues. Validation used unchanged parent, analytic gate and scalar, all trained from zero. No thresholds or metric choices changed after validation.

At day/load 96 the gate changed joint success by **−2.08 percentage points [−3.06, −1.24]** versus parent and **−1.17 [−2.08, −0.26]** versus scalar. For all-assigned day/dose 12 reversal, the change versus parent was **−26.56 points [−39.06, −14.06]**. These are paired stream intervals.

The common parent-eligible revision subset comprised **55/64** episodes. Its stream-averaged day/dose 12 success was parent **80.21%**, gate **53.13%**, scalar **78.13%**; the gate difference was −27.08 points [−39.58, −15.09]. Because eligible counts differ by stream, these means are not pooled success counts divided by 55. Arm-specific eligibility was also reported: parent 55, gate 50, scalar 53. All-assigned results prevent changed acquisition from hiding revision failures.

The immediate acquisition noninferiority checks passed. Day acquisition failed: mean actual margin changed by −0.400 Hz [−0.445, −0.359], and joint success by −6.25 points [−7.29, −5.27]. The gate also produced one anomalous false flip in the common eligible set where parent and scalar produced none; the stream-averaged increase was 1.56 points [0, 4.69]. Low-load and revision gates failed. **Only 2/11 acceptance gates passed.**

Unchanged kernel decay constants and same-state fast increments are consistency checks, not physiological validation of this new rule. No measured physiology was refit and no biological certification is claimed.

## What this changes for the next round

The supported distinction is **information scope versus action selection**. A single local observation was an overly restrictive diagnostic in V67. The present MBON-only bottleneck is weak under the tested feature family, while active-population state contains substantial decodable information. Yet “large stored trace plus aligned fast trace” is a poor stand-in for ownership; it selects writes in the wrong direction and fails a new-stream learning test. We should preserve the positive decoding result without treating it as a mechanism.

The next bounded question should ask which compact, runtime-accessible population quantities predict the **benefit or harm of a particular write**, rather than merely its episode category. Candidate quantities should first be tested on fixed snapshots with expression-weighted effects and same-budget controls, including across increasing load. If a small statistic cannot select beneficial actions there, do not deploy another protection gate. If it can, its anatomical convergence and prospective dynamics still require separate tests. V68 does not justify more channels, more KCs, new physiology formulas or further compression by itself.

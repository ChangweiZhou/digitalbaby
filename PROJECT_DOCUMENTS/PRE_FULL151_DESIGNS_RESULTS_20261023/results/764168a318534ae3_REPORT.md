# MiniFly V73 / Iteration 18 — corrective feedback and partner-resolved memory

14 September 2026. Completed experiment; no learner promoted. The full 5,177-KC iteration5/bi reference, instantiated by iteration10/reference, and the frozen iteration8 observer remain unchanged.

**本轮结论：加强输出到教学回路的反馈，并把慢记忆拆到真实 KC–MBON 伙伴对，并没有改善持久修订。** 16 条全新学习历史、9 个组全部完成。每组的 64 个修订案例在即时和 5 分钟测试中全部成功；次日参考组保留 45/64，单独增强反馈和组合组均为 43/64，单独细分状态为 44/64。再学习 12 个新关联后，各组次日主要修订终点均为 40/64。64 个案例来自 16 条历史，不能当成 64 个独立重复。三个候选均未通过预设的修订改善门槛。

**这个负结果有明确边界。** 增强后的回路能感受到慢状态，也产生方向正确的纠偏，但实际适应后的状态上，每次 30 秒呈现只纠正约 0.055% 的局部慢状态扰动。预先固定的增益按未适应、快慢合计电流校准，未保证慢记忆获得足够大的纠偏。因此，本轮排除的是这一强度及路由实现；不能宣布“反馈纠偏无效”或“突触局部状态无用”。这里实现的是当前输出的比例反馈，也不是已经恢复了完整的奖励预测误差回路。

**真实数据带来的进展是把假说变得可检验。** 同一 FlyWire630 标本中，4 个 MBON14、2 个 PPL106 教学细胞及 3,419 个 KC–MBON 伙伴对支持了一个明确实现；真实伙伴映射没有显示出优于半球内打乱映射的修订收益。伙伴对也不等于单个突触，计数构造的教学路由尚未得到局部多巴胺暴露数据验证。全部 63 项启动检查和 192 项最终核查通过，没有结果出来后的科学参数修改。保留原参考模型，交付小于 10 MB 的结果补充包。

## What changed from the submitted proposal

V72 made receiver allocation an unpromising immediate focus and located much of its reversal-omission benefit in carried prehistory. V73 therefore tests whether the learning loop can react to that history, and whether pooling distinct output partners obscures useful plasticity. It does not run another SPACE receiver search.

The proposal's central anatomical premise was checked against local data before freezing the experiment. The fitted maximum alpha feedback coefficient is 1.3717e−9, compared with 0.3267 for gamma: the existing alpha feedback is effectively silent. Four individual MBON14 cells, two on each side, and two PPL106 DANs are present in the same FlyWire630 graph. Their nonzero KC–MBON counts yield 3,419 partner pairs supported by 1,770 of the retained 5,177 KCs. No BANC/hemibrain identities or edges are merged into this construction.

Three refinements keep the question bounded. First, the main feedback source is current total MBON output, which the effective circuit actually represents; direct access to the slow component is a separate privileged diagnostic. Second, only one feedback gain is calibrated without learning-task labels; no post-result gain search follows. Third, resolution changes individual output clipping and teaching access as well as slow-state storage. Its factorial effect cannot be interpreted as the effect of adding memory slots alone.

The reinforcement-prediction-error literature motivates output-to-teaching feedback, but does not license an arbitrary error formula or unmeasured synaptic access. V73 retains the existing outcome inputs and adds output-proportional feedback; it does not identify a target-valued durable residual or a biological subtractor. The primary paper's equations and limitations and the authors' repository README were inspected; no third-party implementation was imported. [Bennett, Philippides & Nowotny (2021)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-22592-4), [authors' repository](https://github.com/BrainsOnBoard/paper_RPEs_in_drosophila_mb).

## Implemented arms and anatomical constraints

| Arm | Slow representation | Additional alpha feedback | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| REF | One value per KC | None; original fitted route | Accepted parent comparator |
| FB | One value per KC | Current total output | Candidate |
| RES | KC–individual-MBON partner pairs | None; original fitted strength | Candidate |
| COUPLED | Partner pairs | Current total individual outputs | Primary candidate |
| SLOW_P / SLOW_R | Pooled / partner pairs | Slow-only expression | Privileged diagnostics |
| MAP_LEFT / MAP_RIGHT / MAP_BOTH | Partner pairs | COUPLED with within-side identity swaps | Anatomical mapping controls |

All nine arms retain the original PN encoding, 5,177 KCs, two per-KC fast arrays, adaptation and frozen observer. Four individual alpha MBON outputs are instantaneous algebraic units with inherited output bounds; no new persistent firing dynamics are introduced. The resolved route still has only **two shared alpha teaching channels**, not four independent teachers.

The measured MBON→DAN count rows are `(8,20), (18,13), (11,21), (19,8)`. DAN→MBON rows are `(50,48,47,51)` and `(86,37,95,51)`. Feedback uses normalized counts. With KC–MBON count share p_ij, inherited DAN→KC route T_id and DAN→MBON counts D_dj, pair teaching is E_ijd = T_id D_dj / sum_j(p_ij D_dj). Its weighted average recovers T exactly. This **count-product hypothesis is not coordinate-validated dopamine exposure at a particular plastic synapse**.

Individual MBON current weights are normalized so that weighted, unclipped current from tied pair states recovers the pooled reference. Individual clipping then occurs before behavioral pooling. A separate REPOOL recovery control permits pair updates but immediately collapses them back to the original pooled expression. It recovers the reference to numerical precision and is not counted as a discovery or a tenth campaign arm. Three nonidentity swaps exhaust the two-by-two within-hemisphere MBON mappings; they preserve hemisphere/type and count multisets, not every triple's degree. [Anatomy inventory](results/anatomy_inventory.json), [static model](models/README.md), [implementation](src/model73.py).

The shared additional feedback coefficient is **lambda = 0.012074865167937132**. It is 0.1 divided by the maximum one-event Jacobian norm, 8.28166597383911, over 16 existing unlabeled calibration patterns and the pooled/resolved operators. Calibration uses a 30-second event, unadapted KC activity, total fast-plus-slow alpha current and no clipping. Since the inherited alpha write coefficient is negative, the positive output→DAN addition supplies a local restoring contribution. This calibration is neither a global stability proof nor a guarantee of substantial correction in the adapted slow state. The gain, event partition, routing and all science code were locked before fresh outcomes.

## Fresh experiment and decision rule

Sixteen seeds, 7302001–7302016, generate 120 balanced associations each: **3,840 globally unique PN cues**. Exact cue matches to the explicitly listed prior banks and the interface calibration/evaluation patterns are excluded. Overlap in KC representations is retained. Each arm trains its own first 96 associations from zero, six bouts per association; it does not inherit a reference trajectory.

At loads 48 and 96, two predetermined focal ages are challenged without selecting for original correctness. REV gives 12 opposite-role bouts; STABLE gives 12 original-role bouts; ANOMALY gives one opposite and eleven original bouts. A held-out NEW association receives six bouts. Each challenge is probed immediately, at 300 seconds and at one day, then its own learner continues through 12 further new associations and is probed again (MIXED). Delay probes are independent clones: the measured day delay is not inserted into the continuing learning branch. All assignments complete.

The primary endpoint is joint actual-and-observer reversal correctness at one day, after CHALLENGE and MIXED. There are 64 reversal assignments per arm (16 histories × 2 loads × 2 ages). **History is the experimental unit.** Ages and then loads are averaged within history for pooled contrasts; 5,000 paired history bootstrap resamples give 95% intervals. Apart from the prespecified primary contrast, intervals are exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons.

Candidates must improve pooled reversal by at least 10 percentage points with a positive lower confidence bound at both day endpoints, while passing capacity, NEW, STABLE, ANOMALY, old-choice, worst-margin, reader-bias and mutable-state guards. Old-memory aggregate guards weight the four challenge classes equally after focal-age averaging. They permit up to 2 percentage points of loss on the specified choice contrasts and up to 0.05 Hz of increased mean worst-old damage. Case-specific tables remain available because such aggregates can hide a narrower harm. Passing this engineering screen would still not validate the resolved physiology or automatically promote a learner.

## Capacity and old-memory harm remain visible

At load 96, day capacity is 72.27% for REF, 71.61% for FB, 72.27% for RES and 71.68% for COUPLED. Finer slow-state representation provides no capacity gain in this panel. The capacity and aggregate safety guards passing does not make the candidate an improvement.

There is a narrower exploratory harm: after reversal plus mixed learning at load 96, absolute old-memory day accuracy changes by **−0.757 percentage points [−1.382, −0.132] for FB** and **−0.724 points [−1.316, −0.132] for COUPLED**. These effects are inside the two-point aggregate allowance, but their direction is unfavorable and must accompany the primary result. Metrics of losing previously correct choices use each arm's own prechallenge cohort; because trajectories and eligibility can differ, an improvement on that metric alongside lower absolute accuracy is not evidence of protecting the same memories better.

The privileged slow-only source does not rescue reversal and worsens the mean worst-old margin damage after REV/MIXED/day: SLOW_P−REF is **+0.0835 Hz [0.0388, 0.1417]** and SLOW_R−REF is **+0.0866 Hz [0.0428, 0.1432]**, averaging loads within history. Direct slow access is therefore insufficient in this implementation, and these arms are not viable candidates. Their weak effective gain also prevents the stronger conclusion that fast/slow observability can never matter. ## What the loop diagnostics actually establish

All 128 reused V72 snapshots are tested with six arms. Fast state, adaptation, cue and outcome are held fixed while active slow state is perturbed symmetrically to produce a ±0.1 Hz change in the unclipped pooled alpha projection. The change in the next raw slow update, divided by the imposed perturbation, measures the local restoring loop; negative values mean correction. These are development diagnostics, distinct from the fresh history experiment.

| Arm | Mean restoring gain per event |
|---|---:|
| REF | −9.48e−11 |
| RES | −9.48e−11 |
| FB | −5.516e−4 |
| COUPLED | −5.519e−4 |
| SLOW_P | −5.709e−4 |
| SLOW_R | −5.708e−4 |

The added feedback reactivates a previously negligible pathway and has the intended local sign. But **5.52e−4 is only about 0.055% correction of the imposed slow-state perturbation per 30-second event**; it is not the fraction of all memory removed. The actual current output responds by about 0.195 Hz to a 0.2 Hz imposed differential. Complete output silence occurs in only 2/128 pooled and 1/128 resolved snapshots. Pervasive clipping is therefore not the main reason the loop is weak in this diagnostic panel.

This exposes a limitation of our own calibration: a 0.1 total-current Jacobian norm on unadapted cues translates into much less correction of durable state after adaptation and the inherited fast/slow allocation. The experiment cleanly measures a conservative implementation, but is a weak test of the broader proposition that sufficiently effective corrective feedback could improve revision. No stronger gain was tested, and the results cannot be extrapolated to one.

Amount controls use the same prestate and disable the added feedback, then scale the control's entire alpha write (fast plus represented slow) to the candidate's alpha L1 amount; gamma is unchanged. **All 1,024 endpoint comparisons match**, with maximum absolute mass error 4.55e−13 and no zero-denominator exclusions. For REV/day, FB and COUPLED target-margin differences from these controls are on the order of 1e−5 Hz, with intervals spanning zero at both loads. This gives no material direction-specific revision benefit beyond amount on these snapshots. It does not establish that all differences on autonomous, diverging histories are caused solely by dose. [Loop summaries](results/loop_summary.csv), [amount summaries](results/amount_summary.csv), [diagnostic accounting](results/diagnostic_accounting.json).

## Physiology compatibility is separate and limited

The same additive feedback coefficient is applied without refitting to the existing two-address causal physiology kernel. It is scored against all 60 conditioning points using both the fitting workbook and the previously reconciled published means/SEMs. Twelve materially changed published targets are retained in the source ledger, together with uncertainties. These targets are reused constraints, not independent validation. ## What should follow, and what has not been established

V73 narrows one uncertainty: merely reactivating the current alpha feedback at this conservative scale, even with count-constrained partner resolution, does not deliver durable revision. The fine mapping does not add a detectable functional benefit under the tested controls. V73 does not resolve whether an effective error signal can be constructed biologically, whether two alpha teaching channels are adequate, or whether a different gain can correct carried history without damaging old choices.

Before another broad learning campaign, the most informative bounded task would be to establish whether the existing observed signals can produce **material slow-state correction in adapted states while retaining old-choice and physiology constraints**. The current gain-to-effective-correction mismatch should be measured and addressed prospectively, without interpreting a larger gain as a repair to this completed run. An output-feedback model should also demonstrate what desired value it is correcting toward before being called a recovered prediction-error learner. Partner-resolved physiology needs measured sensory and local teaching access; static counts cannot supply that evidence.

No contraction, overwrite, sleep, gamma4 ensemble shift, active-zone mechanism, new time constant or rewiring arm was instantiated here. Their absence is a scope boundary, not a negative finding about those mechanisms. The accepted parent remains the reference because none of the tested candidates met the engineering improvement criterion.

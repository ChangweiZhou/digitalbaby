**MiniFly iteration 6 — retention can be strengthened, but the biological tradeoff remains**

13 September 2026. **Decision: keep iteration 5 as the preferred parent.** One change to the existing trace allocation fixes the original engineering retention gate and improves reserved input streams. It substantially worsens the original physiological predictions and fails revision in two reserved streams. The persistent learning core is still unvalidated. No additional allocation, lifetime or mechanism was searched.

The useful advance is a measured separation of **decay, later writes and expression**. The slow state is already persistent. The engineering failure can be relieved without adding memory, but doing so is not enough to identify the fly's learning mechanism. The attachment's progress scores and “61” label are not adopted; this is iteration 6 in the local project. See the [key figure](figures/retention_decision.png), [declared protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [run instructions](README.md) and [acceptance checks](results/acceptance_checks.json).

**What was held fixed.** The learner still uses the same FlyWire v630 interface: 302 selected PNs, 88 input channels, 5,177 KCs and 27,572 PN→KC edges, with the original KC→MBON weights and restricted direct teaching routes. Sparsification, readout gains, event timing and gamma circuitry are unchanged. The input patterns are engineered; there is no measured odor→PN mapping. No BANC/hemibrain identities are joined, and the BANC spatial result remains unresolved. The [frozen-input record](sources/frozen_inputs.json) and [implementation verification](results/implementation_verification.json) confirm that all 68 recorded inputs/protocol files stayed unchanged.

**The matched experiment corrects an ambiguity in the proposal.** I forked the same saved four-pair acquisition checkpoint into five arms, each with 192 events lasting 15,840 seconds: rest; sensory exposure without teaching or writes; computed teaching without writes; gamma writes only; full learning. Existing traces decay in every arm. Rest has no sensory presentations; the other arms have 96. This model has no separate persistent teacher-controller variable. Sensory and teacher-without-write arms therefore end in exactly identical dynamic states, despite different computed teaching payloads. Gamma-only writes test the actual existing feedback state. [Stream accounting](results/matched_stream_accounting.csv), [diagnostic decision](results/diagnostic_decision.json).

The unchanged parent's original results are recovered within 2.49 × 10⁻¹⁴ Hz. The table uses signed A-minus-B memory contrast: a more negative value expresses the learned association more strongly. These contrasts subtract a plastic-reset clone and are not autonomous behavioral choices.

All four fail half-retention even under rest. Relative to matched rest, full learning adds 2.145, 1.604, 0.356 and 0.635 Hz of loss. Yet sensory exposure alone **improves** pair 0 by 1.322 Hz. Its adaptation changes which part of the stored state is expressed through the clipped readout. For that pair, actual alpha writes then cost 3.468 Hz relative to the sensory/gamma-only arm. Thus “full minus rest” is not a pure measure of damaging writes. The signed, order-dependent differences telescope exactly; they are not uniquely additive physical causes. [Full decomposition](results/retention_decomposition.csv), [signed losses](results/signed_loss_attribution.csv).

Gamma-only writes alter the final alpha readout by less than 4.3 × 10⁻⁹ Hz in this panel. This does **not** show that gamma feedback is irrelevant to learning. The inherited effective direct gamma-MBON→alpha-MBON readout gain is about 2 × 10⁻⁹, whereas gamma-MBON→DAN feedback gains reach about 0.327. Gamma's relevant route acts through subsequent alpha writes, which the gamma-only arm blocks. Anatomical synapse proportions and fitted physiological gains remain distinct. [Effective-gain audit](results/allocation_mechanism_audit.json).

**The slow trace is not rapidly decaying.** Over matched rest, alpha-fast state retains **1.065%**, alpha-slow retains **99.842%**, and gamma retains **0.0214%** of its acquired vector. Each component preserves its own direction to numerical precision. Their sum changes direction under rest because its two components have different decay rates. Rotation of the summed state alone would therefore misdiagnose overwriting. The population vector also contains multiple learned histories; it cannot be treated as a uniquely identified single memory. [State geometry](results/state_geometry.csv).

With adaptation fixed to its acquisition values, the four direct alpha readout projections retain 55.5%, 36.4%, 22.9% and 14.9% under rest. The difference across pairs partly reflects their different ages when the shared checkpoint is taken. The slow component already supplies about 55% of pair 0's acquired clipped memory contrast, versus about 14% for the most recently acquired pair 3. It is engaged, rather than absent from the interface. Shapley averaging over gamma/fast/slow readout ablations recovers each nonlinear memory contrast within 1.8 × 10⁻¹⁵ Hz. [Trace contributions](results/trace_readout_contributions.csv).

Expression also matters. Pair 0's matched-rest pre-clipping contrast is −5.963 Hz, while its actual contrast is −2.849 Hz. Holding adaptation at acquisition gives an actual contrast of −5.428 Hz. This is a combination of stored-state change, adaptation and clipping, not proof that a physical memory disappeared. Conversely, later alpha writes do alter old readout projections: summing their individual decayed contributions reconstructs the endpoint state difference within 3.1 × 10⁻¹⁴ Hz. That is an exact accounting identity within the model, not independent validation of synaptic biology. [Readout details](results/readout_expression.csv), [write accounting](results/local_write_accounting.csv).

**Only one rescue was attempted.** The diagnosis met the predeclared trigger. I changed the fraction of each existing alpha update assigned to the slow component from **0.117981 to 0.5**. Total update before decay, lifetimes, state dimensions and circuitry stay fixed. This is equal allocation of incoming updates, not delayed transfer from fast to slow storage, replay or a new consolidation detector. It is not a biological parameter estimate.

The change multiplies slow allocation by **4.238** and fast allocation by **0.567**. In the original sequential stream, the candidate's component states closely follow those simple rescalings: maximum deviations are below 6 × 10⁻⁸ effective state units. Gamma state is identical. The near-linearity follows from the inherited alpha→DAN feedback gains being near zero. The improvement comes from expressing more of the existing persistent component; it does not come from better local write specificity or a longer lifetime. [Mechanism audit](results/allocation_mechanism_audit.json), [candidate component readouts](results/equal_allocation_legacy_components.csv).

The reserved audit uses 12 fixed, previously unused banks, totaling 288 new PN patterns. These are independent generated streams through one specimen's anatomy, not 12 flies. No parameter was changed after seeing them. The paired improvement in half-retention is 60.4 percentage points; a bootstrap resampling whole banks gives a descriptive 95% interval of 43.8–75.0 points. It does not measure biological uncertainty. Initial memory is stronger too, so this is not a ratio improvement obtained by weakening acquisition. The candidate still fails half-retention for six reserved old pairs. Revision fails in banks 601002 and 601004, ending at −1.621 and −14.257 Hz respectively. Both passed with the parent. Although 10/12 meets the predeclared reserved threshold, this is a visible retention/revision tradeoff, not complete preservation of all behavior. Plasticity-disabled and teaching-blocked controls show zero acquired memory for both allocations. [Failure cases](results/candidate_failure_cases.csv), [legacy acquisition and controls](results/legacy_acquisition_metrics.csv).

**Simple overlap is not a reliable loss predictor here.** Across 48 reserved parent memories, weighted KC overlap exposure has a within-bank-centered correlation of **−0.107** with signed extra loss; the whole-bank bootstrap interval is **−0.406 to +0.176**. Summed Jaccard gives +0.093, interval −0.283 to +0.508. Candidate results are likewise inconclusive. The apparent ordering of the original four weighted exposures does not generalize into a reliable signed-loss rule. Overlap is where writes can interact, but unsigned overlap does not determine whether subsequent writes oppose or reinforce a previous association. No degree-preserving graph null or species-level motif claim is made. [Overlap audit](results/overlap_audit_summary.csv).

**The biological guardrail rejects promotion.** The primary [Huang et al. Nature study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07819-w) and its [released model](https://github.com/schnitzer-lab/Luo_Huang_2024_MB_model) distinguish feedback-gated encoding during additional training from offline consolidation. Their measurements show substantial early decay followed by a persistent tail. Constant equal allocation cannot be justified merely because it protects engineered memories. The local supplementary evidence and source provenance are recorded in [evidence notes](sources/evidence_notes.md).

I applied the same fraction change to the original two-effective-address physiology model, holding every other coefficient and source protocol fixed. This reuses the original data; it does not assign real odors to engineered PN patterns or create a biological RMSE for the hybrid.

**What this changes for the project.** Fixed anatomy and fixed state can support considerably stronger engineered retention. The present failure is not a need for more memory slots, nor simply a slow component that decays too quickly. But increased retention by constant reallocation conflicts with the physiological time/spacing dependence and can impede revision. Keep the successful engineering variant as a diagnostic comparator, not the next biological parent. Further compression would preserve an unresolved dynamical compromise.

The next useful evidence would constrain the temporal learning law and its expression: longitudinal measurements beyond 24 hours, matched interfering/reversing training with readouts, and measured odor→PN activity. Existing source-model calibration and its continuous-time/bout mismatch also remain appropriate audit targets. No additional help is needed to reproduce this iteration; those measurements would help decide the next scientific step more than another generic connectome download.

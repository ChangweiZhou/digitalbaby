**MiniFly iteration 3: compressing a learning circuit and testing its limits**  
13 September 2026. Builds on the frozen sensorimotor and Huang-model releases.

**The new papers support a sharper approach: preserve input-specific learning state and causal teaching pathways, then reduce the representation.** This iteration produces a runnable compact higher-order learning reference and a useful failure suite. The published 2,000-Kenyon-cell model can be reduced exactly to six input-equivalence groups for its specified odor repertoire. However, the supplied learning rule fails important specificity and repeated-training tests. A local eligibility counterfactual improves specificity but does not solve extinction. We have progressed beyond a literature suggestion to reproducible code, without yet obtaining an anatomically integrated or general-purpose learning core.

Three distinct studies were found among five new PDFs; two files are companion figure collections. The main papers are Rachad et al., *Functional dissection of a neuronal brain circuit mediating higher-order associative learning*, Cell Reports 44, 115593 (2025), deposited as `mmc5.pdf`; Weber et al., *Four individually identified paired dopamine neurons signal taste punishment in larval Drosophila*, eLife version of record June 2025; and Sun et al., *A neurotrophin functioning with a Toll regulates structural plasticity in a dopaminergic circuit*, eLife December 2024. File identities are in [the source manifest](sources/local_papers.json), and claim-level evidence and caveats are in [the evidence ledger](sources/evidence_ledger.json). The eLife filenames ending in `v1` do not identify their publication status: the deposited text and publisher pages identify versions of record. [Weber record](https://elifesciences.org/articles/91387), [Sun record](https://elifesciences.org/articles/102222).

**The most consequential correction concerns “teaching address.”** Rachad et al. report that 96.7% of KC-to-MBON synapses in alpha-prime2 are within 2 micrometres of PPL105 terminals, compared with 2.25% for PPL103. Yet PPL103 responds to shocks and is required in their first training stage, whereas the nearby PPL105 does not show a shock response in that protocol. The paper discusses limited direct contacts and alternative routes; it does not demonstrate that dopamine actually diffuses the 24.7 micrometres needed to reach 90% of these sites from PPL103.

Consequently, our earlier nearest-DAN analysis is an analysis of spatial organization, not a validated assignment of task-specific teaching responsibility. A useful address must combine local access with the DAN's activity, intervention evidence and plasticity state. A static nearest-terminal mask can preserve geometry while assigning the wrong teacher. The new paper does not invalidate the observed geometry, establish BANC replication, or justify merging specimens. See Rachad, printed p. 6, Figs. 3/S7, and [the deposited paper's DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2025.115593).

**Higher-order learning supplies a concrete computational test.** In Rachad's paradigm, CS1 is paired with punishment; a new odor CS2 is subsequently paired with CS1 without punishment. CS1 has acquired the ability to act as a reinforcer. The relevant effective circuit contains gamma1 MBON inhibition and alpha-prime2 MBON excitation onto a gamma1 DAN, plus an additional inhibitory MBON02 pathway. The alpha-prime2 module is distinct from the alpha2 module in the preceding Huang analysis: MBON13 versus MBON18, and PPL103-mediated punishment versus the earlier PPL105 role. These models should not be joined by a superficially similar compartment name.

I downloaded and pinned the original Python source at commit `3cb2686e0f57f19cb79e5e166d5303794cd3023c`. The harness executes the authors' actual functions, omitting only top-level demonstrations and plotting. It runs all eighteen phase-by-cell conditions in each of their two circuit versions. An independent implementation reproduces those 36 conditions to within 3.41e-13 in the compared rates, readouts and weights. This is code agreement, not new animal validation or exact numerical reproduction of every figure. [Source provenance](sources/model_provenance.json), [author repository](https://github.com/nawrotlab/higher_order_learning_circuit), [all conditions](results/original_interventions.csv), [checks](results/verification.json).

**A principled form of stratified reduction works exactly here.** The published inputs split 2,000 identical KCs into six equivalence groups: CS1 cells included in the mixture, CS1 cells excluded from it, the corresponding two CS2 groups, control-odor cells, and silent cells. Their sizes are 100, 100, 100, 100, 200 and 1,400. Within a group, every cell has the same allowed input vector, initial weights and update rule. One representative therefore suffices, provided its multiplicity is retained in every summed current and the learning update remains a per-cell update.

Inductively, identical states receive identical updates and stay identical; their aggregate contribution is the representative's contribution multiplied by group size. The reduction preserves two plastic weight vectors and the global CT accumulator: 4,001 numerical state values become thirteen, excluding fixed inputs, multiplicities and parameters. Five named MBON/DAN rate outputs are still evaluated. This is not a six-neuron biological brain, and the reduction is not justified by neuron count or average degree alone.

All 36 intervention conditions pass the original-code comparison. Another 1,000 trial updates across twenty randomized schedules, including checkpoint save/reload, preserve individual-cell and grouped responses to within 3.41e-12. Those schedules use the same permitted input repertoire. New odor patterns, heterogeneous weights or distinct update laws can require new groups. No anatomical equivalence between real KCs has been established. [Implementation](src/reduced_learning.py), [per-condition errors](results/equation_compression_checks.csv).

There are 100 uniform samples per budget. At six samples, 34/100 readouts are undefined because the tested and control outputs both vanish; these failures are retained separately, not discarded as successes. Uniform samples use inverse-inclusion scaling, which preserves expected aggregate input before nonlinear dynamics but cannot reconstruct input identity or plastic state. The four-group control averages mixture input and loses the distinction between cells activated and not activated by the mixture. These are compression controls, not graph degree-preserving nulls. [Every draw](results/compression_controls.csv), [compression figure](figures/compression_scope.png).

A separate synthetic scope check illustrates the limitation: with 48 independently drawn 10%-sparse odor patterns, the median number of equivalence classes is 1,941.5 out of 2,000 cells over twenty seeds. This is not a claim about fly odor statistics. It shows why a six-group result for four fixed patterns cannot establish a universal brain compression ratio. [Repertoire sensitivity](results/repertoire_scope.csv).

**The published source fails stronger learning tests.** Its hypothetical cotransmitter state is a global accumulator driven by punishment-related DAN activity. Subsequent active KC inputs receive facilitation even if those inputs were never active during punishment. That differs from the Methods' statement that only previously depressed synapses undergo facilitation: the supplied code contains no corresponding input-specific eligibility condition.

| Test after three punishment-training trials | Supplied model bias | Local eligibility counterfactual |
|---|---:|---:|
| Three trained-cue/novel-cue mixture trials | -0.14693 | -0.07553 |
| Three novel-cue-alone trials | -0.22398 | -0.000000176 |
| Gamma1 MBON blocked during mixture trials | -0.06758 | -0.0000964 |
| Six mixture trials | -0.33333 | -0.33333 |
| Nine mixture trials | -0.33333 | -0.33333 |

Bias is the model's normalized gamma1-MBON readout; more negative means a stronger aversion proxy. It is not percent behavior correct. “Novel alone” is an explicit diagnostic in which CS1 is absent from stage two, not an exact replay of the paper's temporally unpaired control. The diagnostic shows that source-model transfer does not require the learned cue. Its CT state also has no decay clock, so inserting real elapsed time is not represented by this code.

There is a further source-versus-publication discrepancy: the additional inhibitory pathway reduces the gamma1-blocked bias from -0.23610 to -0.06758 but does not abolish it, unlike the near-zero result described for the revised circuit in Fig. 6. These are native source-function outputs with the repository's stated parameters; I have not altered them to match the figure. This discrepancy is separate from the earlier Huang source-output mismatch. [Stress tests](results/source_stress_tests.csv), [intervention outputs](results/original_interventions.csv).

The paper's behavioral learning strength rises and then declines with excessive second-order training. The supplied model instead saturates at -1/3 for the mixture. Its gamma1 weights only decrease, and the control odor's weights stay fixed. Consequently, with a fixed test input, changing CT alone cannot make that readout recover. An extinction/recovery process or a justified change in readout dynamics is needed. This is a structural limitation of the equations, not just a poorly chosen learning rate.

**One explicit hypothesis improves selectivity, with limits.** After observing these source failures, I tested a local eligibility bit set when a KC is active alongside the punishment-responsive PPL103-like channel. Only eligible inputs can subsequently receive the hypothetical facilitation term. This uses locally available activity and teaching signals; it does not inspect odor names, task labels or desired answers. Gamma1 learning remains driven by the modeled DAN.

This modification nearly eliminates the novel-alone effect and the residual gamma1-blocked transfer, while retaining some conditioned-cue transfer. It changes the original numerical fit and still fails the six/nine-trial decline. The eligibility bit has no calibrated lifetime, and no specific molecule was identified by these tests. It is a post-hoc engineering hypothesis, not a biological discovery or a held-out validation success. The modified grouped and individual-cell models agree across 36 additional cases. [Candidate results](results/eligibility_stress_tests.csv), [all interventions](results/eligibility_interventions.csv), [consistency checks](results/eligibility_verification.json), [learning figure](figures/learning_stress_tests.png).

**The anatomical audit adds constraints that the idealized model hides.** I inspected the same FlyWire v630 graph used by MiniFly, using historical type annotations. All five higher-order model types are retained in both the 8,783-neuron guard and the 9,619-neuron MB scaffold; smaller guards omit PPL103. That does not make the 8,783-neuron model a learner: the prior long-memory roles and KC populations remain separate requirements. [Type retention](results/type_retention.csv), [exact FlyWire identities](results/interface_neurons.csv).

The gamma1 MBON-to-MBON02 inhibitory connection exists across all four source/target side-label combinations. MBON02-to-MBON13 has 22 synapses from a left-labelled source to a right-labelled target and one in the reverse pairing; neither same-side pairing has a direct edge in this export. Ignoring opposite hemisphere labels would incorrectly erase this path. The observed sign follows the signed reference model and transmitter assumptions. These are connections within one specimen, not cross-specimen joins.

The direct MBON13-to-PPL101 edge is weak in this export: one synapse on the right and none in the other side pairings. Two-edge routes exist with mixed signs, including paths through DPM and SMP115-labelled cells. A list of paths does not establish their net physiological effect or an enrichment over a matched null. The paper measures functional excitation and allows mono- or polysynaptic transmission; copying its single effective positive gain into this graph is therefore not justified. [All side combinations](results/feedback_paths.csv), [two-edge routes](results/MBON13_twohop_DAN.csv).

The published model also supplies both plastic outputs with the same 2,000-cell input vector. In our FlyWire export, 4,092 annotated KCs connect to the selected gamma1 MBONs, 886 to alpha-prime2 MBONs, and only 71 to both; 4,907 connect to either. These counts use all retained positive KC edges without a new synapse threshold. Functional odor channels may be aligned across distinct KC populations, but the populations are not identical neuron sets. An anatomical implementation needs that mapping explicitly. [KC routing](results/KC_output_routing.csv), [summary](results/KC_routing_summary.csv).

**The other two papers change the contract without supplying another invented rule.** Weber's larval study separates three things often conflated in models: response to a natural stimulus, sufficiency to teach under artificial activation, and necessity during normal learning. DAN-f1 can teach when artificially activated despite lacking the measured high-salt calcium response; acute DAN-g1 inhibition reduces salt learning while individual f1 inhibition does not. Fig. 6 uses fifteen observations per group. Developmental ablation and acute inhibition can yield different effects. Physiology/connectomics use first-instar larvae, whereas behavior uses third-instar larvae; neither can be joined to adult neuron identities. I also flagged a c1 sufficiency inconsistency between Fig. 5/caption and the Discussion instead of encoding it as an unambiguous benchmark.

Sun's study supports activity- and neurotrophin-dependent structural plasticity, but it is region-, perturbation- and time-dependent. After 24-hour DNT2-neuron activation, PAM synaptic-site counts increase in SMP, without the same count increase in MB lobes. DNT2 overexpression has a different regional pattern and enhances measured 24-hour appetitive memory. These findings motivate a future structural state and perturbation benchmark. They do not identify a rapid KC learning update, a universal synapse-growth law, or the mechanism underlying Huang's imposed three-hour decay switch. The evidence ledger retains these distinctions.

**What is ready, and what remains.** [The runner](run.py) now executes a very small higher-order learning reference and saves a resumable discrete-trial state. [The protocol](PROTOCOL.md) distinguishes the initially specified tests from the subsequently introduced eligibility hypothesis. [The updated contract](learning_contract.json) requires cue specificity, phase-specific interventions, extinction, new-input generalization, bilateral pathways and explicit mapping to distinct KC populations before an anatomical learning claim.

The next implementation target is not an additional unconstrained mechanism. It is to resolve selective facilitation and gamma1 recovery against independent conditions, then map those states onto same-specimen odor pathways. The current references do not meet all those requirements, so I have not merged their rules into the FlyWire LIF engine. Previous timing failures, BANC's unestablished spatial replication, and Huang's prediction discrepancies remain documented. Original model/data arrays and previous release archives are preserved.

All new code and downloaded files are in this project. GitHub supplied the needed model source; no Zenodo retry or manual download was needed. See [run and reproduction instructions](README.md) and [source notices](NOTICE.md).

# Step 6: a fixed fading-pair front end improves native Full151 byte output

## Result

The single frozen FE0-plus-fading-pair candidate passed this run's prespecified **E2 increment** and both crowded E1 guards: `INPUT_BRIDGE_QUALIFIED`. It is the first result in this local output chain in which a changed, never-taught presentation of a complete `a+b=` expression led the **model's own four-channel Full151 memory** to emit the taught answer byte at the fixed `=` response point. The result is limited to four answer bytes, one spacing change, two small crowded panels, the fixed V9.2 checkpoint, and directly taught operand pairs. It is **not learned addition, unseen-sum transfer, multi-byte generation, autonomous stopping, or a persistent-core E3 result**.

Across **32 new paired schedule identities**, the candidate answered all **128/128** never-taught spacing variants in panel A; FE0 answered **100/128**. Both no-write controls answered **32/128**, the default class-0 tie baseline. The prespecified world-paired incremental W−N accuracy effect was **+21.875 percentage points** (two-sided 95% Student-*t* interval **+18.846 to +24.904 pp**), positive in 28 worlds and tied in four, with no negative worlds. It was concentrated in `0+2=`: FE0 answered its variant only **4/32**, while the candidate answered **32/32**. FE0 already answered the other three variants **32/32 each** in this specific four-output assay. The older Step-8 two-letter E2 failure is therefore not a valid numerical proxy for this variant or route.

## What was held constant

Each world used the same 48 class-index order, answer-byte sequence and 165-second record schedule under both FE0 and the candidate. All branches began with the same native Full151 checkpoint. Four copied native Full151 value stores read each expression, chose a byte **before** the answer byte arrived, and then received the existing byte-derived teacher rule: in W, one target and three nontarget native writes per record (**192 per life**); in N, identical bytes and teaching calls with plastic writing disabled. The learner's byte-prediction weights did not update. The only architecture intervention was the 88-channel sensory front end.

The candidate preserved FE0's raw trace and added fixed SHA256-seeded ordered-pair contacts that fade over 30 seconds; the pair route skips spaces but sees their timing through FE0. It used no answer labels, cue IDs, or learned front-end weights. Canonical teaching was always `8 spaces || a+b=`. The scored E2 form was `7 spaces || a || space || +b=`: twelve bytes total, the same `=` and choice time, and **zero teaching exposures** to that raw form. The first scored response came before any variant feedback. A raw exact-key table cannot retrieve it without an engineered whitespace normalization; such a canonicalizer remains a valid countermodel for this narrowly defined E2 task.

## Crowded E1 guards

The directly taught canonical panel A stayed perfect under both routes: **128/128** W outputs. The B panel reproduced the previously observed crowding under FE0: `0+1=` was correct in **2/32**, while candidate FE0-plus-pair was **32/32**. The candidate kept the other three B facts **32/32 each**. In a prospectively specified `+0=` stress panel H, FE0's four fact counts were **32, 32, 32, 21** out of 32; the candidate achieved **32/32 on each fact**. These are prespecified descriptive guards, not separately multiplicity-adjusted hypothesis tests. They show that the E2 gain did not come from erasing the tested crowded associations.

## Engineering and audit boundary

Before science, an excluded technical world (11600) and a separate in-memory dry run checked cloning, source/checkpoint identity, byte-derived routing, native write counts, W/N sensory equality, read-only probe purity, and exact clock alignment. The candidate's technical KC census found no wrong-answer collision in A/B/H. Its minimum different-answer KC separations at that initial state were **82/88/80**, versus FE0 **44/28/36**; this was a descriptive check, **not** the behavioral pass rule. Four fixed equal-bigram-count, different-order sentinel pairs remained distinguishable. The canonical-versus-variant KC Hamming distances for A were **20, 14, 16, 10** at that initial state, so this preflight did not merely give the two raw forms identical KC supports. It did not measure those supports after teaching.

The independent [audit](audit.py) verified the frozen source and Step-5 provenance hashes, the exact 32-world roster and every receipt, schedules, cue and stream hashes, teacher bytes, routes, 192-versus-zero native writes, timing, all class-value argmax decisions, probe rosters, the primary interval and both guards. Six intentional changes to a receipt were rejected. There is no failure receipt, no replaced world and no rerun of earlier science. The 32 lives of each of twelve panel/route/write branches total **384 new branch lives**. ## Interpretation and next decision

Step 5 showed that competing expression content changes learned output at equal write count. Step 6 adds the needed intervention: **holding Full151 and teaching fixed, changing only a label-free input front end rescued the tested same-suffix crowding and this untrained spacing variant.** This supports an input-address contribution to the earlier failure. It does not prove that FE0 was the *only* cause, that Full151 has no capacity limit under heavier load, or that the 30-second pair trace generalizes to arbitrary text. The science worlds varied teaching order, not symbol alphabet, checkpoint, expression family or presentation transform. H was prospective for behavior, but its fixed `+0=` pattern is close to the observed `+1=` problem. The four-store one-byte interface and supplied answer bytes remain engineering scaffolding.

The next substantive test should ask whether this same integrated route can **emit** an answer for a *never-reinforced relation instance* after prior teaching and intervening learning, with a paired no-prior-teaching causal control. V9.9A/B found limited delayed E3 signals through evaluator-read native values; they have not been reproduced as the model's own output. A separately frozen, harder E2 variant family could assess how broad this front-end invariance is, but repeating directly taught arithmetic facts or expanding to 100-fact recitation would not advance the evidence level.

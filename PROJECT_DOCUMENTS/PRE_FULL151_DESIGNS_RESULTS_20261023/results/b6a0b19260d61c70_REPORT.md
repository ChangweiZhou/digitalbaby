## What V79 will resolve

The question is whether adaptation recovery produces better-directed slow writes
at equal expenditure, or whether its advantage depends on spending more. The
frozen [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) narrows the submitted proposal to this comparison.

One native donor trajectory supplies both dense and recovered-adaptation shadow
writes. We compare raw vectors, equal global lower budgets, and equal budgets
within exact anatomical route/sign groups, using the same non-slow carrier.
A raw dense replay must reconstruct the native model. A separate autonomous
adaptation-only arm retains the previous V78 intervention as a reference.

This removes the immediate confound between write amount and vector structure
without asserting that a fly computes global normalization. These are evaluator
controls. A lower-budget negative result cannot establish high-dose sufficiency
or close the adaptation branch. Shared-route matching can discard legitimate
sign/route differences, so retained mass is measured explicitly.

No new allocator, genetic search, compression, reader fit, physiology fit or
model promotion is included. A positive result would identify a narrower target
for a subsequent autonomous learning rule, not demonstrate a persistent core.

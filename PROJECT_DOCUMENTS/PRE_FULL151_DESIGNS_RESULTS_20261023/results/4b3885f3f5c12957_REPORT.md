# V72 discussion: test neighborhood information before adding another memory state

2026-09-14. **Discussion proposal, not a frozen protocol or an executed V72 campaign.** The accepted reference and all V71 assets remain unchanged. This review reads the five PDFs inside the existing `related paper.zip`, checks the cited abstract, and inspects existing model arrays/code. It generates no new learning trajectories, receiver interventions, fitted policies or behavioral results.

The papers are useful, but the supplied attack memo is based on V70. Its proposed sequence must be updated with V71: safe receiver witnesses already exist in many isolated states; unsigned-history priority has already been tested and failed the combined progression screen. The next useful question is **whether a defensible neighborhood supplies information that a cheap coordinate-wise rule lacks**. Neither another oracle-existence campaign nor an unqualified quadratic penalty is the right starting point.

The first paper does not say that spreading a write always strengthens memory: its distributed seven-spine response was weaker than the clustered condition and closer to the single-spine time course. Competition and cooperation coexist. Its molecular resource variables are model abstractions, not measured MiniFly occupancy. The measured endpoint is spine structure over minutes, not arbitrary-association retention over days.

Agnes–Vogels likewise uses local current, spike timing and inhibition, not `abs(slow + raw_write)`. Keeping only a decreasing receiver score removes the cooperative/competitive balance responsible for the model's fixed point. Any such score must be called an engineering translation.

GateON's main formulation has supplied task context; a variant infers changes from loss. Its activity-only relevance approximation does not remove all other training/context machinery. Also, the memo's multiplicative availability update has an absorbing exact zero: if clipping or underflow sets A to zero, multiplication cannot recover it. A future implementation would need explicit numerical semantics; this observation does not establish a bug in the authors' code, which was not audited here.

- Among 128 primary snapshots, the restricted capacity solver found 94 full-mass safe witnesses, 22 positive partial witnesses, 10 zero-fraction results and 2 no-transport states. These are correlated snapshots and restricted, isolated-endpoint witnesses, not a global capacity theorem.
- OPP improved NEW worst damage by approximately 0.004 Hz relative to PROP, but revision intervals included zero, isolated old-choice losses occurred, and its four primary comparisons with BALANCED all had intervals including zero. H costs 25% extra mutable state.
- At full dose, 58.7% of repeated group opportunities had no receiver freedom due to saturation or a singleton receiver. A scoring rule cannot change a unique feasible allocation.

Source: [V71 report](../iteration16/REPORT.md), [protocol](../iteration16/PROTOCOL.md). Reuse its witnesses as diagnostics. Do not imitate one oracle's coordinate vector: constrained optima can be nonunique. Score a proposed allocation by its actual consequences and safety constraints. A failed restricted solver still does not license the claim that all receiver policies, or the biological substrate, are inadequate.

V68 is a further warning against repeating an information-only success. Active-population statistics already decoded reversal versus unrelated learning with AUROC 0.951, while the selected two-score learning gate harmed fresh-stream retention and revision. V72 therefore must not repeat that episode-classification task. Its new question is the safety of competing **feasible receiver actions**, and the neighborhood must add information beyond pooled/averaged local statistics, not merely outperform an isolated coordinate. ## A concrete data limitation discovered in this review

The frozen reference has 5,177 KCs, one slow state per KC and two slow-output channels, representing pooled left/right alpha3 outputs. The parent actually represents gamma1 and alpha3; **it has no gamma4 storage module or active-zone state**. Renaming the existing alpha3 module gamma4 would not instantiate the new fly paper.

Static inspection gives 26 groups formed by hemisphere and alpha3 teacher routing. In every group, the alpha3 Q matrix has rank one, and all nonzero normalized Q rows are identical. No KC projects through Q to both alpha3 output hemispheres. Consequently, a cosine-neighborhood kernel built only from these pooled slow-output vectors is constant within a receiver group. A row-normalized weighted Gram kernel using their scalar magnitudes also cannot supply receiver-specific neighborhood distinctions within that group. With the usual included diagonal this becomes a global summary; excluding the diagonal adds a self-subtraction, not newly recovered anatomy. This is an exact property of the present arrays, **not evidence that the fly's neighborhood is unstructured**. See [inventory](model_inventory.json) and [read-only reproducer](inspect_model.py).

The FlyWire metadata has neuron position and soma fields. These are not the locations of the specific KC-to-MBON contacts represented by a slow weight. The local synapse tables examined in this project are BANC and hemibrain assets; their neuron identities cannot be joined to FlyWire 630 KCs. Type correspondence does not supply a neuron-level spatial kernel.

Two legitimate options remain:

1. **Same-specimen connectivity proxy, already potentially available:** recover the individual MBON14 connection profiles before pooling. The prior audit found two MBON14 cells per hemisphere, with KC input-vector cosines 0.845 and 0.868. Their differences may supply a small shared-postsynaptic-partner operator. This must be named a connectivity-neighborhood proxy, not micrometer or molecular diffusion distance. First measure whether it varies among actual eligible receivers beyond Q magnitude and degree; it may still add no useful information. The existing graph suffices for that bounded check, without changing the learner or adding output channels. [Prior audit](../v68_review/anatomy_audit/NOTE.md).
2. **Physical-neighborhood analysis:** obtain validated same-version FlyWire contact coordinates and, where required, neurite/branch membership; or analyze BANC/HB separately with an explicit site-to-state projection. Euclidean closeness across different cells is insufficient to claim a shared intracellular resource pool. Static EM adjacency alone cannot establish the activity-dependent competition law. This is additional data/model-mapping work, not permission to graft BANC coordinates onto the present model.

The first option is a small, feasible next check. The second is needed for a stronger spatial biological interpretation; it need not block discussion or the connectivity-proxy diagnostic.

## Why quadratic specificity should not be the first gate

For nonnegative L, ordering by L and by L squared is identical. A rank or Top-K allocator would therefore be unchanged. Soft scores `exp(-kappa L)` and `exp(-kappa L^2)` can change allocation concentration, but that does not isolate a biological quadratic mechanism. If L is below one, squaring even reduces its numerical value; scale must be specified. A global L constant within a group also cancels in normalized soft allocation.

V72 should distinguish three questions in order: does neighboring state add information; does its mapping matter; and only then does a nonlinear response help beyond concentration/scale changes? SELF and GLOBAL controls belong in the first analysis, not after a preferred local formula wins. A normalized average `K q` is not automatically finite-resource pressure: normalization can remove the number-of-competitors effect. Specify whether the measured quantity is average occupancy, activity-driven demand or demand per available resource, and control for degree and current write mass.

## Recommended V72 scope

**Primary question: can a fixed, same-specimen neighborhood improve receiver decisions beyond state-local BALANCED without extra persistent state?** Keep gamma4 migration, new availability, hard winner selection and continuously deployed policies out of this initial campaign.

### A. Establish whether the candidate information exists

Use the old 16 histories only for development. Inspect the unpooled connectivity proxy, coverage, within-feasible-group variation and relation to Q magnitude, teacher routing, KC subtype and degree. Freeze one operator from anatomy alone; no radius or partition selected using future damage. A low-rank factorization can avoid a dense 5,177-squared matrix. Count fixed storage, temporary work and persistent state separately.

Compare existing-state predictors using signed slow state, `abs(slow + u_raw)`, raw write/capacity, current activity/adaptation and known output gain against the same predictors augmented with neighboring quantities. Including output sensitivity is necessary before attributing a gain to local competition. H can be an already-tested diagnostic comparator; do not treat it as a new V72 proposal or a free variable.

Include a pooled active-population baseline and averaged coordinate-local predictions, following V68's scope control. A new neighborhood must improve on those summaries for action-risk prediction; another high reversal-classification AUROC would not answer the question.

Evaluator-only old-cue margins and feasible perturbation outcomes can label risk during development. They must never be inputs to a runtime allocator. Separate predicting damage magnitude from predicting which old choice is lost; control for the old margin already being near threshold. Keep whole histories together in cross-validation. If fitting any policy, use a small prespecified interpretable family and report parameter cost; do not train on oracle allocation coordinates as though they were unique biological labels.

**Stop condition:** a constant/unsupported kernel, or no held-out incremental predictive value over the state/output-gain baseline, ends this particular neighborhood candidate. It does not prove local biological interactions are absent. This result would favor a resolution/mapping investigation over more penalty formulas.

### B. Only a qualified information source gets a receiver intervention

Freeze one candidate before new confirmation outcomes. Retain the 5,177-KC reference, frozen reader, raw writes, fast/adaptation dynamics, donor identities and per-group route/sign budgets. Use the V71 common-prestate assay with the prespecified 90% condition; full dose is an informative saturation control, not an alternative rescue endpoint.

The required comparisons are PARENT/NO_WRITE references, PROP, BALANCED, the candidate, SELF/GLOBAL ablations and matched neighborhood shuffles. Hold the functional form fixed in the mapping contrast. Build nulls within the relevant hemisphere/support/type/degree strata and retain feasible caps; record the invariants exactly. Report realized concentration and max-coordinate changes. If they differ materially, an additional feasible concentration-matched comparison is required before claiming a specifically nonlinear benefit. Fewer valid randomizations must be reported, not silently replaced with a weaker null.

New confirmation histories must be unseen by every development fit and formula choice. A provisional 32-history panel is a planning quantity, not a power guarantee: lock sample size and useful-effect/noninferiority margins after a history-level precision calculation, before opening confirmation outcomes. Multiple presentations and receiver draws remain technical repeats, not independent n. No additional seeds chosen because a confidence interval nearly passes.

Primary endpoints remain total old joint accuracy, losses among PARENT-correct old choices, worst old damage and target acquisition/revision. Mean damage and top-decile damage are supporting measures. Require improvement over BALANCED as well as the mapping null, test NEW and REVERSE separately, and retain exact nonlinear continuation under the unchanged parent after the local action. Keep all assigned snapshots, including no-freedom and no-transport cases, in the primary accounting; stratified effects are explanatory. Point-estimate equality is not proof of statistical noninferiority, especially for rare choice errors.

Passing this stage licenses a later test of repeated use. It does not show that independently safe local actions remain safe when deployed throughout a new trajectory. Failure should remain failure; do not promote another descriptive arm or tune the penalty after seeing results.

## Where the new fly forgetting paper belongs

It warrants a separate future question: can we distinguish retention loss from explicitly programmed disposal or compartment-dependent trace conversion? Its gamma1-to-gamma4 sequence includes a change from depression to potentiation; it is not simple mass-conserving copying of the same signed weight. The supplied abstract does not give enough kinetics, readout calibration, trigger detail or interference testing to fit a module here.

A useful later design would hold immediate acquisition and added storage fixed while comparing a compartment-specific transition with blocked-transition and matched-passive-decay controls. It must assess both retention of still-valid associations and replacement of an obsolete target. The current reversal task already distinguishes the revised target from unrelated old memories. It should not be relaxed to excuse collateral loss. Nor should blocked forgetting be declared a solution without showing that subsequent acquisition and revision remain possible. These are proposed computational tests, not findings of the abstract.

The missing full text and supplement would help that branch; they are not prerequisites for V72's information audit. A verified manual-access link is [Huang et al., DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2025.05.047](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2025.05.047). The publisher full-text fetch failed; no full-text claim is made and no retry is required. The co-plasticity author-code fetch also timed out: [author repository](https://github.com/ejagnes/codependent_plasticity). The local paper is sufficient for the present equation review. The [MultiSpineModel repository](https://github.com/meggl23/MultiSpineModel) exposes processed-data and model files, but none was downloaded or executed. No Zenodo retry was made.

## Disposition

Recommended next work is a bounded **neighborhood-information and model-resolution audit**, with a conditional single-candidate common-prestate test. V71's unsuccessful H policy remains unsuccessful. A connectivity proxy must earn predictive value before acquiring a biological interpretation; a learned rule must earn behavioral value over BALANCED before acquiring additional state or a deployment campaign. No V72 experiment or promotion has occurred in this review.

## What V86 tests

Primary arms: `REF`, `V82`, `S82_D81`, `V84E`, `V85E`.

## Interpretation guardrails

- Fixed REF-Q observer is primary; candidate-coupled and actual-only outcomes are shadows, not substitutes.
- Long-life jobs never reset between stages. Age-local damage probes operate on copies and cannot alter the main life.
- The first 36 memories not selected in the initial 12-target challenge are never directly challenged later; they form an untouched early cohort.
- Held-out pairs 168-175 measure unsupported/spurious responses; pairs 176-177 are used only for copied-state age-local damage probes.
- History/seed is the independent unit for paired intervals.
- No scalar winner is computed. Judge acquisition, revision, retention, damage, and specificity jointly.

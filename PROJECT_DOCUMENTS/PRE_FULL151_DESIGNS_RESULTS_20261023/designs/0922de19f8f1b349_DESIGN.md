# MiniFly V9.8 exploratory unseen-input trial

**Locked scope before trial worlds:** 26 September 2026. This is a small, prospective E2/E3 qualification, not confirmation or model selection. It uses the frozen V9.2 parent checkpoint and unchanged V9.7 REF and HASH8 implementations. No neural parameter, topology, router, or learner rate is fitted here. Engineering world 9901 is excluded from results; trial worlds are fixed at 1201–1212. Parent sources and this design/runner are SHA-256 locked before those worlds run.

## Questions and claim limits

1. **E2 presentation invariance:** Can an association taught under four task-irrelevant 9-byte prefixes be retrieved under a fifth, never-reinforced prefix? Success excludes an exact 12-byte-string table, but a canonicalized ordered-pair table can still solve it. It is **not E3 transfer**.
2. **E3 minimal feature transfer:** Can teaching on six cross-class symbol pairs alter the first choice on three never-reinforced pairs made from the same six symbols? A learned per-symbol rule is a legitimate small transfer; exact ordered-pair lookup cannot solve held-out pairs. This is not NLP or broad task generalization.

For each task and mode, `W` and `N` receive the same bytes, outcome byte `!`, timing, and binary teaching labels. `W` enables native value writes during the old teaching episode; `N` disables only those writes. Both enable writes during a later unrelated cohort. The engineered V9.2 byte predictor must remain identical between W and N. Value probes use cloned native state and occur before any feedback on the probed form or pair. Thus W−N on held-out probes tests the contribution of earlier native teaching; the predictor's own success cannot be silently credited to value memory.

## Fixed stream and probes

All records have 12 cue bytes, `!`, and newline at V9.5's fixed timing. Each original cue receives 12 reinforced presentations; the outcome/Teach schedule is unchanged between W and N. The phases are: old teaching; immediate probes; passive 24-hour interval and probes; one 72-record unrelated or partly overlapping new cohort, taught to both branches; immediate probes; passive 24-hour interval and final probes. All probes are read-only on saved/cloned state. The early and delayed scores are always reported, even if the early result is negative; there is no selective continuation within a trial world.

**E2:** Use V9.5's six cyclically labeled old ordered pairs and its first new cohort. The cue bytes are `prefix[9] || a || b || space`. Prefixes 0–3 are `spaces[9]`, `0+spaces[8]`, `space+0+spaces[7]`, `spaces[2]+0+spaces[6]`. Across 12 bouts each old cue sees each training prefix exactly three times; permutation of cue order is seeded by world and bout. Prefix 4, `spaces[3]+0+spaces[5]`, is withheld from every old and new teaching record and used only for probes. The generator declares prefix position irrelevant to the ordered-pair label. The trained-prefix-0 probe is an E1 competence comparator; prefix-4 is the E2 target. Record exact KC-code equality, support overlap, and HASH8 bank agreement between the two renderings at each stage. A changed address is a diagnosis, not proof of the behavioral cause.

**E3:** Draw six distinct uppercase bytes and assign three at random to class P and three to class N. The nine P–N unordered pairs form a 3×3 grid. Hold out a random perfect matching of three edges; teach both orientations of the other six edges for 12 bouts (144 records). For every edge, `(P,N)` is aversive and `(N,P)` neutral. Each symbol appears in exactly two trained edges and in both byte positions with equal frequency; all held-out pair bytes are new combinations in the same format `spaces[9] || a || b || space`. The first-choice target is the three held-out orientation comparisons. A simple decoder that infers each symbol's class from training labels solves all three; an exact-pair table with no relation inference scores chance. A separate three-symbol new cohort uses disjoint symbols and balanced cyclic labels, 72 records, taught to W and N. The W−N contrast on held-out pairs is the causal target. Exact trained pairs are an E1 acquisition guard. Random class assignment and the matched N branch control fixed symbol/value bias and exposure-only shortcuts.

## Measurement and decisions

Primary descriptive quantities per task/mode/stage: held-out pair choice, mean signed value margin, W−N difference, trained-pair choice, and the number of worlds where W exceeds N. Report all pair margins and no-write results, not just a thresholded headline. E2 also reports exact code/address agreement. The task-solving positive controls are the generator's pair-identity oracle for E2 and class decoder for E3; the exact-key countermodels fail their respective withheld tests by construction. No-world teaching or label-swap inference is made from this small panel.

With 12 worlds, this is a **feasibility trial**: report paired descriptive intervals and directions, not significance or promotion. A positive E3-looking signal requires a later frozen confirmation with delayed/interference results, extra shortcut controls, and a resource budget. A negative held-out result is diagnostic only if the exact-cue acquisition guard and the relevant input/code checks pass. If REF retrieves variants but HASH8 does not and the latter changes bank, prioritize address invariance; if both fail to represent the pair, prioritize byte input. If E2 works but E3 does not, test information extraction/use before adding banks. More bank counts and an evolution search are out of scope.

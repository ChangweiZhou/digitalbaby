# MiniFly V9.7 — fixed-address banks without the B transfer

Frozen before prospective worlds 801–1056 are evaluated. V9.7 is a controlled byte-association experiment on the existing V9.5 four-cohort lifetime, not a new fly-connectome inference or a search over routers.

## Question and interventions

All five arms start from the same V9.2 checkpoint and see the same bytes, rewards, teaching flags, timing, and evaluator probes in each world. Only the inherited native value-memory transition changes. The engineered byte predictor remains shared and must have an identical digest across arms and write/no-write branches.

| Arm | Value-memory intervention |
|---|---|
| `REF` | V9.5 single native alpha store, no B. |
| `GEOM4+B` | Exact V9.6 four-bank router and frozen A8/B write reassignment. |
| `GEOM4` | Same four stores and geometric router, without B. |
| `HASH4` | Four stores, no B; historical exact-current-KC-code hash to eight address classes, then the frozen V9.6 four-bank grouping. |
| `HASH8` | Eight stores, no B; the same eight hash classes, each its own bank. |

The SHA-256 hash serializes the **current complete 5,177-dimensional float64 KC code** exactly as `WorkstreamFB1_Followup_v1/fu_common.py::code_bytes`: finite contiguous little-endian float64, negative zeros canonicalized, domain tag `FB1-followup-code-v1\0`, dimension as little-endian uint64, then raw bytes. Address is the low three bits of the first digest byte of `SHA256(birth_key || code_bytes(x))`. Each world receives one deterministic 32-byte birth key, common to its HASH4 and HASH8 arms. The learner receives the key at birth; it never receives world index, cue identity, set label, reward, pair structure, or a future query. HASH4 uses the historical centre-class-to-bank grouping `(0,4),(1,7),(2,6),(3,5)`; HASH8 is identity. A logical cue whose current KC code changes may migrate banks. No alternative raw-cue, support-only, or frozen-code router is selected after seeing results.

Store updates and passive decay follow V9.6 exactly, except that the no-B arms call the original native event directly. A queried value reads only the bank selected by that query's current KC code. `GEOM4+B` is rerun contemporaneously: V9.6's earlier +7.9 percentage-point result cannot by itself identify the effect of banking.

## Engineering gate and source lock

World 401 is engineering-only. Before prospective evaluation, require: (1) REF probes exactly reproduce the archived V9.5 world 401; (2) `GEOM4+B` probes and native state match the frozen V9.6 implementation on world 401; (3) one-bank no-B is exactly REF, one-bank+B is exactly B_TRANSFER on a nonzero trained life; (4) all stores decay correctly during passive time; (5) clone, snapshot/restore, and disk save/load preserve every bank, key, counter, and prediction; (6) reads do not mutate state; (7) a fixed-code hash route is deterministic and HASH4/HASH8 are nested. Probe scores are independently recomputed from the three pair margins. A failure blocks science rather than becoming a statistical result.

The one-bank modes are qualifications only, never science arms. The observed 71/72 write-to-delayed-read agreement under an earlier diagnostic key does not define this algorithm. The gate checks whether any V9.7 world-401 mismatch under its own birth key is explained by a changed exact KC code; it must not silently modify the router to force agreement or claim to reconstruct the earlier mismatch count. Lock the design, implementation, inherited sources, calibration vector, historical hash sources, checkpoint, and world-401 reference by SHA-256 before science. Worlds 801–1056 are disjoint from V9.5 (401–656) and V9.6 (701–780). No parameter tuning, optional stopping, or sample-size extension is allowed on these worlds.

## Two separate outcome axes

**Causal write harm.** For old choice at D4, estimate each arm's own `WWWW − NNNN` on the exact common prechallenge state. For every planned contrast `c − r`, estimate

`DID(c,r) = [old_D4(WWWW) − old_D4(NNNN)]_c − [old_D4(WWWW) − old_D4(NNNN)]_r`.

Positive DID means less incremental choice damage from subsequent writes. It says nothing on its own about absolute competence. The five planned contrasts are `GEOM4+B − REF` (fresh replication), `GEOM4 − GEOM4+B` (remove B), `HASH4 − GEOM4` (router at four banks), `HASH8 − HASH4` (bank count within hash), and `HASH8 − REF` (net architecture). Holm-adjusted two-sided p-values across these five form the causal primary family; paired 95% intervals and signs are also reported. A confidence interval spanning zero is unresolved, not evidence of equivalence.

**Deployed competence.** On all-write life, report old D4 choice, four separate new-cohort D4 choices, their equally weighted mean, immediate old/end and each new E1–E4 acquisition, analog margins, and pair-loss transitions. The same five contrasts for old D4 and mean new D4 form a ten-test Holm family for the two scalar summaries. The four cohort results and margins remain visible; a mean cannot hide a cohort-specific collapse. `HASH8 − GEOM4` is a descriptive, roughly resource-matched comparison, not an additional confirmatory test. Do not call a candidate a useful learner from DID alone, or call a candidate a write-harm repair from absolute choice alone.

For old memory, explicitly display `old_end` immediately after acquisition, `prechallenge` after 24 hours and before any new cohort, old `E1…E4`, and delayed `D4`. Define `Δrest = prechallenge − old_end` and both `Δwrite_life = D4_WWWW − prechallenge` and `Δno_write_life = D4_NNNN − prechallenge`. The latter two include time and exposure; their difference, rather than `Δwrite_life` alone, isolates the assigned write flag in this assay.

Pair-loss rates for across-arm comparisons use the **same reference-at-risk old pair IDs**: old pairs correct at REF prechallenge, evaluated in every arm on those identities. Arm-specific at-risk/lost counts may also be shown descriptively, clearly labeled.

## Address and resource diagnostics

For every banked arm, record total and phase-specific actual plastic-event occupancy and the L1 magnitude of its **final raw slow and fast writes**, excluding passive decay. For each old cue, compute its routed bank at prechallenge and at D4 in both WWWW and NNNN. Then count what fraction of new-phase plastic events, and what fraction of actual final-write L1 mass, land in that old query's prechallenge or D4 bank. Report cue-bank migration and write-to-D4-read consistency by cohort. These are diagnostics of interference opportunity, not behavioral end points or proof that each collision caused forgetting. They must be based on candidate state and exact encoded cue at the specified time; no pair-opponent-same-bank surrogate is used.

Report deployable persistent bytes, added mutable bank bytes, fixed router/key and B bytes, total counted process allocation, and duplicate native scratch. The B vector may be loaded by the Python process for all arms, but is charged as a deployed parameter only to `GEOM4+B`; disclose this distinction. Exclude evaluator-only traces from deployable state and report their diagnostic footprint separately. The HASH8-vs-GEOM4 near-resource match is an empirical ledger question, not an assumed equality.

## Precision and acceptance

Run 256 untouched paired worlds, fixed indices 801–1056, up to four local workers, with atomic resumable receipts. Each receipt includes source-lock digest, schedule hashes, probe margins and independently validated scores, learner/predictor digests, address summary, and resource ledger. On completion, an independent read-only audit verifies all receipts and rederives headline contrasts from canonical margins. Keep raw receipts in the project; deliver a compact bundle of report, summary tables, code/config, lock and acceptance files, and a manifest of unchanged parent assets. Do not duplicate datasets or parent archives.

The V9.6 old-choice DID variance suggests an **unadjusted** n=256 interval half-width around 3.6 percentage points, but the new contrast variances and multiplicity may make 5-point effects difficult to establish. This experiment can identify large effects or sharpen limits; it cannot establish 1-point noninferiority or biological optimality. The fixed learner seed and synthetic byte workload limit generalization. A11 is separate and is not used to select V9.7 interventions.

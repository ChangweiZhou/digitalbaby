# V68 conditional analytic pilot

Written after the information stage selected S1, before threshold calibration or generation of fresh validation cues. Original development outcomes remain in decision.json; this additional stage was conditional in PROTOCOL.md. The high-dimensional diagnostic classifier is never deployed.

One family only. Before each update, F = ||dx*slow||2/(||dx||2+1e-12). E = sum(abs(dx*alpha_fast) where alpha_fast*raw_slow>0) / (sum(abs(dx*slow) where slow*raw_slow<0)+1e-12). A conflicting slow increment is retained only when F >= theta_F AND E >= theta_E. All nonconflicting slow increments and both fast increments are retained. No additional dynamic state, cue lookup or experience counter is used. Two fixed thresholds add 16 numeric bytes, not mutable memory. These are population statistics with unvalidated biological access.

Calibration is deliberately small and separate from fresh validation. On the first eight V67 development streams, theta_F is each of the 25th/50th/75th percentiles of first-bout reversal F; theta_E is each of {0.25,0.5,1,2}: twelve threshold pairs. On bouts 6–12, compute per-stream fractions of conflicting slow mass retained for reversal and unrelated episodes, then average streams. If any pair retains at least 80% reversal conflict mass, restrict to those; otherwise retain all pairs and explicitly report that no pair met 80%. Select maximum reversal-minus-unrelated retention gap, breaking ties by lower theta_F quantile then lower theta_E. This is a shadow selection on fixed trajectories, not a learning outcome optimization. No second family if this fails.

With that one threshold pair, run ordinary from-zero candidate training to load 96 on the remaining eight V67 development streams to measure candidate mean raw slow-write retention relative to its own counterfactual unattenuated writes. Freeze a scalar slow-write fraction equal to the ratio of the summed retained and raw masses. Scalar leaves both fast traces unchanged and attenuates all slow increments uniformly. This matches a development aggregate raw-dose statistic; altered trajectories can cause realized validation doses to differ, which must be reported. Do not retune the scalar after validation.

Freeze thresholds, scalar, source hashes, input-generation rule, evaluation and acceptance rules before generating any validation cue. Generate 16 new streams 6803001–6803016, 96 distinct 8-of-88 cue pairs per stream, excluding all cues in iteration10/11/12 banks and duplicates across validation streams. Roles balanced within each twelve-association block with RNG seed+77. Existing classifier streams and fresh pilot streams must remain disjoint.

Three arms: unchanged parent, analytic gate, scalar control. Train from zero, six bouts per association. Measure acquisition for each association immediately and after a cloned 24h delay; measure every stored association at loads 12/24/48/96, immediate and 24h. Probes are neutral copies and never enter learning. Report all assigned associations, including initially failed acquisition.

Revision/anomaly starts from each arm's load12 checkpoint, first four assigned associations, for a common set of 64 focal episodes. Report the unchanged parent's pre-intervention eligible set as the primary comparable denominator, all assigned episodes, and arm-specific eligibility counts. Reverse each focal and probe the reversed contingency at dose0/6/12, immediate and 24h. Separately give one reversed A/B bout and compare its false flip to an exposure-matched no-write twin, with both original and reversed outcomes. Never filter on post-intervention success.

Acceptance requires every gate: day/load96 joint choice gain >=5 percentage points over parent and paired lower interval >0; >=3 points over scalar and lower >0; immediate and day acquisition mean actual-margin loss within 0.25 Hz AND joint-choice loss within 5 points (paired lower bounds); day/load12 and load24 joint-choice noninferiority within 5 points; day/dose12 revision joint-choice noninferiority within 5 points versus parent for both common-eligible and all-assigned; no positive mean increase in common-eligible anomalous false flips versus parent. Report intervals for every endpoint. Confidence intervals use the fixed 2,000 paired stream bootstrap, seed6809001. No pooled synapse or cue bootstrap, no alternative acceptance metric, no additional candidate if rejected.

Startup checks must verify gate-open and scalar-one exact parent equivalence, clone isolation, fast increments unchanged from identical pre-state, no amplification, zero activity/passive decay unchanged, nonconflicting slow writes untouched, and mutable bytes unchanged. This is a computational consistency check; unchanged time constants are not physiological validation of the new plasticity rule. The measured paper phenotypes are not refit and no biological certification is claimed.

Final validation decision is separate from the positive development information result. A rejected pilot does not erase the latter, and the latter cannot excuse failed retention or acquisition gates.

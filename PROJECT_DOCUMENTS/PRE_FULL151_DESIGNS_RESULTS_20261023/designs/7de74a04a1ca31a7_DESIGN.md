# Step 6: one fading-pair input candidate, native learned-byte output

## Question and evidence boundary

This is a bounded **input-architecture intervention**. Keep the V9.2 birth checkpoint, native Full151 connectome and fast/slow plasticity, four copied output stores, ASCII answer-byte router, four-write teaching policy, 48 teaching records, 165-second record, and one-byte argmax at the fixed `=` response point. Replace only FE0's 88-channel sensory representation with **one** fixed, causal, label-free candidate: FE0's raw trace plus decaying ordered adjacent *non-whitespace* byte-pair coincidences. The candidate has no task labels, answer table, cue index, or learned front-end weights. The byte predictor remains frozen during these records.

The primary question is whether the candidate improves **E2 changed-presentation retrieval** of directly taught arithmetic strings. A positive result is not addition: every scored operand pair was taught in its canonical frame. Crowded E1 is a required guard, not another primary inference. No held-out sum, delayed E3 reuse, ten-digit alphabet, multi-byte output, or learned stop is tested. The recitation countermodel can solve canonical E1; a hand-built whitespace canonicalizer plus an exact-key store could solve the E2 probe. Thus even a pass supports only the stated interface under one prespecified spacing change.

## Frozen candidate and exposure

The candidate retains FE0's raw 88-PN trace (time constant 10 s). Every ordered pair of consecutive **non-space and non-newline** bytes stimulates eight SHA256-seeded fixed PN contacts; its second trace decays with time constant 30 s and enters the 88-PN readout at gain 1.0, normalized once before the unchanged PN→KC projection. Spaces are still seen by the FE0 trace. A newline terminates the pair chain; no pair crosses records. This generic whitespace treatment is an engineered nuisance policy, not an arithmetic parser. It cannot be tuned using science outcomes. The excluded technical check measures exact KC support at the actual response time; no numerical Hamming threshold licenses learning.

All worlds teach the canonical twelve-byte forms `8 spaces || a+b=`. Three panels have one fact for each teacher byte `0`–`3`:

| Panel | Four taught operand pairs | Role |
|---|---|---|
| A | `(0,0),(0,1),(0,2),(1,2)` | known learnable E1 guard and E2 source |
| B | `(0,0),(0,1),(1,1),(2,1)` | Step-5 crowded reference |
| H | `(0,0),(1,0),(2,0),(3,0)` | prospectively frozen `+0=` suffix stress test, not used to choose the candidate |

In each panel the answer byte is determined only by the visible sum. A schedule identity fixes 12 independent bout permutations of the four class indices. That same index and answer-byte sequence is used for all panels, routes and W/N branches. W makes four native plastic writes per teaching record (192 per life); N sees the same bytes and native teaching calls with writing off. No teaching record contains the E2 form. The only changed presentation is `7 spaces || a || space || +b=` for panel A, twelve bytes total with `=` and the answer-choice time unchanged. It is read on a discarded clone at the final probe before any variant feedback. Canonical and variant raw forms, and their KC codes, are reported separately.

One excluded engineering world is 11600. Science uses exactly 32 new paired schedule identities 11601–11632, disjoint from all earlier steps. Each identity contains FE0 and candidate × W/N × A/B/H; the world/schedule identity is the uncertainty unit. The earlier A/B outcomes do not count as new evidence. H is a prospective stress guard against tuning to B. Source and roster freeze after engineering qualification and before science; any scientific exception stops with a failure receipt and no automatic restart or replacement world.

## Qualification, outcomes, and interpretation

The technical check requires source/checkpoint identity, identical birth native states, time and teacher-byte parity, teach/read address parity, equal sensory state within each route's W/N and output copies, exact write counts, probe nonmutation, and no code collision among different-answer expressions in A/B/H at the checked starting state. It also checks several equal-bigram-count order counterexamples, including `ABACA` versus `ACABA`, on the candidate at the actual answer time. Any failure stops before science. It records, but does not require a particular positive distance between, canonical and changed-frame KC codes. An identical code would indicate engineered address invariance, not generalization across internal addresses.

**Sole primary interval:** for each science world, subtract the FE0 shifted-panel-A W−N four-fact accuracy gain from the candidate shifted-panel-A W−N gain. Compute the two-sided 95% Student-*t* interval across 32 paired worlds. If its lower bound is above zero, candidate shifted accuracy is at least 75%, and candidate canonical-A W accuracy is at least 75%, declare `E2_INCREMENT_DEMONSTRATED`; otherwise `E2_INCREMENT_NOT_DEMONSTRATED`. Zero variance makes interval-based inference unresolved. A direct candidate shifted W−N accuracy and the exact-frame positive control are reported irrespective of verdict. The reference FE0 shifted behavior must be measured here; the older two-letter, two-alternative Step-8 result is not a numerical comparator.

**E1 guards, reported separately:** for B and H, report each fact's W/N accuracy, the focal correct-class margin, and candidate−FE0 paired effects. `CROWDED_B_RESCUED` requires candidate B's `0+1=` and all other B facts to be correct in at least 24/32 worlds. `HELDOUT_SUFFIX_GUARD_PASS` requires each of H's four facts to be correct in at least 24/32 worlds. These are fixed descriptive gates, not separately tested claims. A combined `INPUT_BRIDGE_QUALIFIED` requires primary E2 and both E1 guards. A partial result is reported as partial; no gate is silently relaxed after seeing outcomes. Inference is limited to this checkpoint, encoder, byte frame, spacing variant and synthetic expressions.

An independent reducer must rebuild schedules and hashes, verify source lock and all world receipts, byte-derived teaching routes, writes, time, first response, class-value argmax, probe roster, exposure absence for the variant, paired interval and gates. Tampering with a cue hash, teacher route, native write count, E2 output, code digest or source digest must be rejected. No raw trajectory rerun is permitted for reporting.

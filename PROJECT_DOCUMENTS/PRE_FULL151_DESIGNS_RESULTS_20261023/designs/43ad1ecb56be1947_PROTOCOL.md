# Iteration 10: controlled KC subsampling

Frozen before running new learning outcomes. This experiment measures functional preservation under physical array reduction; it adds no plasticity rule and does not validate biological memory capacity.

## Construction

Reference: unchanged iteration5/bi through the parent RetentionLearner, with the iteration8 current_RBF64 readout head. No equal-allocation candidate, Oja term, stochastic write gate, replay, or meta-training is introduced. All 302 original PNs/88 PN-type channels, four effective teaching channels, four effective output channels, feedback, time constants, thresholds, and per-KC update equations remain fixed.

Two sampling families select 2,588, 1,294 or 647 of the 5,177 KC identities. Uniform sampling uses prefixes of one random permutation per sampling seed. Stratified sampling uses the 23 existing KC-type × side strata. Keep at least one cell in each nonempty stratum, then allocate remaining quotas by proportional largest remainders among remaining cells, with ties resolved by sorted stratum name. Within each stratum use a seeded random permutation. This deliberately overrepresents singleton strata; report it. Sizes share randomness but stratified quotas need not be nested. No redraw based on connectivity coverage, calibration success or task score. Missing output coverage is a recorded construction failure, not permission to replace a seed.

For each subset, construct two variants. RAW slices the parent's PN→KC matrix, output Q rows, teacher T rows and KC-side vector; it retains their values. CALIBRATED uses the same subset and only multiplies each of Q's four columns by the ratio of full-parent to reduced mean unadapted drive on the original 16 unlabeled development stimuli. This is a fixed four-gain calibration, not Oja normalization or task-outcome fitting. T and feedback stay unchanged. Zero calibration drive is an invalid calibrated construction and remains a failure. Preserve the original top-5%-per-side encoding rule, including its ceiling and input-reachability limits; do not silently hold the absolute active count fixed. Record active counts and effective input, output and teaching coverage.

Mutable fast/slow/adaptation arrays have the reduced N, with no hidden full-size dynamics or cue cache. State accounting is 32N+24 bytes; fixed matrices and readout storage are reported separately. Fixed PN-channel mapping stays at its parent size. The reader pools current reduced activity using the exact Q of that learner, then uses the unchanged RBF head and thresholds. It never accesses the removed KC states, cue identity, punishment, score labels or an oracle reset. The fitted four-input head is not retrained. Baseline extrapolation failure must be distinguished from actual-memory failure.

## Inputs and schedules

Screening crosses eight fixed sampling seeds with eight new shared input streams. Each stream has 96 association pairs and eight untrained probe pairs. All stimuli have eight active channels out of 88. Cues are unique and disjoint from all saved iteration9/prior inputs and confirmation inputs. Within each block of twelve associations, randomly choose six with A punished and six with B punished; the association stays fixed on repetitions. The role assignment is supplied only as experienced punishment during training and as an expected outcome to the evaluator. This removes the fixed A-role ordering of earlier primary panels, so use a newly run full-size reference on exactly the same histories. Prior all-A panels are regression fixtures, not the new baseline.

Train six 330-second A/rest/B/rest bouts per association. One main learner continues through cumulative loads 12,24,48,96 without readout probes entering its training stream. At each load, evaluate after 300 seconds and 24 hours. SNAPSHOT pairs are independent non-writing diagnostic branches. SERIAL branches execute actual forward A/B probes for all pairs, allowing readout exposures to update the learner. Branches do not feed another delay/load. Probe eight untrained pairs from the snapshot. An explicit no-learning learner receives the identical training stream; evaluate eight trained sentinel pairs and the eight untrained pairs at each delay/load to detect reader false alarms.

Separately train the first eight pairs in isolation, with both standard delays and age-matched rest for loads 24 and 96. These age controls match time since learning, not sensory state during acquisition. The iteration9 same-checkpoint no-write result remains prior evidence; this round does not repeat that intervention for every reduced model.

Revision has a separate actual-serial history: train four pairs, probe all four; train eight later pairs, probe the first four. Save the pre-reversal state. Reverse pair 0 for 0,6,12 bouts; record the diagnostic target contrast after every bout without writing those probes into the training stream. Evaluate target-first serial outcomes immediately and after REST padding to a common 12-bout endpoint plus 24 hours. Probe the other eleven associations for collateral effects. Training both cues remains counterbalanced according to their original association. Report unconditional reversals, old association survival, and the joint event OLD_CORRECT_BEFORE_AND_NEW_CORRECT_AFTER. A model cannot earn preservation by losing its old memory before revision. Also report performance on the fixed subset of streams where the full reference retains the old choice.

## Causal and implementation checks before outcomes

Require all-cell reconstruction to match original parent state and rates; cropped reader and unchanged head to reproduce parent scores; reduced save/resume to be exact; all arrays to obey declared sizes; gain calibration to use only the 16 development inputs. Check sampled IDs/types, input disjointness and model hashes. Test that current MBON/readout predictions cannot depend on current punishment before the update, while subsequent states can. Alter evaluator-only metadata without altering sensory/outcome history and require identical state/predictions. Stable random cue assignments are a positive learning control, not something that should collapse when labels change. For unpredictable independent balanced outcomes, score before revealing feedback and compare with chance by the exact binomial null. Controls may expose problems but do not trigger a rule/threshold change in this experiment.

## Screening and independent confirmation

Compare each family (method × size × gain) with its matched full reference. For each endpoint form an eight-draw × eight-stream matrix of differences. Independently resample both axes 2,000 times; use the two-sided 95% percentile interval. These crossed-bootstrap intervals are empirical uncertainty estimates for the sampled families, not biological confidence or a KC lower bound. No individual draw is selected by performance.

All preservation gates must pass: isolated 5-minute/24-hour choice and actual-memory accuracy; snapshot and serial 24-hour choice and actual-memory accuracy at loads 12/24; joint old-to-new 24-hour revision at doses 6/12; and non-target 24-hour choices at dose 12. Lower interval bounds must be at least −0.05 versus the parent. At both acceptance loads, untrained-pair decision-rate upper difference must be at most +0.05. The frozen observer must have pooled no-learning RMSE ≤1 Hz and single/pair false-positive fractions ≤0.05. For each behavioral endpoint at least seven of eight draws must have a mean difference within its same 0.05 margin. At least four reference streams must retain the old association before reversal; otherwise that gate is inconclusive. Missing/invalid constructions fail the family, never disappear from its denominator. Stress loads 48/96, margins and generalization magnitudes are reported but do not choose thresholds or candidates.

Screening is descriptive across twelve families. If any passes every gate, select the smallest N; tie-break RAW before CALIBRATED, then STRATIFIED before UNIFORM. Freeze that selection before evaluating eight previously unused sampling seeds crossed with eight unused input streams. Only this one family receives confirmation, using identical gates and no retuning. If none passes, report no qualifying compression and do not search another family or mechanism. If confirmation fails, do not switch to another candidate. Even a confirmed result is preservation of this limited engineered reference, not a validated biological core. The parent remains scientifically preferred unless independently justified beyond these tests.

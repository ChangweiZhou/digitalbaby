# Step 9: calibrated single-cue emitted output on never-taught relations

## Question and claim boundary

Can the **unchanged Step 8** fading-pair input, two native Full151 stores, external Boolean teacher, and training-only scalar output baseline use earlier teaching to emit `0` or `1` for a never-reinforced V9.8/V9.9A relation instance after 24 h, disjoint intervening learning, and another 24 h? The primary result is a world-paired final held-out W−N difference. A positive result supports only **limited E3 transfer within this fixed synthetic symbol-class family, at a fixed response point with an external teacher**. It does not demonstrate addition, multi-byte generation, self-derived reward or a general persistent core. Step 7/8 worlds are not reused as trials.

## Fixed exposure, model and output

Inherit `make_e3(world)` exactly: six old symbols in randomized hidden P/N classes; six of nine P–N edges taught in both orientations (12 distinct 12-byte old cues, 144 records); three edges withheld in both orientations (six held-out cues); a 72-record cohort on disjoint symbols; fixed `!` outcome; 165 s records; and the original two 24-hour gaps. Every old symbol is taught in both byte positions. No held-out cue is ever taught. An exact-pair or whitespace-canonicalized pair table has no held-out answer, and the balanced labels make an output default worth 50%; a one-bit class rule *learned from old teaching* can solve this family and is a valid, narrow E3 explanation.

Keep the Step 8 two native stores, FE0-plus-fading-pair input, frozen predictor, target-neutral/opponent-aversive external teaching, W/N old-write flags and new-write flags unchanged. Each branch maintains its own FIFO of the latest 12 **unlabeled prefeedback training** differences `d=value_1−value_0`; its threshold `b` is their mean, initially zero. The response to one cue is byte `1` iff `d>b` (tie→`0`), emitted before outcome or feedback. Raw `d>0` is a shadow diagnostic. Probe clones carry the branch's frozen `b`; no probe cue, probe label, hidden class, or later feedback updates it. W and N see identical bytes, labels, timestamps and teaching calls; only W has old native writes enabled. Both write on the new cohort. At old-end and after day 1, probe old trained cues; at new-end/final also probe new trained cues. The six held-out cues are probed **only in the excluded technical world and confirmation worlds**, at old-end, old-day, new-end and final, before any feedback on them. The pilot never computes their output.

## Roster and feasibility gate

Technical world **12000** is excluded; pilot worlds **12001–12016** are excluded; confirmation worlds are exactly **12101–12164**. These were checked against existing receipt paths before execution. Lock this design, code, auditor, inherited sources/checkpoint and exact rosters before the technical world. The technical timeline verifies source hashes, exposure, model birth state, W/N byte/time parity, response-before-feedback, training FIFO, read-only probe, clock/write counts and no held-out leakage. An independent receipt auditor must pass its technical/tamper tests before any pilot. No completed world is rerun or replaced; a scientific exception writes `FAILURE.json` and stops.

Pilot continuation uses **only directly taught cues**, across 16 fresh worlds. All seven checks must pass: calibrated W old-end old-trained accuracy ≥80%; calibrated W final old-trained accuracy ≥70%; final calibrated W accuracy for each answer byte separately ≥60%; final calibrated W−N old-trained difference ≥10 percentage points; and calibrated W and N new-end new-trained accuracy each ≥65%. If any fails, stop without generating confirmation-world trajectories. Pilot held-out answers do not exist, cannot guide model selection, and never enter confirmation.

## Confirmation endpoint and acceptance

For each confirmation world, final held-out accuracy is the mean of **six independently emitted bytes**, three for each answer. The sole primary contrast is the world-paired calibrated `W−N` final held-out accuracy. Compute a two-sided 95% Student-*t* interval over the 64 world differences; its lower endpoint must be greater than zero and variance nonzero. Additional required guards: final held-out W accuracy ≥60%; W accuracy on each held-out answer class ≥55%; the seven pilot trained-cue guards recomputed on the 64 confirmation worlds; and the paired final old-trained W−N interval lower endpoint >0. If all hold, verdict `LIMITED_E3_CALIBRATED_OUTPUT_SUPPORTED`; otherwise `NOT_DEMONSTRATED`, naming every failed guard. Do not relax a guard after seeing data. Report raw-shadow output, all probe stages, class counts and per-world results descriptively; only the single final held-out W−N contrast is confirmatory. No multiple input arms or role-feature search are part of this experiment.

If a pilot trained-cue guard fails, E3 is **not tested**, not negative. If trained guards pass but the confirmation primary fails, this architecture has **not demonstrated E3** under this protocol; input/teacher/readout attribution remains unresolved. Even if confirmation passes, the engineered baseline and external teacher remain explicit limitations.

## Independent audit and resource boundary

Every world writes one compact atomic receipt with ordered training margins/baselines, per-cue values/output bytes, source hashes, times, write counts and state-nonmutation attestations. The independent reducer regenerates fixture rosters and exposure hashes, checks no held-out teaching, recomputes every baseline/output/metric/interval/verdict, and rejects changed or omitted records, teacher bytes, outputs, baseline, source digest and roster. It does not rerun learner trajectories. Source-locked pilot and confirmation worlds remain disjoint from all Step 7/8 worlds; `caffeinate` stays attached to the active runner. No raw trajectory CSV or copied checkpoint is produced.

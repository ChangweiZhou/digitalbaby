## Refinements required by the actual model

1. The parent slow coordinates are signed effective increments without per-synapse upper/lower clipping. Only pooled MBON outputs clip. Synaptic headroom/homeostatic exhaustion is therefore **not an observable in this model**. Receiver action caps are intervention constraints, not biological weight bounds. No Jiang/homeostasis license is derived from them.
2. The pooled alpha3 output is rank one within each hemisphere/route group. A cosine kernel on those pooled outputs is uninformative. Recover individual MBON14 partners from the same FlyWire 630 graph, verify exact sums against the unchanged interface, and retain hemisphere/KC mapping. No BANC/HB neuron join, no FlyWire version upgrade, no soma-distance proxy.
3. A single added coordinate changes total dose. Receiver labels instead use **feasible equal-mass exchanges** within one group, referenced to 90%-dose PROP. The coefficients are diagnostic; exact original-algebra clipping supplies labels even when a perturbation crosses a clipping boundary.
4. Excluding a receiver is mechanically infeasible when the remaining caps cannot carry the budget. Label that fact separately from minimax regret and restricted solver failure. An arbitrary optimum's allocated mass is never a safety label.
5. Old and reversed net scores on the same A/B pair are negatives. They cannot be simultaneous independent memories. Decompose effective expression into a passively decayed pre-reversal state and the actual subsequent write residual, using symmetric two-factor contributions through the original nonlinear output. This is not molecular trace measurement or selective erasure of a named memory.
6. Properly counted USE equals V71 H (actual raw slow write, then decay); discard the duplicate. ACT and decayed write-event COUNT are offline diagnostics only. No association-identity counter or new-trace candidate is needed.
7. Full dynamic trajectories cannot have identical raw writes and donors after receiver choices change feedback. If a candidate qualifies, freeze a separate fresh-stage implementation/protocol before opening the reserved bank: natural budgets measure the deployable policy; any shared-minimum-budget comparator is a coupled diagnostic, not a claim of autonomous exact-dose matching. V72's development result alone licenses neither promotion nor repeated-use safety.

## Receiver teacher and oracle opportunity

At same-dose PROP, current recipient additions a_i=M cap_i/sum(cap). To test recipient i, add delta=min(.1 M, .25*(cap_i-a_i), .25*sum_{j!=i}a_j) and remove equal mass from others proportional to their a_j. Signs are fixed. Singleton/no-room groups have no exchange label. Exact delayed probe differences produce per-unit harm. R is max harm/slack over PARENT-correct old pairs and both horizons, with slack=max(min(actual-1,reader-threshold),.05 Hz). D is max unnormalized harm across all old pairs/horizons. Target changes are also recorded. These are **exchange-relative risks conditional on the compensators**, not intrinsic receptor labels or whole-policy guarantees. Labels, old cues, margins and oracle constraints are evaluator-only.

Linear coefficients use the PROP clipping region and are compared with exact exchange changes. Record every boundary crossing/error; exact labels are used uniformly, with no effect-dependent teacher switch. Startup checks compare the accelerated algebra to independent cloned PortFly probes, including large shifts that cross clipping regions.

The oracle repeats the V71 fixed-region minimax safety problem at fraction .9 (same target/old guards and 1e-6 choice buffer). For each recipient whose removal leaves enough group capacity, solve with its coefficient zero. Retain base and exclusion statuses, constraints, nonlinear validation and objective regret. Separate required-by-capacity, base-unresolved, restricted-infeasible and rejected solutions. Full-domain infeasibility or true capacity is never inferred. Optional forced-use LPs are omitted. No averaging only successful solutions into learner performance.

Group opportunity records risk and neighborhood spread, mass share and availability of choices. Worst damage is nonlinear and cannot be uniquely apportioned additively to groups; no invented additive 'share of worst damage' is reported.

## Donor diagnostic

Remove 10% of each nonzero raw slow increment with no replacement, keeping everything else fixed. Record exact old-worst improvement per removed mass and target change at both horizons. Compare selected donors with remaining active coordinates; report selected-mass share with positive day benefit. This is explicitly a **dose-changing diagnostic**, not proof of donor-policy quality or a receiver control. It cannot promote a donor rule. Low positive-benefit share flags unresolved donor quality; ties at a max endpoint are not proof that a donor has no effect on other memories.

## Unconditional reversal block

For each original load/focal, run REV (original full history then 12 SPACE reversal bouts), OMIT_REV (repeat original history with only the focal's original writes omitted, preserving its sensory exposure; then same reversal schedule), and NO_REV_WRITE (full original history with matched reversal exposure/time and all corrective plasticity disabled). Omission changes downstream learning feedback, so it is focal-untrained within an otherwise replayed history, not globally naive or an isolated molecular deletion. Do not train to a newly selected criterion. Report all 32 load/history assignments and the original old-choice eligibility flag.

At bouts 0/1/6/12 and delays 0/300s/day, record reversed and old net scores/choices (explicit negatives), collateral old endpoints and realized raw mass. For each branch, passively decay its initial gamma-fast, alpha-fast and slow coordinates to the endpoint; subtract from live state to obtain realized subsequent-write residual. With the same endpoint adaptation, evaluate ZERO, PREFIX-only, UPDATES-only and TOTAL. Symmetric two-factor expression contributions must sum to TOTAL-ZERO exactly. A persistent negative prefix contribution versus weak positive updates identifies an expression-level tradeoff, not independently stored opposing associations. Report immediate-to-day success/loss transitions. No automatic teaching/homeostasis/compartment mechanism license follows from these measurements.

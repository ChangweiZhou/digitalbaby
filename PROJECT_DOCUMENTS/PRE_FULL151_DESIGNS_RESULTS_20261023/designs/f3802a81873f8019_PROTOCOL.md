## Repetition timing

Every pair keeps A30s/rest135s/B30s/rest135s. Challenge12bouts has either0 or900 additional rest seconds before bouts2..12. This adds11*900=9900s; the longest challenge lasts13860s. This is a defined between-bout schedule diagnostic, not a literal reproduction of Huang's within-bout ISI or proof of a universal15-minute optimum. Counts, cues and reinforcement are matched. LAST0/day probes use each arm's actual final bout; COMMON0/day pads the shorter schedule after training to13860s. Thus COMMON compares equal total elapsed time with different placement of rest; LAST compares equal time since final learning. Report both. No extra rest is added before the first or after the last bout, beyond the inherited135s trailing rest.

MIXED starts after COMMON and adds12 new associations,6bouts each at the inherited baseline cadence. This isolates challenge spacing; it does not change the subsequent training schedule. Matched no-write clones receive exactly the same sensory schedule and elapsed time with all plasticity off. Every probe is on an independent evaluator clone and never advances training.

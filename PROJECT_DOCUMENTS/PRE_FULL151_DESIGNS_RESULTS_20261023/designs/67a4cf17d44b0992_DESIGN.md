Inference: 5 primary / 45 dense / 6 sparse intervals, alpha .02/.02/.01. Equal fixed-stratum paired means and within-stratum Satterthwaite variance; 32 strata for high load, four for canonical. Observation: evaluator state is held outside the learner. Only cue activity, physical time, reinforcement and plastic flag enter the deployed transition.

## Refinements to the supplied suggestion

1. Distinguish P/R attribution from actual removal. For each source S and
   construction P, use background native−S, residual R=S−P, and exact two-player
   nonlinear Shapley attribution. Separately evaluate native−P versus native.
   Never sum individual nonlinear effects to infer joint removal: evaluate
   native−sum(P) afresh on all active cohorts.
2. Separate self, excluded designated target and each included other source.
   Foreign averages are descriptive; destination cells and tails remain saved.
3. Freeze both donor weightings. Event pooling weights each source mean by its
   event count. Source balancing gives each eligible source mean equal weight.
   Out-of-block prediction excludes the entire recipient block; its retained
   weights supply amplitude only. At K8, each fold has 49 donor sources.
4. Source groups for Phase A retain A6's role×context definition. For the
   upstream native subevents, use context×delivered role×punished/unpunished
   presentation throughout dx/u/w. This prevents mixing the two presentations
   of a pair when comparing their native teaching drives. No grouping is
   selected using outcomes.
5. Raw activity and synaptic writes have different units. Project donor dx into
   slow-write units with the recipient's recorded native teaching coefficient,
   actual allocation fraction and slow decay; project donor u with allocation
   and decay; donor w is already in slow units. No fitted scale or norm matching.
   This is conditional downstream transport, not an upstream causal intervention:
   it freezes recipient feedback/payload and allocation, and cannot by itself
   identify a defective module. Nonzero activity is not called harmful.
6. Use incoming delivered truth and protected informed/unsuperseded committed
   truth at A3 windows. Report incoming fact, other same-source facts, target,
   foreign cohorts and all scored facts separately. These window observations
   are not an independent all-life accuracy benchmark.

## Qualification versus science

Only source locks, index/clock alignment, state/probe invariance, reconstruction,
and algebraic closure gate execution. Numerical tolerance is
1024×float64 epsilon×max(1, relevant state/value scale). Unchanged operations
require exact equality. No accuracy, margin, harm-rate or cosine threshold gates
any arm or selects any predictor.

Blocks are the reporting units. Source/group/checkpoint/fact observations are
repeated diagnostic measurements. Donor sets overlap, and the worlds are reused
development worlds, so fold positivity is not independent confirmation.

Stop after the diagnostic. A signed average collateral component with individual
losses is not a safe online policy. A component useful to self and harmful to
foreign memories is a conflict, not removable noise. Do not add an intervention.

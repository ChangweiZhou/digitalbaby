## Factorial and controls

REF: original pooled slow state, fitted feedback. FB: pooled state, additional positive alpha-MBON→alpha-DAN feedback with strength lambda and the existing anatomical routing. Since fw0<0 this supplies negative feedback into alpha plasticity. RES: partner-pair slow state, individual MBON algebraic output bounds and actual individual feedback routing, fitted near-zero feedback strength. COUPLED: RES plus strengthened feedback. This is a factor of **resolved expression and teaching access together**, not the number of stored variables alone.

For each side, cell weights a_j equal that cell's share of total KC synapses. Individual Q_ij equals its unpooled count times the inherited side gain, divided by a_j. Weighted summed *unclipped* currents recover the pooled current for tied states. Each actual cell clips at the inherited effective alpha rate bounds before weighted behavior pooling. Gamma and alpha-fast states remain per KC; only slow state is represented at pairs. No new firing-rate time constant or persistent neuron activity is invented. No reader refit or gain fit to task outcomes.

Let p_ij=C_ij/sum_j C_ij. Pair teaching routes are E_ijd=T_id*D_dj/(sum_j p_ij D_dj), using measured DAN→MBON counts D and the existing DAN→KC routes T. Thus sum_j p_ij E_ijd=T_id exactly, allowing a redundant repooling recovery control. This is a local count-product **hypothesis**; neuron-level DAN→KC/MBON edges do not establish dopamine exposure at a particular KC→MBON synapse. Feedback R_jd is normalized MBON→DAN count by DAN. There remain only two shared alpha teaching channels; no claim of four independent teaching signals.

REPOOL is checked as a recovery control: untied pair variables and E, but immediately collapse them with p_ij, preserve pooled bounds and original feedback. Its projected dynamics must recover REF without outcomes being used to force agreement. A tied-state instantaneous-current check separately verifies the representation algebra. Neither recovery control is counted as a biological discovery or a main behavioral candidate.

SLOW_P/SLOW_R use the same strengthened feedback but replace its source with clipped expression of baseline+slow state and naive gamma contribution. The behavior readout remains the full live state. These privileged diagnostics are never promotion candidates, regardless of results. MAP_LEFT/MAP_RIGHT/MAP_BOTH permute the two individual MBON identities within the corresponding hemisphere in both feedback and DAN→MBON matrices, leaving KC→MBON counts fixed and recomputing E. They preserve hemisphere/type and count multisets, not every triple's degree. These three nonidentity permutations exhaust the two-by-two within-side mappings.

## One gain, chosen without task labels

Use the original16 unlabeled interface calibration patterns. Compute the spectral norm of the additional one-event feedback Jacobian on total alpha current, including the fast and slow update fractions, at dt30s and unadapted KC activity, ignoring clipping. Include pooled and resolved operators. Lambda is .1 divided by the largest norm. All candidate/diagnostic/mapping arms share this value. This is a conservative reference-scale calibration, not a universal stability theorem for the full nonlinear coupled circuit or arbitrary time steps.

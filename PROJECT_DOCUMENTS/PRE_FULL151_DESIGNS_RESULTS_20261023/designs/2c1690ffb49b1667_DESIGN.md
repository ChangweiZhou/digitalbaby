# Byte arithmetic evolution V2 — large, mechanism-stratified search

## Objective and hard information boundary

Test whether a population of small MiniFly-derived learners can acquire useful four-operation arithmetic behavior **within each life** from ASCII bytes and only consequences of their own first answers. This is a staged engineering stress test, not a biological claim that flies perform arithmetic. The genotype may specify anatomy, temporal circuitry, and plasticity; learned synaptic state is reset between independent lives and never inherited. Test answers, operands, parsed roles, signed errors, and correct output bytes stay in the evaluator.

The V1 6×2 pilot was only an interface/architecture screen. Its finalist scored 2/128 exact on untouched single-operation questions after 512 training examples, below a constant-zero control's 7/128. The flat 257-action head and a tiny fixed-reservoir pilot do not warrant being the sole search space. V2 searches several **computational families** distilled from the project's 56-entry mechanism catalog rather than using 56 independent toggles or rerunning V1 for eight hours.

## Objective-contract audit and claim boundary

The primary endpoint is **E3 transfer within this synthetic arithmetic family** only if prior within-life consequence learning improves the *first* answers to new expressions over matched no-write and zero-shot lives. The full canonical expression scored in a life, including every spacing variant of that expression, is not reinforced in that life. Individual operands, operators, subexpressions, and operator-order templates may recur; the operation rules and the general ASCII vocabulary are shared. A passing Stage 1 alone is narrower than a passing mixed-operation Stage 3. No delay or intervening-learning probe is in this search, so even a full pass would not establish the project's persistent-learning-core goal. A failed run establishes only an E0 interface/architecture boundary for the tested families.

An exact raw-byte table and a whitespace-canonicalized expression table have no lookup hit on the prespecified novel tests. Operand frequencies, answer priors, and an evolved innate calculator could still help without learning: constant-zero, zero-shot, and same-exposure/no-write arms quantify these shortcuts. The latter receives the same training bytes and consequences but no consequence-dependent weight change. The evaluator-only symbolic parser is a positive task-solvability check, never a candidate. If a finalist passes the first gates, a grade-shuffled same-histogram arm is required to attribute the effect to consequence contingency rather than feedback exposure or write dose. State that survives across questions is the candidate's learned head and plastic recurrent connections; expression-local state resets only on an actually observed separator. The scored answer comes from this new head, not the inherited Full_151 value writer.

The promotion thresholds, failures, eight-hour ceiling, state/byte-exposure accounting, and a small four-family preflight precede the broad search. Only a later experiment with delay, intervening learning, and an appropriate causal state control could advance an E3 result toward a persistent-core claim.

## Experience and consequence

Each question appears as a visible one-byte separator `\n`, sequential ASCII digits/operators/spaces, then `=`. The model may reset an expression-local slot when it **observes** the separator; no operand array, operator ID, expression AST, numeric answer, or right/wrong direction is passed. It emits one integer in the fixed range −128…128. The wrapper writes the integer as signed decimal bytes for scoring; autonomous output-byte generation remains a separate milestone.

The training consequence is exact `0` for a correct answer, otherwise one of five negative grades from absolute error: 1–8→−1, 9–24→−2, 25–48→−3, 49–96→−4, ≥97→−5. These bands were chosen before V2 search because V1's narrow grades saturated near −5 on about 69% of a fixed-reservoir diagnostic. Only the unsigned grade reaches the learner. A candidate may internally reason about the two symmetric answer intervals implied by its own action and the grade. It receives no answer, signed displacement, teacher-forced retry, or correction. Evaluation queries are read-only and have no consequence until after the first answer has been scored; no test grade is fed back.

## Curriculum and elimination

Stage 1: balanced unseen `a op b` questions across `+ − × ÷`, with one- or two-digit operands and exact nontrivial division. Stage 2: three operands and two distinct operations, all 12 ordered operator pairs, standard precedence. Stage 3: five operands and each of the four operations once, all 24 operator orders, exact integer division and bounded signed result. The canonical expression used in a life may never appear in that life's training and novel-test sets simultaneously, even under different spacing. The task generator is the frozen V1 benchmark; V2 changes only the visible prefix and feedback bands.

Use **1,024 individuals ×128 generations** (131,072 candidate evaluations), four workers, and an eight-hour resumable ceiling. A four-worker preflight measured approximately 10.2/11.6/7.2 candidate evaluations per second on Stages 1/2/3, with peak worker memory below 240 MB; the full run leaves margin for thermal and source-check overhead. Reserve 256 slots per architecture family; selection is within families, and a failed individual cannot become a parent. All individuals in one generation see matched fresh development worlds. At least two development lives, 128 feedback questions and 64 disjoint novel queries per life, determine ranking. The q90 absolute-error death threshold tightens across Stage 1 from 128→64→32, across Stage 2 from 32→16, and across Stage 3 from 16→8. If a family has no survivors, its slots are filled by fresh pre-seeded immigrants; this event is recorded as extinction, not called evolutionary improvement. The three stages receive 39/38/51 generations; later stages can be explored even when an earlier exact gate failed, but **no candidate is promoted unless all earlier and current sealed gates pass**.

Fitness combines exact novel accuracy, within-life causal gain against the stronger of same-genome no-write and zero-shot, and continuous absolute-error scores. The death threshold is separate from exact competence: merely predicting near the common answer does not count as learning. A candidate cannot be promoted unless sealed confirmation shows Stage 1 ≥50% exact overall and ≥25% for each operation, Stage 2 ≥40% exact, Stage 3 ≥50% exact and ≥25% for each of 24 operator-order strata, each with at least +10 percentage-point gain over **both** the matched no-write and zero-shot arms. The q90 criterion must also pass at each stage's final threshold. These are engineering gates fixed before the large run; failure is reported without post hoc relaxation.

## Search grammar and controls

The four family slots are: (1) a flat Full_151-KC-based answer head retained as a negative anchor; (2) explicit byte-driven recurrent/slot state with a numeric readout; (3) compartmental fast/slow and eligibility/resource allocation; (4) equal-state-budget output-address/topology variants. Exact genotype fields and construction are locked with code after a timing preflight. Every family sees the same byte tasks and grade rule. The frozen inherited FE0→PN→KC path is hashed and reported separately from newly engineered readout, working state, and plasticity. A topology variant must alter actual routing of KC/working-state contacts at fixed size, and be compared with a matched address shuffle; merely renaming KC indices is not a structural result.

The earlier 56 mechanisms are grouped by computational role: temporal eligibility; shared/private memory; representation and topology; local resource/fast–slow commitment; and teaching/error quality. Sleep, individual molecular labels, and unlimited banks are outside this bounded test. A mechanism may enter the genotype only if its state, locality and information inputs can be enumerated and if an equal-resource control is available. A positive new head is not retroactively credited to the native Full_151 value writer.

Record zero-shot, no-write matched exposure, constant-answer, exact-expression cache and a clearly labeled hand-built symbolic upper bound. If a candidate appears positive, add a grade-shuffled same-dose lesion and a temporal-state lesion before calling the gain causal. The symbolic upper bound contains parser and arithmetic primitives; its success is not MiniFly success. Expressions lengthening probes **within-expression working memory**, whereas gain after earlier feedback probes **across-episode learning**. Neither alone proves durable retention; a later interruption/24-hour retention assay is required before calling the core persistent.

## Reproducibility and compactness

Preflight checks source and parent-data hashes, grade boundaries, byte order and visible delimiter, train/test expression exclusion, no answer access in learner APIs, read-only probes, deterministic generation, no-write parity, mechanism-resource ledger, topology budget matching, survivor-only reproduction and resume consistency. Lock source, config and a never-used confirmation roster before search. Keep only compact per-genome metrics/receipts during the run, no per-question trajectories or copies of inherited data. The final result bundle contains `REPORT.md`, final metrics/acceptance tables, model/config, tests, and a manifest pointing to unchanged parent assets; target below 10 MB. If eight hours expire, preserve resumable state and report the actual completed exposure, never a projected result.

## 1. Scientific scope and evidence already available

LOCAL realized-write regressions attain held-out R² 0.783, while the declared GLOBAL regressions attain 0.002. The latter target signed E and cannot exclude magnitude-dependent variance or negative-tail risk. The four-focal ordinary-stream counts are 48/63 with later writing versus 60/63 without; they are not all-association capacity counts. Reversal after six bouts gives 4/62 durable joint successes and twelve bouts gives 52/62 under the V66 checkpoint protocol.

These findings motivate investigating formation of a write, not another model-family search. Focal identities, omit-history footprints, selected old-memory directions and an observer's interpretation are evaluator information. A diagnostic using them is not a deployed conflict detector. No claim here transfers the older V50 byte-task result unchanged into the current MiniFly architecture.

## 2. A precise intervention at the teaching calculation

Use unchanged encoder, anatomical/effective Q, T, F, MM, k0, input channels, adaptation, state size, timing and observer. No source edits to the frozen parent. Any future instrumented wrapper must reproduce the parent exactly when the intervention is disabled.

For one presentation let d be the **same bout-effective KC activity used by the existing kernel**, q = dᵀQ, g the stored gamma-fast vector, f the alpha-fast vector, and s the alpha-slow vector. Indices G and A select the two gamma and two alpha columns. Write the current MBON computations as:

```
rG(g)    = clip_G(k0[3] qG + QGᵀ(d ⊙ g))
rA(g,a)  = clip_A(k0[5] qA + QAᵀ(d ⊙ a) + rG(g) MM)
a        = f + s
p(g,a)   = fw0 [kD ⊙ q + rG(g) FG + rA(g,a) FA] + fwd shock u
clip_G   = [-35.2, 36.46]; clip_A = [-11.2, 19.96]
```

The notation expresses the implemented operations, not a new physiological equation. Retain the original summation order in the intact code path so equality can be exact. Evaluate four port payloads from the **same instantaneous state**, same d and same punishment u:

| Arm | Payload sent to plasticity | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| INTACT | p(g, f+s) | Frozen parent |
| G0 | p(0, f+s) | Remove gamma-history contribution from the teacher, including its route through alpha output |
| A0 | p(g, 0) | Remove own alpha fast/slow-history contribution; gamma-mediated alpha input remains |
| GA0 | p(0, 0) | Remove both historical contributions at the teacher |

Zero arguments are **read-only counterfactual inputs to the feedback calculation**. They do not overwrite the real g/f/s arrays, reset adaptation or replace the actual MBON expression path. Naive sensory feedback remains: rG(0) and rA(0,0) are not generally zero. The existing `feedback_block` sets gamma rows of F to zero and removes naive as well as learned feedback, so it is not an implementation of G0.

At a common pre-update state, actual MBON outputs must be identical across these port settings. Subsequent outputs may differ because legitimate updates cause trajectories to diverge. Log counterfactual DAN drive and the actual payload as separately named quantities; do not conflate an engineered teaching-port manipulation with a measured neuronal firing-rate intervention.

For a six-bout branch, recompute its counterfactual using **that branch's current state at every step**. A pre-recorded intact teacher trajectory replayed into the branch would be a different experiment. Keep that distinction explicit.

Record the instantaneous interaction `p(g,a) - p(0,a) - p(g,0) + p(0,0)`. Clipping prevents assuming an additive decomposition. The total block effect also includes within-block feedback and is not the sum of same-state instantaneous contrasts. A direct-port-only gamma substitution may be calculated as a numerical audit, but is not another campaign arm.

A0 is not an exact-zero test. The per-step payload change due to own alpha history is bounded componentwise by `|fw0| × 31.16 × sum_alpha |F[alpha,j]|`, since clipping limits alpha output differences. Check this bound plus floating-point tolerance at a common state. Propagated multi-bout differences need their own analysis and cannot be forced to the one-step bound. Retain tiny MM/FA gains; do not round them away to manufacture a null.

## 4. Stage F: prospective formation and useful-learning tradeoff

Propose **16 new ordinary streams**, seeds 6701001–6701016, each containing 96 six-bout associations with four initial focals. All new cue supports must be globally disjoint from the V66 bank and its prior-exclusion chain. Counterbalance orientation and preserve every stream/focal, including initial failures. No adversarial candidate enrichment in the primary panel.

The INTACT path is the sole main trajectory. Before each association 5–96, fork all four port arms plus NO-WRITE and a fixed HALF-WRITE arm (multiply every new raw plastic increment by 0.5; preserve passive decay and adaptation). All dynamic branches receive the same six bouts and exposure cost. They do not replace the main trajectory. Probe clones at horizons 0 and 86,400 seconds.

For each branch, measure:

- The new association's absolute two-cue responses, reader and actual margins, joint choice, and **gain versus matched NO-WRITE**. A new cue can already have a preference through generalization; final correctness alone is not evidence of acquiring it now.
- E and D for the four initial focals, with eligibility fixed at the common checkpoint. Include descriptive later-memory probes at the distinct prior-association indices nearest one-third, two-thirds and the most recent position. Do not let their varying counts change the primary four-focal weighting.
- Raw pre-decay increments, teacher contributions, component/state norms, adaptation, clipping, and both old cue responses. A difference score alone can conceal suppression of both cues.
- All learned associations on the main INTACT path at loads 12, 24, 48 and 96, using snapshot probes only. These characterize the new input panel, not an end-to-end intervention candidate.

Primary causal contrast: `mean[D_INTACT − D_G0]` for eligible initial focals at one day, averaged within stream before computing the interval. Report new-association gain and choice differences alongside it. A0, GA0, interactions, alternate ages and immediate outcomes are secondary. Positive primary contrast means G0 reduces negative disturbance, not automatically that it improves a usable learner.

Avoid conditioning pair selection on post-intervention acquisition strength. Such selection could change the tested population. Instead show acquisition–damage coordinates at equal exposure and the fixed HALF-WRITE control. A diagnostic route is promising only if protection is not purchased with a comparable acquisition deficit.

Add one **endpoint slow-norm-matched rescaling sham** for G0, with no extra learning trajectory. From the same block's NO-WRITE endpoint N, define ΔI=state(INTACT)−state(N), ΔG=state(G0)−state(N), and `c=||ΔG_slow||₂/||ΔI_slow||₂`. Construct state(N)+cΔI for the plastic arrays only, retaining N's matched adaptation/counters. Record c. If the denominator is zero and the numerator is not, report unmatched; use c=1 when both are zero. c>1 is amplification, not attenuation. This sham exactly matches the **endpoint slow norm**, not all compartment budgets or acquisition, and is an evaluator-only directional diagnostic. It is not a deployed controller. Compare its old/new effects to G0; do not present it as a feedback-formation trajectory.

Use 2,000 whole-stream bootstrap resamples. Ordinary errors remain clustered across events and focals. The planned sample size is bounded for feasibility, not a guarantee of decisive noninferiority. Inconclusive intervals lead to an inconclusive disposition, not an automatic increase in seeds.

Suggested gate to justify considering the route pilot: lower 95% interval for damage reduction >0, point reduction at least 20% relative to INTACT mean D, lower interval for new-association mean gain difference above −0.25 Hz and joint new-choice difference above −5 percentage points. The primary route under consideration is prespecified G0; do not switch to whichever of four arms looks best. These are provisional engineering thresholds to freeze before execution. Directional advantage over the norm sham and HALF-WRITE is separately required before describing protection as more than weakened writing.

## 5. Stage L: a finite test of information available at a modeled coordinate

Reuse the 16 Stage-F four-association checkpoints; this is not another independent confirmation set. For every focal, create four matched 12-bout schedules from the identical parent state:

1. TRUE REVERSAL: that old pair receives the opposite contingency throughout.
2. SINGLE ANOMALY: the same opposite contingency in bout 1, followed by the old contingency in bouts 2–12. This is one anomalous complete A/B bout (two presentations), not a single anomalous shock pulse.
3. STABLE REEXPOSURE: the old pair and old contingency throughout.
4. UNRELATED REPETITION: a predetermined fresh pair and its fixed contingency throughout; associate one of the first four later pairs with each focal, without outcome-based selection.

Timing and number of presentations match. Old-pair odor exposure cannot match the unrelated-pair arm by definition; report the distinction. All repeated unrelated episodes remain in the panel, whether or not they damage a particular focal. A harmful subset can be identified for a secondary analysis by the held-out old-memory outcome, but cannot determine data generation or be passed as a feature.

TRUE REVERSAL and SINGLE ANOMALY have **identical causal histories through bout 1**. Their internal states and feature vectors must match exactly until their first different observed consequence. An early perfect classifier would indicate label/schedule leakage. Evaluate evidence accumulation at equal bout counts and elapsed times; never provide the label, bout index, future schedule or final outcome as input. No method can infer the future difference from that identical prefix.

Primary allowed feature tier for a modeled alpha coordinate: its current effective KC activity, adaptation, pre-write alpha-fast and alpha-slow state, actual routed alpha teaching scalar, proposed raw alpha increment, and fixed sign products among those quantities. These are available in the current simulator, not proven colocated biological measurements. Do not use KC identity or a high-dimensional routing fingerprint to memorize cue identities. A separately labelled secondary tier may add the modeled KC's gamma trace and the actually delivered gamma teaching scalar; biological access across compartments would require justification.

Forbidden inputs: old-cue code vectors or IDs, lists of protected memories, omitted-history footprints, observer scores, future effects, global arrays of all KC states, elapsed association index, and condition labels. Evaluator labels are permitted solely to assess the diagnostic. At the coordinate level, do not feed a population-wide feature summary to a supposedly local classifier.

Use one fixed regularized logistic diagnostic per declared binary contrast and feature tier, with no model search. Train and standardize within held-in streams; equalize stream, episode and bout contributions so thousands of correlated KC rows do not create false precision. Primary comparisons are reversal versus unrelated repetition and reversal versus single anomaly after their histories diverge. Report held-out AUROC, balanced accuracy, calibration and first-bout leakage checks. Chance performance only rejects the tested representation/classifier; it is not a theorem that all local information is insufficient.

Any trace-gating pilot requires both meaningful held-out separation after repeated evidence (proposed mean AUROC ≥0.75 with lower interval >0.5) and inspection showing the proposed simple sign rule addresses destructive cases without also unlocking repeated unrelated writes indiscriminately. High classification accuracy alone does not select a controller. If these conditions fail, stop this branch rather than adding features or thresholds until it passes.

# Full151 Step 8 — training-only output-baseline diagnostic (draft)

**Status:** design proposal, not frozen; no code or trajectories have been started. Step 7 remains source-locked and stopped at its failed feasibility gate. This document does not reinterpret its held-out outputs.

## Decision this experiment serves

Step 7 used two native Full151 stores and emitted `1` when `d = value_1 - value_0 > 0`, otherwise `0`. It scored 62/96 on the 12 old, directly taught cues across eight excluded pilot worlds, below its frozen 70% guard. The original receipt nevertheless ranks the positive orientation above its reversed taught orientation in all 48/48 pairs. Centering `d` using the **entire batch of 12 test cues** raises the old-end count to 87/96, but is an evaluator-side, post-hoc oracle and cannot be a model output. These facts motivate one fresh test of whether a causal, learner-local threshold can recover reliable one-cue output. They do not prove that it can.

The sole scientific level of this diagnostic is **E1 (trained association)**. It tests the output decision interface under the existing 12-cue load, delay, and later learning. It does not score a never-taught pair, claim E3 transfer, establish an autonomous teacher, or settle arithmetic capacity.

## Frozen variables and single intervention proposed

Use the same V9.8 `make_e3` relation generator, 12 old taught cues and 144 old records, 72 disjoint new records, bytes, record order, simulated days, FE0-plus-fading-pair frontend, two copied native Full151 stores, opponent teaching, and W/N write flags as Step 7. Check the project roster, then name and source-lock the **exact fresh, nonoverlapping engineering and 32 diagnostic world IDs before any new behavior is inspected**. The external Boolean label continues to route teaching; it does not enter the sensory stream or the baseline calculation.

Add one scalar output baseline to each branch. For every training record, immediately after the cue and **before outcome or teaching**, read the two native values and form `d = value_1 - value_0`. Compute both shadow outputs from the same state: raw `1[d > 0]` and calibrated `1[d > b]`, with ties emitting `0`. Only after the response, append this unlabeled prefeedback `d` to a 12-item FIFO. Set `b` to the arithmetic mean of the available FIFO values; initialize `b=0`. No label, correctness bit, outcome byte, cue identity, phase name, or probe score may update `b`. In the inherited generator each old 12-record bout includes every taught cue once; after the new cohort, the trailing 12 records include two complete six-cue bouts. This is one frozen candidate rule, not a parameter search.

At old-end and old-day, probe only the 12 directly taught old cues. At new-end and final, probe those 12 old cues and the six directly taught new cues. Do not calculate held-out outputs in this diagnostic. Copy the current learner and scalar baseline at each stage; each cue must be presented and emitted **independently**. Freeze `b` throughout the probe stage; neither the first nor any later probe cue may update it. Raw and calibrated outputs read exactly the same native state. Keep the no-old-write N branch and let its baseline evolve from its own prefeedback margins under the identical algorithm. Record a compact per-training-record prefeedback-margin ledger, plus per-probe values, predecision `b`, both output bytes, true label for scoring, and native-state digests. The original teacher and all native write calls must be byte-for-byte and time-for-time unchanged by the comparator.

## Predeclared evaluation proposed for the executable protocol

Run one excluded technical world to verify source/checkpoint hashes, exposure and time parity, native-state equality, no label/test leakage into `b`, FIFO update order, probe nonmutation, and write counts. Then run **32 fresh diagnostic worlds** with no adaptive world replacement or parameter tuning. Because this is a short diagnostic, do not add an outcome-driven eight-world pilot. The independent reducer uses worlds, not cue rows, as the inference units.

Primary diagnostic: paired, per-world difference in **final old-taught accuracy**, calibrated minus raw, with a two-sided 95% Student-*t* interval. For a positive localization result, require its lower endpoint above zero, calibrated W old-end accuracy at least 80%, calibrated W final old-taught accuracy at least 70%, the lower endpoint of the paired final calibrated W−N old-taught interval above zero, and at least 65% calibrated accuracy on the six new taught cues in both W and N at new-end. Report old-day and new-end old-taught accuracy, both output labels separately, W/N and raw/calibrated results at every stage regardless of verdict. These proposed numerical guards must be frozen before any fresh behavior is inspected; they are diagnostic headroom criteria, not a change to Step 7's 70% rule.

If the result fails, state exactly which guard failed. A failed online baseline candidate does **not** prove the input code or Full151 store is at fault; it closes this particular output rule. If it passes, the result supports a calibrated-output E1 operating point, **not** retrospective Step 7 success. Only then consider a separately source-locked E3 assay on fresh worlds, with its own W/N control, held-out exposure audit, first-answer-before-feedback rule, delayed/intervening-learning endpoint, and independently reviewed gate. The E3 assay must identify the added baseline as engineered output state.

## Audit and scope boundaries

- Reject any baseline update from a test cue, test-batch mean, expected answer, hidden class, branch peer, or later feedback. Retain the ordered prefeedback-margin ledger so an independent reducer can recompute every `b`.
- Reject any change to sensory bytes, timestamps, native teaching, predictor policy, or native states between raw and calibrated shadow decisions. Reject changed/omitted events and mismatched source hashes.
- Do not infer transfer from exact trained cues. A raw exact-key or canonicalized-key table can solve this E1 task; its purpose is only to qualify the integrated readout under realistic load.
- Do not run a 2×2×3 frontend/teacher/load search inside this experiment. The proposed factorial changes at least two mechanisms and, for 4/8/12 cues, changes graph connectivity, teaching dose, and duration unless separately controlled. It is optional future mechanism work, not an automatic continuation.
- Do not require a 55-fact, ten-output arithmetic capacity test before this synthetic-relation E3 question. That is a distinct E1 program. Pair upweighting and new role features are hypotheses, not established remedies; the Step 7 held-out outcomes were not opened for scientific interpretation.

This is a deliberately bounded answer to one question: **did the fixed two-store zero-threshold output rule hide usable trained-cue information?** If it passes, write a separate fresh E3 protocol before any transfer test. If it fails, close this online-baseline candidate and report what remains unresolved; any further mechanism work requires a separately justified, bounded question. Do not silently grow another open-ended repair chain.

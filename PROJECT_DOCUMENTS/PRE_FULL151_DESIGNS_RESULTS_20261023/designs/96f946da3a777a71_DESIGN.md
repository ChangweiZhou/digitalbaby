# MiniFly V9.10E — replay and geometry audit of the V9.10D failure

**Prospective diagnostic design, 26 September 2026.** This is a post hoc mechanism localization on V9.10D's already-known worlds, not a new E3 confirmation or a new candidate learner. It freezes V9.10D's byte/KC encoder, head update, nine-symbol cyclic fixture, teaching order, and 64 worlds 2101–2164. Nothing in the parent result is edited. A separate engineering world 9907 checks implementation; the eight previous pilot worlds 2051–2058 are used only for technical replay and scale inspection. No model or regularizer changes after seeing these results.

## Question and exposure boundary

V9.10D showed 97.57% accuracy on taught pairs but 51.04% on never-reinforced cyclic pairs. The objective here is to separate (i) a formal teaching-span obstruction, (ii) weak shared geometry or an unsuitable linear inductive bias, and (iii) failure of the particular online NLMS dynamics. E0 mathematical/implementation diagnostics and descriptive analysis of the failed E3 task are permitted; this run cannot promote an E3/E4 learning claim. The actual head still sees only the 288 presented old byte records and their feedback. Neither withheld cue, withheld label, nor hidden class enters its update. The probes may use hidden labels strictly after training to score or form an oracle reachability check; those probes are never counted as fly behavior.

The old fixture's exact/canonical pair table ties at 50% on withheld pairs; an arbitrary learned scalar per-symbol potential is bounded by 6/9 on three withheld directed triangles. An analytic three-class decoder from taught labels can solve all nine, but is not installed. The primary issue is why the fixed online head fails despite this task-level identifiability. The no-old-write behavioral control and first-response result remain the frozen V9.10D record; replay must match it exactly before any new calculation is accepted.

## Frozen calculations

Replay a single native-no-value-write sensory branch and the unchanged V9.10C head on each old stream. Capture its **actual pre-outcome `pending_x`** at each of 288 teaching calls, its corresponding target `y`, pre-update residual, normalization coefficient, and the final head vector. Keep these in RAM only. At old-end, query the held-out and trained cue codes read-only in the same sensory prestate. For each oriented withheld relation, define `r = x_neutral - x_aversive` at that fixed endpoint. For the three true cycles, `c_i` is the sum of their three `r` vectors. The exact identities `w_T = Σ_t α_t ε_t x_t` and `r·w_T = Σ_t α_t ε_t(r·x_t)` must hold to numerical tolerance. These are **trajectory accounting**, not independent causal effects of deleting individual writes. A changing query code may not be inserted into this fixed-endpoint sum.

Let `U = span{x_t}`. Use the Gram eigenspectrum with a prespecified relative cutoff of `1e-10` of its largest eigenvalue to measure the projection ratios `||P_U r||/||r||` and `||P_U c_i||/||c_i||`; repeat at `1e-8` and `1e-12` as numerical sensitivity. Report ranks and ratios continuously. If a cycle vector is orthogonal to `U`, the frozen head cannot score all three edges of that cycle strictly correctly; a nonzero projection is **not sufficient** for learning. A held-out-label oracle linear-feasibility problem inside `U` is an additional leaky *reachability* check only: can some vector in the teaching span orient all nine endpoints? It is not trained model behavior and says nothing about whether available teaching information selects that vector.

One fixed train-label-only batch probe uses precisely the actual 288 teaching `x_t,y_t` pairs and no held-out labels. Normalize all `x_t` by their RMS L2 norm, then solve ridge with dimensionless `λ=0.1`; score its read-only old-end trained and withheld pairs. This estimator and λ are locked before science-world inspection. Its success would show that these stream features contain useful shared geometry under one fixed estimator, not that the online head learned it. Its failure is not a general impossibility theorem. As a cheap secondary arithmetic ablation, compute `w_label = Σ_t α_t y_t x_t` (the same per-record normalizer without prediction correction) on the exact stream. Report its choices and norm relative to NLMS; do not interpret a performance difference as isolated correction necessity because effective update magnitudes differ. An unpaired-label arm is omitted: the intact head's V9.10D causal contrast was already indistinguishable from zero, leaving no positive effect for that arm to explain.

## Acceptance and interpretation

Before full replay, engineering/pilot checks require source hashes, no withheld exposure, exact identity and replay equality to V9.10D's frozen old-end canonical margins for both head and native-no-write value, read-only queries, finite geometry, valid ridge solution, and unchanged record count/clocks. Then process all 64 existing worlds without outcome-based stopping or parameter selection. Store only world-level geometry/probe metrics, exact-check tolerances, aggregated estimates, script/design/report, and a manifest of unchanged parent assets; never store per-record vectors or duplicate parent files. Target bundle <10 MB.

Interpretation is conditional. If the oracle span is infeasible or cycle projections are numerically zero, a formal reachability obstruction is plausible at the current endpoint. If oracle span is feasible but fixed ridge and NLMS both fail, the available features have not yielded transfer under these two biases; representation sharing versus inductive bias remains unresolved. If fixed ridge transfers but NLMS does not, next test online update dynamics (including prediction correction and dose) on **fresh locked worlds**. Any modified representation or mechanism also requires new worlds for behavioral confirmation. V9.10E itself supplies no fresh promotion evidence.

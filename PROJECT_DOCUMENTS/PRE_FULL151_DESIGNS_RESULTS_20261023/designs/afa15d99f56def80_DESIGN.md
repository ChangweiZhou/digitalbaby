# W4 — byte arithmetic with writable MiniFly circuitry

**Status (26 September 2026): design revision, not a launched experiment.** W4 supersedes W3 for future work; W3, V2 and their reports remain unchanged. W4 is not authorized for a long population run until the interface, causal-writer and integrated-cost gates below have passed. Its immediate implementable unit is a small interface/feasibility pilot. The user's objective, not convenience of attribution, governs candidate inclusion: evolve a small fly-derived learner that uses earlier byte experience to answer new arithmetic expressions with few examples, first with answer-revealing warmup and then with graded punishment. More operations, longer expressions and stricter elimination follow measured progress. The answer to a scored question is emitted before feedback.

## What W3 got wrong

W3 copied V2's frozen FE0→PN→KC sensor into every arm, froze all Full151 native writes, and searched mainly a new AdamW answer decoder. This was a useful engineered control but an invalid restriction on the fly candidates. It also made a random-key memorization test a universal entrance exam, used across-life marginal operator medians that a population of specialists could pass, left the teacher sensory event potentially decorative, and distorted the hard-division answer distribution. A second audit and Claude's audit document these defects. W4 retains frozen-native and raw-byte learners as **controls**, while native and newly added KC-local plasticity are explicitly eligible in the candidate population. A positive engineered-only result is an engineering result, not evidence that Full151 learned arithmetic.

## Claims, exposure and progression

This is a staged test of a *single living candidate*, not a majority vote across specialists. Stage A asks for first-response transfer on two-operand expressions containing any of `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, with the **same individual** meeting all four operation floors. A held-out unordered operand pair is absent from all teaching and grading under **every** operator; the same individual may still have seen each operand with other partners. An exact expression cache, whitespace-normalizing cache and reversed-pair cache therefore have no scored hit. An acquired arithmetic or other compact rule can legitimately earn E3 in this named task family if it beats matched no-prior-teaching and noncompositional controls. This does not by itself establish the persistent core.

Stage A has three distinct endpoints: (1) answer-warmup acquisition on held-out pairs; (2) extra learning from five-band grades after answers disappear, versus a frozen and a shuffled-grade continuation; (3) delayed use after time and a genuinely learned, disjoint intervening cohort. The last endpoint is required before a persistent-within-family claim. The separate long-term objective remains mixed expressions: after Stage A passes, Stage B uses three operands/two operations and Stage C five operands/all four operations. Each needs a **new locked task support and final roster**, a per-individual all-pattern gate, sample-efficiency curve and independent delay/interference test. Stage A alone never counts as mixed-arithmetic success; failure or success must not silently redirect the project to a recitation benchmark.

The instruction stream consists of one byte at a time: newline, expression ASCII, then `=`. Newline may clear short sensory and recurrent working state but not learned synapses, native fast/slow memory, optimizer state, or an explicitly evolved persistent slot. The model emits its own canonical signed decimal answer bytes and `;` before any result is revealed; the permitted integer range is -256…256 and at most five output bytes including `;` may be emitted. Invalid, unterminated or out-of-range output is incorrect and gets grade -5. The output grammar constrains syntax only, not operands, operation or answer. The model receives no parsed numbers, AST, signed error, hidden correct value or test-time answer. Short separators are an explicit Stage-A scaffold, not a claim of natural unsegmented text reading.

During the first 384 training questions (96/operator, interleaved), after the first answer the environment delivers the five-band consequence and then the correct answer as visible bytes. During the next 128 questions (32/operator), it delivers the consequence only. Let `e=|own integer answer - correct integer answer|`; grade is 0 for e=0, -1 for 1–8, -2 for 9–24, -3 for 25–48, -4 for 49–96 and -5 for e≥97 or invalid output. The exact post-choice feedback transcript is `!d;` with one ASCII digit `d=-grade` in 0…5, followed only in warmup by `#<correct ASCII>;`. The grade is a permitted punishment event of that same magnitude, delivered **once after the terminal `;` of `!d;` has arrived**; before that byte there is no grade or native write. New circuits may also read the visible grade bytes, but a scalar punishment event must be reported as such, not called byte-only grade interpretation. Correct-answer bytes are never supplied outside warmup. The environment computes these events only after recording the first answer. Read-only probes receive neither event.

One external byte interval is `Δ=30/14` simulated seconds in the primary protocol. The emitted answer consumes exactly five output slots: early `;` is followed by silent padding, and output bytes are not fed into FE0 in the baseline. The grade transcript consumes three input slots; after its terminal byte the action-conditioned native adapter writes the **cached pre-feedback** eligibility code, advancing Full151 time exactly once for the pending interval. The answer-reveal transcript occupies six input slots including silent padding; grade-only histories also spend six silent slots, so feedback removal does not change elapsed time. A new output-reafference or timing policy is a separately matched topology family, not a hidden difference between lesions. Fixed external timing keeps answer length from changing native plasticity dose. FE0 at 1 s/byte remains a historical control, never silently mixed with the primary clock.

There are two kinds of paired comparison. In a **mechanism replay**, a recorded reference action, grade, teacher bytes and clock are fed identically to writer-on/off clones; this isolates a write/read path but is off-policy for a clone that would have chosen differently. In **autonomous behavioral lives**, each arm emits its own first answer and receives its own on-policy grade; the question schedule, correct-answer bytes and random-number schedule are paired, but realized grade bytes and dose may differ and are reported. The true-versus-permuted-answer comparison is run as a separate **answer-only** diagnostic with no grade in either branch, avoiding a wrong teacher contradicting a true grade. A no-answer, on-policy-grade branch isolates the marginal contribution of answer reveal in the primary combined protocol. A no-prior-write replay, with identical earlier sensory and outcome history but **all** candidate cross-question writes off (native, KC-local, engineered predictor, decoder and optimizer moments), is the E3 causal state control at held-out probes. Time-dependent decay/adaptation may continue identically. The replay is a counterfactual mechanism control; autonomous on-policy controls are reported separately.

Answer-revealing warmup is a deliberately privileged bootstrap authorized for this experiment; it is not a claim that real flies are told correct arithmetic answers. The grade-only continuation is the first test of learning without that privilege. A rule inferred from the emitted answer and the *unsigned* grade is permitted. For example, a separate engineered control may update toward the set of all answers consistent with the five-band error interval; it must preserve the two possible error directions and may not receive the actual answer. This control is not attributed to the native Full151 writer.

## Which synapses can change

The inherited Full151 **native value writer is enabled in primary fly candidate families**, with a matched `write=False` lesion. Its state must actually enter the answer-selection computation. Calling `byte(..., learn=True)` is insufficient: in the present V9 wrapper that flag updates a separate engineered byte predictor, while the native writer is called by `teach`. The existing `teach` accepts only binary 0/1 and writes the KC pattern immediately preceding an outcome byte. It cannot, by itself, assign credit to the chosen numeric answer or bind a later teacher string to the earlier question. W4 therefore treats the following as versioned engineering additions, with their own tests and resource accounting:

- **Action-conditioned native credit.** Before feedback, retain an eligibility code made only from the question KC state and the fly's *own proposed answer bytes*. A source-locked, arithmetic-free action encoder must produce exactly the same code for a candidate string before choice and for those bytes if actually emitted. For a native-scored candidate set, compute every `value_at(code(question, candidate))` read-only from the same native state and time, then add `gain × value` to the candidate's other answer score; gain is a bounded nonnegative gene, with zero the readout lesion. The grammar may enumerate valid output strings but supplies no arithmetic answers. Once the fly chooses a string it emits precisely those bytes. The chosen code is cached until the completed grade event, when the new adapter writes it using the inherited plastic substrate. The mapping from own bytes to code may be fixed or genetically altered, but contains no correct-answer lookup or parser. Both `value_at` and the event adapter are **new action-conditioned Full151 extensions**, not existing `teach` functionality. The adapter must consume exactly the still-pending clock interval, not call `rest` and then advance the same interval again. Legacy parity on an unmodified KC code, finite/nonnegative/sparse codes, candidate-code collision rates, clone-safe same-time reads, and a constructed writer→later-choice effect removed by a readout lesion are Gate-0 checks. If the adapter fails, native action-credit remains **unresolved**, not enabled by a cosmetic flag.
- **Punishment dose.** The legacy native wrapper has only 0/1 outcome. The first native arm uses one binary event: 1 for any error and 0 for exact, with `write=False` as the true no-write control (`r=0, write=True` is still a write). A proposed graded-native arm may map severity to `p=-grade/5` in the lower-level event API, but that is a **new adapter and a new dose hypothesis**. It needs boundary, monotonicity, clone, clock and dose-yoked controls before joining a search. All five grades remain visible to eligible new plastic circuits regardless of whether the inherited native writer uses their magnitudes.
- **KC-local answer plasticity.** A fly candidate may add bounded, writable KC→answer contacts, compartment routing, eligibility traces, working-state cells or output sites. It can learn from the observed answer bytes or grade, but the source of each synaptic update must be traced to sensory activations, the candidate's pre-choice/own-action eligibility and the visible feedback. The primary fly arm cannot take raw answer token IDs directly as a hidden supervised target while calling the observed `#answer;` event its teacher. A separate raw-byte/AdamW model may do that as an explicit engineering benchmark. Any KC teacher decoder or byte prototypes trained before arithmetic must be fixed, counted and validated on independent byte streams; they may encode byte identity but no arithmetic answers. Hold raw target IDs constant while lesioning teacher-sensory activity in the engineering arm, and hold all visible bytes/timing fixed while lesioning the KC teacher route in fly arms. If a lesion does nothing, report that the route contributed nothing.

Use a small factorial attribution matrix on the *same candidate topology and history*: native writer on/off × KC-local teacher update on/off. Add the raw-only/AdamW benchmark and KC-only/raw+KC input-route controls at matched trainable-state budgets. Lesion the native **readout** separately from the native write to distinguish a written but unused memory. A candidate whose own learned KC-local synapses pass without inherited native contribution is a MiniFly-derived engineering result; do not credit it to the inherited writer. Fixed and mutable bytes, active KC count, time policy and topology edges are reported separately. Before any population run, cap **new mutable state at 16 MB per individual** (the unchanged inherited state is itemized separately); match engineered controls to this ceiling and use a low-rank full-KC readout when needed rather than silently pooling away cells. FE0 at 1 s/byte with 128-bin pooling is retained as a historical control, not the sole fly path. Test full-KC and LAT4-style order-preserving sensory variants under a fixed byte clock, plus matched parameter/state budgets. LAT4's prior two-byte result does not prove it can read multi-digit arithmetic; interface tests must decide that.

## Fixture and recitation controls

`W4-HARD1` uses two decimal operands in 0…99, one of four operator bytes, exact integer division with quotient at least 2, and outputs within -256…256. A scored hard question has distinct operands both at least 2. The 0…99 operands widen exact-division support; the ±256 output range widens valid products. Both changes keep operand spellings at two digits or fewer and the longest signed answer at four digits/sign bytes. The *primary* exclusion unit is the unordered operand pair across **all** operators and all training phases. Ordered expression and exact byte spelling are also recorded; reversing subtraction changes the answer and reversing division may be invalid. Within a life, every scored pair is new, although its individual operand symbols have appeared. Train, development, qualification and final rosters must pass a source-locked disjointness checker before learning.

Reserve one global qualification roster of 16 hard questions/operator and a separate, one-use final roster of 32/operator, all with different operand pairs across all operators. For division, use 10/6 low (quotient 2–9)/high (≥10) in qualification and 20/12 in final; these approximate the widened hard support's 170/106 split rather than W3's forced ≥50% high quotient. No quotient may occur more than eight times in a division roster. Report the two quotient strata separately; the aggregate is primary and the high-quotient stratum is a named extrapolation diagnostic. Subtraction rosters and training are sign-balanced by orientation. Development questions are 16/operator/life; training has 96 answer-revealing and 32 grade-only/operator/life, with at least 64 and 24 hard multiplication/division questions respectively. The global scored pairs are blocked from training under every operator. The support witness in `fixture_probe.py` constructs eight feasible life draws at these counts and was checked over 100 consecutive random seeds; it is **not** a sealed roster and is not evidence of learning. Its hard supports are 441 multiplication and 276 division pair groups. Training rosters and repeated evolutionary lives therefore still reuse finite mathematical facts; new seeds are independent learner histories, not new arithmetic facts. A later confirmation must widen the domain or use a prospectively untouched support.

Score constant answer, operator-conditioned answer mode, raw/canonical/reversed exact caches, mode plus pair symmetry, operand-marginal, raw-byte nearest-neighbor and grade-free controls trained on precisely the available exposures. A symbolic solver and parsed-number regression are privileged task-validity/upper controls, not candidate competitors. Register the shortcut implementations and fixed tie-breaking name order before qualification. For each life, choose the strongest nonsymbolic shortcut by **total exact count on that life's fixed roster**, breaking ties by lower q90 absolute error and then the name order; choosing the comparator this way can only raise the bar for the candidate, not train it. A shortcut that crosses a proposed promotion gate invalidates that gate. Report exact first response, per-operator exact, mean/q90 absolute error, invalid rate, and the earliest answer-revelation count at which the same life reaches 25% then 50% held-out exact with all four operator floors. Score checkpoints at 0, 32, 96, 192 and 384 revealed answers, and report the trapezoidal area under the held-out paired-gain curve, divided by 384, as a sample-efficiency measure (paired gain is true-teaching exact fraction minus no-prior-write exact fraction at each checkpoint); all controls use identical items. Invalid outputs receive an absolute-error sentinel of 513. At each checkpoint, score each held-out item from its own full-state clone at one common time, discard that clone, and verify the live training-state hash is unchanged. A sequential no-feedback test stream is a separate context-sensitivity diagnostic, not the primary score. A qualification/final file hash and sealed-access log prevent the search process from viewing either roster before its allowed stage; this is a procedural firewall, not cryptographic secrecy.

## Gates, causality and persistence

**Gate 0 — interface, not arbitrary-memory selection.** Test byte-stream chunk parity; all grade-band boundaries; complete state clone/resume; no test-side write; clock and FE0 reset semantics; pre-choice invariance to future feedback; teacher-sensory dependence; and native action-conditioned read/write/lesion. For the native adapter, require legacy `teach` parity on the unmodified KC code (fast, slow, adaptation, elapsed time, pending state and value); no write before a complete `!d;`; exactly one write after it; no second advancement of the pending time interval; equal clocks/adaptation in external-history writer-on/off replay; `value_at` read-only and candidate-order invariant; selected and subsequently emitted answer strings producing identical action codes; and a constructed writer→later-choice effect abolished by the native-read lesion. An independent online byte-recording table should learn a randomized key→two-digit-answer mapping from post-choice answer bytes, proving the harness can carry information. This E1 table test is **not** an entrance exam for every arithmetic candidate: a rule learner need not memorize arbitrary keys. Each proposed candidate must pass its own stated update/lesion tests and demonstrate nonzero within-life train-set response to its designated feedback, without accessing a future byte.

**Gate 1 — tiny arithmetic efficacy and fitness reliability.** Before any population search, run a few source-locked candidate topologies and the raw benchmark on development-only worlds. Measure training exact, held-out exact by operator, combined warmup versus no-prior-write replay, combined warmup versus on-policy no-answer/grade-only lives, separate answer-only true-versus-permuted teaching, true-grade versus shuffled/frozen continuation, and candidate-same-genome variation across lives. Include a specifically tested native-on and native-off pair. If no candidate shows a reproducible held-out *causal* signal, improve the input or credit interface on development data; do not spend the search budget. Do not demand W3's 37.5% exact from an engineering pilot, and do not infer an upper bound from the older GRU's 5.5–10.9% on a different fixture. For an initial reliability check, score at least 16 pilot genotypes on two independent batches of four lives and calculate Spearman rank correlation of their paired-gain scores. If correlation is below 0.3 or the bootstrap interval includes zero, use a replicated small grid or more lives rather than truncation selection on noise. This decision concerns search efficiency, not scientific acceptance.

**Gate 2 — bounded evolution only after Gate 1.** The initial population is diverse; after that, every offspring descends from surviving parents through logged mutation/crossover, with no extinction-triggered random replacement. Genomes may alter internal sensory time constants/front-end (never the externally fixed byte interval), KC→output routing, eligibility/dose rule, recurrent/working-state topology, and memory allocation inside declared size and time budgets; they may not encode scored answers or a parser. Maintain native-writable and engineered comparison strata so a cheap raw decoder cannot erase the scientific comparison. Re-evaluate parent and offspring on two fresh matched development lives each generation, then re-evaluate the provisional top quarter **and every incumbent parent** on four further matched lives. Only those six-life candidates can become parents. For parent selection, use the lexicographic tuple `(mean final paired exact gain - standard error across the six lives, mean worst-operator exact, mean area under the paired-gain learning curve, -mean q90 error)`; all terms and tie-breaking seed are fixed before search. This is a ranking score, not a confidence interval or acceptance statistic. After a candidate meets an absolute development gate, prefer fewer answer revelations to that gate among otherwise qualifying candidates; before that, retain relative-best parents without resetting an extinct family. Record lineage, realized writes, independent-life variance, selection score and peak memory. Four workers are permitted only after an integrated full-life benchmark proves RAM/thermal safety on this Mac. Population × generations is determined from measured p95 *complete* life cost, rather than imported from V2; cap a single run at eight hours and checkpoint for resume. No qualification/final items or their performance may enter evolution.

**Gate 3 — qualification and one-use confirmation.** Freeze the model, genotype, training schedule, interface, shortcut choice, roster hashes and all thresholds before viewing qualification. Qualification may be inspected once; failure returns to a newly named development version and consumes that roster. The final roster is used once on eight fresh lives with the same items but independent training order and initial trainable state. At the post-warmup checkpoint, at least **six of eight individual lives** must *each* reach 64/128 exact, at least 8/32 on **every** operator, and at least 13/128 more exact than each same-history no-prior-write replay and the strongest registered shortcut; median q90 absolute error over those eight lives must be ≤32. The same-life conjunction, rather than four separate medians, is the survival condition. Qualification uses five lives and a scaled 64-item version: at least four lives must each reach 24/64 overall, 4/16 per operator and 8/64 causal gain over the same-history no-prior-write replay and shortcut. The on-policy no-answer/grade-only and answer-only true-versus-permuted controls are separately reported, not mislabeled as identical-history comparators to the combined protocol. Report paired life-level and item-level uncertainty; eight lives reusing one final roster are not eight independent samples of mathematical facts. No passing result can be attributed to native Full151 unless both native-write and native-read lesions, on the same histories and matched outcome dose, each lower exact performance by at least 4/128 in at least six of the eight lives. Report the continuous paired effects and uncertainty even if this attribution guard fails; the four-item threshold is a decision convention, not a biological effect size.

**Gate 4 — grades, delay and interference.** From each post-warmup full-state clone, continue with 128 grade-only questions in frozen, true-grade and exogenously grade-shuffled arms on matched visible inputs. Require the *same* qualifying lives to retain their four-operation floor. Claim grade-only incremental learning only if at least six of eight lives each gain at least 7/128 exact over **both** its frozen and exogenously grade-shuffled forks and gain at least 1/32 on every operator over the frozen fork. The exogenous shuffle permutes the **indices** of the reference trajectory's grades within each operator with a preregistered seed, preserving the histogram; it does not require every grade value to change. Try at most 1,000 seeded index permutations and choose the first changing at least 25% of grade values, or the one changing most if none reaches that level. The yoke is deliberately off-policy and its total/grade-band dose is reported. If fewer than 25% change even under that search (including an all-one-grade history), the shuffled control is uninformative and the grade-only learning claim is **inconclusive**, not passed or failed by a fabricated contrast. For the persistence endpoint, compare old-teaching write-on versus no-old-write branches under the same externally replayed old bytes/actions/feedback, then wait 24 simulated hours. Give **both** branches the same disjoint 72-question new cohort (18/operator), replaying one reference action/grade/teacher transcript with new writes enabled in both; verify acquisition of that cohort in both against their new-write-off twins. Wait another 24 simulated hours and re-probe the old never-reinforced pairs with no feedback. An autonomous on-policy replay is separately reported because its outcome histories may diverge. This deliberately keeps grade-only learning and delayed interference as separate causal questions. A persistent-within-family claim requires, in at least six of eight lives, an old-teaching versus same-history no-old-write advantage of at least 6/128 on the delayed old held-out roster, no negative paired advantage on any operator, and verified new-cohort acquisition of at least 8/72 exact over its no-write twin. These numeric floors are decision conventions. Freeze the cohort roster before qualification; do not call mere post-warmup retention grade-only learning.

Progression to mixed expressions is conditional on the *individual* Stage-A result, but it is not optional if the project claims four-operation mixed competence. The next design must reserve new supports and set progressively tighter error tolerance while increasing length; it cannot use this final roster again. A Stage-A failure localizes the bottleneck; an engineered-only Stage-A pass does not turn into a MiniFly-core claim.

## Implementation and release boundary

Build and inspect only the fixture checker, byte transcript API, native action-credit adapter, exact feedback-flow tests and a handful of development pilot lives first. The inherited source/checkpoint stay immutable. A test must show that `write=False` matches all timing/exposure while stopping native writes; `r=0, write=True` is not a no-write control. Native and engineered state plus RNG must clone identically; a read-only probe must leave the live state hash unchanged. Verify output truncation and invalid-answer scoring at every possible 5-byte boundary. Audit actual FE0/KC teacher-byte information under the selected clock, and record per-life computation/memory before choosing a population.

The compact eventual bundle contains the report, design, source/config, final aggregate tables, acceptance/audit checks, key figures and a manifest of unchanged parent assets; target under 10 MB. It does not duplicate source datasets, archived runs, caches or raw per-seed trajectories. **No W4 learner or evolution result exists at this revision.**

## Evidence anchors for this revision

The inherited writer's behavioral relevance is not hypothetical: [V9.9A](../BYTE_CORE_V9_9A/REPORT.md) found a +17.19 percentage-point old-teaching advantage on never-taught symbol combinations after delay and intervening learning, and [V9.9B](../BYTE_CORE_V9_9B_RANK/REPORT.md) found +9.57 points in a second, still narrow synthetic relation. Neither demonstrates numerical operations. [The V2 arithmetic run](../BYTE_ARITH_EVOLUTION_V2/runs/full_v2/REPORT.md) failed its own grade-only gates, and [the warmup preflight](../BYTE_ARITH_WARMUP_PREFLIGHT_20260926/REPORT.md) showed rough magnitude learning without dependable exact answers. [The order-preserving LAT4 report](../BYTE_READ_ORDER_ITER2_20260925/REPORT.md) supports a sensory comparison, but only on a two-byte order task. These are the reasons to test writable native credit, observed teacher-byte access and sample-efficient transfer separately rather than assuming one successful component solves the others.

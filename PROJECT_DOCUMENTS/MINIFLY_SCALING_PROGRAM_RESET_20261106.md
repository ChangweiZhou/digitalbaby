# A controlled scaling program for MiniFly reasoning

**Research memo · November 6, 2026**

MiniFly has measured how larger stores retain facts and how more varied experience improves prediction. It still lacks a comparison that identifies which combination of model capacity, experience quantity, and experience diversity improves reasoning. Earlier runs used different learners, interfaces, and outcomes. The next program should keep one learner family and one evaluation fixed long enough to measure how those three scaling choices interact. ## Where the program lost continuity

**Memory scaling produced a working reference.** V197 completed its planned width comparison. At 4,096 facts, doubling the store raised oldest-fact recall from **64.6% to 73.7%** and doubled the float32 readout from **64 to 128 MiB**. The component carried into the byte-interface V201 at the 1,024-fact scale, where it ran with explicit frame routing. The research question shifted from how far memory capacity scales to whether components could coexist in one byte-processing loop. V201 established that integration under supplied routing. The record contains neither a broader V197 width sweep nor an explicit decision to retire that sweep. [V197 results](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/e79732d38830b7796d3449db9e7c370ea0661c4e/minifly_v197_long_life_factual_memory/RESULTS.json) · [V201 integration](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/tree/e79732d38830b7796d3449db9e7c370ea0661c4e/minifly_v201_end_to_end_byte_interface)

**V216 crossed experience quantity and diversity at one model size.** Each of eight independent families compared four versus 32 environment instances at 1,000, 10,000, and 100,000 events per learner. The 100,000-event campaign passed its registered follow-up gate. The design called for a separately authorized million-event continuation with the same core and new target families. The subsequent assessment recommended continuing, and the published record ends before that continuation. The **1.6 million** reported events are the sum across 16 lives. [V216 design](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/74d1f1544ae1d0c540cfa44f89bd0f865f6f2a1e/minifly_v216_experience_pretraining_design_20261104/DESIGN.md#L26) · [V216 decision](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/74d1f1544ae1d0c540cfa44f89bd0f865f6f2a1e/minifly_v216_experience_pretraining_design_20261104/campaign_20261104/SUMMARY.json) · [Follow-up assessment](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/2750e76f2632d45c1faa22b372c23f047a5ba7a6/PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_V216_V218_CROSS_BRANCH_ASSESSMENT_20261105.md#L35)

**The portfolio changed both the learner and the test.** V193 demonstrated reuse of a learned successor representation under supplied operand positions and time strides. Its frozen readout answered **191/192** changed held queries after clean observations, versus **3/192** for the fully frozen arm. V223 asked a different question using a direct-byte GRU and arithmetic. At the final dose, its forward learner answered **20/192** taught facts and **7/1,536** held sums. Its factual gate kept the retention and correction stages unopened. These studies locate different strengths and bottlenecks. Their scores do not form a shared scaling curve. [V193 source and results](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/tree/e79732d38830b7796d3449db9e7c370ea0661c4e/minifly_v193_reusable_relational_operator) · [V223 report](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/e79732d38830b7796d3449db9e7c370ea0661c4e/minifly_v223_learnable_recurrent_reference/OFFICIAL_REPORT.md)

The more diverse stream improved complete first responses at both increases in dose. The less diverse stream peaked at 10,000. On paired questions, the learned high-diversity arm got one pair right at the middle dose and zero at the final dose. This corrects the shorthand that its paired score was zero throughout. More importantly, an evaluator-side lookup indexed by a few hand-selected fields solved **128/128 pairs** at 10,000 and 100,000 events in both diversity arms. Exact whole-prompt lookup scored zero. The difference shows that fresh surface forms and changed answers within a pair still left previously seen cases recoverable through a canonical key. A later gain on this panel would be useful evidence of prediction or format transfer. A reasoning claim needs a panel that defeats this shortcut. ## How to compare scaling combinations

1. **Qualify one reasoning benchmark before choosing the winning settings.** Hold out whole underlying combinations, including their equivalent forms under a canonical key. Make the observed history sufficient to derive each held answer. Verify that a rule-based evaluator can solve the task and that normalized lookup, recency, and frequency leave measurable room. Balance the counterfactual pairs so a fixed answer fails. Use V193 as a positive reference for the kind of reuse to measure. Its supplied operands and strides belong in the reference’s evidence record.
2. **Cross capacity, dose, and diversity in one learner family.** Start with two model sizes, two environment counts, and two experience checkpoints. Four continuous training runs per paired world provide the eight cells if the shorter dose is a true prefix of the longer dose. Vary one declared size dimension while preserving the input, output, and learning rule. Match streams across sizes and exposures across diversity settings. Fix the evaluation and the held families before training. Profile the full roster before choosing doses and the number of fresh families.
3. **Rank settings by reasoning and report the cost.** Score complete generated answers with the learner’s own termination and the fraction of counterfactual pairs with both answers correct. Report taught recall separately. Compare paired families at equal dose and show the performance achieved for actual compute and memory. A useful combination must improve the held endpoints above the applicable shortcuts. If the model arms remain at the floor, the matrix has found a limitation and supplies no winning configuration. If they separate, confirm the selected combination on fresh families before a larger run.

## Which question each follow-up should answer

The original V216 million-event continuation remains an open question about **experience scaling on the same learner and task**. It can test whether the more diverse stream’s first-response gains continue. Retain the original protocol and baselines to keep that comparison interpretable. The proposed V223 repeated-versus-varied filler test remains a separate **acquisition diagnostic**. The crossed reasoning experiment is the test that would choose among scaling combinations. Each line should carry a recorded decision with its source, planned next comparison, and status so that a completed run, a deferred continuation, and a scientific stop remain distinguishable.

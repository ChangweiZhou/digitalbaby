# MiniFly progress survey and next experiments

10 November 2026

The next experiments should locate where one learner loses the ability to acquire, retain, or revise a relationship. MiniFly has positive results for each of several component skills. They were obtained on different architectures, tasks, and feedback budgets. The goal is a single bounded learner that acquires relationships from its observations and uses them on unseen cases after intervening learning and changes in the environment.

## What the experiments established

| Line of work | Result and scope |
|---|---|
| Native Full151 and R_center | R_center reached 97.1% taught OLD recall and had a negative causal effect on held Latin completion. V9.9 had narrow transfer on a learned one-bit relation. COV-R's bounded addition used supplied role, magnitude, and ordinal features. These results give us distinct memory and transfer baselines. |
| Experience and action | V216 improved complete first responses from 6/256 to 25/256 but scored 0/128 on both-correct counterfactuals. V218 learned from consequences and reached 99.41% in greedy evaluation. A small observed-transition table reached 100%. |
| Temporal credit | E18's sign-feedback initialization phase reached 94–96% of the backprop reference. E22's rule-trained initialization and later learning cleared all three gates in three paired seeds under answer supervision and replay. Unrehearsed development skill fell from about 90–95% to 29–36%. E19's forward-only gradient calculation passed its equivalence check and its sign-rule implementation missed the learning threshold in every seed. |
| Composition and retention | E20-A's windowed backprop reference scored 21–43% both-correct on twins with an operand out of view. The corrected E16 hard-pair gate passed on only 2 of 15 post-selection seeds despite stronger family averages. Under the tested zero-replay schedule, matched backprop also ended near 1% on held plus-minus pairs. |

## E20-B and Target Test Step 0

**E20-B** compares windowless recurrent backprop architectures on the registered arithmetic composition task. The seven-amendment audit was filed in `522174b`. At `9031eff` the coordinator reproduced the inherited-source findings, dry-ran the bank2 comparator, and added amendments 8–16. Stage 1 covers implementation and reduced-dose qualification. The runner still needs to demonstrate matched birth and all-seven-checkpoint life parity, scorer and architecture fixtures, and birth provenance. Stage 2 awaits review of that record and coordinator authorization. The amended selection rule requires both whole-answer marks in the same two or more seeds, prioritizing G3 if it qualifies. The G3 comparison measures adding a 32,768-weight feedback matrix as a package. The registered decoder reprocesses the prefix for each generated byte, so it provides development feasibility evidence under that evaluator. [Filed audit](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/522174bbb95e69fd3e53c000471c0290055f51c3/PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_E20B_AMENDMENTS_AUDIT_20261110.md) · [Second amendments](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/9031effb07875f456d71e87e3fb7f4e65421b1b5/OPUS_TASK_E20_COMPOSITION_FROM_MEMORY_20261110.md#L99-L166) · [Coordinator update](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/9031effb07875f456d71e87e3fb7f4e65421b1b5/PROGRAM_STATE_20261107.md)

**Target Test Step 0** is a separate generator and evaluator task for a continuing learner. It uses held context–cue pairs with an identical recent suffix and distinct earlier contexts that imply different answers. A twin passes when the learner produces both complete answers including EOS, before either reveal. Step 0 can proceed independently of E20-B qualification. It has two concrete design corrections:

1. **Reserve a permanent test pool before generating the stream.** The proposed 216 taught and 72 eventually revealed pairs exhaust all 288 pairs per family. An illustrative split is 216 taught, 48 first-encounter pairs across the three phases, and 24 permanently sealed. Qualify the actual split by checking that the teaching graph determines every held answer at the relevant phase. Track distinct semantic pairs separately from repeated filler renderings. Score the two members of each sealed twin from equivalent copies of the same pre-probe state.
2. **Qualify visibility with the same rule-aware evaluator under two input conditions.** A 40-byte window exposes the context to a lookup table and still leaves the held mapping absent. Give the rule-aware evaluator the complete episode for its positive control. Restrict it to the shared suffix for the visibility control, under which a deterministic response can match at most one member. Keep trained exact-key and cue-recency tables as separate memorization controls. Audit whether the no-prior-teaching control can infer the answer through another family that shares context values.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Use completed E20 checkpoints to diagnose composition.** Compare the first intermediate, the full autonomous answer, and a diagnostic continuation supplied with the true intermediate. Compare ordinary generation with recurrent state reset just before the shared suffix. An intermediate rescue locates a useful intervention point. Loss of an existing twin advantage after reset measures sensitivity to the earlier state. Keep both probes separate from the registered autonomous score.
2. **Qualify the target task with a reference before comparing learning rules.** After Step 0 passes, run acquisition only with one recurrent backprop reference and visible versus hidden versions of the same held cases. A visible-context miss directs work toward teaching, optimization, or the interface. A hidden-only miss directs work toward maintaining and using earlier context. Once both pass and the prior-teaching control distinguishes them, compare the accepted sign sweep on matched architecture, observations, and budget. Add interference and revision to the same learner state after acquisition is established. A causal held-out reuse claim needs the exposure audit and prior-teaching control. [Objective contract](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/522174bbb95e69fd3e53c000471c0290055f51c3/MINIFLY_OBJECTIVE_CONTRACT.md)
3. **Measure retention cost from the same acquired checkpoint.** Keep fresh observations and their order fixed and add rehearsal as extra updates, with a matched additional-update control for causal attribution. Record retention, new acquisition, revision, supervised bytes, stored bytes, and update and scoring CPU. Then a single slow-weight or consolidation candidate can be tested for a specific improvement over the measured replay cost curve. [Retention plan](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/522174bbb95e69fd3e53c000471c0290055f51c3/PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MINIFLY_REVIEW_20261109/NEXT_STEPS_NOTE_20261110.md#L58-L78)
4. **Test the every-byte failure with one controlled change.** Change filler predictability while matching lengths, byte frequencies, answers, and target count. Compare sign feedback with backprop on the same architecture. The prior systematic-drift prediction missed, so any rescue would support the effect of the tested filler structure and guide the next feedback experiment. [Every-byte result and drift check](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/522174bbb95e69fd3e53c000471c0290055f51c3/PROGRAM_STATE_20261107.md#L239-L245)

Start with the saved-checkpoint probes and the Step 0 corrections while E20-B qualifies. Keep the native store for a later matched test with a clear job, such as remembering a once-seen fact through intervening learning. Compare it with a simple index at the same information, storage, and compute budget.

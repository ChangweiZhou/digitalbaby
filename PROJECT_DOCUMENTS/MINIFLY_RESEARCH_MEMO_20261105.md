# MiniFly research findings through V223

**Research memo · November 5, 2026**

MiniFly has demonstrated durable factual memory, limited reuse on unseen relations, and learned closed-loop control. These successes came from different learners. The clearest shared finding is that learning worked when the cue preserved the information needed for the answer and feedback reached the state or action responsible for the prediction. The remaining goal is to get those capabilities working together in one continuing raw-byte learner using local updates without backpropagation, attention, or content-addressed slot lookup. Hand-designed routing, teaching markers, and task-specific operations should be removed progressively.

The most useful next evidence would come from two working references. The recovered sparse store can test **which experiences to write**. The V223 direct-input recurrent learner can test **what prevents reliable acquisition as the byte stream gets harder**. These questions require different starting points and controls.

## Where the failures occurred

**Acquisition and interference require different diagnoses.** In the original V39 S2 code, every seed acquired all facts initially at 32–256 facts. Old recall after later waves fell **98.4% → 19.7%** across those loads. V157 identified four complete-answer losses attributable to sampled later writes in matched same-state forks. Conversely, at V223’s four earlier large-stage checkpoints, only six forward-arm keys and four BPTT32 keys ever produced an exact answer. Three in each arm were wrong at the final checkpoint. The useful retention denominator is the set of keys already demonstrated correct, measured again after matched writes, observations, or idle time. [History audit](#historical-catalogs-and-protocol) · [Failure recount](#recount-inputs)

**A supplied cue, stored value, and correct selection are separate links.** V203’s near-perfect canonical QA1 score used a trigger aligned with a familiar prefix. In V204, changed cues eliminated target writes. Supplying the right write position recovered about **100%** under those cue changes, while a distractor before the destination reduced the oracle to **16.5%**. V206’s earlier raw context anchor improved that kind of address loss under supplied timing. V209B’s unseen-template selector scored **0%** with the correct candidate always available. In V211C’s same-person interference assay, the right location candidate was absent on **497/512** probes. Evaluate the write, the available evidence, and the answer choice separately before changing the architecture. [Late chronology](#later-experiment-results) · [Failure audit](#failure-and-control-reports)

**Better component scores can leave exact behavior weak.** STOP-ONLY improved conditional stopping by **22.10 points** while complete new recall remained near **11%**. V156’s fitted witness scored **1,536/1,536** while the native learner recalled **0/64** new answers. V166’s memory re-entry succeeded on a structured two-hop task with BPTT. V168’s local credit recipe recovered **3.3%** of its matched BPTT advantage. V173’s exact-gradient reference missed its own gate, leaving the local comparison unopened. The 37,313-scalar V181 learner completed its later campaign, where coherence gating failed to beat the matched sham. These cases distinguish what a representation contains, what a forward computation can use, and whether the tested learning rule can train it. [Whole-repository audit](#early-and-october-results) · [Middle chronology](#early-and-october-results)

**Integration and experience helped under specific interfaces.** The byte-interface V201 matched tagged replay using explicit frames to route bytes to three resident components. V207’s ordered 10 MiB pretraining improved frozen prose loss from **2.786 to 2.708 bits per byte** over 1 MiB, while the train-only Markov-3 reference reached **2.492** and QA transfer stayed unopened. V214B’s true-cue recall improved causal compression to **0.5776 bits per byte** versus **0.6578** shuffled and **0.6600** disabled. Its keyed retrieval and fixed hops make it an information-value reference where such lookup is excluded. V216’s larger, more diverse experience improved relative metrics, while its high-dose arm reached **25/256** complete answers and **0/128** jointly correct counterfactual pairs. The estimated durable write effect was **+1.56 points** with an interval crossing zero. In V217, prior experience raised object QA **60.4% → 98.1%** with disjoint vocabulary and supplied state operations. Transfer was weaker on reused vocabulary. [Late chronology](#later-experiment-results)

**Search and credit changes did not rescue the tested learners’ behavior at their achieved doses.** V219 completed about **73.3 million delivered symbols**. At each audited trained panel, all **12,288** pooled outputs missed the first byte and reached the length cap without emitting an end symbol. V220’s limited recovery of Claude’s search found that disabling fast writes improved loss on a generation-10 genome. Final genomes remain missing. V221 verified the credit plumbing. In V222, causal probing improved end-symbol prediction under the true prefix, but both trained arms still had **0/128** exact taught sums and **0/512** exact held sums. A small response-weight pilot reached at most **1/8** exact. These results narrow particular recipes and doses. [Late chronology](#later-experiment-results) · [Raw output recount](#recount-inputs)

## Where the raw-byte reference stands

V223’s direct-input models reached **8/8** complete outputs in each of three small repeated-record screening worlds using 32-symbol backpropagation through time (BPTT32). The larger A stage increased the number of facts, reduced presentations per fact, and interleaved many more distinct sums. Three fresh paired worlds compared exact forward credit and BPTT32 on the same graph and horizon. [V223 screen implementation](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/e79732d38830b7796d3449db9e7c370ea0661c4e/minifly_v223_learnable_recurrent_reference/screen.py#L54)

| Final V223 A endpoint | Exact forward | BPTT32 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Exact taught facts | 20/192 | 18/192 |
| Well-formed answers using a value outside that world’s taught fact set | 142/192 | 146/192 |
| First byte correct on primary held sums | 1,144/1,536 | 1,111/1,536 |
| Exact primary held sums | 7/1,536 | 6/1,536 |
| Both full answers correct on carry counterfactuals | 0/384 | 0/384 |
| Exact answers on longer operands | 0/768 | 0/768 |

Both arms emitted their own end symbol on all primary held sums. Both beat the leading-digit baseline’s **922/1,536** on the first byte, while the no-carry shortcut got **278/1,536** whole answers right. Even with the correct preceding bytes supplied, loss at positions three through five was about **3.3 bits**, near the uniform ten-digit benchmark. A units-digit edit that required a higher-column change left the predicted higher columns unchanged in **277/384** forward pairs and **290/384** BPTT32 pairs. These results locate a content problem after the first byte and a weak response to carry changes. The internal cause remains unresolved. Zero reference worlds met the acquisition gate, so the revision and return stages remained unopened. ## What the research process needs to preserve

- **Preserve learner identity and test difficulty.** The historical arithmetic line changed representations, equations, optimizer, and repetition together. The earlier literal addition reference received roughly 280 presentations per item, while the later mixed items received four. Record branch, commit, package, forward and update paths, interface, exposures, and allowed feedback. V191 and V201 each refer to two different experiments. Spell out what was measured because evidence labels also changed over time. [History audit](#historical-catalogs-and-protocol)
- **Audit the actual intervention and score.** Package B’s relation-write controls performed the treatment writes in all **832** receipts and matched all **69,888** paired relation probes. The fact controls did withhold writes. The recovered V191 parity replay scored **143/144** on an all-even holdout where a constant answer scored **144/144**. V199’s strict end-marker scoring reduced one unseen-template result to **0–0.8%**. V217’s gold-dependent loss fallback invalidated its compression interpretation, while the audited pre-feedback top-1 remained usable. [Historical recount](#recount-inputs) · [Late chronology](#later-experiment-results)
- **Separate execution, evidence, and adoption.** V4’s integrated write port had a reversed sign despite upstream parity. V5.1 R2’s resource stop left a causal control incomplete. The Learned Bank reducer could turn a partial 3/4 result into supported 3/3 when a failing control was removed in replay. Persistent Core V2 improved post-delay revision **40.49% → 96.35%**, but its compute-normalized improvement bound crossed zero, so the adoption rule retained V1. Budget and commit matched worlds as units. Record completed negative, invalid, unopened, and bounded positive outcomes separately. Verify the expected roster, actual writes, and emitted outputs before assigning a scientific verdict. [Failure audit](#failure-and-control-reports)

## Proposed next comparisons

1. **Use V223 to isolate acquisition.** Keep the direct-input learner fixed. Vary the number of facts, revisit spacing, and intervening records in separate paired contrasts. Record complete generated answers per key after teaching and at fixed later checkpoints. Once enough keys are acquired, fork the same state into write, matched observation without writes, and matched idle continuations.
2. **Use the working sparse store to remove one supplied decision.** Hide the teaching-position flag from the candidate while keeping the byte stream, store, and scoring fixed. Retain the flagged run as a positive control. Compare a gate using allowed byte history and prediction errors with randomized writes at matched effective counts and magnitudes. Score old and new facts, corrections, obsolete answers, and unseen renderings separately. This tests whether the learner can discover where to write.
3. **Keep rule reuse as a separate promotion step.** Use the V193 frozen-readout comparison as a reference, with balanced fresh relation cases and counterfactuals that defeat simple shortcuts. Score the learner’s complete generated response, including its own termination. Check transfer again when each supplied cue, role, or operation is withdrawn.

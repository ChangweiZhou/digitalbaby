  `earned_authority_v1_20261007/local_integrity.py:22` use `is not False` as the test of `if ...: raise`, which is
  fail-closed. That form is exempt; the LEARNED-BANK assignment form still fires.
- **R-CATALOG (1 row):** `origin/charming-head:human_learning_mechanisms_20261023/CATALOG.md:223` matched "22.10"
  inside a DOI (`fpsyg.2022.1032003`). Number quotes now need number boundaries.
- **R-CLOSURE (1 row):** `minifly_stop_only_confirm_v1_20261023/SOURCE_LOCK.json` hashes `PARENT_CLOSURE.json`, which
  hashes every parent module. Hashed closure manifests are now followed, but only when their bytes still match the

- **R-LITERAL (no status change, fewer hits):** literals in test files (`tests/`, `test_*.py`, `TestCase`, pytest) are

- **R-LOCKTIME (no status change):** technical-cycle receipts that the lock itself hashes are accepted inputs, not
  governed results. A lock that hashes non-cycle results gains nothing from this. At a shallow boundary the row is

## 7. Limitations

- **Local-only evidence.** Everything comes from this clone. Private persistence, local Downloads copies, raw receipts
  that were never pushed, and owner conversations are invisible. LEARNED-BANK and two sibling deliveries are read from
  `origin/charming-head` as of this clone.
- **Shallow history.** In 18 deliveries the R-LOCKTIME row is NOT_APPLICABLE at a grafted boundary (16 at `66f304ca`,
  2 at `94f58ea5`). 13 more are bulk imports (a commit adding 3 or more delivery directories, such as `f0cfbcca`). A
  deeper clone (`git fetch --unshallow`, not run: retro only reads) could make SAMPLE-GEN measurable. R-LOCKTIME has no
  PASS anywhere on the real history, so its false-flag behaviour is tested only on synthetic cases.
- **Summaries without raw rows.** Many deliveries publish compact results without raw receipts. For rcenter, only world
  310001's receipts are in git. The 64-world statistics are checked through the report, `PUBLIC_ACCEPTANCE.json` and
  the analysis code, never recomputed. "Quiet" means "not falsely flagged", not "verified correct".
- **Thin coverage of V51.** Two adapters apply. The reconciliation itself (`AUDIT.json` with its status enum,
  `CLAUDE_AUDIT_RECEIVED.txt`) has no retro adapter.
- **NOT_APPLICABLE items.** SAMPLE-GEN ordering (shallow history); STOP-ONLY LAD-02 and v5.1 ADM-FIX/ADM-GATE
  (prospective-only); V51's six non-applying adapters; rcenter R-CTRL (no W/control digest receipts).
- **Pattern adapters.** These are shape rules. R-LITERAL follows LOCK-08 as written and also flags fail-closed
  check-then-record literals (rcenter), so its 52 FLAGs overstate defects. One residual R-CLAIM hit is a description,
  not a claim: `PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/PRE_FULL151_DESIGNS_RESULTS_20261023/designs/2a54ffdef3862514_DESIGN.md:62` quotes
  a past audit issue, "reuse of one development panel for confirmation".
- **Interpretation.** Scoped plus adjudicated is the retro implementer's reading of "applied checks" in SPEC 12.4. It
  is offered for confirmation, not settled. Under the raw reading the quiet list fails (29.4%).
- **Moving kit.** `checker_sha256` is the kit as it stood during the run, while other modules were being edited.
  `retro_py_sha256` pins the retro code.

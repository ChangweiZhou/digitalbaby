# Hostile audit of E22 and the amended E20-B / E18b dispatch

10 November 2026

**E22’s command loads the intended birth and completes a technical dry life. Its scientific verdict needs amendment before full dispatch.** The main weaknesses are a parity gate that permits common collapse, ambiguous aggregation, a five-point comparison confounded with separate cloud hosts, and incomplete binding between the birth’s training history and the life’s admission. E20-B still needs an implemented, qualified runner. E18b remains conditional on the every-byte lives and needs narrower interpretation of its acquisition gate.

This is a static audit of `40cb1c208adec3f39351356e52d3f69eba4ade17`, the commit supplied with the dispatch note. I read the three briefs, source paths, manifest and E22 dry JSON, and used parallel reviews for the three jobs. No project code, tests, training or evaluation ran. The pinned branch did not advance during the check. This memo recommends admission changes. It supplies no efficacy result for any of the new jobs.

**P22.2 can pass when both learners finish badly.** Being within five points is a relative comparison. For example, if both arms acquire mod-97 at the first checkpoint and later forget it to the same low accuracy, P22.1 and the mod-97 portion of P22.2 can both pass. The current gate does not impose final absolute competence or preserved hard-operation competence. The mean over add, sub, max and min can also hide a deficit on addition and subtraction. If max/min are perfect, a family mean of 80 requires only a hard-operation mean of 60.

Keep the family parity measurement, but give acquisition, final competence and old-skill retention separate verdicts. Before launch, state the required absolute end performance for the intended claim, report add/sub as well as every operation, and report all six birth-to-life curves. Any stronger gate must be registered before training and justified by the existing diagnostic goal. Parity after common collapse should remain a measured failure to retain useful behavior. [Registered predictions](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/40cb1c208adec3f39351356e52d3f69eba4ade17/OPUS_TASK_E22_RULE_BORN_LIFE_20261110.md)

The P22.2 definition also needs a literal scorer. Specify whether “within five” means absolute difference ≤5, or a one-sided deficit of at most five. A rule-born learner outperforming by ten points passes the latter and fails the former. Specify k=12, complete own-byte/EOS accuracy, equal weighting of operations, checkpoint six, and whether both moduli must pass in the same two seeds. Three fixed seed pairs support a descriptive comparison at the chosen dose. They cannot establish statistical equivalence simply by landing inside the margin. The earlier starting-score difference is already disclosed correctly in the brief.

**Separate-instance allocation weakens the five-point inference.** One instance per birth arm makes host and birth history co-vary. The project already observed cross-machine divergence, including a large composition swing in E16-RP. Same machine class does not guarantee matching libraries, CPU features or numerical trajectories. At minimum pin source, software, thread settings and six birth digests, verify checkpoint-zero reproduction, and quantify cross-instance drift with a common checkpoint and dry trajectory. That is useful admission evidence but cannot prove full-life equivalence. The stronger design executes matched birth-history partners on a shared host, within the process cap. If the one-arm-per-instance policy is retained, label the result as a comparison of those two execution packages and avoid attributing a five-point difference solely to birth history. [Reproducibility record](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/40cb1c208adec3f39351356e52d3f69eba4ade17/PROGRAM_STATE_20261107.md)

**A birth digest authenticates a file’s identity, with training provenance still to check.** The six expected E22 births should be pinned in a run manifest with seed, architecture, head, feedback, trace reach, loss, update dose, birth source and recorded final step. Check checkpoint zero against the loaded birth on an identical panel. The life and original birth panels use different distractor renderings, so their raw scores need not match. The source’s adjacent-receipt admission accepts a truthy completion and matching weight hash without validating those scientific fields. The historical manifest is an explicit legacy admission by filename and hash. Both can be used with an audited expected roster, but neither makes a renamed or misdescribed checkpoint scientifically correct on its own. Do not allow the legacy override in this dispatch. Require identical life configurations and panel digests across partners, allowing only the declared birth identity and output namespace to differ. **The accepted rule needs an accurate name.** With `E16_TRACEUP=all`, the source retains the temporal graph, uses a backward pass with sign-modified feedback and disables the eligibility scan because the backward pass already carries temporal credit. This fits the recorded owner ruling. The title “no backprop anywhere” obscures the actual computation. Describe E22 as a sign-feedback temporal-sweep learner trained by the same accepted rule in both birth and life. Record sweep length and include birth cost in any total-sample or compute claim. It remains answer-supervised and replay-supported. Passing E22 would satisfy the birth-lineage condition for this bounded single-operation test; the persistent-core promotion condition remains separate. **The new cloud sizing is a sensible concurrency limit.** The inspected imports set numerical threads to one, and three training processes on four effective cores leave room for orchestration. Record effective CPU quota, not just a host-wide logical-core count, alongside peak RSS and free storage. A foreground wait does not establish durability. The new owner permission allows cloud lives under the stated cap, so the earlier blanket cloud hold should not be treated as a conflicting prohibition. The briefs still need a declared crash policy. E21 has not supplied an accepted implementation. If an instance restarts, preserve the partial attempt, stop the affected cell and report it under the brief’s no-rerun rule. A later restart or resume needs explicit authorization and a validated state. **E20-B’s sizing amendment leaves its scientific admission open.** The G0 reference is a useful addition. Its parity check must compare the new runner against the current bank2 runner on the same host, birth weights, dose and stream through all six stage transitions. The brief’s first-two-checkpoint test covers only F1 and cannot qualify composition parsing, reservoir use or F3. An arbitrary dry trajectory cannot be required to reproduce historical full-dose checkpoint losses. Each GRU/LSTM/G3 implementation also needs shape, causality, state-reset, BOS/EOS, gradient-flow and optimizer-group checks.

G3 can keep the exact shared bank2 dimensions and add its declared feedback matrix within the parameter allowance. Instantiate shared parameters identically before adding the connection, and compare an enabled connection with a disabled counterpart. Separate instances reproduce the same host issue as E22. Use E20-owned names and explicit birth ownership, because reusing `develop17()` can accept a cached G0 birth and the old life paths can overwrite historical receipts. Parameter matching makes the GRU/LSTM arms feasibility packages. A collective miss at one dose does not identify teaching as the cause or eliminate architectural and optimization alternatives. The brief’s old premise that no learner composes from memory must also acknowledge the completed E20-A reference result. [E20-B brief](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/40cb1c208adec3f39351356e52d3f69eba4ade17/OPUS_TASK_E20_COMPOSITION_FROM_MEMORY_20261110.md)

**E18b remains held behind the every-byte lives.** Its improved gradient reach is a valid reason to test the changed objective. Its ≥30% max/min gate is too weak for a learned-operation interpretation. On an unfiltered uniform pair table, returning one operand gives approximately 50% exact max/min accuracy if the output grammar is correct. Compute the actual copy-a, copy-b and frequency controls on the sealed panel. A 30% result can show acquisition of some output behavior while losing to those shortcuts.

The claim that the loss mask is the only change also requires machine matching with its historical cloud partners. The existing B3 answer-loss dry reproduction and gradient checks are useful, but they do not establish full-trajectory matching for both B2 and B3 on a new host. Add matched answer-loss controls if making a causal loss-only claim, or preserve the historical-package qualification. P18b.2 failure would bound this package at this dose. It would not prove that every target requires longer credit. P18b.4 failure also does not alone identify dilution as the cause.

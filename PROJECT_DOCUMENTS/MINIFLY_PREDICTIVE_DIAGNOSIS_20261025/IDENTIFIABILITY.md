## Artifact boundary

The immutable Git tree preserves 783 Recoverability and 812 Capacity files, even though these folders are omitted from this sparse checkout. Inventorying their names does not restore them. The analysis reads compact JSON/source blobs in memory; it does not restore raw arrays, checkpoints or ZIP parts. Eight local V147 calibration checkpoints were already present; six are read for the disposable E0 fixtures. V147 H worlds remain ungenerated and unscored.

The existing 53-delivery catalogue is reused. No cross-project registry or new numerical version is created. All cases keep their historical substrate, dose and statistical unit. Correlated value lives, heads and both Capacity sizes are kept within each world's validation fold.

## Why some proposed validations are not performed

Recoverability has four grouped worlds, a common time/write dose, and floor-valued final complete scores. A time/write-dose predictor cannot be identified across those worlds, and fitting a rich predictor to a constant endpoint would give false confidence. Compact files do not expose per-query margins. Historical checkpoints cannot reconstruct a single suppressed write without a corresponding branch or a trajectory replay; no such replay was done.

Capacity supplies temporal pre-value geometry and early OLD scores for eight paired worlds, allowing a narrow exploratory prediction check. Its construction fixture is aggregate per arm, not a per-assessment-world teaching measurement. It cannot explain which world later retains more OLD information. The expansion also changes connectivity and Q normalization/self gain; population size is a bundle label.

V147 permits code-verified one-update math but no new E3 conclusion. Its lower mean teacher loss alongside worse complete answers weakens that loss as a readiness proxy. Unique code groups do not prove linear separability or learning convergence.

## Minimum next measurements, only in a separately authorized experiment

Record early target-oriented margins before and after a small specified teaching increment and matched no-write copy, preserve the same random tape and observations, then measure a later held or protected-OLD endpoint. Keep related-held influence separate from unrelated damage. Freeze the predictor and future comparison before those outcomes occur. No global kernel or whole-network audit needs to become part of the deployed core.

For raw-trace × relational-teaching hypotheses, future protocol details still need a design: one pinned learner and native output route, gradual warmup, training-only scale statistics, bounded comparisons of actually observed outputs, total comparison/re-encoding cost and complete own-byte/EOS unseen-addition endpoint. The current register is not a runnable experiment lock or authorization.

# Next MiniFly addition round: four distinct learning operations

11 October 2026. **Design under joint audit; no efficacy run or adoption yet.** This round tests four separate, bounded operations. U055 is one candidate, not the theme of the round. The frozen prior experiments and user-adopted development default are unchanged.

## Claim and scope

The primary claim available here is **E3 within finite, single-digit addition**, with the existing byte parser, left/right identity features, additive value head and ordinal digit renderer openly disclosed as engineering priors. The learner sees noisy or missing *actually delivered* answer bytes for taught pairs; it must emit its own digit before feedback on both taught and never-reinforced pairs. The aim is more accuracy on never-reinforced NEW pairs after the same OLD320 history and 20/40/80 NEW observations, without materially losing OLD reuse or requiring disproportionate work. This cannot establish an unmodified Full151 core, discovery of arithmetic without priors, or NLP ability.

The [objective contract](../../MINIFLY_OBJECTIVE_CONTRACT.md) governs exposure, recitation controls and promotion. The baseline geometry, byte stream, noise, causal branches, held-out pairs and countermodels come from [SAMPLE-GEN V2](../addition_sample_generalization_dev_v2_20261011/results/CONFIRM_REPORT.md) and the later [SUPPORT assay](../addition_observation_support_v1_design_20261011/EXPERIMENT.md). No scored held-out semantic orbit or its reverse receives an answer at any time. Changed byte wrappers alone would be E2; this assay scores never-taught combinations. Every answer is selected at `=` before its optional observed digit arrives. The current arm's actual pre-feedback output, not a helper's output, is scored.

The two common anchors are confirmed **COV-R** and Mr. Zhou's user-adopted **COV-SUPPORT**. The latter's low-sample NEW improvement was not independently confirmed. The primary statistical unit is one fresh world, not records, two orientations, checkpoints or branches. The U034 SUPPORT-DIR dose/window/trigger recipe is closed at DEV and is not retuned here. The full [108/136/254 review](../PROJECT_DOCUMENTS/MECHANISM_SCREEN_20261007/FULL_REVIEW_POST_U034_20261011/REVIEW.md) prevents renaming older replay, topology or recurrence variants as new.

## Common interface and state

The existing 31-vector `x` has one active left-symbol coordinate, one active right-symbol coordinate and bias coordinate 30. `v=xᵀw`; digit emission uses the existing tie rule. `w(0)=0`, `P(0)=10I`. At each *allowed, present* observed answer `y`, use the cached pre-answer `v`, `r=y-v`, `q=P x`, `u=xᵀq`, the inherited `SUPPORT` factor `s∈{1,1/4}`, and `γ=s·min(1,1/|r|)` with the zero-residual factor defined as one. A normal SUPPORT update is `d=1+γu`, `w←clip(w+γr q/d)`, `P←sym(P−γqqᵀ/d)`. Existing support-history semantics are identical across all applicable arms. `P` is an algorithmic full-correlation state, **not** a calibrated posterior variance.

All candidates receive identical external byte sequences, timing, role/additivity/ordinal priors and permission flags. No arm receives the latent numeric map, private truth, noise class, held-out membership, clean reconstructed target, future record, evaluation outcome or phase label. Arm-specific caches hold only actually observed, permitted `(x,y)` records. The only persistent state is declared learner state; all extra records, statistics, graph edges, RNG and counters are serialized, cloned, erased and included in state digests. Queries run on disposable clones and cannot teach or change later training state. Missing/forbidden observed answers cannot change target-dependent state. Reuse of a record consumes computation; it is not another independent external observation.

## Candidate roster: one operation per arm

| ID | Source family and one changed operation | What it may establish if it wins |
|---|---|---|
| COVER | U047; choose a buffered *actual observation* whose current full-`P` constraint direction is least covered, then perform one extra weight-only correction | Whether information-complementary selection extracts more from a fixed external sample budget than matched replay selectors |
| RETRO | M034, with M106's drifting-reference warning; revise the contribution weight of a *prior actual observation* when later actual evidence supplies a strict same-key majority | Whether a bounded causal correction of earlier noisy evidence helps, beyond forward-only SUPPORT |
| ROLE | M077/M105; use actually observed opposite-order pairs to gate shrinkage between corresponding left/right symbol contributions | Whether data-earned use of an explicit same-byte cross-role prior helps beyond an untied or fixed-strength prior |
| KCI | U055/M094, limited to a KC-like **axo-axonic plasticity-suppression proxy**; current active feature nodes inhibit collateral writes to graph-neighbour inactive coordinates | Whether the site of one write matters beyond a smaller global correction |

No arm combines mechanisms. U059 update quota remains a separately costed comparator concept, **not a fifth mechanism**, because merely selecting buffered observations under a quota would duplicate U047. DPM cross-stream binding is not appended to KCI: the present two role routes receive the same prompt bytes, and no KC-to-31-coordinate or DPM homology map exists. The [Manoim et al. paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9613607/) supports KC-to-KC axo-axonic suppression of calcium/dopamine-evoked cAMP, not this numerical 31-coordinate graph or any retrospective batch solver. The [Okray et al. preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.28007) motivates a separate multisensory DPM question, not a current addition-arm result.

### COVER / U047

Maintain a FIFO ring of at most 32 actually observed `(x,y,s)` *occurrences*, including repeated occurrences of one key. Stored `s` is that record's arrival-time SUPPORT factor and is never recomputed when later votes change. Following each allowed external SUPPORT update, choose from the **pre-insertion** ring the record maximizing `x_iᵀP x_i` using the current `P`, tie by oldest insertion. Recompute its present output and robust residual `r_i=y_i−x_iᵀw`; set `q_i=P x_i`, `γ_i=s_i·min(1,1/|r_i|)` (zero residual factor one), `d_i=1+γ_i x_iᵀq_i`, and make exactly one extra `w←clip(w+γ_i r_i q_i/d_i)` correction. Do **not** contract `P`, alter SUPPORT votes or count this as new evidence. Then insert the current record. If the ring is empty, do no extra correction. Selection uses no `y_i` beyond the cache and no future labels; scan, refresh, correction, RNG and bytes moved are charged.

Its locked peers use the same ring, one weight-only correction, timing and `P` convention: uniform selection with public seed `20261011047`, and deterministic maximum absolute current residual with oldest-insertion tie. Log selected indices and the fraction of replay events that repeat the same winner; because replay leaves `P` unchanged, the maximum-leverage item can remain the winner indefinitely. Residual-priority REPLAY-P on a prior compact substrate and full-COV COV-REPLAY were already tested; COVER's novelty is only this selector on the present SUPPORT substrate. If it beats SUPPORT but not these peers, replay or added work may help but *coverage selection* has not been isolated. Repeated noisy records may be actively harmful.

### RETRO / M034

Use a separate bounded 32-record ledger of permitted `(x,y,γ_arrival,γ_effective)` and running full weighted-observation statistics `H=0.1I+Σγ_effective xxᵀ`, `b=Σγ_effective yx`; `P=H⁻¹`, `w=clip(H⁻¹b)`. This is a **new declared weighted-ridge writer**, not an assertion that clipped sequential SUPPORT is exactly batch ridge. Its matched `RETRO-FIXED` peer maintains the same statistics, ledger, solver schedule and **arrival-weight rule computed from its own pre-answer state**, but never revises an earlier weight; COV-R and SUPPORT remain external anchors. All observations older than the ring remain in `H,b` at their last committed nonnegative weight.

Event order is fixed: compute the SUPPORT gate from **past** same-key votes and own pre-answer output; compute the Huber arrival weight; add the current actual `(x,y,γ_arrival,γ_effective)` occurrence to `H,b` and the FIFO; evict the oldest if necessary while leaving its latest contribution frozen in `H,b`; then inspect the last eight **still-cached** occurrences of the current exact ordered key, including the current answer. If at least three exist and one answer has **at least 75%** of those votes, set each *older occurrence within that eight-record window* discordant with the mode to one quarter of its own arrival weight and each aligned older occurrence to its original arrival weight. Without such a mode, restore those older in-window occurrences to arrival weight. The current occurrence is never retrospectively revised at its arrival. Cached rows leaving the last-eight same-key window keep their last committed weight until they re-enter it (which ordinarily cannot occur under FIFO); evicted rows are permanently frozen. Apply each signed weight difference once to both `H` and `b`, then recompute `P,w` from the statistics; at most seven older records can change per event. Every solve, scan and matrix operation is charged. A later majority reversal can restore or change a still-eligible prior contribution, never invent a clean target. Independently reconstruct `H,b` from both frozen evictions and current ledger, require SPD/positive finite solves, and test majority appearance/loss/reversal, FIFO eviction, repeated-trigger idempotence and frozen-weight parity. The exact SUPPORT baseline need not be bit-identical to `RETRO-FIXED` because clipping makes the writers distinct; both comparisons are required.

The diagnostic random peer, if RETRO reaches confirmation, computes the same majority condition from **its own causal ledger** and permutes the resulting multiset of desired quarter/full factors across eligible older records of that same ordered key, using public seed `20261011034`. The number of desired quarter factors is matched, but realized signed mass and number of changed weights need not match because arrival weights and earlier assignments may differ; report these differences. A factor is always in `[0.25,1]` times that record's own nonnegative arrival weight, so no downdate can make a contribution negative. This distinguishes evidence-directed revision from generic downdating. No old record is silently replayed as a new sample.

The fixture has five taught semantic orbits and ten ordered records per epoch: eight non-diagonal ordered keys plus one diagonal key repeated twice. At NEW20, a non-diagonal key has at most two observations, so RETRO cannot act on it. Three unanimous votes meet the ≥75% trigger but produce no discordant downdate; the first nonzero discount needs at least four votes with a 3:1 split. Log majority events **and actual nonzero changes to `H,b`**, separately for OLD/NEW and diagonal/non-diagonal keys, plus first triggering external-record ordinal. Technical forced cases prove the rule; full reserved histories reveal whether it reaches the intended checkpoints. Inactivity is an unexercised recipe, not a scientific negative, and cannot justify post-DEV threshold repair.

### ROLE / M077–M105

Retain the normal SUPPORT writer and one global FIFO queue of at most 32 unmatched actually observed answers, indexed by ordered taught key; expiration removes the oldest unmatched record, so no pair may use an evicted answer. When `(a,b)` with `a≠b` arrives and a previously observed unmatched `(b,a)` exists, consume the **oldest** such record once and do not enqueue the current record; otherwise enqueue it. Self-pairs are never queued or compared. Only permitted present observations set per-symbol `seen_left`/`seen_right` flags; the queue, flags and counters are learner state. Call the pair agreeing when `|y_ab−y_ba|≤1`; update causal `n_agree,n_disagree`. The threshold is an engineering noise tolerance, not access to hidden equality. Set `η=0.05·max(0,(n_agree−n_disagree)/(n_agree+n_disagree+4))`; before any such evidence `η=0`. After the one ordinary SUPPORT update, on **each permitted present observation only**, for each *distinct current symbol* `i∈{a,b}` actually seen in both roles, let `m=(w_Li+w_Ri)/2`, `δ=(w_Li−w_Ri)/2`, then write `w_Li=m+(1−η)δ`, `w_Ri=m−(1−η)δ`; leave `P` on its ordinary SUPPORT update. Thus later pre-answer output uses the changed `w`. This is an explicit data-gated *same-byte identity prior*, not inference of cue identity from equal answers, not learned commutativity from scratch, and not exact RLS after projection.

The fixed-strength peer uses `η=0.05` once each symbol has appeared in both roles, with the same queue/accounting; a separately qualified permuted-right-symbol peer (public seed `20261011077`) applies the same causal `η`, arrival stream, update frequency and eligibility but uses the **true-identity seen-in-both-roles eligibility schedule** but pairs each eligible left symbol with a fixed, distinct permuted right endpoint, which may itself be unobserved in that role. This is a deliberately wrong-relation specificity control for the engineered byte-identity prior, not discovery of the identity map. The training generator supplies both orientations of taught orbits, but the learner may couple only after the opposite actual answer has arrived; it cannot synthesize a reversed example or query a withheld pair. If no actual pairs form, ROLE is technically inert and cannot be promoted by a different assay after seeing DEV.

### KCI / U055

Freeze a sparse directed, nonnegative graph `A` separately within the existing 15 left and 15 right identity coordinates, with exactly two unit-weight outgoing edges per source column (`A_ij=1` routes activity from `j` to `i`; otherwise zero), no self or bias edges and public graph seed `20261011055` independent of world, symbol numerical meaning and answers. This role grouping is an **engineering choice, not KC-subtype homology**. At an allowed external SUPPORT write, compute the ordinary `q=P x`, `d`, `γ` and prospective `Δw=γr q/d`. For each of the 30 sensory coordinates define `g_i=1/(1+λ(1−x_i)(A x)_i)` with fixed `λ=1`; set `g_30=1`. Apply `w←clip(w+g⊙Δw)` but retain the ordinary `P←sym(P−γqqᵀ/d)`. Thus one actual answer causes one weight update and one covariance contraction, while active cue coordinates retain the same immediate write and neighbours' inactive collateral correction is attenuated. `λ=0` or `A=0` must reproduce SUPPORT bitwise on the same prestate. No event-internal iteration, persistent activity trace or DPM mechanism is claimed.

This is a **new writer**, not an exact weighted-least-squares recursion: its `w` and `P` need not remain a coherent posterior pair. The existing Huber factor and clipping already limited that interpretation, but this gate adds a distinct approximation. Positive `γ` and the unchanged full-`P` contraction preserve `P` symmetry/PSD in exact arithmetic; qualification tests numeric tolerances, saturation, `q=0`, `r=0`, and a correlated `P` where inactive `q` is nonzero. The current cue's postwrite `xᵀw` must match SUPPORT from an identical prestate (including clipping), while later other cues may differ. A failure may mean collateral corrections were useful for E3 or `P` contracted despite gated `w`.

The primary peer is a **global-norm** gate: from its own prestate compute `c=||g⊙q||₂/||q||₂` (`q=0 ⇒ c=1`) and use `cΔw` with the same ordinary `P` update. This matches only the proposed **unclipped** step norm, not necessarily the realized clipped step; report preclip and realized postclip norms for both arms and never call it an exact-dose control. A degree-preserving within-role graph-label shuffle is a technical diagnostic; without a fresh graph-specific science control there will be **no claim that actual KC edge placement was validated**.

## Three technical design–audit–test cycles

These are **qualification cycles, not three scientific search rounds**. They use reserved public technical worlds and synthetic hand cases disjoint from DEV/confirmation, and cannot select the winning mechanism or tune a dose from behavior. Baselines and candidate-specific peers share one byte/branch harness and one auditor.

1. **Operation and source lock.** Freeze all four equations, constants, graph/selector seeds, parser timing and arm/peer names. Hand-check a correlated 31-D `P` for KCI, a two-record weighted-statistic downdate for RETRO, one actually paired reversed observation for ROLE, and COVER's selector versus uniform/residual. Verify all zero-effect identities, finite denominators, PSD of `P`, one external answer→one recorded arrival, and actual output before answer. Rejected variants remain in a dated audit, not silently rewritten.
2. **Causal/null and cost audit.** Independently verify no target-derived state in missing/forbidden branches; queries use clones; W/N_OLD/N_NEW and shuffled branches never share learned cache, support votes, RNG, reweight ledger, role-pair queue or graph-derived learned state. Check restore/erase/digest completeness, deterministic resume, matched peer information and extra-update counts, finite work/bytes and deliberate tamper rejection. Check ROLE fixed/prior versus evidence gate, RETRO fixed-weight parity and U055 global-norm limit. Distinguish technical failure from scientific negative.
3. **Full technical life and admission.** Run reserved complete 2,560-record technical histories across both anchors, four candidates and every potential confirmation peer on at least PATH_LOOP/STANDARD, PATH_LOOP/BLURRY, STAR_LOOP/STANDARD and STAR_LOOP/BLURRY. Include a minimal frozen TRIANGLE_TAIL and ordinary geometry/branch reachability check, without using its scores for selection. Independently reduce compact checkpoint summaries once; measure production CPU, wall time, peak RSS, persistent/temporary bytes, traversed edges, cache scans, matrix solves and archive size. Test concurrent status/reducer access, cold resume, atomic receipts, safe whole-job stop, final bundle and source hashes. Lock a bounded worker/time plan **before** inspecting any DEV score. Technical cases prove implementation and admission, never efficacy.

## Three cycles

1. **Design/audit:** implemented evaluator-only random byte relabelling, output-balanced whole-orbit splits and orbit-consistent target shuffling. Kernel imports no evaluator arithmetic. The local update matches the V146 parent numerically; all 256 byte identity traces match, FIXED preserves W/b, and NO-WRITE preserves parameter arrays. One startup attempt failed from an inherited module-name collision before any learning; it was preserved and the evaluator renamed v147_data.
2. **Test:** all eight declared D1/D2 × FIXED/LOCAL × naming-rate calibrations ran through 256 coherent passes, with gradual 1/16-to-1 warmup over 1,024 records. No shrinkage, map, replay, backprop or new interaction module was used. No held scores selected parameters.
3. **Audit/revise:** exact roster, saved generation scoring, exposure totals and source hashes were checked; five corrupt artifact fixtures were rejected. A fresh technical trajectory was checkpointed and resumed in a separate process and matched uninterrupted learned state and RNG. Technical continuations were charged, not treated as independent evidence.

All percentages use the same orbit weighting. This reduction uses saved taught predictions, with no new generation, training or held scoring. Most higher-rate failures already have a wrong first payload; fixing EOS alone would leave acquisition far below readiness. The LOCAL route often emits a well-formed one-byte answer but names the wrong byte. The evidence supports isolating payload readout/credit acquisition next, while preserving autonomous EOS scoring. It does not prove a specific cause or establish a successful alternative.

Total: **812,187 complete record applications and 1,624,378 target events**, including paid resume continuations. No held relation was reinforced.

Cycle3 used 3.984 process CPU-seconds plus 0.996 separate resume-child CPU-seconds. Supervisor overhead and tool/document preparation are not fully measured; no all-in project compute-efficiency claim is made.

This surrogate uses canonical PN/KC assets with a local delta naming store. It is not native Full151 value memory. Source/graph hashes and a pre-fit machine manifest were recorded; full production asset restoration and assessment scheduling are not certified by this developmental audit. Checkpoint fixtures do not replace a completed long assessment. Raw predictions and checkpoints stay local.

## Decision

Stop as ACQUISITION_NOT_READY. The four-arm 16-pass rehearsal and 4,096-pass assessment are not run; only a small technical resume fixture follows the failed gate. We therefore have no new E3 outcome, positive or negative. The next proposed question is whether the fixed-address local naming route can reliably acquire these taught byte/EOS mappings; do not add tasks or claim lack of abstraction before that is understood.

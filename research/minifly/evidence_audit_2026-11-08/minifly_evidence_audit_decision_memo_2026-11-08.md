## 2. Source and terminology corrections

**2.1 Directly observed biological result is not sufficient engineering evidence.** Hige, Aso and others demonstrate fly compartment-specific plasticity and teaching signals. They do not show that the project's supervised output target is itself generated by fly dopamine error circuits. The current T cell in [exp11_cell.py](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/5e7743f2a7914988e007457ee4bae8af4b6320c0/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/E16V_SOURCE_SNAPSHOT/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp11_cell.py#L92) uses a target-byte cross-entropy loss and readout-gradient-derived eligibility; the original E16 synthetic task is defined by an arithmetic `apply` oracle.

**2.2 Recurrent and multiplicative mechanisms already exist.** [exp11_cell.py lines 92–115](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/5e7743f2a7914988e007457ee4bae8af4b6320c0/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/E16V_SOURCE_SNAPSHOT/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp11_cell.py#L92) implements `h_t = r_t*h_(t-1) + w_t*c_t`, so recommending generic gated working state or multiplication as new is unjustified. [exp3_state.py lines 91–104](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/5e7743f2a7914988e007457ee4bae8af4b6320c0/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/E16V_SOURCE_SNAPSHOT/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp3_state.py#L91) implements `S = sign(W)*mean(abs(W))` on the backward path; it is dynamic sign-symmetric feedback, **not fixed random feedback alignment**, nor exact transposed backprop. [Lines 118–183 of exp11_cell.py](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/5e7743f2a7914988e007457ee4bae8af4b6320c0/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/E16V_SOURCE_SNAPSHOT/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp11_cell.py#L118) implement temporal eligibility multiplied by a *supervised readout-loss learning signal*. Neither label alone resolves end-to-end locality.

**2.3 Explicit MiniFly navigation exists, in a different lineage.** The [closed-loop gain-fit report](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/5e7743f2a7914988e007457ee4bae8af4b6320c0/output/minifly-connectome-navigation/FIT2_REPORT.md#L1) says 3/3 seeds meet revised combined heading/goal-steering criteria (native 79/96 vs rewired 21–22/96 in its task). The [path-integration report](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/5e7743f2a7914988e007457ee4bae8af4b6320c0/output/minifly-connectome-navigation/PI_REPORT.md#L1) says 0/3 seeds meet all locked criteria. Its [follow-up](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/5e7743f2a7914988e007457ee4bae8af4b6320c0/output/minifly-connectome-navigation/PI_FOLLOWUP_REPORT.md#L1) explicitly states that wiring supplies an instantaneous vector and **temporal displacement accumulation is supplied by the model**, and shows return residual remains too high with clamped heading. This is not evidence of an autonomous learned byte-to-byte composition rule, but refutes claims that no navigation circuits have been implemented or tested.

**2.4 The E16 replay explanation is not settled.** The pinned [E16W report](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/5e7743f2a7914988e007457ee4bae8af4b6320c0/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/E16V_SOURCE_SNAPSHOT/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/E16W_REPORT.md#L15) has 12/12 completed runs: local uniform replay loses **45.58/46.42/48.08 percentage points** of previous composition final-answer accuracy; a feedforward backpropagation control using the same recorded training batches also loses **44.17/42.25/38.92 pp**. A 25% composition-share condition improves final accuracy **6.25/10.88/8.17 pp** but does not meet its prespecified one-third loss-reduction gate. The [E16V audit](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/5e7743f2a7914988e007457ee4bae8af4b6320c0/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/E16V_REPORT.md#L282) reads archived replay-batch traces and reports **13.59–21.40% wrong full composition replay targets** for uniform replay late, while target correctness for the corresponding share arm was **not recorded**. Thus wrong teaching targets, sample coverage, architectural interference and task exposure remain confounded in different ways. Avoid 'coverage is the unique cause' or 'local learning is uniquely at fault'. E16-WR is an outstanding dependent campaign unless a completed current result bundle has been inspected; none was verified in this review.

**2.5 The scaffolding is substantive.** [exp16_integrated.py](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/digitalbaby/blob/5e7743f2a7914988e007457ee4bae8af4b6320c0/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/E16V_SOURCE_SNAPSHOT/MOD97_LOCAL_RULE_20261106/exp16_integrated.py#L59) stores structured tokens such as `(family, task, operands)` and re-renders bytes while recomputing arithmetic truth. The learner receives ordinary bytes and specified targets, but replay selection and target construction rely on task/operand structure outside it. Replacing that with raw-stream replay would remove *that* parser only if stored examples carry their actual observed bytes/targets rather than reconstituting a hidden truth generator. A generative replay writer has a different problem: self-generated targets may be false (as already measured) and can compound under further training. An internal selector must preserve coverage and must not read evaluation stage identities. Continuous full-byte prediction would also change the objective and exposure distribution; it is **not a small cosmetic code change**.

**2.6 Important paper corrections.** [Bennett, Philippides & Nowotny (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-22592-4) is a *computational* MB RPE model, not a new fly experiment. Its actual Eq. (1) is a quadratic reward-prediction loss; Eq. (2) defines a valence-specific plasticity rule and Eq. (3) states `d_+ - d_- = received_reward - predicted_reward` in the model. Its [author MATLAB code](https://github.com/BrainsOnBoard/paper_RPEs_in_drosophila_mb) includes `mb_vs.m`, its flagged plasticity rules and methods; the code has not been run in this audit. [Felsenberg et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.08.021) supports **parallel opposing memories**, not superior performance of a specified two-weight algorithm over equal-resource banks. [Yamada et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.79042) establishes biological **second-order conditioning**, not systematic symbolic rule composition. [Rachad et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2025.115593) is in **Cell Reports**, not eLife.

**2.7 Westeinde author correction.** [The 11 March 2025 Author Correction](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08245-8) to [Westeinde et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07039-2) amends one fly genotype in the Methods (‘Flies’ / ‘Origins of transgenic stocks’) and the Extended Data Fig. 2c PFL2 split-GAL4 heading. It is **not** a retraction of the reported steering transformation. The original source register did not mention this detail.

**2.8 Okray correction/retraction.** The original work, *Multisensory learning binds neurons into a cross-modal memory engram*, was published in **2023** (DOI [10.1038/s41586-023-06013-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06013-8)); the [retraction notice](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10355-4) was published **25 March 2026**. It specifically identifies unreproducible *voltage-imaging* measurements in Figs. 3a–f and 4a,b and the “Neurons gain cross-modal activation” section; the authors report reproducing behavioral and connectomic observations but the **entire paper is retracted**. Neither 'all supporting observations were disproved' nor 'the original cross-modal mechanism is validated' follows. This notice must not be presented as evidence for F13 re-anchoring.

## 3. Distinct computational benefits versus current coverage

The 20 records are not twenty validated absent MiniFly algorithms. The original source already maps a large number of 70 legacy labels onto overlapping operations; for example 19/21 are literal tagging overlap, 5/25 heterosynaptic overlap, and several replay labels overlap without being mechanistically identical. The independent dispositions in the JSON distinguish **merged overlap**, **out-of-scope mammalian analogy**, **unverified source**, and **fly observation without engineering validation**. They preserve original IDs rather than substituting a different ranking.

- **Storage/write mechanisms (F03–F12):** plausible operations concern local suppression, teaching channel separation, learning-generated teaching, parallel expression, and post-event reinforcement. Their actual teaching-signal origins and feedback topology—not their biological names—determine whether they add capability. The current supervised E16 learner already has recurrence, readout gradients and eligibility; exact adult fly dopamine circuits were not established in the inspected code.
- **Representation and computing (F13–F20):** a persistent internal coordinate and remappable sensory projection (F13) are distinct from the already implemented recurrence (F14), biophysical evidence accumulation (F15), vector coordinate transforms (F16), fitted goal steering (F17), algebraic multiplication (F18), efference-copy cancellation (F19), and innate motor sequencing (F20). The separate navigation line has measured both fitted steering success and path integration failures, so any transfer proposal must identify the missing operation *within its lineage*.

## 4. Ranked decision memo — new experiments NOT authorized

**Selection criterion:** relevance to an observed E16 deficit, independent biological evidence, a distinct missing computation, and a falsifiable small resource-matched comparison. 'Test later' means design discussion, not permission to interrupt E16-WR or run an experiment.

| Rank | Record / status | Distinct value and reason | Decisive comparison (design only) | Falsifier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **SCF-1 — unresolved, inspect current controls first** | The largest directly measured defect is replay target correctness/coverage under a structured task parser, not absence of a named fly circuit. | Freeze learner, total training tokens and target-byte access; compare logged raw observed examples, structured re-rendered examples, and model-generated targets with equal buffer bytes and realized diversity. | Improvement vanishes when target correctness, exposure or storage is matched, or an alleged raw approach still accesses task IDs or answer generator. |
| 2 | **F13 sensory re-anchoring — test later, conditional** | Could preserve trained downstream task across a changed input encoding *if sensorimotor/co-occurrence or feedback supplies correspondence*. | Fixed downstream rule; equal-size local afferent remapping vs ordinary adapter with identical paired/continuous observations; measure both representational similarity and old/new rule accuracy. | Gains require oracle permutation labels or do not recover task accuracy. |
| 3 | **F09 learned teaching transfer — test later, conditional** | Adds second-order associative teaching from a learned cue; fly evidence is not rule induction. | Two-stage conditioning plus confidence/reliability diagnostics versus matched ordinary associative chaining using same rewards and states. | False teacher outputs amplify errors or no transfer to withheld association links. |
| 4 | **F11 opposing traces — test later, conditional** | Potentially preserves contradictory experience and separately controls expression, though two generic banks could do the same. | Explicit opposing traces vs equally resourced generic two-store and selector under reversal/dormant-return schedules. | No improvement over matched two-store, or selector needs future task/phase truth. |
| 5 | **F08 reward-prediction feedback — not justified for current byte task** | Bennett offers a real computable MB RPE loop, but E16 has supervised answer error, not reward. | Only after defining a consequence-only stream: compare same-information recurrent RPE loop vs matched standard reward residual teacher. | Difference disappears under matched reward signal or requires privileged answer bytes. |

**Costs** are included for each shortlisted candidate in the machine-readable evidence register: raw stream O(BL) bytes; F13 O(S_anchor) weights/traces; F09 O(S_teacher + S_student + S_feedback); F11 O(n_+ + n_- + p_selector); F08 O(S_KC−MBON + S_feedback + K_modulator). These are **symbolic counts**, not arbitrary claims of fixed numeric parameter budgets; all preprocessing, routing, masks, and sample-selection memory must be included.

## 5. What remains unknown and prevents a 'complete verified' claim

1. Figure-level primary-paper reading is *not complete* for all 35 registered sources. In the claim register, an explicit field distinguishes a checked full-text/figure/equation/retraction from a paper available only at abstract or metadata level. A DOI alone is **never** marked full-text validation.
2. For most F01–F20 entries, an *exact implementation of the biological mechanism* is **UNKNOWN**, even where a mathematically related MiniFly operation is verified. Unsupported 'already tested' declarations have been replaced with explicit code-level and receipt-level classifications and separate lineages.
3. The relative causal contributions of replay coverage, replay target quality, cell credit allocation, format-switch address changes and readout contamination remain unresolved. The E16-V/E16W completed receipts constrain explanations but do not uniquely discriminate them.
4. The conditional architectural ideas above should not be represented as **'test now'**. **No additional biological mechanism is yet justified for immediate implementation** from this evidence alone; decisions should respect the currently running/unfinished replay campaign.

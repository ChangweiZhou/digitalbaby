# V151-R1 — revision after the three-cycle exercise

Status: completed bounded diagnostic and interpretation revision. No new run, core adoption, addition assessment or automatic continuation is authorized.

## Resolved question

The tested Full151/R_center stores can execute these local teaching directions, including in V150's trained states. The native value response was diagonal and linear to numerical precision over the tested interventions. A scalar gain of about two transported well enough to recover B's large same-nominal-rate disadvantage. The hypothesis of a generally broken native gradient-execution interface is not supported by these cases.

Do not turn that conclusion into a claim that V150's entire failure was caused by gain. We intervened once on a fixed state, not on the training process that created it. Matched B's advantage over A was small, and its actual output displacement was not perfectly equal. The result motivates correcting the dose comparison, not declaring a superior kernel.

## Remaining problem

All 16 trained source cases had lower target loss after teaching, but none selected the correct target class. The native-only write supplied much more immediate improvement than the code-only step. Some different-target peers were harmed, and same-target peer responses depended on address sharing. Immediate descent is therefore not equivalent to acquiring, retaining or generalizing an answer through a sequence of competing writes.

The empty prefix is a separate, concrete null address in this configuration. It must remain visible in generic I/O testing. Do not attribute nonempty addition failures to it or silently add a tonic/BOS feature under the guise of a gain adjustment.

## Next experiment direction, to be designed separately

Return to the addition goal rather than extending the text/operator diagnostic indefinitely. Use fresh canonical native births and one fixed generic byte stream. Keep the current A rule as a control, B at its old rate as a control, and a simple fixed-factor B condition as the focused candidate. A factor of two has an analytical cold-start rationale and is supported by the V151 calibration range 1.9635–1.9980; it is an amplitude control, not an arithmetic prior or a learned response-matrix controller.

Specify the factor before a new learning history. Do not tune it per target or use held-out loss. Keep the exact gradual warmup counter and include every calibration/warmup/repeated presentation in the budget. Freeze code C initially: the code-factorial gain was too small, and learned-code necessity remains unproven, to justify changing both components at once.

The causal question should be narrow: after the same paid feedback, is an already taught addition answer acquired as the model's own byte/EOS output, and does it survive later, conflicting teaching? Track exact acquisition immediately after teaching, after a fixed delay, and after prescribed interference. Split loss, own-answer accuracy and class margins; do not use a reduction in loss as a substitute for an exact answer. Do not replay V150 trajectories or inherit COV-R/U042 efficacy into this runtime without implementing and testing their actual paths.

Only an explicitly bounded addition design with meaningful acquisition can justify its already prespecified never-taught test. Keep that holdback unopened until its conditions are met. An exact-key recitation control may explain E1 acquisition; E3 requires first responses on never-reinforced relations, delayed/intervening learning and a prior-teaching causal control. No arithmetic parser, relations, commutative augmentation, number features, attention, Transformer or ELM enters the learner.

## Limits of the revision

This document does not fix a new roster, sample count, efficacy threshold or authorization for that next experiment. Those belong in the separately reviewed addition design. If exact acquisition still fails under the gain-corrected comparison, use its temporal failure pattern to distinguish weak acquisition from interference before choosing a representation/memory change. Do not respond by widening the output code or running an unbounded rate/replay sweep.

V151 ends here with a positive E0/limited E1 mechanism result and an unresolved addition objective. Historical designs, V150 checkpoints, the active arithmetic pointer and user adoption records remain unchanged.

# V154 revision after three cycles

**Status: completed bounded development exercise; no core adopted. This revision is a proposal, not authorization for another run.** Claude's V153 and the provisional V153 design are preserved. The report, scientific audit and closure audit accompany this document.

The endpoint remains **retained complete own-byte/EOS generalization to never-taught addition per all training samples and actual compute**. Keep gradual warmup, local candidate updates and the native lineage. No arithmetic parser, ordinal digit feature, sum/carry rule, answer mask, forced EOS, attention, Transformer or ELM replacement is proposed. Expected answer bytes remain legitimate feedback during warmup.

## What the exercise changed

1. **The native arms are not uniformly stuck at empty EOS.** Permutation arms acquired small numbers of complete taught answers. Identity arms retained none at both phase ends in both worlds. Immediate correctness was 848/15,360 updating presentations overall, not high taught-item mastery.
2. **That acquisition did not become reproducible useful unseen learning.** N_2B/P_SLOW scored W/SH_OLD = 3/1 out of 50 in world 5401 and 0/0 in world 5402. N_A/P_SLOW scored 1/0 and 0/0. N_2B/P_FAST scored 3/1 in world 5401 but failed acquisition admission in world 5402; its second unseen score is missing, not zero. The first-world positives exceeded their own registered W frequency counters. They do not establish a robust gain or improved sample/compute efficiency.
3. **No exact incompatible-target collision was found in the recorded raw RHO, X or OBS views.** This does not establish that the representations are adequate, well conditioned, linearly decodable or sufficient for complete generation. It removes one specific demonstrated-collision explanation in this assay.
4. **Output family and finite optimization matter to probability loss.** On P_SLOW/OBS, qualified batch full-port validation loss was 2.1845/2.3236 BPS across the worlds; matched C16-family loss was 3.8678/3.2634. Their next-byte argmax accuracies were nevertheless similar. Local C16 loss was 4.6981/4.5056. This is evidence about the declared output geometry, parameter penalty, scaling and finite learning process. It is not proof that a larger decoder would improve complete native arithmetic. All twelve identity-front full-port batch fits were unresolved, so they cannot support a ceiling claim.
5. **The old recurrent EOS failure is co-adapted.** Full V152 checkpoints terminated immediately on all 576 anchor occasions. Removing q produced CAP on all 576 and recovered zero complete answers. Q-only behavior also differed across checkpoints. Bias affects stopping at the learned state, but a bias ban does not follow; no Q0 retraining was performed.

These observations do not justify another broad search through front-end settings or a return to noise handling. They also do not justify calling the current native wrapper equivalent to the strongest historical memorization configuration: N_A here is the precise V152 equation and registered coefficient.

## Next mechanism question: what does a signed native write actually do?

Prioritize a **native write-to-read transfer audit**, before choosing a new architecture. This addresses the gap between a categorical error vector and the memory circuit's actual change. It uses generic bytes and the unchanged circuit equations, not arithmetic relations.

Let `v(m,x)` be the sixteen expressed native values at the registered read time; let `F(m,x,a)` be the actual signed-write transition including its event schedule, fast/slow stores and subsequent read. The current categorical feedback is

`g = P Cᵀ(p − e_y)/4`, with `a = 0.2 s(n) g`.

Define the local response operator using the actual teaching-sign convention:

`K(m,x) = −∂v(F(m,x,a),x)/∂a | a=0`.

At a matched no-write reference read, write-dependent loss change is approximately `−g_refᵀ K a`, where g_ref is the categorical value gradient at that reference read. With the current command it becomes `−0.2 s(n) g_refᵀ K g`. It reduces to a `−gᵀ K g` form only when the teaching and reference gradients coincide; native timing and decay must be checked rather than assumed. It is not automatically `−||g||²`. The registered one-context displacement norms varied by front; they did not identify K, its signs, conditioning, saturation or behavior over an entire history. A categorical gradient upstream of a non-unit native write is not by itself a proof of a categorical gradient step in expressed values.

The next design should therefore:

- Derive K and the relevant finite-step remainder from the pinned native equations, with the exact write/read timing. Identify which factors are locally available at a compartment or synapse. Do not infer K from arithmetic accuracy or compensate for it with an outcome-selected rate grid.
- Qualify the derivation against bounded generic-byte fixtures at declared warmup counters, signs, activity levels and store states. Distinguish a synthetic counter setting from an actual learned warmup history. Count every observation and retain every attempt.
- Check whether the existing coefficient gives the predicted direction and bounded finite response. Also check cross-context interference and decay: a correct one-context derivative is not retention or generalization.
- Propose **at most one** changed local writer only if the derivation identifies a concrete mismatch and supplies a stable bounded correction. Preserve its unchanged parent as a comparator. No change to the output codebook, input representation, bias policy and writer should be bundled into one causal contrast.
- If the existing transfer is already correct and well conditioned, retain that negative result. Do not manufacture a gain-normalization mechanism. Reassess the fixed output-family/calibration restriction separately, because the present BPS gap does not establish an argmax/sequence benefit.

This is a design prerequisite, not a license to run additional trajectories now. A future candidate still has to earn improvement on retained own-generated addition answers, against the unchanged parent and branch-specific frequency controls, at the same total exposure and actual compute. A successful derivative check earns only E0.

## Protocol refinements before any later execution

**Operations:** give cumulative CPU and cell CPU distinct schema fields. Smoke-test one synthetic end-to-end cell commit, summary and audit path before real feedback. Commit source, score files and receipt before journaling completion; after an interruption, recover a hash-verified completed cell rather than replaying it. Preserve the original defect and clocks. V154's completed first fit/cache were recovered this way through a separately locked operations adapter; zero native histories and zero completed fits were repeated.

**Encoding diagnostics:** retain the separately promised raw trace magnitude, in addition to operative and normalized taps, when collecting a new authorized dataset. The omission in V154 is disclosed and will not be repaired by replay. Keep exact raw collisions distinct from normalization-induced ambiguity and finite decoder failures. An equal tap need not imply equality of every trained native internal state or elapsed-time effect.

**Output diagnostics:** compare probability calibration and next-byte decisions separately. Within a family, retain matched objectives and exposure. Across full/C16 families, explicitly account for the induced function-space penalty and logit scale. Do not call either batch fit an information ceiling or use it to answer for the fly.

**Readiness and assessment:** V154's minimal improvement gate admitted a case with only 1/64 NEW answers; it was an assay gate, not a success threshold. Do not change it retrospectively or reopen closed assessments. For a later small, justified native roster, precommit whether to score every E0-qualified arm once on fresh held orbits, avoiding a selectively observed factorial; if a cost-based acquisition gate is retained, publish its selection pattern and missing cells. Do not invent a higher accuracy threshold from this run.

**Evidence and cost:** publish E0, E1 and conditional descriptive E3 separately. Use worlds as the paired units; branches, targets and repeats are correlated. Report native training, evaluator queries, feature collection, optimizer target reuse, audits, clone movement and global sorting. State the actual KC dimension (5,177 here). The current implementation is not a scalability demonstration.

## Stop and delivery decision

The requested three cycles are complete and audited. Current evidence supports a native input-path acquisition effect and identifies concrete output/write questions; it does **not** establish better unseen-addition generalization per sample or compute. Preserve the completed data and sources. Do not adopt an arm, extend the run, retune on held scores, introduce a new task or launch a replacement experiment automatically. The next deliverable should be a derived and auditable single-mechanism proposal, not another unbounded redesign.

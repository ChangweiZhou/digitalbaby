# MiniFly mechanism sweep design memo

**November 6, 2026 · For Mr. Zhou · Proposed engineering screen**

Run one parallel campaign to find which mechanisms add a useful capability at MiniFly scale. The leading question is whether a small model can use an internal state or repeat a learned computation to solve a new combination. The companion questions are whether a mechanism improves learning from delayed feedback, protects useful knowledge during later learning, or supports closed-loop navigation. Give each mechanism a task on which it could matter and a comparison that could rule out its proposed advantage. Compare mechanisms within their tasks. Keep a common evidence and cost ledger across the campaign.

The survey ranks eleven motifs. The roster contains **ten candidate comparisons and one attention reference block**. Mr. Zhou authorized attention as a baseline for this sweep. The candidate working-memory gate controls when a fixed state register is written and has no content-addressed selection. Fast memory is a weight path with a fixed readout, and replay uses chronological storage with fixed eviction. The cerebellar analogue uses plastic temporal features and an online local update, preserving the exclusion of ELM. ## Comparisons

Each candidate block has a candidate, a matched reference and a targeted control. The attention block compares a reference with two controls. Run each block on three paired seeds at one dose. Compact analogues use the motif to test a specific computation. The central-complex arm uses the available v630 anatomical extract.

## Fixtures and doses

- **Byte learning.** Use up to **12,288 updates at batch 64** per history. Start from comparable initial competence. Qualify no-distraction arithmetic separately before testing delays. When a task inserts random distractor bytes, all arms receive those bytes and the loss applies only to the answer and EOS. The composition fixture teaches each primitive and selected composed cases, then holds out whole operator-order templates. In held pairs, preserve the initial state and the multiset of operators, reverse their order, and require both distinct answers to be right. Freeze **64 held pairs** per seed. The oracle checks that the order change matters. [Prior mod-97 harness](../minifly-remaining-experiments/OFFICIAL_REPORT.md) · [Prior composition fixture and controls](MINIFLY_REASONING_SCALING_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN_20261106.md)
- **Delayed reward.** Use **4,096 episodes** with at most **24 steps** per episode and **64 held pairs** per seed. Match initial observations, opportunities, horizon and action space across arms. Deliver one binary outcome after the learner's committed choice. The PFC task tests an earlier cue through interference. The actor–critic task tests assignment of a delayed outcome to an earlier action. Probes provide neither updates nor feedback. Record every outcome and effective write.
- **Continual association.** Give each history **1,024 A presentations**, **256 B presentations**, then **128 A′ presentations**. Teach B on disjoint associations and reverse a designated subset of A during A′ while leaving the other A outcomes valid. Freeze **64 tested associations per slice** where the fixture supports them. Evaluate acquisition, after B and after A′. Keep the writable model and optimizer state throughout. From the post-A checkpoint, replay the B inputs and elapsed time with writes disabled in a read-only diagnostic fork. This separates elapsed time and input activity from the effect of later writes. Record unique associations, external teaching and internal replay separately. [Prior continued-life and restart fixtures](../minifly-recurrent-worked-examples/CYCLES.md)
- **Navigation.** Use **64 held trajectories per seed** and separate calibration fixtures. Pair starts, goals, observation noise and simulated time. The steering check uses up to **80 steps at 50 ms**. The displacement check uses up to **200 steps**. Normalize displacement error by path length on moving cases with at least **0.25 arena widths** of travel and score zero-motion drift separately. Set an analytic positive control on the same inputs to test the scoring and the feasibility of each trajectory. Replay order reversals and zero-motion cases. Use simulator truth for scoring, with any goal or observed self-motion supplied to a circuit documented in the interface. [Existing v630 runner and controls](../minifly-connectome-navigation/qualify_dynamics.py)

Use the same three seeds across paired arms. Separate fixture development, calibration and final evaluation with domain-separated seeds. Start from paired random births by default. Any warmup uses the same permitted observations across paired arms and counts toward the exposure ledger. Split unordered operand pairs, cue identities or structural templates across the entire history, including warmup and replay, whenever the claim depends on their novelty. Save an exposure audit and run exact and canonical lookup, frequency, recency and task-specific shortcut controls. For composition, include order-insensitive and single-operation policies. For delayed state, hold the final observations constant across opposite earlier cues. The scoring model gets the same checkpoint for both members of every pair, generates its own output before feedback and leaves weights unchanged.

Match writable capacity, observation and teaching budgets within each causal comparison. Aim for a **60,000-weight compact reference** where compatible with the task. Report actual active and mutable weights, fixed wiring, replay storage, optimizer bytes and operations per event. Where a comparator intentionally changes work, such as one pass versus three, keep the work-matched comparison alongside it and disclose both costs. Full-error teaching, signed reverse feedback and scalar outcomes answer separate questions. Exact feedback or a gradient-trained reference earns a claim under that signal budget. ## Decision rules

Qualify in three steps. First check the oracle, input access, reward delivery and shortcuts. Then compare each mechanism with its controls on development data and verify the permitted feedback path. Finally profile one complete training and evaluation block, including an exact checkpoint restart and a read-only scoring check. For a learning task, require a capable reference to reach **48/64** on the same type of held pairs at the planned dose. A supervised ceiling can check whether the input is sufficient. A reward-only reference needs to show that the outcome arrives often enough and supports acquisition before interpreting a failed reward-only mechanism. If the reference stays at the floor, classify the block as **unqualified** and budget one repair on a fresh engineering fixture in this sweep. Freeze code, dose, comparisons and thresholds before opening the campaign panel. Other blocks continue independently.

- **Byte learning and delayed reward.** A candidate earns follow-up at **48/64** or better and at least **seven additional correct pairs** over its designated causal control in each seed, with both answers required for paired cases. For the temporal-prediction row, a case is an exact prediction on a held cue and lag. For cortical feedback, exact backpropagation is a reference and random feedback is the designated control. For the loop, require a gain over the three-pass reference before attributing it to the feedback topology. For the gate and actor–critic rows, require a gain over both controls.
- **Continual association.** Require at least **48/64** correct on the original A acquisition panel. After B, require at least **70% retention** among cases originally correct in both arms and a **10-point improvement** over the designated matched reference in each seed. Use at least 32 jointly acquired cases to interpret that comparison and report unconditional old scores too. After A′, the candidate may give up at most **5 points** on B acquisition or A′ correction relative to the reference. For sleep replay, prioritize matched interleaving. For the two-store model, require a gain over the single-store reference and use the two-store control to attribute it to coupling.
- **Navigation.** For the central-complex row, require median final goal error **≤20°** and at least **seven more** starts within 20° than either control in each seed. For the grid row, require median displacement error **≤20%** of travelled distance and at least a **20% relative reduction** versus ordinary recurrence in each seed. A recurrence lesion and zero-motion drift help distinguish stable integration from an output shortcut.

Report each seed and the weakest margin. Three seeds supply a screen for practical gains. Results can be **advance**, **no gain at this dose**, **unqualified**, **held**, or **reference only**. Heading and motion plumbing are E0, and taught association, retention and reversal are E1. Changed presentations can support E2. For an E3 claim, reserve genuinely new relations, beat the registered shortcuts, and confirm with a same-birth branch that receives the same observations and timing without the relevant prior teaching. The sweep can nominate that confirmation. Any persistent-core claim also needs the delayed and intervening-learning probes on the same operative learner. ## Budget and deliverables

**99 planned histories**, from eleven blocks × three arms × three seeds. The estimate is **6–18 CPU-hours**, including a **25% reserve**, or **2–5 hours wall-clock** with about six single-threaded workers on the current M3 Pro. The allowance covers compact simulation and scoring. Preparation and qualification of missing adapters take separate engineering time. Path integration, delayed reward and replay are the least certain cost estimates. Profile representative candidate and control jobs on engineering fixtures, including evaluation and restart, and revise the forecast before allocating the full dose. [Measured MiniFly runtime anchors](../minifly-remaining-experiments/OFFICIAL_REPORT.md#accounting) · [Parallel-run accounting](../minifly-ordered-front-end/CAMPAIGN_REPORT.md#run-receipts)

The implementation package should contain a roster and source hashes, executable arm and fixture definitions, one checkpoint receipt per phase, raw predictions or trajectories, timing and feedback ledgers, and a one-page scorecard of all eleven entries. Record skipped, invalid and capped runs separately. Keep any follow-up at a second dose, larger width or combined architecture in a separate roster so the first sweep still identifies what each mechanism contributed.

# V156 — three-cycle diagnostic report

**Verdict: finite taught decisions are feasible; native acquisition remains unreliable. No unseen-addition improvement is established.**

The three authorized cycles completed without changing the native learner, its gradual warmup or the fixed roster. All case audits and the final saved-artifact audit were accepted. No assessment labels were scored, no core was adopted, and nothing was pushed to GitHub.

## What changed in the diagnosis

The native writer is not simply reversed or inactive: its own full-record teaching improved **178/178 required target margins** relative to the matched no-write fork. Yet **0/64 just-taught NEW records** became a complete correct own-byte/EOS answer immediately. Helpful local increments are not sufficient acquisition.

Every sampled frozen feature matrix had full numerical row rank and admitted a verified C16-without-bias witness; the nested C16+bias and full-linear families also passed. All **1,536/1,536 evaluator-only taught rollouts** completed correctly, and all 4,392 correct-prefix feature rows matched their caches exactly. These free batch interpolants demonstrate finite taught separability, not native-store reachability, efficient learning, arithmetic laws or E3 transfer.

The retention hypothesis also needs narrowing: all **15 initially correct OLD answers** in the two slow-permutation starts were correct at the final continuation and delay probes. There were transient write-associated losses, but this run does not demonstrate permanent loss of those acquired OLD answers. The identity starts had no acquired OLD answers, so retention was unmeasurable there.

These losses count transition events, not distinct lost lives or permanent forgotten records. Write-associated gains were 6 and 4 in the slow-permutation cases. Other-record margins improved as well as worsened; it would be wrong to describe every competing write as destructive. Matched observed-minus-idle effects averaged near floating-point roundoff. Time-only evolution changed margins and could improve an answer, but caused no complete-answer losses in these sampled transitions. This does not prove time constants or adaptation never matter.

The exact telescoping decomposition passed independent recomputation. The decomposition uses the preregistered order and reference; it is not a stationary learning kernel or a unique biological causal factorization.

### Shared preferences and clipping

Both identity-front starts had an EOS-dominant panel-common logit vector at q=0. Empty-EOS counts were **80→80** and **64→78** of 80. Therefore an explicit bias parameter is not necessary for collapse. The slow-permutation starts had no empty EOS; their common preferred first byte was class 49, and many continuation digits were wrong despite generally correct true-EOS decisions. Suppressing EOS alone cannot explain or repair both patterns.

No gamma/MBON clipping was recorded at the sampled teacher-prefix reads. That rules out observed read-time saturation at those positions, not clipping in every hypothetical write evaluation or at every earlier warmup event. The common vector is panel-dependent; no component was subtracted from a native policy.

## Three cycles and revisions

1. **Design/audit/technical fixtures.** Qualified the signed native path, warmup counters, all-byte/EOS scorer, matched passive clocks, seconds-versus-steps idle API, immutable reads, independent witnesses and mutation rejection. No arithmetic rule entered the learner.
2. **First reused world.** Executed each declared front/family/native continuation once. The identity start had no learned OLD answers; the slow start showed transient write losses and modest net gain. Kept the other world’s settings frozen rather than reacting to its outcomes.
3. **Second reused world and closure.** Repeated the registered comparison on its other world, not the first trajectories. All feasibility witnesses replicated; native complete acquisition remained poor. Revised the next proposal away from automatic port repair or permanent-memory protection, toward a matched exposure-order test.

See [revised design](REVISED_DESIGN.md). It is a proposal only. A numerical witness does not justify installing an external decoder, and a local margin improvement does not justify another large confirmation.

## Exposure, recitation and evidence level

Lane F earns **E0** finite decision/interface evidence; Lane M earns **E1** descriptive acquisition/retention evidence. OLD and NEW labels came only from the prior observed data. Validation/assessment rows were filtered out before fitting and panel selection. No held accuracy, arithmetic generalization effect, E2/E3 promotion or sample-efficiency gain is claimed.

Each loaded OLD state already contained eight passes over 64 OLD records. Its true stores, clock and n were preserved; this exercise did not restart or accelerate warmup, and does not diagnose the initial birth-to-EOS-collapse trajectory. An exact-key table could recite the taught panel; logged raw-key, favorite and position counters remain evaluator-only. All native responses were generated through unchanged Full151/R_center N_2B and the fixed C16 port, with all 257 classes and its own EOS.

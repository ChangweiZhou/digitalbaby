## Reading (the brief's readings, applied)

**1. P24.1 and P24.2 hold.**
- Here, backprop learns from every byte from nothing. It loses 1.7–3.5 points against its answer-target baseline.
- The sign sweep does not. It ends at 0.8–1.2, and its max/min mean of 13.8–23.8 is below the operand-copy level of 50.
- The penalties differ by 81.8–86.2 points, all three seeds agreeing.
- In the brief's terms, this is an interaction between objective and configuration, in this network at this dose.
- The two configurations also differ in weight decay (0.30 against 0.25), so the update is not isolated.
- The next step the brief names is to look at what the sign feedback does with targets that cannot be predicted.

**2. T-all learns at first, then collapses.**
- It reaches 7.8–14.7 at 8,192 updates, then falls to about 1 by 12,288, where it stays.
- That is a loss of learning, not a failure to start. It is descriptive and not one of the registered marks.

**3. P24.3 holds, and here the weighting does help.**
- T-bal minus T-all is +80.7, +78.8 and +82.2 points; the three seeds agree, and the gains are large.
- Revision 3 asks that a gain be attributed to the weighting only where T-bal is above T-all in the same seed. That holds in every seed.
- So, in this configuration, giving the answer bytes half the loss restores learning, to within 1.4–10.9 points of the answer-only sign sweep.
- This says nothing about where such a weighting could come from without an answer mask. The weighting uses the mask; it is a diagnostic, not a mechanism.

**4. Embedding movement by role and the credit census** are in the tables.
- At the first update, every operator, digit, equals sign, letter and task code received a gradient in every new birth, under both updates.
- At the end, T-all has moved its embeddings about as far as the others: operator rows 0.47–0.77, against D-all's 0.61–0.90. The collapse is not a frozen table.

**5. The baselines cross no host class.**
- D-ans was born on this host class and equals E20-B's G0 births.
- T-ans uses the cloud-born originals, one of which was reproduced bit for bit here.
- The owner's-machine D-ans births (91.2 / 94.5 / 90.3) are not used.

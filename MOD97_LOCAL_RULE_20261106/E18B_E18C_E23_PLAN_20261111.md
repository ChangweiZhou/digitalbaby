## Step 3 — E23: the time scale of the upper bank's target

Owner's design: the lower bank predicts the near observation; compare the upper bank predicting **the same near target** with the upper bank predicting **a later observation**, matched in target count and feedback budget, plus a time-shuffled control. Implementation (audit findings 1–3):

- **Base H0** = B2 with `E16_HEAD1LOSS=all`: the first bank's head is trained on **every next byte** (lower bank predicts near); the main head keeps the answer loss (it is what generates answers). Without this the lower bank would predict only answer bytes.
- The upper bank gets an extra head (`E16_FARHEAD=d`, reads the second bank's state only; its error reaches the second bank alone — checked), trained on the byte `d` steps ahead **at prompt-byte targets only**: a position whose far target is an answer digit or EOS is excluded in every arm, so no arm gets extra answer supervision.
- `E16_FARCTRL=shuffle` permutes those targets among the same item's valid positions at every update (own generator).

`d = 4` fixed in advance, not tuned. Matching: N1, F4 and F4s have the same heads, parameters, rule and loss weight; F4 has about 3 fewer valid targets per item than N1 (782 vs 590 targets on the check batch of 64), averaged loss so equal weight. The far targets inside a prompt are mostly unpredictable (distractor letters, operand digits); both F4 and N1 carry such targets, N1 fewer. **Caveat registered in advance:** if F4 < N1, target entropy under sign feedback (review 9.88's unchecked hypothesis) explains it as well as timescale. A cross-item control is not run.

**Credit horizon vs target time.** Held fixed here (credit through time at every layer, `E16_TRACEUP=all`). The crossing with short credit is **NOT_RUN**: designed only, its value depends on E18b's B3-all.

**Endpoints.** Hard mean and max/min mean (main head, k = 12, final), checkpoint-mean hard mean, CPU-s; diagnostic only: far head's teacher-forced top-1 on prompt targets and on digit targets, against a structural baseline (most frequent target given input-byte class × target class, computed on the same items).

- **P23.1** F4 final hard mean ≥ N1's + 5 in ≥ 2 seeds. *~20%.*
- **P23.2** F4 final hard mean ≥ F4s's + 5 in ≥ 2 seeds. *~25%.*

- **P23.4** H0 final hard mean within 10 of B2's in ≥ 2 seeds (every-byte targets on the first head alone do not damage the package). *~55%.*

## Step 2 — E18c: the relay between banks as the only difference

Question (mechanism 1): with the two heads, the losses, the parameter count and the sign rule with credit through time all held fixed, what does letting the error pass between banks change?

E18's comparison B0 vs B1 changed heads, losses, rule and relay together. The harness already has the one-factor switch `E16_HEADS=aux` (`arch17.LOCAL_DETACH=False`: first bank's head kept, relay kept), verified by `e18_check.py`'s leak fixture.

| Cell | Environment (all: `E16_ARCH=bank2 E16_BOOKDECAY=off E16_BIRTHRULE=T E16_TRACEUP=all`) | Birth stem | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| B2 (cut) | `E16_HEADS=local` | `_lh_bT_tall` | reused from step 1 (same host) |
| A2 (relay) | `E16_HEADS=aux` | `_ah_bT_tall` | `detach` removed at the two inter-bank reads; nothing else |

Then one life from each birth (6 lives), in the copy folder, so that each life starts from the same-host birth:
`E16_REPLAY=raw E16_CAP=65536 E16_OUTTAG=_e18c` + the cell's environment, `python3 exp16_integrated.py T <seed>` (the E22 life command with the heads option added). Lives score at every block: F1 (mod-97, taught first), F2 (operator pairs), F3 (mod-73), held compositions, the first bank's head.

**Endpoints.** Births: hard mean (add89, sub89 at k = 12) and max/min mean, first bank's head, CPU-s. Lives: F1 mean at k = 12 after block 6 (after F2 and F3 interfere) and its ratio to F1 after block 2; F3 after block 6; held compositions after block 6; CPU-s.

**Predictions (E18C_PREDICTIONS.md, committed before the first A2 birth).**
- **PC.1** A2 hard mean ≥ B2 hard mean + 5 in at least two seeds. *Held at ~60%:* E18's B0 − B1 package gap was 7–9 points; part of it may be heads and loss.
- **PC.2** A2 birth CPU-s within 15% of B2's on this host in every seed (the relay is not a compute cost at this scale). *~80%.*
- **PC.3** After block 6 of the life, A2's F1 mean ≥ B2's + 3 in at least two seeds. *~50%.*
- **PC.4** A2's first-bank head add89 at birth ≥ B2's + 10 in at least two seeds (the relay changes what the first bank learns, not just the top). *~60%.*

Reading, before results: PC.1 holding says the relay itself is worth points when everything else is fixed; PC.1 missing (|gap| < 5) says E18's 7–9 point package gap came from heads/loss/rule, not from the relay. PC.3 is the only E2 endpoint here; births alone do not show retention.

## Step 3 — E23: the time scale of the upper bank's target

Question (mechanism 2): given that bank 1 predicts the next byte, does training bank 2 (also) to predict a **later** byte help its answers, beyond an equal extra target at the next byte, and does it depend on the **correspondence in time** or only on the bytes?

New option (this job): `E16_FARHEAD=d` gives the second bank a third head (`lmf`, `l3f`: C→H→257, 196,865 extra parameters, the same in every E23 arm) reading the second bank's state only, trained at every position on the byte `d` steps ahead, loss weight 1, under the same sign rule with credit through time. With `E16_HEADS=local`, its error reaches the second bank alone (checked). `E16_FARCTRL=shuffle` permutes the far targets among the valid positions of the same item at every update: same bytes, same count, same head, timing destroyed. Main head and first head keep the answer loss of B2.

| Cell | Extra environment on top of B2's | Stem | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | `E16_FARHEAD=1` | `_lh_bT_tall_fh1` | extra head at the **near** target: matched target count and feedback budget |
| F4 | `E16_FARHEAD=4` | `_lh_bT_tall_fh4` | extra head **four bytes ahead** (from the second operand's last digit: the answer's second digit's successor; from mid-prompt: later prompt bytes) |
| F4s | `E16_FARHEAD=4 E16_FARCTRL=shuffle` | `_lh_bT_tall_fh4s` | time correspondence shuffled |

`d = 4` is fixed in advance and not tuned; no sweep is run. B2 (step 1, same host) is the no-extra-head reference.

**Credit horizon vs target time.** Mr. Xu's note asks to separate when the target arrives from how far error can travel back. This job holds credit fixed (credit through time at every layer, `E16_TRACEUP=all`) and varies only the target time. The crossing with short credit (`E16_TRACEUP=none`) is **NOT_RUN** here: at answer loss that rule learns nothing (B3), so its cell is uninformative until E18b's B3-all says whether short credit learns at all under denser targets. Designed, not authorized as part of this job.

**Endpoints.** Hard mean and max/min mean (main head, k = 12, whole answers); far head's teacher-forced top-1 on its own unshuffled target (all positions; answer-byte positions) as a diagnostic only; CPU-s.

**Predictions (E23_PREDICTIONS.md, committed before the first E23 birth).**
- **P23.1** F4 hard mean ≥ N1 hard mean + 5 in at least two seeds. *~30%.*
- **P23.2** F4 hard mean ≥ F4s hard mean + 5 in at least two seeds. *~40%.*
- **P23.3** (diagnostic) F4's far head top-1 on answer-byte positions ≥ F4s's + 10 in every seed. *~70%.*
- **P23.4** N1 hard mean within 10 of B2's (same host) in at least two seeds (an extra every-byte head under the sign rule does not break the second bank). *~50%;* bears on review 9.88's unchecked hypothesis.

Decision table, before results: P23.1 and P23.2 hold → the later target, with its timing, is worth a life test and the credit-horizon crossing; P23.2 holds, P23.1 misses → timing matters but no more than a near target; P23.1 and P23.2 miss → no benefit from a later target in this package at this dose: park mechanism 2 for arithmetic births; P23.4 misses → every-byte heads under sign feedback damage the bank they train, and E23 cannot be read as a timescale test.

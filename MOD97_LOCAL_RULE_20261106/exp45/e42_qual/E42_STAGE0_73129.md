**The faster forward.** It changes only how the computation is arranged. (i) The input-side period sums G(t) are formed for all positions at once,
from gi padded with zeros before the start symbol. (ii) The recurrent side is one crossing per position. Each cell adds that crossing, times
c[k] for its step k = (f_i − t) mod p_i, to a running sum. The running sum restarts after each of the cell's updates. So at the cell's update
positions, the only positions where its value is kept, it equals the spec's Σ_k c[k]·crossing(t − k). In `source` the crossing at position t
uses, for each cell, that cell's row of the step-k matrix. These composite matrices repeat with period K, so K of them are built once per
forward. (iii) The masked matrix, and under the sweep its sign times the spec's own scale, are formed once per forward. `G` runs
`arch26.forward` itself.

# Address capacity V1 static design review

Status: design consistency review only. No implementation, native trajectory, saved-response analysis, head fit or three-cycle exercise has been executed for this proposal. Runtime qualification is still required before a future pilot.

## Evidence and scope

The proposal preserves the completed 32-pair stopping confirmation and distinguishes conditional stopping from complete OLD and NEW recovery. It does not combine the older joint-head ranking feasibility result with STOP-ONLY. CREDIT's null remains a result about its barely exercised gate, not a family-wide exclusion. All proposed behavioral tests remain E1; exact-key recitation can solve their taught random associations.

The submitted feedback is preserved verbatim in RECEIVED_REVIEW.md. EVIDENCE.json identifies the consulted reports and executable parent definitions by hash. Those evidence hashes are provenance, not a claim that a new runtime dependency closure has already been qualified.

## Changes made during review

The native contrast now explicitly expands B and every N-shaped state, retains original side-specific activity limits, and removes the old population-ratio gain rule. It defines seeded input construction and unlabelled gain calibration instead of leaving “more cells, same activity” to an incompatible size helper. The first block is retained before declared calibration, not falsely described as numerically unchanged afterward.

The calibration matches mean output drive only. Self-write and cross-write measurements remain required, and the proposal expressly limits size-only attribution when teacher/readout coupling differs. Extra cells are an engineering extension whose wiring is part of the intervention.

All saved-response readouts preserve real payload signs. The EOS FOREIGN control moves only the tensor supplied to stopping, including its skip input. Every conditional comparison uses the same real-payload eligibility denominator. Fixed-length references are all reported, with no outcome-selected winner.

The native pilot has one candidate and one reference; warmup timing, teaching weights and external heads are unchanged. The behavior screen requires better OLD and no observed NEW regression in every pair. It is not a statistical noninferiority guarantee. Overlap, recruitment balance and self-gain cannot substitute for those endpoints.

The earlier wording called the saved stopping analysis E0; it was corrected to E1 analysis of taught associations. E0 is reserved for the proposed technical fixtures.

## Limits requiring runtime qualification

Unlabelled gain ratios may be invalid, positive-score recruitment may fall short of the fixed caps, or the added inputs may offer little effective diversity. The proposal freezes the graph rather than searching for a favorable replacement. The same frozen EOS head may respond poorly to expanded-state tensors. All are reportable outcomes, not permission to change the recipe after observing values.

The native implementation still projects and sorts globally and sweeps cell state. Measured CPU, memory traffic proxies, persistent storage, peak RSS and copy/audit costs accompany any gain. Fixed active count is not evidence of computational sparsity or better compute efficiency.

A favorable pilot would justify discussing a fresh confirmation of this storage extension. It would not qualify a persistent general core, host, learned address encoder or arithmetic transfer.

# MiniFly next-core budget v2

This is the operational implementation of `MINIFLY_NEXT_CORE_BUDGET_V2_20261006/EXPERIMENT.md`. The scientific model, byte interface, tasks and mechanisms are imported unchanged from `next_core_mechanism_v1_20261006`.

## Scope

The objective remains better retained/revised taught byte knowledge per unit online CPU, with limited unseen-input reuse as an optional upgrade target. Exact-key recitation is E1, not proof of E3 or autonomous learning. The existing prompted native-output interface and arriving-byte teaching remain explicit scaffolding. No NLP, scaling, new candidates, extra samples or AGI tasks are added.

The screen has 8 paired worlds, 11 physical configurations and 176 jobs. Only one configuration and one target may proceed to 48 fresh confirmation worlds. Maximum total is 656 jobs / 1,968 lives. Q_HALF shares ERROR learning and adds separately charged readout cost.

## Qualification

`qualify.py --cycle 1`, `--cycle 2`, `--cycle 3` and `--cycle final` execute development qualification only. The first cycle uses synthetic statistical cases; the second checks actual signal-pause/resume and final W export; the third checks complete lives, 4/8-worker parity and an accelerated real dispatcher deadline. Synthetic confirmation results never count as scientific evidence.

Historical full scientific-implementation qualification is reused. New tests do not re-execute the prior 29 full development jobs.

## Scientific launch gate

No science starts without current `results/QUALIFICATION.json`, `SOURCE_LOCK.json`, the historical world registry and a separately created `LAUNCH_AUTHORIZATION.json.gz` matching the frozen source and plan. The three-cycle request does not create that authorization. No GitHub upload is performed.

After explicit authorization, `pipeline.py` executes screen and at most one confirmation. It can finish with no candidate or RETAIN_V1. These are completed experiments. An unresolved result cannot extend n or try a replacement candidate.

## Safe sessions and failure policy

The dispatcher binds caffeinate to itself, stops dispatch at 8h, asks for record-boundary checkpoints at 9h, and forces unresponsive workers out at 9.5h. Analysis/audit/packaging also respect the session clock. Continuing the identical manifest in another window requires explicit `--resume-session`. Committed jobs are skipped; scientific failures do not restart automatically. A forced worker kill is STOP_NEEDS_REVIEW, never described as a safe pause.

S3_RAND legal-pool exhaustion is the only registered diagnostic-unavailable exception. Its partial record prefix and yoke provenance are preserved; no score is fabricated, and no S3 mechanism claim is permitted. Birth, source, write-branch or conservation failures stop execution.

Complete W states are exported before later no-write branches. They contain native state, predictor, frontend, mutable graph and RNG/yoke cursor, without pickle. Models are restored and their actual read-only outputs compared. Confirmation exports retain all world instances; examples use the lowest world ID.

All planning times are estimates, not a promise of completion in one ten-hour window. The enforced session limit is distinct from the cumulative scientific budget.

# Next-step assessment after SAMPLE-GEN V2

This is a proposal for discussion, not execution authorization or a frozen new protocol. Existing science and its gates remain unchanged.

## What the completed experiment establishes

The accepted result is bounded E3: learning a limited addition mapping from noisy/missing observed answer bytes, emitting exact answers on never-taught operand combinations after simulated delay and intervening learning. Identity/role features, an additive model class and ordinal answer rendering are disclosed engineering priors. This is not discovery of an arbitrary arithmetic operator or a general intelligent fly.

On the 64 opaque primary confirmation worlds, COV-R has sample-curve score 0.6825 versus COMPACT-ID 0.1884 (paired difference 0.4941; one-sided 95% lower bound 0.4553). Both OLD-write and shuffled-label causal lower bounds are positive. All frozen adoption gates pass. OLD final unseen-combination accuracy is 93.98%; NEW final accuracy is 60.70%.

The pooled NEW number conceals a material weakness:

| Opaque noise regime | Worlds | OLD final unseen | NEW after 80 examples |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| STANDARD: 80% correct, 10% wrong, 10% missing |32|99.38%|80.00%|
| BLURRY: 60% correct, 20% wrong, 20% missing |32|88.59%|41.41%|

OLD persistent performance almost matches immediate performance. This schedule does not demonstrate substantial forgetting as the remaining bottleneck. The difference between OLD (320 records) and NEW (80 records) is consistent with limited-sample acquisition being difficult, but those phases also differ in task/history; it does not isolate sample count as the sole cause.

The same-world DIAG-R comparison already exists: score 0.3172, OLD final 42.42%, NEW final 33.91%. The large COV/DIAG difference is diagnostic evidence favoring full input-correlation tracking in this recipe. No separate attribution rerun is necessary. It does not prove every diagonal/local rule must fail.

Production training CPU on the opaque population is about 0.0388 seconds per COV-R full job versus 0.0352 for COMPACT-ID. This measures instrumented training calls, not total experiment expense or future scaling. COV-R mutable payload is 14,690 bytes versus 7,374; full covariance has quadratic state/work in feature dimension. Worker RSS is about 200MB because fixed inherited assets/runtime and audit state are additional. The old dispatcher lock defect consumed wall time and is not core-learning cost.

## One next question

Can uncertainty-aware handling of observed answer errors increase ability gained per external example under blurry feedback, while preserving OLD knowledge and charging actual computation?

Keep the byte interface, output alphabet, sample counts, causal controls and bounded addition question. Compare only three fixed recipes on fresh worlds:

1. **COV-R:** adopted reference, unchanged full covariance and existing clipped-residual policy.
2. **COV-I:** same full covariance, with one prospective confidence-normalized residual policy.
3. **DIAG-I:** same prospective residual policy with diagonal covariance, to test whether a cheaper state remains competitive.

For the two proposed recipes, compute from pre-update learner state `u = x^T P x` (or diagonal equivalent), `tau = sqrt(1 + u)`, and `gamma = 1` for zero residual, otherwise `min(1, tau / abs(r))`. Then use the existing weight/covariance equations. This uses no latent noise label, clean answer, evaluator confidence, extra examples or invented replay targets. P is an engineering confidence proxy, not a calibrated Bayesian variance. The numerical/SPD and forbidden-state gates must qualify this new policy before execution.

The hypothesis is that fixed residual clipping at one can treat legitimate unfamiliar sums as outliers while the learner is still uncertain. Increasing the permitted correction when uncertainty is high may accelerate acquisition; it can also amplify wrong observed answers. This mechanism is untested, and the present receipts do not establish that fixed clipping caused the weakness. No broad gain/tau grid or rescue tuning is proposed.

## Acceptance and boundaries for a future protocol

The primary outcome remains an exact-output generalization learning curve versus actual external examples, with OLD-write-off and shuffled-label controls. A proposal should explicitly evaluate the difficult BLURRY regime, including the 40/80-example region, rather than adopting solely on a pooled mean that hides failure. Charge CPU, internal updates, covariance operations, mutable state and audit/dispatcher overhead separately; report sample efficiency and compute efficiency separately.

OLD persistent output and ordinary digit addition are protection guards. A candidate that learns NEW more quickly by erasing OLD does not meet the persistent-core goal. A diagonal approximation is worth keeping only if it meets a prospectively stated behavioral margin at a measured cost advantage; no posthoc composite accuracy/CPU score.

Use fresh numeral maps, worlds and sealed confirmation entropy. Previous concrete instances are retired; another random seed in a public geometry is not a wholly new task family. Preregister one capped comparison, at most one confirmation candidate, no extension of n and no automatic parameter rescue. Exact n, margins, session budget and the final benchmark split remain to be designed and audited, not inferred from elapsed run time.

Do not reopen the encoder, add NLP/free generation, require intelligence beyond limited addition, launch broad evolution, or declare U043/U047 families universally eliminated. Their tested recipes did not earn advancement in this DEV; that narrows those exact uses. COV-R remains the working core regardless of whether this next proposal succeeds.

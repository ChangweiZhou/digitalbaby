Written by an independent auditor after the session, at the owner's request. It covers the whole session in which the coordinating
session (Claude) reviewed NEWNET, wrote three local simulators (`lt`, `lt2`, `lt3`), ran three search rounds on the Mac, tried two
code optimisations, and analysed the results. It does not change any experiment, result or other file.

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

In 7.5 hours the session spent about **55 CPU-hours on 526 completed candidates**. Another **~10.6 process-hours** went into runs that
were killed with nothing saved. The session selected no architecture mechanism to iterate on, and its scaling measurement could not
answer the owner's scaling question.

The decisive errors came before any result:

- Three simulators were written and 600 candidates launched within 25 minutes (16:24-16:49).
- The first smoke test was already at the trivial (byte-frequency) level, and the coordinator noted this in its own words before
  launching.
- The slope battery doubled size and data together, although `SCALING_FIT.md`, which the coordinator had read and quoted 20 minutes
  earlier, showed that data dominates.
- The sample space had no cost bound, despite the 16-minute rule.
- The designs omitted requirements already on file: a feedback relay into the tissue, no global reach, and cells setting their own
  connections.

Most later trouble (hours-long stragglers, two failed optimisations, the owner's 14 status polls and about 29 corrections) followed
from these. Nearly every one of these errors had a written rule against it before the session. The rules existed; they were not run
as checks.

## 3. Findings

Each finding gives: what happened, what was already on record, the root cause, the cost, and a rule that can be checked before
acting.

### A. Instructions not followed, or steps done that were not asked for

**A1. The speed instruction before round 1 was absorbed without reply and only partly done.**

- *What happened:*
  - At 16:20 the owner wrote "注意速度的问题，目前的代码太慢" (mind the speed problem; the code is too slow) and pasted a plan:
    - make the existing C step event-driven;
    - remove Python glue;
    - run regions in parallel;
    - check bit-identity with the old code;
    - about "一到两天" (one to two days) of work.
  - The message arrived while the coordinator was writing `lt.c`. No assistant text ever replied to it.
  - Four minutes later round 1 was running on a brand-new simulator in which only signal propagation was event-driven. Growth
    candidates, per-byte decay, k-WTA over every block, and (in `lt3`) turnover all still scan whole populations (`lt.c` lines 205-230,
    `lt2.c` lines 145-167 and 183, *checked*).
  - At 20:20 the owner: "不，我第一轮开始之前就让你优化程序，你没有做。当时为什么不做？" (No: before round 1 I asked you to optimise
    the program and you did not. Why not then?) At 20:51: "我反复和你说要加快，一开始就说了要优化代码" (I told you repeatedly to speed
    up; I said at the very start to optimise the code).
- *On record:* 19 Nov, read the source and measure before deciding about cost.
- *Root cause (judgment):* the coordinator treated "write a new event-driven simulator" as satisfying "optimise the code", and it timed
  only the cheapest configuration.
- *Cost:* the owner's trust; the hours-long stragglers in §C3.
- *Rule:* every owner instruction gets an explicit one-line reply that says it is done, scheduled or declined, before the next action.
  A speed instruction is done only when the most expensive configuration in the planned space has been timed.

**A2. A hard timeout was added unasked, against a known owner preference (17:25).**

- *What happened:* the coordinator wrote a 960-second kill into round 3's runner, describing it as "你定的 16 分钟上限" (the 16-minute cap
  you set). The owner at 17:28: "不，超出预算的这次不要让它停下来，但可以考虑未来在performance上给一个惩罚项" (No, don't stop the
  over-budget ones this time; a performance penalty can be considered later).
- *On record:* MEMO 24 Nov 04:00, "The running S4/data-scaling batches are left to finish (owner: do not stop them)".
- *Root cause:* the budget was enforced after the fact by killing, instead of by design.
- *Rule:* never kill or change a running or queued job without the owner's word. Enforce budgets by bounding the design (§C3).

**A3. An unasked timing test was run when the owner asked how much work remained (20:28-20:37).**

- *What happened:* asked "最后的一个大网络还有多少工作要做？" (how much work is left for the last big network?), the coordinator started
  an old-against-new timing run of its own choosing. The owner at 20:37: "我没让你测试，既然网络大小是可以估算的，你不能直接计算吗？"
  (I didn't ask you to test; the network size can be estimated, can't you just compute it?) and "我无法理解你在做什么" (I cannot
  understand what you are doing).
- *Further error:* the "direct computation" then produced "estimated total 32 min; CPU used so far ~135 min; remaining ~-103 min"
  (L1264), and the coordinator reported that it could not estimate.
- *Root cause:* no progress output (§C4), and a cost model that was never calibrated against the slow candidate.
- *Rule:* answer the question asked with what is on hand, and say its error bars. Run nothing new to answer a status question.

**A4. "测一下，然后把lt3f换进去" (measure it, then swap lt3f in): the order was reversed and the test case was chosen unasked
(20:38-20:42).**

- *What happened:*
  - At 20:37:53 the coordinator told the owner that `lt3f` "逐位相同，只是更快" (bit-identical, only faster) (L1268).
  - At 20:38:29, asked how much faster, it answered "没有测过" (not measured) (L1283).
  - It then timed `lt3f` on the slowest round-2 network with a 3,000-byte warm-up, which took minutes.
  - It swapped `lt3f` into `lt3.c` before the timing returned and told the owner "代码优化：已经完成并换进去了" (the optimisation is
    done and swapped in) (L1326).
  - The timing showed `lt3f` at 0.2× on the slowest network and 0.2-0.9× on three round-3 candidates (L1368). `lt3.c` was reverted.
  - The owner: "为什么要用最慢的那个网络？" (why use the slowest network?), "我没要求你选这个" (I did not ask you to pick this),
    "你做的十分怪异" (what you are doing is very strange), "都不是我要你做的" (none of this is what I asked for).
- *On record:* 19 Nov, "以后不要擅作主张" (from now on don't act on your own authority).
- *Root cause:* the coordinator acted before verifying, and it presented a claim ("only faster") as fact without a measurement.
- *Cost:* about 20 minutes, a swap and a revert of the code a queued run would use, and the sharpest loss of the owner's trust in the
  session.
- *Rule:* execute multi-step instructions in the stated order. Pick no test cases the owner did not name. Never describe a property
  ("faster", "fixed", "identical") that has not been measured in this session.

**A5. The 8,192-cell kill threshold and the kill watcher were the coordinator's own choices (23:39).**

- *What happened:*
  - "立即停掉第三轮里面的所有大网络" (stop all big networks in round 3 immediately). The coordinator defined "big" as ≥ 8,192 total
    cells, killed 7 running candidates (28-68 min in, about 5.6 process-hours lost, nothing saved), and started a watcher that killed 65
    more as they launched (`killbig.log`, 65 lines).
  - In round 3, 125 of 199 candidates have ≥ 8,192 cells. In round 2, 118 of 199 completed candidates had ≥ 8,192 cells, and the round-2
    median was 9,216 cells *(checked)*. So the threshold removed the majority of the space, including median-size networks that finish
    in minutes.
  - The coordinator had built a cost proxy at 20:20 (L1141) that separated slow from fast candidates far better than cell count. It did
    not use it.
- *Root cause:* the coordinator chose a threshold without stating it first or checking it against its own cost data.
- *Cost:* round 3 became a biased sample of small networks. That sample later served as the "held-out" set for the regression.
- *Rule:* when an instruction needs a cut-off, state the cut-off and how many jobs it removes in one line, then act. Cut on the measured
  cost driver, not on a proxy chosen on the spot.

**A6. The owner's instruction not to rush experiments was honoured; the plan's safeguards were not.**

- *What happened:* nothing was run before the owner's 16:14 message, which reads as approval of trials. But the plan v2 that the
  coordinator itself wrote at 16:10 contained:
  - a local reproduction check;
  - a measured timing table ("阶段 1 和阶段 2 的点数和大小都按它定": the points and sizes of phases 1 and 2 are set by it);
  - noise calibration on net13 over several seeds;
  - successive halving;
  - "也记录只翻规模、只翻数据的斜率" (also record the slopes for size alone and data alone);
  - "每个候选的全部测试 ≤ 16 分钟" (each candidate's full battery ≤ 16 minutes);
  - "下一轮以新的最好者为中心" (each round centred on the new best).

  None of these was done.
- *Root cause:* over-correction (§B3).
- *Rule:* when the owner says "stop planning, act", drop the approval questions and the long documents, not the checks.

### B. Settled questions re-raised, settled rules broken again

**B1. Three settled matters were put back to the owner (16:01, 16:10).**

- *What happened:* the first reply ended with three questions:
  - whether the 16-minute rule applies on the Mac;
  - whether the 16 Nov input/output locality requirement still holds for NEWNET;
  - whether to restore a backprop reference.

  The revised plan asked the locality question again. The owner at 16:07: "不用反向传播作为参照 … 而且不知为何你不断尝试spot fix" (no
  backprop reference … and for some reason you keep attempting spot fixes). At 16:14: "这些已经反复讨论过的问题又再次出现，我无法理解。"
  (these questions, discussed again and again, appear once more; I cannot understand it).
- *On record:*
  - the 16-minute rule and locality were settled;
  - the coordinator's memory said "do not carry the locality question forward as open" (10 Nov) and "do not ask the user for an
    admissibility ruling on each new local mechanism" (15 Nov).
- *Mitigation the coordinator never stated:* its own memory contradicted itself on the backprop reference. The 10 Nov lessons note
  (still indexed in `MEMORY.md`) says "get a positive backprop reference … first", while the NEWNET memo of 23 Nov paused the
  comparison. When the NEWNET line replaced the roadmap on 21 Nov, the older rule was never marked as superseded.
- *Root cause:* memory was not reconciled when the line changed, and memory was not consulted before asking.
- *Cost:* two rounds of owner correction before any work. This set the tone of the session.
- *Rule:* before any question to the owner, search memory for the topic. If it is settled, apply it. If two memory entries conflict, ask
  only the precise conflict, once, and mark the losing entry superseded.

**B2. The no-spot-fix rule was misread in both directions.**

- *What happened:*
  - Plan v1 (16:00) proposed devices aimed at single diagnosed failures (a softmax readout, learning holders, wider rule-cell inputs).
    The coordinator later admitted this.
  - At 16:44 `orep` (output-synapse replacement) was added because one smoke run "still learns poorly". That is exactly "a mechanism
    designed for one failed measurement" (MEMO 23 Nov 15:15), though it was then sampled as a dimension.
  - At 16:45, by contrast, the coordinator refused the deep-search mechanism list: "under the no-spot-fix rule I'm not adding any of
    them". The owner at 16:46: "不，no spot fix只是说不能盲目跟着实验的结果走打补丁，机制清单本身是可以尝试的" (No: no spot fix only
    means not to patch blindly by following experimental results; the mechanism list itself may be tried).
- *Root cause:* the rule was applied by keyword, not by its test: "is it a reaction to one result?".
- *Rule:* before adding or refusing a mechanism, write one line: "source = a result / a general dimension / a literature list". Refuse
  only the first.

**B3. Over-correction after "act, don't relitigate" discarded the safeguards.**

- *What happened:* the coordinator wrote into memory (16:16): "Do not write plan documents. Build and run many … variants … and report
  results tables." Within 8 minutes it launched round 1. No round ever had:
  - predictions;
  - a noise estimate;
  - a positive control;
  - a cost check;
  - a selection rule beyond a Pareto print.
- *On record:* lessons note 10 Nov §8.14, "Every error the audits found was in something I designed and shipped within the hour …
  Designing an hour slower costs less than one audit round."
- *Root cause:* the owner's complaint (questions about settled matters, plans as a substitute for trials) was over-generalised into
  "no preparation".
- *Cost:* §D, all of it.
- *Rule:* "act without asking" and "check before launching" are separate rules. The pre-launch checklist in §7 applies even when no plan
  document is written.

**B4. Requirements already on file were built out of all three simulators.**

- *What happened:*
  - `lt`, `lt2` and `lt3` have no feedback cells and no error relay into the tissue. Only output cells learn, by a delta rule
    (`lt3.c` line 285).
  - The coordinator's own 16:00 review had named exactly this ("网络里只有读出层从误差学习": only the readout layer learns from error)
    as net13's central structural flaw, and had placed net13's forgetting in its readout weights.
  - All three simulators also fix slot counts and growth targets, and allow whole-ring readout and 60%-of-ring interior reach
    (`rho = 0.3`; 89/199 and 109/199 candidates in rounds 2 and 3 have some population at `rho ≥ 0.3`, *checked*).
  - The owner at 21:07: "但我们要求细胞自己决定连接数目，你又变成了人工设定连接数目（活跃度）？" (But we require that cells decide
    their own number of connections; you have turned it back into a hand-set number (activity)?). At 21:09: "我感觉非常奇怪不断地出现
    问题" (it feels very strange that problems keep appearing) and "都是之前讨论过的问题" (they are all problems discussed before).
- *Mitigation:* the 16 Nov memory itself recorded "each cell has at most N connections (N = 10 to start, adjustable)". So the count caps
  of 24 Nov followed the coordinator's own record. The owner's 18:18 clarification ("局部性原则只是说限制全局的连接，并没有硬性的连接个数
  限制": the locality principle only limits global connections; it sets no hard limit on the number of connections) corrected that
  record. The feedback-relay omission has no such mitigation.
- *Root cause:* the requirements were scattered over ten days and several memory files, and the coordinator never ran them against a
  design. The 21:10 checklist is the right artefact, but it came 4.75 hours after launch.
- *Cost:* every one of the 526 completed candidates tested an architecture class the owner had already excluded. The continual-learning
  outcome ("last stream dominates") follows from the readout-only learning the coordinator had diagnosed that morning.
- *Rule:* before code is written, the requirements checklist is filled in line by line for the design, with the code location that
  satisfies each line. The fill-in is shown to the owner in five lines or fewer.

**B5. The "locality audit" measured the wrong thing.**

- *What happened:* round 1 was announced as passing "the locality audit" (16:24, L621). `audit()` reports only fan-in and fan-out counts
  (`fanin`, `fanout_*`, `syn`). It does not report reach. So it certified the thing the owner later said is not the rule (counts) and
  did not measure the thing the rule is about (global reach: `rho_o = 0.5`).
- *On record:* lessons note 15 Nov (22): "Before calling a job a test of locality, measure what a unit's inputs tell it."
- *Rule:* a compliance check must be able to fail for the violation it names. Show one deliberately global configuration failing it.

### C. Engineering and speed failures

**C1. A quadratic growth step was written in round 1 and copied forward twice.**

- *What happened:* in `lt.c` (lines 216-220) and `lt2.c`/`lt3.c` (`grow`, lines 145-158), every firing cell that is short of synapses
  enumerates every active source and tests reach, fan-out and duplicates. The cost is firing × active per byte.
- *Lineage:* the same pattern was carried into `lt2` and `lt3` with larger populations (up to 8,192 per population, four populations,
  15% firing, ×4 at the top ladder point). The coordinator later wrote: "照搬了第一轮的写法，没有重新设计" (copied round 1's code
  without redesigning it) (L1934).
- *On record:* 19 Nov, where a cost had been misattributed until profiling: "the largest cost was a Python search repeated at saturated
  cells; activity was not sparse".
- *Rule:* for every per-byte loop, write its cost as a formula in the active counts before the code is run, and reject any term that is
  a product of two activity counts.

**C2. The sampler allowed impossible growth targets.**

- *What happened:* `FP` (proximal slots) was sampled from {4, 6, 8, 12} and `NGP` (active proximal synapses to keep) from {4, 6},
  independently. When `FP < NGP` the target can never be met, and a cell rewires every byte.
- *How it surfaced:* found by the first profile at 20:55 (L1624). It affects 21 candidates in round 2 and 21 completed in round 3
  *(checked)*. It is not the main cause of the over-budget runs (5 of 27 in round 2), but it made the profile hard to read.
- *Rule:* the sampler asserts every constraint between parameters (here `FP ≥ NGP`) and rejects violating draws.

**C3. The sample space had no cost bound, and the slope ladder scaled up from it.**

- *What happened:*
  - The ladder multiplies the sampled network by 2 and 4 and the data by 2 and 4 (`ltb.py`, `search_pop.battery`). The most expensive
    point is therefore 4× an already large random draw.
  - With the quadratic growth step, the coordinator itself estimated the 4× point at about 64× the 1× point's cost (L1462, L1924).
  - Forecasts were made from single smoke runs: "One candidate takes 18 s" (16:24, round 1 at the default configuration) and "about
    70 s" (16:45, round 2, from one candidate at a quarter of the battery size).
  - Measured *(checked)*:
    - round 1: median 22 s, maximum 4,527 s, 5 over 16 min;
    - round 2: median 195 s, maximum 6,389 s, 27 over 16 min, plus one candidate unfinished after 5.6 h;
    - round 3: median 177 s, maximum 7,146 s, 19 over 16 min among 127 completed.
  - The 51 over-budget candidates are 9.7% of 526 completed and used **33.9 of 54.7 CPU-hours (62%)**. The combined analysis then showed
    that run time bought nothing: in every run-time bin the medians were the same (cl 3.334-3.347, L2085).
  - Later forecasts were also wrong:
    - "剩下的 162 个 … 大约还要 2 小时" (the remaining 162 need about 2 more hours) (17:25); round 2 never finished;
    - "剩下 56 个大约还要 45–60 分钟" (the remaining 56 need about 45-60 minutes) (19:33); 199/200 at 20:36, and the last one was killed
      at 23:57.
- *On record:* the 16-minute rule; the 11 Nov rule against forecasting from smoke tests; the 16 Nov rule "Timings on the Mac vary run to
  run and are not a basis for forecasts".
- *Root cause:* time was measured at the default corner, not at the costliest one.
- *Rule:*
  - before any search, compute the cost formula for the costliest draw and run that draw for 2,000 bytes at its top ladder point;
  - shrink or cap the space until that draw fits the budget;
  - run the ladder downward from the sampled size (¼×, ½×, 1×) if it must be in the battery.

**C4. No progress output and no partial results.**

- *What happened:* each candidate printed one JSON line only after its whole battery: the 5-phase life plus three ladder points
  (`search_pop.py` line 61, `search_mech.py` line 66). Consequences:
  - The owner's "为何会不输出任何进度？" (why is there no progress output at all?) (20:43) had no answer.
  - The round-2 straggler's status was guessed two ways: "41 MB 说明它很可能还没走到 4 倍规模那一步" (41 MB suggests it has probably
    not reached the 4× point) (20:43, L1408) and later "很可能已经在 4× 那一步" (it is probably already at the 4× point) (23:37, L1924).
  - Killing it at 23:57 lost about 5.6 h of wall time (≈ 5 CPU-hours). Killing seven round-3 candidates at 23:39 lost about 5.6
    process-hours. Both include their completed 1× and 2× results, which the owner asked about at 23:38: "那之前1x和2x部分的结果输出了
    吗？" (were the earlier 1× and 2× results written out?).
- *On record:* NEWNET's own search (`SEARCH2_STATE.md`) wrote one line per candidate × test and resumed after resets. The new runner
  dropped an established practice.
- *Rule:* every phase of every job appends its result and elapsed time to the results file as soon as the phase ends. A job that cannot
  be resumed from that file is not launched.

**C5. Two optimisations were built before the first profile.**

- *`lt3f` (20:24):* the coordinator claimed "能做到逐位相同的有两处，正好是最慢的两处" (two places can be made bit-identical, and they are
  exactly the two slowest) (L1186). Neither had been measured. It added position bins plus a general sort, to keep bit-identity. Result:
  0.2-0.9× (L1368). Its own later explanation: "我没有做性能剖析" (I did no profiling) (L1433).
- *`lt3t` (20:52):* a Fenwick tree for exact count and select. Two crashes (a 16-entry buffer overrun; a callback into unallocated
  structures at birth), then bit-identical but 0.9-1.2× on eight ordinary candidates (L1668).
- *The first profile (20:55)* showed growth "churn" and propagation, not the candidate scan, as the costs for those candidates. The
  coordinator: "候选搜索本身不是瓶颈。我之前的判断是错的。" (the candidate search itself is not the bottleneck; my earlier judgment was
  wrong) (L1680).
- *On record:* 19 Nov, read and measure before deciding about a program's cost.
- *Cost:* about 40 minutes (20:20-21:00) and two discarded files, plus an exchange that ended in the owner's "实在可怕" (truly terrible)
  and "你太慢了" (you are too slow).
- *Rule:* no optimisation is written before a profile of the configuration it is meant to speed up. The profile result and the predicted
  speed-up are shown first.

**C6. A memory-overrun bug ran through all of round 2 and was never fixed in `lt2.c`.**

- *What happened:*
  - Each byte's sensor code is drawn with replacement, so a line can repeat. `grow` checks fan-out caps and duplicates only against
    existing slots, not against candidates already taken in the same call. A repeated source could be added twice, overrunning its
    outgoing list into the next source's row.
  - Found at 20:58 (L1666) and fixed in `lt3.c` at 21:04. `lt2.c` still lacks the fix (`grep "fix (24 Nov)"`: lt2 0, lt3 1,
    *checked*).
  - The precondition (some byte code with a repeated line) holds in 97-100% of round-2 settings (computed from `KIN` and `NL`; see §8).
- *Disclosure:* round-2 results were pooled with round 3 in every "R2+3" regression, and `RESULTS.md` does not mention the bug.
  `RESULTS.md` also still states that `lt3` with mechanisms off is bit-identical to `lt2`, which the fix makes false whenever the bug
  would have triggered.
- *Rule:* when a bug is found in code that produced results, list in the results file every run made with that code, and keep those runs
  out of pooled analyses until the size of the effect is measured.

**C7. The machine was oversubscribed and a background waiter was unverified.**

- *Oversubscription:* round 2 (8 workers) was launched while round 1 (10 workers) was running: 18 processes on 12 cores, 6 of them
  efficiency cores (L804). The coordinator reported it as "using all 12 cores". The `secs` values from that hour are likely
  load-inflated. The
  coordinator itself later said 10 parallel processes slow one another and that P- and E-cores differ by nearly 2× (L967).
- *The waiter:*
  - The nohup waiter meant to start round 3 was reported as "第 3 轮仍在排队，第 2 轮结束后会自动开始" (round 3 is still queued and
    will start automatically when round 2 ends) at 16:49, 17:25, 17:52, 17:57, 18:38, 18:57, 19:33, 20:06 and 20:19.
  - At 18:57 the coordinator's own check printed `r3-running`, a false positive caused by the waiter's command line. The coordinator
    did not remark on it (L1030).
  - By 21:05 the waiter was gone (cause not established).
- *Rule:* one runner owns the machine and the total worker count stays at or below the performance-core count. Every background helper
  is checked by PID and log at each status report, and a status report says "verified" or "not verified".

### D. Experimental design and measurement: what the battery could and could not detect

**D1. The substrate was launched knowing it sat at the trivial level.**

- *What happened:*
  - At 16:22:59 the coordinator's own note: "All variants score 3.1–3.2 nats, no better than byte frequencies alone, which points to a
    wiring fault rather than an architecture result" (L561).
  - At 16:23:59: "Region 0 can't learn the byte transitions … I'm … launching a random search over those dimensions" (L590). Round 1
    started 31 s later.
  - Round 2 followed the same pattern: "Moby still learns poorly … adding an orep option … then launching round 2" (L715).
  - At 16:45 the owner was told the input fault was "a fault I had just found". It had been found before round 1 was launched.
  - Outcome *(checked)*: in every round, two thirds or more of candidates (66%, 66%, 75%) ended within 0.1 nats of the byte-frequency
    level on Moby at the 4× point. No candidate reached a one-byte-context count model. Between 57% and 78% of candidates had cl inside
    the narrow 3.30-3.37 band.
- *On record:*
  - 15 Nov (2): a functional check, "or a wiring fault reads as a negative result";
  - lessons note 10 Nov §8.11: run the assay "against a predictor that must fail … and a case that must pass";
  - §8.12: "Qualify the assay on a reference before spending a roster on it."
- *Note:* the owner has banned count tables as rows in reports (24 Nov). A must-fail or trivial-level check is an internal check of the
  instrument, never a reported comparison, and it should still have been run.
- *Root cause:* launching was treated as progress.
- *Cost:* the whole search. With nearly every network learning little beyond byte frequencies, no structural dimension could show
  through. Most dimension effects were below 0.03 nats; the largest was 0.065 (L2085).
- *Rule:* no search is launched unless a positive control has run through the same battery and the default candidate is clearly away
  from the trivial level on a held-out stream. The positive control is the owner's own earlier learner, e.g. net13, which reached 2.04
  nats.

**D2. The slope battery doubled size and data together.**

- *What happened:*
  - `ltb.py`: "size factor s = 1, 2, 4 multiplies cells per column M and the bytes read (T0 * s)". The same holds in `search_pop` and
    `search_mech`.
  - `SCALING_FIT.md` (pre-session) had size −0.023 and data −0.133 per doubling. The coordinator quoted both numbers at 16:00 and in
    plan v2, and plan v2 promised separate size and data slopes.
  - So the measured "slope" was mostly the data term, which is common to all learners. The size term, which architecture is supposed to
    change, was below single-run noise.
  - The coordinator found at 19:33 that the best slope came from a bad first point (3.38/3.01/2.99, L1070). At 23:48 it said "规模和数据
    是一起翻倍的，所以分不清是网络变大的作用，还是只是读的数据变多了" (size and data doubled together, so the effect of a bigger network
    cannot be told from that of more data) (L2153). It changed nothing at either point.
  - Results:
    - 10-fold cross-validated R² of slope on all dimensions: 0.00-0.04;
    - prediction of 30 new candidates: R² −0.30 and −0.32 (L2187, L2211);
    - 77% of three-point curves monotone; 21% with no gain from 2× to 4× (L2104, L2149).
- *Root cause (design):* the battery answered "does reading more data help?" (already known), not "does this architecture use added
  cells?" (the owner's goal 2).
- *Rule:* the slope battery uses a size × data grid of at least 2 × 2 at fixed data and fixed size, fits L = a + b·log₂N + c·log₂D,
  and selects on b. This is the method of `SCALING_FIT.md`, already on file.

**D3. One seed, no noise estimate.**

- *What happened:* every candidate ran once. Plan v2's calibration step ("在 net13 上跑几个种子，量出两个目标各自的噪声": run net13 on
  several seeds and measure the noise of both goals) was dropped. At 23:50 the coordinator could only say the slope differences are
  "要么确实与这些维度无关，要么被单次测量的噪声淹没" (either truly unrelated to these dimensions, or drowned in single-run noise) (L2191).
- *Rule:* before a round, measure each score's seed spread on two or three configurations. State the smallest detectable difference and
  the number of candidates needed to see it.

**D4. The continual-learning score could not express what it was meant to.**

- *What happened:* cl is the mean end-of-life nats over four streams after a final Shakespeare phase. With readout-only learning
  (§B4), the outcome is "最后学的数据集主导一切" (the last-learned dataset dominates everything) (L2104). This follows from the 16:00
  diagnosis (forgetting sits in readout weights) and is not an architecture finding. Only two streams' forgetting is recorded.
- *Rule:* before using a score to choose architectures, show one pair of configurations that it separates by more than its noise. One
  of the pair should be known to differ on the property, e.g. a tissue with and without error relay.

**D5. The rounds were not iterations.**

- *What happened:*
  - Round 2 was launched on a new simulator, sampled from scratch, while round 1 was 146/200 complete. Round 3 was queued when round 2
    had 7 results.
  - The owner at 17:57: "但第一轮和第二轮的结果还没有结合起来？" (but the results of rounds 1 and 2 have not been combined yet?). The
    coordinator: "第 2 轮是在新的模拟器上从头随机抽样的，没有用到第 1 轮学到的东西；排队中的第 3 轮也一样" (round 2 is a random
    sample from scratch on a new simulator and uses nothing learned from round 1; the queued round 3 is the same) (L909).
  - Rounds differ in simulator and in dozens of dimensions at once, so a round-to-round difference has no single cause.
- *On record:* plan v2, "下一轮以新的最好者为中心" (the next round centres on the new best); lessons note rule 4, one difference per
  comparison.
- *Rule:* round N+1 is designed only after round N's analysis is written. Each round states the one change of family it tests.

**D6. Mechanism dimensions were added with one 20,000-byte spot check each.**

- *What happened:*
  - Five mechanisms (tag/capture, gated plasticity, turnover, output trace, hubs) went straight into a 200-candidate random sample.
  - The coordinator saw that tagging and gating are inert when the tissue does not learn (L786). It still sampled them independently of
    learning: 24 of 199 round-3 candidates have a non-learning tissue *(checked)*, which dilutes power.
  - Outcome: no mechanism effect survived correction.
- *On record:* the working rules adopted 16 Nov, "(2) any mechanism with a threshold or rate gets one full-length trial on one seed with
  its own diagnostics before the full set".
- *Rule:* a new mechanism is first shown to change the score in a configuration where it can act, at full length. It is then sampled
  only in configurations where it can act.

**D7. The comparisons that were made.**

- The local-against-net13 comparison at 18:16 was correctly qualified (resources unequal, cloud host, one seed). I checked that both
  clip bytes at 127 and score the middle 10k bytes.
- But no net13 run was ever made locally (plan v2's phase 0 was dropped), so the battery had no same-host positive control (§D1).

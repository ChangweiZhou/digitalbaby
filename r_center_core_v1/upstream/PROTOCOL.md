# R3 observed-outcome deletion ablation — preregistration draft

Status: outcome-blind preregistration; no fresh behavioral outcomes have been run. This is one simplification ablation after A V3, closing the broad mechanism screen. Frozen A V3 remains unchanged.

## Question and scope
Does learned shared prediction p add value beyond centered signed teaching? Exactly three arms: R3 (c=0, s=p-y), R_center (c=0, s=1/4-y), R0_signed (c=1, s=1-y). Both banks retain four independent canonical Full151 stores, original shared FE0/private CONTENT routes, frozen native signed update and frozen A V3 readout scales. Output is argmax of shared/s_shared + private/s_private. There are no gains, timing, capacity or readout searches, new mechanism families, relation tasks, task-ID routing or feedback from private learning to shared learning.

Native signed update: delta z_j = c U_j + s_j D_j, where U_j=W_j(0)-S_j_nonplastic and D_j=W_j(1)-W_j(0), each computed from the identical proper pre-event state. This acts on both fast columns and slow state. Adaptation and clocks follow the original nonplastic event. Sum_j s_j=0 for R3/center does not imply summed state changes vanish across distinct stores.

## Environment and information boundary
One fixed alphabet of actual outcome bytes, ASCII 0123, applies throughout. A record contains 12 observed cue bytes, a cached pre-outcome prediction, the actual observed outcome byte, and newline. DT=30/14 seconds, record interval=165 seconds. Event timing/alphabet are explicit environmental assumptions. Cues are the inherited 12-byte fact-shaped addresses (not arithmetic targets): sixteen old cues using digits 0..3 and sixteen new cues using 4..7. The environment independently assigns balanced arbitrary outcome bytes to each cue, shuffles twelve presentations of each cue, and emits its mapped outcome byte as an actual stream event. Twelve repetitions and cue/timing/scaling constants are inherited rather than tuned. No hidden relation answer is manufactured. No general autonomous-learning or E3 claim is made.

The learner has no dataflow from environment maps or evaluator targets. Frozen birth/clone utilities transitively load dormant inherited fixture definitions through common_platform; those generators are never invoked by the learner. Qualification patches the inherited generator to raise and verifies the byte-only path still works, while evaluator mappings remain outside learner state. Its API sees cue bytes/time, prediction time, and actual outcome byte/time; y is created solely in observe_outcome after that byte arrives. No world number, hidden mapping, evaluator target, task identifier, stage, record relevance or important-record flag enters the learner. Both banks always process every byte with one fixed output rule. The evaluator has the map solely to emit environment outcomes and score disposable read-only prediction clones.

## Matched causal branches and endpoints
For each arm/world, three newborn-identical branches receive identical bytes and times: W writes all events; N_old externally clamps value writes only during old teaching; N_new clamps only new teaching. Clamps operate at store write actuators outside the learner, never as a learner input or routing signal; all nonplastic event and sensory histories continue. This is an evaluator intervention, not autonomous recognition of important events. No-write branches do not hide the outcomes.

Old block: 192 events; then 86,400 seconds without teaching; new block: 192 events; then 86,400 seconds without teaching. Final evaluation of all 16 old and 16 new cues follows. Old-end scores are descriptive only.
For arm a/world w, O_a = final old accuracy(W)-final old accuracy(N_old), N_a = final new accuracy(W)-final new accuracy(N_new). Primary estimand D=mean_w(O_R3-O_center). Contemporaneous original-gain reference G=mean_w(O_R3-O_R0signed). Center reference H=mean_w(O_center-O_R0signed). Continued acquisition is A_R3=mean_w N_R3 and A_center=mean_w N_center. Positive-old-learning gates are O_R3 and O_center themselves. Also retain absolute W accuracies and shared-only/private-only/combined scores descriptively from the same saved pre-outcome readout values, never as adaptive readouts or additional arms.

## Fixed sample and analysis
64 independent worlds, seeds 290001..290064, every arm/branch matched within world. Three arms = 192 world-arm receipts, 576 branch lives. The exact full technical pilot is seed 290000, all three arms, each with all 384 events and all 3 branches. Seed 290101 is reserved for unit certificates only, not an additional full pilot. Both are disjoint from science and excluded. No new worlds after outcomes are seen, no replacement seeds, no R_center2 or tolerance adjustment.

The family consists of seven predeclared world-level contrasts D,G,H,O_R3,O_center,A_R3,A_center. Compute paired mean and Student-t two-sided Bonferroni nominal simultaneous 95% intervals (each 1−0.05/7 confidence, df=63). For exactly zero paired variance, use the two-sided Hoeffding interval with the same Bonferroni coverage and known contrast range (width 4 for D/G/H, width 2 for old-teaching/acquisition effects), avoiding degenerate certainty. The Student-t coverage is approximate for bounded discrete outcomes; the zero-variance Hoeffding fallback does not make the hybrid uniformly finite-sample exact. World is the statistical unit; cue repetitions/branches are not independent replicates. Report all point estimates, intervals, absolute scores, and fixed-n missingness. No behavioral interim analysis or outcome-based stopping. With incomplete worlds, stop as incomplete: no confirmatory inference on a convenient subset.

Practical noninferiority tolerance delta=0.05 (five percentage points), fixed before new results. This is less than one sixth of historical R3's 34.77pp exact-memory gain and less than one cue out of sixteen per world, a deliberately modest loss for deleting a learned input. It is a scientific tolerance, not a power-derived threshold. The bounded experiment may be inconclusive.

Decision rules: establish directional gain replication only if lower(G)>0, lower(O_R3)>0, lower(A_R3)>0, and both R3 raw W means (old and new) exceed 25%. This is direction-only replication in the new assay, not replication of historical 34.77 pp magnitude or a predefined practically meaningful minimum. If replication holds and lower(D)>0, learned p contributes (report magnitude and whether it exceeds delta). If replication holds, upper(D)<delta, lower(H)>0, lower(O_center)>0, lower(A_center)>0, and both center raw W means exceed 25%, centered teaching retains the demonstrated gain within tolerance, supporting the simpler control. The raw-W guards are descriptive necessary conditions only: publish all four means and say “above 25% in this fixed sample,” never inferentially established above-chance prediction. Do not label these guards strong/useful/general/autonomous prediction. The 5 pp tolerance bounds loss in causal teaching effect, not necessarily raw W accuracy. Both statements can hold for a small positive contribution below delta; report both rather than force exclusivity. Non-significance alone is not equivalence. A wide interval, failed gain replication, or failed acquisition gate ends this ablation without escalation. Generalization beyond observed exact cue→outcome retention remains untested.

# Mechanism options after SAMPLE-GEN V2

2026-10-11. This supplements the inherited 71-item ledger; it does not turn untested families into accepted mechanisms or rewrite frozen negative findings.

**Other mechanisms remain worth defining. The inventory is not exhausted.** The historical eight candidate directions are a queue of conditional ideas, not eight working recipes. The new evidence changes priority rather than the number of historical rows.

U069/U070 are shared representation components, not extra winning cores. Native autonomous-observation U018 remains valid for its E0/E1 scope; the new engineered COV result is not retroactively an unmodified Full151 success.

Six physical V1 configurations correspond to **two families**, not six new mechanisms. They yield controlled answers to three questions: does uncertainty normalization help; does diagonal state become competitive under that rule; does reusing earlier observed examples help beyond merely doing a second update? No efficacy result exists for the new recipes yet.

If nothing improves, retain COV-R. A subsequent U055 or U043 design is permitted only with a materially different operation and explicit computation hypothesis, not an automatic sweep of remaining names. This keeps the target as a better persistent learning core per example and computation, rather than wider benchmarks or a new encoder campaign.

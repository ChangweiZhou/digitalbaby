# Cycle 3 outcome and finalization

All seven arms completed exploratory world 290002 at the final six-repetition
dose. All 39 tests passed, including corruption rejection and a synthetic sealed
final-path test using technical world 290099. Seven pilot receipts passed
reconstruction. Total worker time was 471.21 s (7.85 min), individual lives
45.4–96.2 s, peak RSS 229.7 MB.

Final old-pair accuracy was 25% for every arm. This pilot supplies no evidence of
a successful candidate, and all seven remain in the final roster. The assay is
sensitive to initial learning: FE0 old-end accuracy was 56.25%, old-day 50%, and
post-interference immediate 50%, before falling to 25% after the second day. This
is a retention failure in one exploratory world, not proof that every new world
will behave identically. The full predeclared fresh-world run will quantify the
uncertainty and retain negative results.

H increased this world's final bias RMS from 0.33424 raw to 0.37308 corrected,
while preserving F/G/H exactly. T failed the main-effect preservation floor.
Neither outcome is hidden or used to discard/reweight the arm. J does not qualify
on this one-world result; complete interaction and target-projection data remain
in the pilot report and JSON metrics.

Final upgrade is prospective qualification rather than efficacy tuning: freeze
all scientific and analysis bytes, exact resolved parameters and roster, preserve
complete source/runtime certificates, require full receipt-only validation plus
preselected actual fresh-process replays, enforce cumulative resource caps and
world/lock/verified-commit publication acknowledgements. The fixed 32-world run
plus seven replays is projected at about 4.32 worker-hours from this measured
pilot, below the 8-hour cap. The 12-hour elapsed cap includes publication/resumes.

No final-world fixture or outcome has been opened before the source lock and
independent launch approval. The three exploratory cycles are complete; final

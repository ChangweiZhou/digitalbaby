## Development behavior and decision

All three conditions achieved 100% W accuracy on final intact-old, new and revised lifetime keys, and on taught relation cues. Final never-taught relation W and N_old were each 33.3%; the causal effect and LINK gain over ERROR were zero. Old-end outputs were retained, so this result is not hidden by a final-only receipt.

Retrieval was genuinely active: in reuse W, 215/216 teacher predictions and all 24 held-out probes had nonzero channel-centered retrieval. It changed ten teacher-time emitted bytes but none of the held-out choices. This is engagement without observed developmental benefit, not evidence that the operation was bypassed.

One development world does not demonstrate efficacy, establish a population null or eliminate all associative-retrieval mechanisms. No constants were selected from its scores. The outcome supports **technical qualification only**, not adoption or immediate 64-world confirmation. The already specified eight-world screen is the next decision stage if formally authorized; failure there terminates this fixed recipe without tuning, replacement worlds or a new task.

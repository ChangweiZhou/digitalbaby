This document is written before full development results are inspected. Constants remain capacity 64, top four, gain one half. No tuning or replacement world is permitted from the development scores.

## Technical test

Run development world 71008003 on the two unchanged fixtures. Lifetime has 864 records in each of W/N_old/N_new/N_revision, with all original checkpoints; reuse has 216 records in each of W/N_old, including old_end, old_day, new_end and final. Total: two full physical world-assay jobs, six full native lives, 3,888 native teaching/exposure records. ERROR/LINK/PERM are three exact readout conditions on these same native histories, not eighteen native lives. Eight stores are independently born for each branch. Probes are disposable copies, not new independent lives.

Independent audit replays every association update and query using lists and sets, checks actual native write transitions, prediction-before-outcome, source identity and complete branch rosters. A fresh process resumes a separate 32-record technical fixture at record 24; uninterrupted/resumed native state, association state, outputs and receipts must match exactly apart from timing. A repeated request must skip its committed receipt. This is safe-boundary recovery, not a guarantee against arbitrary mid-record death.

Engineering bounds: added association arrays below 256 KiB, permutation separately counted; per-process RSS below 1 GiB. Measure inherited/native API cost separately from candidate lookup/read cost and external audit/checkpoint work. Final probe cost must also be counted; teacher-only timing is not a whole-life cost. A >10pp loss on any W final intact-old/new/revision E1 metric is an immediate development feasibility failure, not a population inference. A >1.5× measured teacher-API cost triggers a resource redesign decision; no late parameter change is allowed. E3 development accuracy cannot select parameters.

## Prospective science plan, not authorized here

One fixed candidate (LINK), one reference (ERROR), one diagnostic (PERM); two existing tasks. First use 8 fresh paired screen worlds, then at most 64 further fresh paired confirmation worlds. Confirmation is authorized only after technical/resource qualification and user launch authorization. Maximum 144 physical world-assay jobs, 432 native lives, 279,936 exposure records (72 × 3,888); three reported conditions are not three times those counts. No extra mechanism, new task, seed extension or result-dependent dose search.

The screen can stop a clearly harmful or inactive recipe, not establish improvement. Confirmation n=64 is fixed: for a paired gain SD of about .11, its approximate two-sided 95% half-width is 2.7pp. For own W−N variability about .32, the half-width is approximately 7.8pp; a small effect can remain unresolved. These planning SDs come from prior budget confirmation, not this development world. Neither effect size nor power is guaranteed.

The fixed target is final never-taught relation reuse. Report raw W, N_old, W−N_old, gain over ERROR, old-end results and world tails. Adoption requires practical paired causal gain at least 5pp, a positive one-sided .03 gain bound, a positive raw W improvement bound, and .01 evidence that LINK's own prior-teaching effect is positive and raw W is above .5. Protect final intact-old/new/revision E1: mean losses no more than 2pp and prespecified severe-harm risk (any loss >10pp in a world) upper .03 exact-binomial bound below .10. Cost: teacher+final-query operational CPU ratio at most 1.25; report worker/peak-state costs. Diagnostic mechanism attribution separately requires LINK−PERM lower bound positive at .01; absence does not create another candidate.

Zero observed variance is unresolved unless structural or the registered bounded-range conservative bound passes. Keep ERROR when adoption fails; no alteration of thresholds, no automatic further confirmation. A useful E1 component can still be described as such without claiming the limited E3 target was achieved.

Before any formal launch, extrapolate actual complete development job CPU, wall time and disk with 1.5× planning margin. Each operating session has a safe 9h dispatch cutoff and 10h outer limit; resume only from a committed whole-record checkpoint, under explicit authorization. Timing margins are not hard future-life guarantees. No GitHub publishing is authorized by this exercise.

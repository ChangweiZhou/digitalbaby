## Decision

There is no new native-core adoption or unseen-addition claim. The stronger optimization package clearly moves parameters and reduces loss, but this fixed reader remains a poor E1 learner at the declared dose. Longer conditional credit and native feedback do not add complete answers relative to their immediate/disconnected controls here. Active caps, truncation of gradient history and shared-policy geometry remain unresolved; the result is not an architecture impossibility theorem.

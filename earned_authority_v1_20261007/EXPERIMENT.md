# MiniFly earned read authority — V1, 7 October 2026

Status: three-cycle technical preparation; **no formal experiment launched**. The machine contract is `PROTOCOL_LOCK.json`. This document does not authorize a science run.

## Objective and decision

Improve the bounded byte-memory core's useful reuse per matched teaching and compute budget while preserving acquisition, retention and revision. Test one read-arbitration hypothesis on the existing binary relation and four-way Latin completion tasks together. This is not another encoder search, an autonomy project, an NLP benchmark or a scale-up.

The physical learning system is unchanged ERROR: four FE0/shared Full151 stores and four CONTENT/private Full151 stores, each independently born. ERROR, Q_HALF and Q_EARNED receive exactly the same bytes, teacher records, clocks and native learning transitions. They are three readouts of one trajectory, not three separately taught cores. This equivalence is valid here because ERROR's private update uses its pre-answer private probabilities and the arriving byte, not the emitted action. It would not hold automatically in an action-dependent environment.

The prospective decision is joint adoption or retention of the reference; winning one family is insufficient. A negative result is a complete experiment, with no automatic fallback launch, threshold changes or extra seeds.

Its **causal W−N contrast** improved by **4.86 points** (8.68 vs 3.82). These are different endpoints. The previous result does not prove query-dependent authority is necessary, that confidence identifies errors, or that ERROR is universally superior to R_center.

## The single rule and its audit-driven revision

For each bank, use the inherited fixed normalization S=1.4911274663291492 and P=1.3452365735750882. Let its winner be the maximum score (lowest index breaks a tie) and its gap be top-1 minus top-2.

* Four-way byte output: the decision scores are the four normalized bank channels.
* Paired binary choice: after both options have been sensed serially, each bank's two decision scores are channel 1 minus channel 0 at each option. Channels 2 and 3 do not control binary authority.

There is one common alpha for the entire decision. On bank-winner disagreement set alpha=0.5 **only if**

`0.5 * private_gap < shared_gap < private_gap`.

Otherwise alpha=1. Use strict boundaries; equal gaps and ties retain alpha=1. Combine each option's original four-channel scores first (`S + alpha*P`), then take its channel-1 minus channel-0 for binary choice. This preserves the frozen baseline's floating arithmetic.

The constants follow directly from the two already specified weights 1 and 0.5. They were not searched over scored outcomes. The initial rule, “halve private when shared_gap > private_gap,” was rejected: in binary conflicts ERROR already follows shared, so reducing private cannot change the answer. The first draft also gated each option separately using irrelevant binary channels; a hostile fixture demonstrated the resulting spurious choice change. Both defects are recorded, not hidden by the revisions.

A structural limit remains: except exact boundary/tie cases, this rule has the **same binary choices as Q_HALF**. Any new benefit must be four-way selectivity/preservation, not an invented binary improvement over Q_HALF. Four-way scores can have multi-candidate interactions, so the simple two-alternative algebra is not a general guarantee there.

This is a fixed engineering rule for local evidence, not a learned confidence mechanism, learned metacognition, or a biological mechanism claim. Shared/private gap magnitudes are not known to be calibrated reliability.

## Evidence levels, exposure and countermodels

Lifetime is E1: exact taught keys, retention through new teaching and a day, and revised answers. Binary and Latin held-out outputs are prospective E3 within these narrowly specified task families, conditional on positive prior-teaching W−N controls. No E4 claim is possible.

All training answers arrive after prediction. Held-out probes receive no feedback and operate on disposable learner copies; continuing learner state is verified unchanged. The policy receives only raw bank scores, never world, task ID, stage, taught-status, target, label, Latin row/table, future answer or evaluator correctness. The inherited output organ knows only its sensory protocol, including whether one cue or two options were presented.

An exact-key or canonical-key table can explain E1 but has no reinforced answer for the held-out identities. Binary held-out labels are balanced; Latin's four withheld outputs are balanced. Frequency-only responses achieve chance (1/2 and 1/4). The source symbol-class rule and Latin missing-symbol completion rule are solvability witnesses; a simple rule learned from old experience is acceptable modest E3, not intelligence. Hidden generator tables remain evaluator-only. Same cue keys across phases, four-visible-byte validity, teaching exposure and disjoint old/new symbol rosters are checked against the unchanged fixtures.

Causal branches use the independent explicit map:

| Branch | old writes | new writes | revision writes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| W | on | on | on |
| N_old | off | on | on |
| N_new | on | off | on |

Lifetime uses all four. Binary uses W/N_old. Latin uses W/N_old/N_new. Instrumentation observes actual native calls and compares disabled-write state transitions against plasticity-off reference transitions; metadata alone is insufficient. All eight stores follow the flag. Teacher bytes and time remain identical between corresponding branches.

## Single joint adoption rule

Every condition must pass at the prespecified one-sided alpha=0.05:

1. Both E3 families: W−N lower bound >0 and W raw-accuracy lower bound above their chance values.
2. Equal-family mean E3 gain over ERROR: lower bound >0. Each individual family change vs ERROR: lower bound >−0.025.
3. Final taught endpoints: lifetime intact-old/new/revised and both E3-family old/new means ≥0.90; paired lower bounds vs ERROR >−0.05 for each.
4. Across the same complete world vector, the probability of any taught endpoint loss >0.05 has an exact binomial upper bound <0.10.
5. Online compute ratio upper bound ≤1.05 versus ERROR. Same information and teaching budget; report efficiency gain.

This is one intersection-union promotion claim, not separate selectable “wins.” Requiring every elementary gate controls that joint decision at alpha≤0.05; reported individual intervals are not simultaneously covering intervals. Q_HALF remains a diagnostic, not a second adoption candidate. A failed, unresolved or unavailable condition blocks adoption.

World-level paired t bounds are the explicitly approximate inferential method for nonconstant endpoints. No degenerate bootstrap pass is allowed. If every world value equals c, anchor c on the first predetermined world and use the remaining 63 trials to bound the mass outside c: `u=1-alpha**(1/63)`. On known support [a,b], use `LB=c-(c-a)*u`, `UB=c+(b-c)*u`. This conditional atom bound prevents falsely assigning zero uncertainty. Fewer than two complete worlds means unresolved. Harm probability uses Clopper–Pearson. No trajectories are regenerated to repair a failed scientific gate.

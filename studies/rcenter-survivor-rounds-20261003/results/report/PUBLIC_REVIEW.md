## Inference

The statistical unit is the world, N=64. The registered family contains 11 contrasts. Two-sided Bonferroni-adjusted 95% Student-t intervals use df=63. Independent inversion of the t CDF gives critical value 2.9430085852325796. Every estimate, standard deviation, interval endpoint, method, input identity and gate was compared with the official result; the largest numeric difference was 1.4572×10^-16, attributable to arithmetic evaluation order.

No observed contrast has zero variance. The prespecified fallback was nevertheless checked independently: the unclipped Hoeffding radius is width × sqrt(log(440)/128), equal to 0.21806633750765475 for raw-accuracy contrast width 1 and 0.4361326750153095 for branch/policy contrast width 2, then clipped to the registered support. Nonzero-variance Student-t intervals provide approximate, not exact finite-sample, coverage.

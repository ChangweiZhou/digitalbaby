# Frozen R_center Round 1: held-out table completion

Date: 4 October 2026 (UTC). Status: all 64 official worlds and the frozen terminal analysis completed; independent result verification accepted.

## Answer

The frozen model retained taught examples and learned new examples, but did not establish positive taught-dependent completion on the never-reinforced table queries. On this fixture, old teaching reduced final held-out accuracy relative to the matched branch with old plastic value writes disabled. Both the current combined readout and shared-only diagnostic had negative causal contrasts whose multiplicity-adjusted intervals excluded zero.

This is evidence about the registered table-completion fixture. It does not establish a general inability to transfer, nor does it identify a particular missing biological mechanism.

## Frozen decision

- The joint retention/acquisition and corresponding raw-accuracy gates pass.
- The combined and shared-only useful-completion gates fail; the causal E3 intervals are entirely negative.
- Alpha 0.5 is not an accepted secondary candidate: held-out E3 is negative, and both old and new performance violate the prespecified 5-percentage-point noninferiority requirement.
- The registered route is C: source review before any possible formation-focused next-round plan. This does not authorize a later run or prove transfer is absent in other fixtures.

No read-authority-only success, new learning mechanism, or later-round launch is inferred. Any later round still requires its own three separate design, independent-audit and test cycles, source-legality check and frozen launch review.

## What was tested

The actual frozen R_center implementation was used: shared FE0 and private CONTENT, observed-outcome teaching, unchanged core timing/state/scales, and private centered signal 0.25 minus the observed outcome. No identity ledger or new learning mechanism was added; CORR2 was not instantiated.

Each world relabeled a four-by-four V4/XOR Latin table. Twelve cells were taught and four balanced held-out cells were never reinforced. The taught cells uniquely identify the missing answers within the registered class of all 576 order-four Latin tables. A row-wise missing-symbol shortcut can also solve this fixture, so success would not by itself have demonstrated abstract operator composition.

The W branch was compared with matched N_old_relation and N_new branches. The controls selectively disabled old or new plastic value writes in both banks while preserving the registered byte stream, simulated clocks and nonplastic evolution. Thus E3 measures the effect of old plastic teaching in this exposure schedule, not a separately localized relation module or complete absence of experience.

Per world, old teaching comprised 192 records and new teaching another 192. The registered schedule included 86,400-second simulated delays and a final simulated time of 236,160 seconds. All 240 probes per world used independent disposable clones and the first pre-feedback prediction. Shared, private and combined scores were recorded. The lowest-ASCII argmax resolved ties. Alpha 1 was primary, alpha 0.5 fixed secondary, and alpha 0 diagnostic.

The state-equivalence justification for readout rescoring applies to this fixed open-loop environment, where output selection does not change the teaching byte. It does not establish state equivalence for action-dependent feedback environments.

## Execution and checks

Three distinct preflight cycles were accepted before launch: source/science contract and smoke test; causal, leakage, clocks and readout-state adversarial tests; then recovery, persistence, resource, full-life pilot and fresh-process exact replay. Rejected intermediate operational reviews and a failed preflight operations attempt remain preserved. Official worlds were disjoint from preflight worlds.

Exactly worlds 310001 through 310064 were run, once each, with zero failed official attempts and no replacements, extra worlds, outcome-driven stopping or parameter search. The terminal analysis used all 64 and the locked 11-contrast family. Each world and final analysis passed the required private backup and exact-byte readback barrier.

Official learner work consumed 8,860.66 charged seconds in total (2.46 hours); the highest official worker RSS was 302,440,448 bytes. Total charged worker time including qualification and final analysis was 9,258.70 seconds. Per-attempt limits remained 900 seconds and 768 MiB. The final analysis was separately supervised and validated.

Operational scheduling included one recorded 20-second unplanned overlap between world 310007 and a Lean check, followed later by explicitly approved monitored light-Lean concurrency. The affected world passed the original resource, simulated-clock, receipt and accounting checks. No result, clock or budget was adjusted and no world was repeated. A delayed handoff after world 54 was recorded as a scheduling gap without an established resource or private-barrier cause. Wall-time effects are not interpreted as model learning effects.

## Independent acceptance

The independent audit rechecked all64 receipts, raw-score predictions, ties, teaching/probe contracts, clock records, source/evidence locks and all11 statistical contrasts without executing the frozen analysis functions. Every official result field matched, with maximum numerical difference 1.46×10^-16. All65 private completion barriers and exact archive/readback bytes passed. The 11 report rows and four absolute accuracies were also checked independently. No scientific discrepancy was found. See PUBLIC_REVIEW.md and PUBLIC_ACCEPTANCE.json beside this report.

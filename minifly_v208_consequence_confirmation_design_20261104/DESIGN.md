# MiniFly V208 · Confirming consequence-driven action learning

**Status** DESIGN_ONLY  
**Date** November 4, 2026  
**Primary experiment** Fresh-world confirmation of V201's action–consequence loop.  
**Conditional follow-up** A separately locked test with two actions awaiting consequences. V206 QA write selection has its own experimental track.

## Decision

Does MiniFly again learn and retain the safer action after an unsignaled reversal when it receives only the outcomes of actions it chose? The development result was 100% final greedy choice on withheld renderings in 16 worlds, against 67.19% for shuffled action–outcome pairing. Actual safety during live reversal was 63.70%. V208 asks whether the paired advantage and live benefit reproduce with the same learner, a fresh roster, and explicit accounting of what the learner experiences.

The anchor is the **closed-loop V201 action–consequence experiment**, whose archived receipts are in the consolidated branch. Pin the executable V201 environment, learner, V200 address dependency, technical runner and official lock. The prior run keeps one pending action explicitly, gives exploration a fixed rate, and learns from binary outcome valence. Preserve those priors so a fresh-world result can be attributed to the same mechanism. The nominal 64-byte neutral gap in V201 was generated but never consumed by a learner sensory or clock transition. Keep the same no-update schedule for parity and label it as an inert placeholder. Charge generated bytes separately. Retention is measured after new action experience on other cues.

## Frozen learner and environment

Generate 32 worlds from the first 64 bits of SHA-256 of MINIFLY|V208|CONFIRM|i for i = 0 through 31. The engineering and V201 rosters must be disjoint. Reuse V201's random streams and rules within a world. Freeze source, seeds, generated schedules and evaluator before an acting world is opened. No tuning based on confirmation results.

- Each world has two main cues with opposite safer actions. A preferred action yields safety with probability 0.8 and the other with probability 0.2. After 256 episodes, reverse both mappings without a visible phase signal. After 256 reversal episodes, run 128 interference episodes on two different cues. In each block balance cue, trained rendering and one- versus three-byte outcome delay as in V201.
- ALIGNED sees the raw cue rendering, chooses one of two motor bytes with the V201 policy, and receives only the consequence sampled by the simulator from that action. The generic valence mapping is safety = 1 and harm = 0. The learner sees no preferred-action labels, counterfactuals, environmental probabilities, cue identity or phase.
- Keep the original 16-byte sparse address, two value columns, protected local update, one pending eligibility slot and 16-episode block flush. Preserve epsilon 0.25, the tie rule, and step sizes 0.1 and 0.3 in the first two blocks followed by 0.6. Neutral delay values advance the simulator only. They do not enter the eligibility address.
- SHUFFLED and NO-WRITE are nonacting shadows on the exact ALIGNED log. SHUFFLED permutes observed outcomes within each block and delay stratum, preserving selected actions, update order and row-importance increments. NO-WRITE consumes the same logged experience with writes disabled. The comparison estimates the effect of credit assignment on the realized history, which differs from comparing two independently exploring policies.

Each action must enter the simulator before its outcome is produced. Audit the action, eligibility slot, actual delivery time, observed outcome, write target and credited action. Counterfactual sampling is allowed only in engineering canaries. Greedy probes at birth, acquisition end, reversal start, reversal blocks 1, 4, 8 and 16, and after interference read a frozen state without creating an action episode, advancing an exploration RNG, or emitting feedback.

## What counts as confirmation

**Primary endpoint.** Final greedy choice accuracy on the two never-trained crossed renderings of both original cues after reversal and interference. Each world contributes one mean over four probes. Exact ties count one-half as in V201. Use paired two-sided 95% Student-t intervals across 32 worlds for ALIGNED minus each shadow. Record every world and count all preassigned probes. A failure or missing world blocks a complete verdict.

Require the original V201 gates on the fresh roster:

- Final held-rendering ALIGNED accuracy at least 75%. Its paired mean advantage over each shadow must be at least 15 percentage points, with both lower confidence bounds above zero.
- Final trained-rendering ALIGNED accuracy at least 80%. Each held rendering and each main cue must reach at least 65% in the world aggregate.
- Trained accuracy at acquisition end at least 80%. Under the changed mapping, trained accuracy must rise at least 30 points from reversal start to reversal block 16 and reach at least 80%. The mean held score may fall by at most 10 points after interference.
- Complete exposure, paired-write, output integrity and resource audits.

**Live safety guard.** Also require the world-level mean safety rate across the 256 live reversal actions to exceed the uniform-action expectation of 50%, with the lower bound of a two-sided 95% interval above 50%. This is an additional prospective guard for the environmental claim. The acting learner explores throughout. A perfect greedy preference would therefore yield 72.5% *expected* live safety under the locked exploration and simulator probabilities. Report observed safety, preferred-action rate and exploration rate separately for each phase, plus fixed reversal block bands 1–4, 5–8 and 9–16. The 100% development figure was a frozen choice score.

**Shortcut and reference audit.** Score uniform, fixed global action, wrapper-only and exact-prompt outcome tables. Exact prompt lookup abstains on unseen renderings. Add evaluator-only per-canonical-cue/action exponential averaging with update step 0.2, initial value 0.5 and the same observed log throughout. It receives no reversal notice. This separates success against a stale all-history average from success against a recency baseline. The evaluator alone extracts cue identity for the canonical comparator. Report its result descriptively and keep it outside the registered replication gate. A canonical cue table can solve this task. This is E1 acquisition and reversal plus **E2 transfer across renderings of known cues**. It does not establish general policy induction, multiple-action credit, or superiority per sample and compute over simple tables.

A full pass supports preparing the two-pending-action experiment. If only the frozen probe passes, report the limited choice result and the live guard failure. If the primary fails, preserve the development result and analyze whether the failure lies in acquisition, reversal, retention, or the credit contrast. No threshold or roster changes after exposure.

## Conditional follow-up · Which pending action gets the consequence?

Only after V208 passes, lock a separate design and obtain launch authorization. Keep the same environment payoff and local writer. Give the learner two bounded pending slots. Schedule two distinct cue/action events before either consequence can arrive. Randomize delivery order independently of the acting policy and balance in-order and reversed delivery. The simulator uses the actual action for each event. No correct-action signal or hidden event ID reaches the candidate.

A consequence can carry raw, observable context bytes from its originating event, alongside its safety or harm. Provide the bytes in the same channel to all arms. Use held combinations of raw forms so literal full-string matching does not alone establish reusable binding. Compare a bounded matcher over the two saved byte states with FIFO, most-recent, randomized assignment and evaluator-only true-slot assignment. The true-slot reference establishes whether the value learner can benefit given correct binding. Separate a no-identity stream, where two otherwise indistinguishable pending events make exact assignment unidentifiable without other evidence. Audit available information before training. Keep both candidate and non-oracle controls blind to the simulator's latent identity.

Score assignment against simulator provenance **before** looking at policy gains. Then measure safety and reversal behavior in separately acting policies, where trajectories can diverge, as well as matched-log credit contrasts. Require that any advance survives the out-of-order panel. The learner must improve because it credits the right earlier action, rather than because recent actions are usually correct or because it sees an answer label. No follow-up science is included in V208's counts.

## Census and three-cycle qualification

The fresh roster contains **20,480 real action episodes and outcomes**, **61,440 arm episode applications**, **40,960 learning updates**, and **6,144 read-only probe decisions**. It includes **184,320 consumed simulator sensory bytes** across prompt, intervening neutral and outcome emissions, plus **2,048 generated but unconsumed placeholder bytes**. These are environment-interface counts. The learner's value address consumes prompt bytes and its writer consumes valenced outcomes. Report motor emissions, row visits, cache use, actual outcome deliveries and probe counts separately. Hold full traces locally and retain compact source-bound receipts.

Proposed cumulative caps include all new qualification work: 24,000 physical action episodes, 72,000 arm applications, 48,000 updates, 10,000 probes, 300 CPU seconds, 600 active wall seconds, 512 MiB peak RSS and 64 MiB retained output. The earlier V201 campaign is historical evidence with its own cost ledger. Any replay used now is new charged work. The V201 roster and carry-in may not be silently recycled as confirmation.

1. **Refine → audit → test the source bridge.** Verify parent source and archive hashes, exact single-pending action semantics, zero privileged labels, and isolated role of the evaluator. Reproduce a bounded historical fixture on its original schedule. Record any interface changes and require behavioral parity.
2. **Refine → audit → test the fresh-world protocol.** Use disjoint engineering seeds. Test action-dependent consequences, reversed mappings, exact matched shuffle marginals, no-write state, probe immutability and the separate generated-byte ledger. Test positive and negative reducer cases, including the live safety guard and incomplete-world failure.
3. **Refine → audit → test the production entry point.** Run a small fresh technical roster through all phases, shortcut scoring, checksum commits and archive readback. Dry-generate and hash all 32 confirmation schedules. Lock the full cost projection and launch only after explicit authorization.
